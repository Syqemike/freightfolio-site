About

Built for small businesses, from the Gulf Coast

Freightfolio is a web design service based in Gulfport, Mississippi, focused on one thing: websites for small local businesses.

Most small businesses do not need a marketing agency. They need a website that looks professional, tells customers what they do, and brings in calls and bookings, without becoming a second job to maintain.

That is the whole service. We design the site, build it, host it, and manage it month to month. You pay one monthly price starting at $149 with $0 upfront, and you never have to think about the website again unless you want to change something.

We work with businesses across the Mississippi Gulf Coast, nearby Louisiana, the Alabama Gulf Coast, and small businesses anywhere in the United States. If you run a shop, a trade, a practice, or a service, and you want a website that pulls its weight, that is who we build for.

Every project starts the same way: a free design concept built around your business, so you can see what you are getting before you commit to anything.

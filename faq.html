Questions

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers. Where a detail is still being finalized, we say so instead of guessing.

Is it really $0 upfront?

Yes. Design, build, hosting setup, and launch are all covered by the monthly price. You never pay a setup fee or deposit. The first charge is your first month of the plan you choose.

How does billing work?

Billing is month-to-month: Starter $149/mo, Professional $199/mo, Business Growth $299/mo. There is no annual contract.

What exactly do you build and manage?

We design a custom website for your business, build it, host it, and manage it on an ongoing basis: content updates, new photos, and small changes. Higher tiers add contact forms, Google Business Profile assistance, booking integration, and monthly reports. See the Services page for the full breakdown.

Do I need any technical skills?

No. You never touch code, hosting logins, or settings. If you want something changed, you email us and we handle it.

What is the free website preview?

Before you choose a plan, we design a concept of your website based on your business, and send it to you free. It shows the design direction, layout, and sections we would build. No payment and no commitment required.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Billing is month-to-month and you can cancel whenever. There is no minimum commitment. Email us at hello@freightfolio.net to cancel.

Do I get a refund if I cancel mid-month?

No. Billing is month-to-month, so there are no refunds. If you are unhappy, cancel.

Who owns the domain name?

We do. Your website's domain is owned and managed by us while you are subscribed. If you want to own the domain yourself, you can buy it from us.

What does "ongoing management" include each month?

Ongoing management covers content updates, new photos, and small changes to keep the site current. Each plan includes a set number of updates per month: Starter, 2 updates; Professional, 5 updates; Business Growth, 10 updates.

What happens to my website if I cancel my subscription?

Your website stays up for 1 month after your subscription ends, then it comes down. If you would rather keep it permanently, you can buy the website outright for a one-time payment of $2,500.

Will my website show up on Google?

We build every site with search-friendly basics: clean structure, written content, and mobile-friendly design. Professional and Business Growth plans include Google Business Profile assistance. We do not promise specific rankings, because no honest company can guarantee those.

How fast can my site go live?

It depends on the concept approval and how much content we need from you. We move as fast as your feedback allows, but we do not promise instant delivery. A realistic timeline is discussed when you approve your concept.

What happens if my payment fails?

If a payment fails, we will send you 4 notices, one each week. If payment still has not been made after the fourth notice, your website goes down until payment is made.

Still have questions?

Email us any time, or start with a free preview and ask us directly.

The service

Freightfolio designs a custom website for your business, builds it, hosts it, and manages it on an ongoing basis according to the plan you choose:

  • Starter ($149/mo): custom website up to 3 pages, hosting, ongoing management.
  • Professional ($199/mo): custom website up to 7 pages, hosting, ongoing management, contact forms, Google Business Profile assistance.
  • Business Growth ($299/mo): custom website up to 12 pages, hosting, ongoing management, booking integration, monthly reports.

Every engagement begins with a free design concept. Work on the full website begins after you approve the concept and select a plan.

Pricing and billing

  • All plans are billed month-to-month. There is no annual contract.
  • $0 upfront: design, build, hosting setup, and launch are covered by the monthly price.

Outright purchase

You may buy your website outright for a one-time payment of $2,500, instead of continuing the monthly subscription.

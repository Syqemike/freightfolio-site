Terms of service

The fine print, in plain language.

Freightfolio is a done-for-you freight billing documentation service. You send us your paperwork; we organize, validate, and process it into billing-ready packets and actionable reports. These terms describe what you can expect from us and what we expect from you.

1. The service

Freightfolio processes freight documents you provide — rate confirmations, bills of lading, proofs of delivery, invoices, and related paperwork — into billing-readiness reports. A “carrier ready” determination means a load’s paperwork is payable to the carrier based on the documents received; it is not a guarantee of payment, billing approval, or any specific financial outcome. Our reports are only as good as the documents you send: missing, contradictory, or unreadable paperwork will be flagged, not guessed at.

2. Plans, billing, and renewals

Monthly plans (Starter $39, Business $149, Premium $369) are billed monthly in advance and renew automatically until canceled. Each plan includes a monthly document volume (100 / 400 / 1,000 files). Documents uploaded beyond your plan’s volume are currently held to the plan allowance; overage billing terms will be confirmed in writing before any overage charges apply. You can cancel anytime from your account or by emailing hello@freightfolio.net; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you keep service through that date.

3. Mini-pilot and the $9.99 first-month credit

The $9.99 mini-pilot is a one-time purchase covering up to 10 documents; no subscription is created. If you complete the mini-pilot and then subscribe to a monthly plan, a one-time $9.99 credit is applied to your first subscription payment. The credit applies once per customer, cannot exceed $9.99, and is not a free month.

4. Refunds

Because the service is work performed on your documents, completed processing is non-refundable. If we have not yet started processing your documents, you can request a full refund before work begins. Subscription cancellations stop future charges; we do not refund partial months.

5. Missing-Document Recovery add-on

The Recovery add-on ($99/month, 50 document requests per month) authorizes us to contact carriers on your behalf to request missing paperwork, using only the contacts, templates, cadence, and escalation rules you approve. Requests beyond the monthly allowance wait in the queue for the next billing cycle at no extra charge. You can pause or cancel the add-on at any time; active chases complete their current attempt and then stop.

6. Acceptable use

You agree to upload only documents you have the right to share, to keep your login credentials confidential, and not to misuse the service (including attempting to access other customers’ documents, uploading malware, or reselling the service without written agreement).

7. Data and deletion

How we handle your documents is described in our privacy & data-handling policy. Uploaded documents and reports are automatically deleted 30 days after upload; you can request earlier deletion any time and we confirm in writing when it’s done.

8. Liability limits

Freightfolio does not guarantee faster payment, billing approval, or any specific financial outcome. To the maximum extent permitted by law, our liability for any claim related to the service is limited to the amounts you paid us in the three months before the claim arose. We are not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

9. Changes

We may update these terms as the service evolves. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date, and continued use of the service after the change constitutes acceptance.

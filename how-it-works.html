The process

How it works

Four steps. No technical knowledge needed on your end. Click any step to see what happens.

Tell us about your business

Fill out a short request form: your business name, what you do, and what you want customers to know.

It takes a few minutes. Tell us about your services, your hours, and anything that makes your business different. If you have a current website, share the link so we can see what is working and what is not.

We create a free website concept

We design a preview of your website, built around your business. Free, with no obligation.

Each concept is designed from scratch for your business: layout, colors, typography, and sections written for what you do. You see it before you spend a dollar.

You approve and select a plan

Like the concept? Pick the plan that fits: Starter $149/mo, Professional $199/mo, or Business Growth $299/mo.

Want changes to the concept? We revise it with you until it feels right. Then choose your plan based on how many pages you need and which features matter: contact forms, Google Business Profile help, booking integration, and monthly reports on the higher tiers. Still $0 upfront.

We build, host, and manage everything

Your full website goes live. We host it, update it, and keep it running. You run your business.

After launch, ongoing management is part of your plan: content updates, new photos, and small changes handled for you. Billing is month-to-month, and the site stays live as long as your subscription is active.

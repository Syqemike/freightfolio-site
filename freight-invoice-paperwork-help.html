Where it breaks

The paperwork problems that stall invoices.

The missing POD

The load delivered fine, but the signed proof of delivery never came back. Without it, most brokers will not invoice and most factors will not fund. Days pass while someone tracks it down.

The rate that does not match

The rate confirmation says one number, the carrier invoice says another. Somebody has to find both documents, compare them line by line, and sort out which one is right before the invoice can go.

The exception with no trail

Detention, lumper fees, and truck-ordered-not-used charges arrive as stray receipts in someone’s inbox. If they are not matched to the load, the invoice goes out short, or does not go out at all.

The re-keying treadmill

Shipper, consignee, commodity, weight, dates, typed by hand from PDFs into a spreadsheet or TMS. Every load, every week, hours of work a machine should do.

The problem

The invoice is good. The paperwork isn’t.

Missing or unsigned PODs are the single most common reason a good invoice sits unbilled. Most factors won’t fund a load without a signed POD — so the invoice waits, AR ages, cash tightens, and somebody spends the week calling drivers and digging through inboxes instead of moving freight.

Illustrative example — plug in your own numbers

40 loads delivered per week × 1 in 5 PODs missing on first pass = 8 chases/week
~15 minutes per chase (call, text, email, log it) = 2 hours/week
That’s a full workday every month spent chasing paper — before counting the days each invoice sits unbilled.
Cash view: on $500k of monthly credit sales, each extra day of DSO is roughly $16k of your cash sitting idle.

Illustrative figures, not promises or guarantees of results.

Invoices wait on paper

A delivered load with an unsigned POD is an invoice that usually can’t go out — and generally can’t be factored. Every missing signature is days of your cash sitting still.

Re-keying eats the week

Shipper, consignee, commodity, weight, dates — typed by hand from PDFs into spreadsheets and TMS fields. Hours per week of work a machine should do.

Exceptions hide in inboxes

Detention, lumper, and TONU receipts arrive as email attachments with no trail. When the packet doesn’t tie out, the invoice — and the money — stalls.

FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers.

Why not just buy Docparser for $74/month?

You absolutely can — it’s a solid tool. The question is who builds the templates, who checks every extraction, and who assembles the billing-ready packet per load. Docparser gives you software; we give you the finished outcome — extracted, matched packets and a weekly billing-readiness report — without adding to anyone’s plate. If you’ve got someone with ten free hours a week to run the tool, buy the tool. If you want us to also chase missing documents and resolve exceptions by hand, that’s our managed service.

Can we start smaller?

That’s what the mini-pilot is: $9.99, up to 10 documents, 3–5 business days, on your actual paperwork. Complete the pilot and subscribe, and you get a one-time $9.99 credit toward your first subscription month.

What if our document volume grows?

Overage is $0.40 per document beyond your plan’s volume, and we always bill the cheapest eligible total — so exceeding a tier by a few documents costs a few dollars, not a tier jump. If your volume keeps growing, we’ll tell you when managed volume pricing makes sense.

Is our data safe? These documents have our rates in them.

Fair question. Your documents are processed for your business only — never used to train shared models, never sold, never shared with anyone. Retention terms go in the service agreement, and you can request deletion any time.

What if something gets misread?

Automated checks run on every packet, and low-confidence extractions get flagged for human review instead of silently passed through. The pilot proves accuracy on your own documents before you pay for a month of service. If you want hands-on exception resolution as an ongoing service, that’s our managed tier.

We don’t have time to onboard something new.

That’s exactly why there’s nothing to install and no system to switch. Your team forwards documents to an email address — that’s the whole change on your side. Onboarding takes about two hours of your time total: first findings land within 7–10 business days, and the complete pilot report is in your hands by the end of week three.

What if it doesn’t work — can we get out?

Then we part ways and you keep everything: every report and all structured data we produced, exported to Excel. Monthly plans cancel any time. The mini-pilot itself is built as the exit ramp — $9.99, a few days, and the go/no-go call is yours.

Do you chase missing PODs for us?

We flag them — precisely. Every morning your exception list shows exactly which documents are missing, from whom, and for which load, so the chase is a two-minute email instead of an afternoon of digging. The actual follow-up stays with your team, because only you have the driver and shipper relationships.

Can you guarantee we’ll invoice X days faster?

No — and you should distrust anyone who guarantees that before seeing your paperwork. What the pilot does is measure your actual before-and-after on your own loads, so you decide with real numbers.

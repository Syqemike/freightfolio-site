What does $0 upfront actually mean?

No setup fee or deposit. Design, build, hosting setup and launch are covered by your monthly plan. Your first charge is the first month, after you approve your concept and start your plan.

What happens after my website goes live?

We host and manage it. Content updates include new text, photos and small layout changes. The published terms include 2 updates/month on Starter, 5 on Professional and 10 on Business Growth.

Can I cancel, and who owns the website?

You can cancel by email with no minimum commitment. There are no refunds. Your website stays up for one month after the subscription ends, then comes down. Your supplied content remains yours; Freightfolio owns the design and code, licensed during your subscription. Freightfolio owns and manages the domain while subscribed. See the full terms for purchase and domain options.

How soon can we launch?

Timing depends on concept approval, content and feedback. We discuss a realistic timeline when you approve the concept. We don’t promise instant delivery.

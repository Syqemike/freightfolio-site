The problem

Missing PODs and BOLs stall your billing.

A delivered load with an unsigned POD is an invoice that usually cannot go out, and generally cannot be factored. Every missing signature is days of your cash sitting still while somebody calls drivers and digs through inboxes instead of moving freight.

Invoices wait on paper

Most factors will not fund a load without a signed POD. When the paperwork is incomplete, the invoice waits, AR ages, and cash tightens.

Re-keying eats the week

Shipper, consignee, commodity, weight, dates, typed by hand from PDFs into spreadsheets and TMS fields. Hours per week of work a machine should do.

Exceptions hide in inboxes

Detention, lumper, and TONU receipts arrive as email attachments with no trail. When the packet does not tie out, the invoice and the money stall.

FAQ

Questions brokers ask us.

What does a freight broker billing service actually do?

It takes the document work off your plate: extracting data from rate confirmations, BOLs, PODs, and receipts, checking that every load’s packet is complete, and telling you exactly what is ready to invoice and what is blocked. You still send the invoices and make the billing decisions, we make sure the paperwork behind them is right.

Do you chase missing PODs for us?

We flag them, precisely. Every morning your exception list shows exactly which documents are missing, from whom, and for which load, so the chase is a two-minute email instead of an afternoon of digging. The actual follow-up stays with your team, because only you have the driver and shipper relationships.

How is this different from document-parsing software?

Software gives you a tool. Freightfolio gives you the finished outcome: checked packets and a weekly billing-readiness report, without adding to anyone’s plate. If you have someone with ten free hours a week to run the tool, buy the tool.

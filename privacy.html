Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Freightfolio ("we") provides website design, hosting, and management services for small businesses. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Request form details. When you request a free website preview, we collect the information you enter: your business name, your name, email address, phone number (if provided), current website (if provided), business type, desired features, and comments.
  • Basic technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log standard technical information such as IP addresses and browser types for security and reliability.

How we use it

  • To respond to your preview request and communicate about our services.
  • To design the website concept you asked for.
  • To operate, secure, and improve this website.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their marketing.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Essential, strictly functional storage may be used by our hosting provider to serve the site reliably.

Data retention

We keep request form submissions for as long as needed to respond and provide our services. You can ask us to delete your information at any time by emailing hello@freightfolio.net.

Your rights

You can request a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@freightfolio.net and we will respond.

Children

This website is intended for business owners and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and post the new version on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@freightfolio.net, Gulfport, Mississippi.

Our Story

Small-town roots, high-end craft

Elysian Salon Spa was founded in 2017 by co-owners Bach Kimball and Lindsey Fowler, and expanded in early 2025 to bring the salon and spa experience together in one space.

The idea has never changed: bring the polish of a high-end beauty destination to the Mississippi Gulf Coast, delivered with the warmth of neighbors who know your name.

Bach Kimball & Lindsey Fowler, co-owners

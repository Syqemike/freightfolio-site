About

Windsor Manor Wedding Venue is a historic 1921 Tudor venue at 3594 Windsor Spring Rd in Hephzibah, in the Augusta area. The venue offers all-inclusive weddings for up to 100 guests, with dedicated planners and coordinators, European-inspired gardens, and an outdoor pavilion. Tours are by appointment only.

About

Benny Ravello's is a late-night pizza shop on Upper King in Charleston, South Carolina, opened in 2017 in a building carrying the original Benedito's persona. The Ravello name refers to the Italian coastal town. Dough is made fresh in-house daily, and the shop sells pizza by the slice and 28 inch pies. Flavors include cheese, pepperoni, garlic mushroom, pesto chicken, sausage, and a mac n cheese slice. Counter seating and takeout are available, and delivery runs through DoorDash, Grubhub, and Uber Eats.

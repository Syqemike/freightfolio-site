A family operation, five generations in.

The Nelson family has fished this area for five generations. John Nelson and Matt White formed Nelson Charter Fishing in 2010, starting from a rustic fishing marina and boatyard and running trips into the secluded backwater areas around Gulf Shores.

Trips run in the mornings, afternoons, and at night. The focus is inshore water: calm, shallow, and close to shore, with scenery, sea birds, dolphins, and alligators along the way.

Redfish and speckled trout lead the list. Depending on what is biting, trips also go after white trout, sheepshead, flounder, and more.

What is biting changes with conditions. The charter fishes for whatever species is biting at the time.

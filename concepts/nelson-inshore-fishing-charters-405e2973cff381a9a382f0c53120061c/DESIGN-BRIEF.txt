DESIGN BRIEF - Nelson Inshore Fishing Charters concept
Business: Nelson Inshore Fishing Charters, Gulf Shores, AL
Category: Fishing charter, inshore trips and dolphin cruises

AESTHETIC: Working marina, not resort brochure. Deep bayou teal panels, warm
sand paper background, and one rust red accent taken from redfish coloring.
The hero pairs a dark teal text block with a wide sunlit water photo, so the
first screen reads as a ledger entry from a charter office rather than a
generic travel template.

TYPOGRAPHY: Fraunces (slab serif) for headings, for a weathered, printed
ledger feel. Karla for body copy, kept plain and direct. No oversized display
type, no gradient text, no pill buttons.

PALETTE: Deep teal #143c3a, darker teal #1e524e, sand #f5f0e6, paper #fffdf7,
ink #1e2422, rust #b4552d, hairline #d8cfbc.

IMAGERY: Four real photos from the business's own website (water, redfish
catch, trout catch, dolphin). Each caption describes what the photo actually
shows; two source filenames were misleading and were corrected after viewing.
No stock photos, no illustrations, no SVG artwork anywhere.

LAYOUT: Single page. Disclosure bar on top, business name and section nav
below it. Hero text block beside the hero photo. Trips as a rate ledger with
price columns, not cards. Photos in a two column gallery. Waters and target
species as a two column reference section. Contact block in dark teal with
call, email, and directions actions. Mobile layout stacks every grid to one
column and keeps the call button prominent.

COPY RULES APPLIED: Every trip length and price comes from the charter's own
services page. No catch guarantees, no availability claims, no licensing
claims, no urgency language. Rating appears exactly once with source and
count. Hours carry a variance note because listings disagree. No Book Now
button because no online booking destination was verified.

About

A Lexington salon since 2007

Opened by Carla Brown

The Hive Salon opened in 2007, founded by Carla Brown.

The salon focuses on precision cutting and ammonia-free, eco-conscious color, and it showcases wares from local artists.

It serves men, women and children from its Deweese Street location in Lexington.

Services

The Hunting Company in Gulf Shores is a worldwide hunting, fishing, and bowfishing guide service led by Dustin Mizell. The company offers personally guided hunting in the southeastern United States, books hunting trips around the world, and runs full-service outdoor film production, plus taxidermy, consulting, land sales, sporting goods, and live animal sales. Fish-Kabob bowfishing is also offered.

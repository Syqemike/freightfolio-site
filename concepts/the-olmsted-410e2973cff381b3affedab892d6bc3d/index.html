About

The Olmsted is a historic wedding and event venue at 3701 Frankfort Avenue in Louisville, Kentucky, built in 1925 to 1927 on the Masonic Home campus, on 80 acres. The venue hosts weddings, galas and fundraisers, and corporate events in three event spaces for up to 600 guests. Masterson's Catering is the in-house caterer, serving Kentucky Hot Brown, Derby pie, and specialty cocktails. Hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Call (502) 899-7670 to ask about an event.

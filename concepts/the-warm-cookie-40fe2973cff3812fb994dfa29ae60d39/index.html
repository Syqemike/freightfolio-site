About

The Warm Cookie is a family-run bakery in Lincoln, Nebraska, since 2014, at 3700 South 9th Street, Suite A. Warm cookies are baked fresh and delivered to your door, sold by the dozen, gift-wrapped with a handwritten card, with free delivery within Lincoln city limits. In-store pickup is also available. Flavors include The Rachel, an oatmeal-chocolate-butterscotch cookie, Snickerdoodle, gluten-free chocolate chip, and cinnamon rolls. Hours are Monday to Friday 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and Saturday and Sunday closed. Call (402) 310-2881 to order.

About

Sakura Express is a Japanese restaurant in Pascagoula serving lunch and dinner at the counter. The menu covers sushi and sashimi, hibachi entrees, bento boxes, and yakisoba, with rolls that include the Sakura Roll and the Dynamite Roll. Guests order at the counter and eat in for lunch or dinner.

About

Wood and Thread Boutique is a contemporary women's boutique in Winston-Salem with boho styling. The racks hold dresses, graphic tees, soft knits, and casual layers, plus accessories, candles, home goods, and baby clothes. The shop keeps a relaxed, wearable range for everyday dressing.

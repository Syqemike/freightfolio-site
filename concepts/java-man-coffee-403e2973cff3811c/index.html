A calm stop downtown

Java Man Coffee sits in the heart of downtown Portland, close to Pioneer Square. The shop is known as a quiet, plant-filled room where people work on laptops over coffee, and it offers free Wi-Fi.

Breakfast here runs from bagel and croissant sandwiches to beignets and an almond croissant, and lunch covers a BLT on croissant, a turkey and cheese sandwich, and paninis. Smoothies and milkshakes round out the drinks list alongside espresso, lattes, and matcha.

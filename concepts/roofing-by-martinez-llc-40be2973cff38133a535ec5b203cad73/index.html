About

Roofing By Martinez LLC is a roofing company at 2119 Lakeshore Dr in Shreveport, serving the Shreveport-Bossier City area. The company is licensed, bonded, and insured, and its work carries a 1-year workmanship warranty in addition to manufacturer shingle warranties. It is a CertainTeed Master Shingle Applicator, and its crews hand-nail shingles.

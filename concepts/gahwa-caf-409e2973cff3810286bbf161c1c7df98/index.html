About

Gahwa Café is a Mediterranean cafe and hookah lounge in the Harbor Village Plaza in Jacksonville. The menu runs from Turkish sand coffee and Arabic coffee and teas to specialty lattes, with hummus, lamb shawarma, Turkish pizza, and baklava and Arabic desserts alongside.

King Cakes, Fresh from Our Ovens

Our king cakes are a local favorite, made fresh on premise. One season, the King Cake flavor of Cammie's Old Dutch ice cream was made with king cakes from Two Sisters. Ask about whole king cakes for parties and Mardi Gras season.

The patio

Dine Under the Oak Trees

Guests love the spacious deck shaded by majestic oak trees. Bring the dog along. The patio is dog-friendly, and reviewers say it runs ten degrees cooler under the trees on a Gulf Coast afternoon.

"Great food and cool patio. Literally, 10 degrees cooler under these amazing oak trees." - a recent guest review

Morning on the Patio

Indoor seating is available too, right off Scenic 98. Coffee, breakfast, and the morning sun, your choice of table.

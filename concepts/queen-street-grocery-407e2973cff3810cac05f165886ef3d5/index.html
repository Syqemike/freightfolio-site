A cafe, grocery, and coffee shop on Queen Street in Charleston.

Queen Street Grocery is a cafe, grocery, and coffee shop in Charleston, serving savory and sweet crepes, hot-pressed sandwiches, all-day breakfast, smoothies, and salads, plus groceries and local craft beer and wine to take home. Signature items include the Tenacious Turkey, the Italiano, the Cubano, a granola parfait, and a burrito bowl.

Queen City Bakery is a scratch-made bakery and cafe in a renovated historic building in downtown Sioux Falls, run by Mitch Jackson and Kristine Moberg, per Wanderlog. Customer favorites include monster cookies, brown butter latte, honey nut cheerio latte, spinach bacon cheddar quiche, chocolate espresso cake, and three berry pie.

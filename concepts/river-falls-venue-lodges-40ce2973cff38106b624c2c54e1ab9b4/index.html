About

River Falls Venue and Lodges is a wedding venue and lodges on the edge of Palo Duro Canyon near Amarillo. The property offers multiple ceremony sites, two event spaces, and three lodges with on-site accommodations. Rentals run from day-only to full-weekend stays.

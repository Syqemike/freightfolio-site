A food truck at Trailhead Beer Market

El Gato Peligroso is a Mexican-inspired food truck at Trailhead Beer Market, 1317 Island Home Ave in Knoxville. The menu includes smash burgers, double fried fries, and street corn, plus rotating weekly specials such as a taco special, banh mi, halloumi tacos, pastrami, schnitzel, and salmon dip. Seating is family-friendly and outdoors. DoorDash ordering is available per research.

A coffee house in Gautier

Delo's Heavenly House of Coffee is a coffee house at 3880 Gautier Vancleave Rd Ste 8 in Gautier, run by Ms. Delores. Reviews describe 20 plus years in coffee. The menu includes specialty lattes, a blueberry scone, chocolate banana bread, and cookies, plus Death by Chocolate. There is an upstairs loft with free Wi-Fi, takeout, and outdoor seating.

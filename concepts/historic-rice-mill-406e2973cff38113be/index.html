An 1861 waterfront venue

Historic Rice Mill is a waterfront wedding and event venue at 17 Lockwood Dr #101 in Charleston, built in 1861 and listed on the National Register of Historic Places. The ground-floor event space has exposed brick, wide plank wood floors, large double windows, pressed tin ceilings, a mahogany and marble bar, and a dance floor, plus two marina patios with sunset views. Capacity is up to 300 people. The exclusive caterer is Good Food Catering. Call to ask about an event.

Our Story

Four decades on the boulevard

Hair Unlimited sits in the M.L.K. Plaza on North Martin Luther King Boulevard, in the heart of the historic Westside known as the Black Strip.

For decades the shop has been a gathering place for the neighborhood. Barber Mack Smith has cut hair here since 1981, and generations of regulars have passed through the chairs.

The shop takes part in the Southern Nevada Health District Barbershop Health Outreach Project. The space is wheelchair accessible, and visits are by appointment.

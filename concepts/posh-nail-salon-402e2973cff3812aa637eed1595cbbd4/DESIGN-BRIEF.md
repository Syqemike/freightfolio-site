# Design Brief: Posh Nail Salon (Gulf Shores, AL) — Website Concept

## Aesthetic
COASTAL BRIGHT. Playful beach-town warmth for a Gulf Shores nail salon: sunny, airy,
friendly. Big rounded photo arches, horizontal-scrolling service chips, generous
whitespace, color-blocked bands. Deliberately NOT plum/editorial, NOT minimal-zen,
NOT classic-brass elegance. No gradients, no glowing blobs, no illustrations, photos only.

## Typography
- Display: Fraunces (soft serif, friendly and rounded, headings and brand mark)
- Body: DM Sans (clean and legible, body copy, nav, buttons)

## Palette
- Sea-glass teal `#2a9d8f` (primary, hero accents, CTA buttons, chip borders)
- Warm sand `#f4f1de` (section backgrounds, calm breathing room)
- Coral pop `#ff7f6e` (secondary accents, waxing band, call buttons)
- White `#ffffff` (page base, cards)
- Ink `#20313a` (body text), deep teal `#1f7a70` (headings on light)

## Imagery plan
7 real photos of the actual salon from its public directory listings
(bestprosintown.com photos page; sources at cdn*.localdatacdn.com):
- hero-color-wall.jpg: polish color wall with salon interior behind (hero arch)
- storefront.jpg: salon sign on the building (visit page)
- color-wheel.jpg: O-P-I gel display rings inside the salon (about/services)
- mani-ombre.jpg: ombre french manicure (services/gallery)
- mani-pink.jpg: pink manicure (gallery)
- mani-gray.jpg: gray gel manicure (gallery)
- pedi-lavender.jpg: lavender pedicure (services/gallery)
No stock photos, no labeled stock needed. No concept art, no SVG illustrations.

## Layout
- Centered airy hero with a big arched photo, eyebrow label, Fraunces headline,
  sand background, rating line under the hero on index only.
- Horizontal scroll chips for the nail services (mobile-friendly swipe row).
- Two full-width color-blocked bands: teal for eyelash extensions, coral for waxing.
- Alternating photo arch + text rows on the home page; plain grid gallery;
  hours card + map link on the visit page.
- Sticky slim header with brand mark, section nav (Home, Services, Gallery, Visit),
  and a coral "Call to book" pill linking tel:+12519480404.

## Customer journey
Land on a bright, welcoming hero that names the business and town. Swipe through the
services, read the salon's own words, see real photos, check Mon-Sat hours, then tap
to call. No online booking exists, so the phone CTA is the single conversion path.
Every page carries a "Website Design Preview" disclosure so nothing reads as an
official business site.

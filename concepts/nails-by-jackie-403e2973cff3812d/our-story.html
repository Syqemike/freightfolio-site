A family salon, fifteen years strong

Nails by Jackie has served the Chesapeake community for more than 15 years. We are a family-run salon, which means the people who greet you at the door are the people whose name is on the sign.

Over the years, many of our customers have become regulars who trust us with their manicures, pedicures, and special-occasion sets. We keep our prices moderate, our work careful, and our door open to walk-ins.

If you are new to the salon, come say hello. We will take care of the rest.

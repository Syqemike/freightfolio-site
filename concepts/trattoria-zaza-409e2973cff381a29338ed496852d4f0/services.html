Services at Trattoria Zaza

A mom-and-pop pizza shop in downtown Birmingham, near the Lyric and BJCC, serving Roman-style pizza al taglio by the slice.

Services

Roman-style pizza al taglio by the slice

Roman-style pizza al taglio by the slice with creative toppings: the TBONE pizza with prosciutto, potato, gorgonzola, and rosemary, plus three-cheese and Sicilian pizzas.

Pasta

Pasta: fettuccine cacio e pepe, penne all'arrabbiata, and breakfast pasta.

Breakfast and brunch

Breakfast and brunch: French toast, eggs all'arrabbiata, and breakfast pizza.

Salads, soups, sandwiches, and desserts

Salads, soups, sandwiches, and desserts, with vegan and vegetarian options.

Counter service with outdoor seating

Counter service with outdoor seating and no reservations. Online ordering runs through third-party listings; call to ask about ordering options.

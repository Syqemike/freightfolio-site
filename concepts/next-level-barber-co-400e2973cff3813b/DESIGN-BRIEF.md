# Design Brief: Next Level Barber Co. (Portland, ME)

## Business personality
Modern-traditional men's shop in Portland's Old Port. Owner Greg + barber Joe. Skin-fade specialty, family-friendly. A 5.0/94 Google reviews shop that feels premium but approachable.

## Design direction
Editorial two-column layout: left text column with a brass eyebrow label and an italic accent headline, right stacked photo with a floating "Skin-fade specialists" stamp. Serif display (Fraunces) + clean sans (Inter).
Palette: pine green (#23402f), warm cream (#f6f1e4), brass (#b0894f). Grounded in a classic-meets-modern Portland men's shop.
Differentiated from Don's (Bitter + mono, dark brown paper) and Presidential Grooming (Cormorant + Jost, charcoal + gold) by the light cream ground, Fraunces italics, and split editorial hero.

## Section order
Disclosure > sticky head (name + nav) > split hero (h1, rating once, Book Now) > services (two-column ruled list) > about (Greg & Joe, family-friendly) > gallery (dark green band, 4 photos) > hours + visit card > footer.

## Imagery
5 stock photos (Pexels): modern barbershop haircut, clipper fade close-up, haircut detail, boy's haircut (family-friendly), razor/comb styling. All labeled "Stock photo" in captions.

## Customer journey
Visitor sees name, specialty, rating, and Book Now immediately. Services list with one verified price (scissor cuts from $50). Hours table, visit card with tel: link and Vagaro Book Now (booking page verified: title matches business name and Portland ME).

## Verified facts used
5.0/94 Google reviews. Vagaro booking page vagaro.com/nextlevelbarberco verified via page title. Address 437 Congress St, Portland ME 04101. Phone +1 207-808-8542. Hours Mon-Fri 9-6:30, Sat 9-5, Sun 10-2. Services per research. No invented facts.

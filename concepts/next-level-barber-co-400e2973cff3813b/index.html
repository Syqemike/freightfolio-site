The shop

Old Port grooming, done right

Next Level Barber Co. sits on Congress Street in Portland's Old Port, pairing modern style with the traditional barbershop playbook. Greg runs the shop alongside barber Joe, and the work speaks for itself in the reviews.

Whether you want a razor-sharp skin fade, a classic scissor cut, or a full hot-towel shave, you will leave the chair looking like yourself on your best day.

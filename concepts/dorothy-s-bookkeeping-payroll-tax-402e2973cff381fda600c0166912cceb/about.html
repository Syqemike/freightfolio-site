Dorothy's Bookkeeping Payroll & Tax is a bookkeeping, payroll, and tax office at 719 E 18th St in Owensboro, Kentucky.

The focus is small business. Owners have enough to run without also carrying the books, the payroll, and the tax paperwork on their own shoulders. That is the work this office takes on, handled by people you can reach by phone.

Larger companies are served too. The same careful approach applies either way: steady records, payroll handled, taxes filed.

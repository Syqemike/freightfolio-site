Our Story

Serving the Northshore since 2006

Marys Bed and Biscuit is a family-owned, independent business in the heart of Olde Towne Slidell. For nearly twenty years, local pet owners have trusted Marys with the members of the family who happen to have four legs.

The philosophy is right there in the name: bed and biscuit. A comfortable place to rest, good care, and maybe a treat, like home.

## Aesthetic
Eclectic coastal maximalist. A joyful, personality-packed treasure-hunt gift shop, not a generic boutique. Bright saturated color, playful rotation and sticker-style cards, a scrolling text marquee strip. Feels like walking into a shop where every shelf holds a surprise.

## Typography
- Headings: "Baloo 2" (Google Fonts), bold rounded playful display serif feel.
- Body: "Nunito" (Google Fonts), clean and highly readable.
- Accent labels: uppercase, letter-spaced Nunito for section eyebrows.

## Palette
- Coral #f4655a (primary pop)
- Teal #12836f and deep sea teal #0d4f4a (grounds the page)
- Sunshine yellow #ffc93f (badges, highlights)
- Cream #fff6e8 (page background)
- Ink #233a3a (body text)
Cards rotate slightly and use pastel tints (blush, mint, butter) for the treasure-map grid.

## Layout
1. Slim teal disclosure strip: "Design preview" notice (top of page).
2. Header: cream, playful wordmark, simple nav (Treasures, Signatures, Visit), coral "Call the Shop" button.
3. Hero: full-bleed photo-1 with deep teal overlay, big Baloo 2 headline, award line, single rating badge, two CTAs (Call the Shop / Plan Your Visit).
4. Marquee strip: sunshine yellow, scrolling text listing departments (pure text animation, no graphics).
5. "The Treasure Map": staggered, slightly rotated pastel cards for the six departments (Statement Jewelry with photo-2; Headwear; Cards & Napkins; Local Art; Home Decor & Furniture with photo-6; Lighting).
6. "Only at Social Chair" signature section: photo-3 large + copy on signature hand-painted chairs; two mini rows: free gift wrapping (photo-4), interior design services (photo-5).
7. Owner note: Yuki Northington, HGTV Design Star alum. Factual only, no invented quotes.
8. Visit section: hours card + address/phone/maps card, "Call the Shop" CTA, Facebook link.
9. Footer: design-preview disclaimer, "Concept by Freightfolio" + https://freightfolio.net link.

## Customer journey
Land on a burst of color -> feel the treasure-hunt promise -> scan departments -> see signature services -> find hours/address -> call the shop or open Facebook/maps.

## Copy rules observed
No em dashes. No "No X, no Y" triples. No banned AI phrases. No invented facts, quotes, testimonials, or availability promises. CTAs are tel: calls only.

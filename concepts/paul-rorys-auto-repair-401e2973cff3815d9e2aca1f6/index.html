The shop

A family shop, not a chain.

Paul & Rory's is a family-owned repair shop in Slidell. When you call, you talk to the people who will actually work on your car. When you drop it off, it goes on the lift with techs who specialize in the make you drive.

European cars reward a shop that knows them. We keep up with the diagnostics and the service needs of German, Swedish, and British makes, and we treat every car that rolls in like it belongs to family.

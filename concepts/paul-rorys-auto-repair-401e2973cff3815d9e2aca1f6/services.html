A note on makes

European first, domestic welcome.

Our specialty is European: BMW, Mercedes-Benz, VW, Audi, Mini, Volvo, and Jaguar. These cars need specific diagnostics, oil specs, and parts knowledge, and that is where this shop shines.

We also service the domestics and imports most families drive: Toyota, Lexus, Honda, Acura, Infiniti, Ford, Chevy, Jeep, Mazda, and Hyundai. If you are unsure whether we work on your car, call and ask.

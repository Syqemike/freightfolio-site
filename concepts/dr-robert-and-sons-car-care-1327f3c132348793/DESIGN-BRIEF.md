# Design Brief — Dr Robert and Sons Car Care Clinic (dr-robert-and-sons-car-care-1327f3c132348793)

Family-owned auto repair: Dr. Rob (Senior A-Level Master Certified Technician), wife Kristi (financial manager). 3725 Pontchartrain Drive, Slidell, LA 70458. Phone (985) 641-8300.
"The Car Doctor" / "Car Doctor Shop" branding (from their own newsletters).
Services: complete automotive and light truck repair (preventive maintenance through engine and transmission exchange), tires, brakes, batteries; DEQ-certified repair shop; specializes in 80s-90s classic vehicles alongside modern makes.
Hours: Mon-Fri 8-5; Sat 8-12; Sun closed. Rating: "4.8 from 17 reviews on its business listing" (shown once).
No standalone website; no verified online booking: all CTAs "Call to book" tel:+19856418300.

## Aesthetic
Honest neighborhood garage with character. Worn red + charcoal + off-white. Mono "diagnostic stamp" accents. The "Car Doctor" identity runs through the copy.

## Typography
Display: "Zilla Slab" (sturdy slab serif). Body: "Inter". "JetBrains Mono" for the stamp and service numbers.

## Palette
- Worn red: #b33324 (buttons, borders, classics band), deep #8c271b
- Charcoal: #22211f (hero, visit band, topbar)
- Warm paper: #f6f3ec, white cards, hairline #e2dccf

## Layout
1. Top bar: "Dr Robert & Sons Car Care Clinic" (Dr in red), "Design preview" chip, Call button.
2. Sticky nav with red underline: Services, Classics, The family, Visit, Contact.
3. Hero: dark split. Left: "THE CAR DOCTOR SHOP" mono stamp, headline "Your car, diagnosed right.", lede, rating line, Call to book + See services. Right: full-bleed shop interior photo.
4. Services: 3 numbered cards with red top borders (Maintenance and repair; Tires, brakes, batteries; DEQ-certified shop).
5. Classics: red band. Classic Beetle B&W photo + "Classic car people, too" (80s-90s specialty, Dr. Rob credential).
6. Family: "A family shop" with Dr. Rob / Kristi member cards + mechanic photo.
7. Visit: dark band. Address, hours table, directions link; "Talk to the Car Doctor" contact card (call only) + brake repair photo.
8. Footer: disclosure (labeled stock photos), "Concept by Freightfolio" + freightfolio.net.

## Customer journey
Visitor sees the Car Doctor identity, scans services, notes the classics specialty and family ownership, checks hours, calls. Primary CTA: Call (985) 641-8300.

## Copy rules applied
No invented pricing, service durations, or appointment promises. No em dashes. No "No X, no Y" triples. No banned AI phrases. Rating shown once with source. "The Car Doctor" usage matches their own newsletter branding. Distinct from batch-22 siblings: red/charcoal slab+mono vs others.

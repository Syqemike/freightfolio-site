The Falls Event Center is an event venue in Manchester with more than 7,000 square feet of function space. Its rooms include the Amoskeag room at 1,940 square feet for up to 160 guests and Niagara B at 1,478 square feet for up to 150 guests, with a seating capacity of 225 and standing capacity of 250 per Cvent. The venue offers a stage, a dance floor, and on-site catering, does not allow outside food or beverage, and has a full-time event manager.

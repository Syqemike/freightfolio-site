A craft coffee shop in Gulf Shores

Foam Coffee is a beach-inspired craft coffee shop at 313 E 22nd Ave Ste B in Gulf Shores, described in research as Gulf Shores' first craft coffee shop and a USA Today 10Best Best Independent Coffee Shop nominee in October 2026. The menu includes the Sea Salt and Honey Latte, a Cuban latte, and the Chagaccino, plus avocado toast, a toast flight, smoothies, snacks, and seasonal drinks. Seating is indoors and outdoors, with a pet-friendly outdoor area.

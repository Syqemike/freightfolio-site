Programs by age

From 10 weeks to 6 years, children learn best when their day fits their stage. Here is how the Kabin meets each age where they are.

Babies · from 10 weeks

Our youngest children get calm routines, cozy spaces, and one-on-one attention. Teachers follow each baby's own rhythm for naps, meals, and snuggles while gently introducing sights, sounds, and textures.

Toddlers

Toddlers learn by doing. Their days are full of active play, simple art projects, story time, and chances to practice sharing and taking turns. Teachers keep the energy up and the routine steady so little ones feel secure.

Preschool · up to 6 years

Older children build the skills school asks for: early letters and numbers, listening and following directions, working with friends, and finishing what they start. It all happens through play, projects, and plenty of reading together.

Alabama Quality Stars · STAR 5 Center

Quality rated, top to bottom

Every age group benefits from our STAR 5 rating through Alabama Quality Stars (Program Type "Center 5").

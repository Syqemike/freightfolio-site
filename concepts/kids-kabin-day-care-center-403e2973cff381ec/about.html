Why families choose the Kabin

Our approach is simple: a well-rounded day for every child, from babies to preschoolers, in a center built for early learning.

Alabama Quality Stars · STAR 5 Center

STAR 5, the highest quality rating

Alabama Quality Stars rates early childhood programs on the things that matter: teaching, learning environments, and how children are cared for. Our STAR 5 rating (Program Type "Center 5") means Kids Kabin met the program's top standard.

Well-rounded days

Play, art, stories, music, and time outdoors all get a place in the day. Children grow in every direction at once, and our schedule is built for that.

A real center

Kids Kabin is center-based, with dedicated rooms for each age group and space to run, build, and create.

From 10 weeks to 6 years

One place for the whole early-childhood run. Siblings can be in the same center, and your child can stay from infancy through preschool.

Teachers who know your child

Classrooms stay warm and personal. Teachers learn each child's personality, notice new milestones, and keep parents in the loop about how the day went.

See the difference in person

The best way to know is a visit. Call to schedule a tour of the Kabin.

A downtown Milton taco joint

Taco Town is a Tex-Mex fast food restaurant in downtown Milton, a family-friendly restaurant with quick service. Its menu includes tacos, a reviewer-praised crispy taco salad, a taco burger, a smothered steak burrito, chalupas including the Maxi Chalupa, nacho supreme, enchiladas, salads, and cinnamon munchies. The restaurant offers catering for workplace luncheons, and delivery and pickup are available through DoorDash.

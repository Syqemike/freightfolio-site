# Design Brief: Polished Nails (Dundalk, MD)

## Aesthetic
Classic salon elegance, centered and refined. A quiet, upscale manicure-studio feel:
symmetrical compositions, generous whitespace, fine hairline rules, brass dividers,
and framed photography presented like gallery pieces. Deliberately NOT plum/editorial,
NOT coastal bright, NOT black and gold. No gradients, no glowing blobs, no illustrations.

## Typography
- Display: Cormorant Garamond (headings, brand mark, pull quotes)
- Body: Jost (navigation, body copy, labels, hours table)

## Palette
- Warm ivory `#faf7f0` (page background)
- Deep forest green `#1f3d2b` (headers, nav, footer, primary text accents)
- Brass `#b08d57` (rules, dividers, frames, CTA fill, seal accents)
- Ink `#2e2a24` (body text), muted `#6f655a`, hairline `#e3d9c6`, soft card `#fffdf8`

## Imagery plan
3 real photos from the salon's own public Yellow Pages photo gallery (nail art by the
salon, confirmed on the "Polish Nail Salon AKA Polished Nails Salon" listing at the
salon's address): purple glitter/heart nail art (framed hero), burgundy glitter nails,
blue nail art. 4 nail-focused stock photos (Unsplash), every one labeled "Stock photo"
in its caption. Gallery page is the centerpiece (7 pieces, art-forward masonry-ish grid
with brass frames). Technician-at-work stock photo anchors the services page intro.

## Layout
- Disclosure ribbon directly under a thin forest top bar; sticky centered header with
  the brand mark "Polished Nails" and simple uppercase nav (Home, Services, Gallery, Visit).
- Home: centered symmetrical hero (eyebrow label, Cormorant headline, framed photo,
  rating seal below), service menu preview with hairline rules and brass section
  dividers, gallery strip, visit teaser.
- Services: elegant menu list; each service row separated by hairline rules with a
  brass diamond divider; service descriptions stay neutral (no prices, durations, or
  invented claims); technician stock photo with honest caption.
- Gallery: centered heading, 7 framed pieces with captions, real photos noted as from
  the salon's public gallery, stock photos labeled.
- Visit: address card, directions link, exact researched hours table (Tuesday shown as
  Closed since it is not listed), prominent Call to Book CTA (tel:+14435038245).
- Rating "4.8 from 154 Google reviews" appears exactly ONCE (home page seal section).

## Customer journey
Land on a refined, centered home page -> see the salon's own nail art in a framed
hero and the Google rating seal -> browse the services menu -> enjoy the art gallery
(hero content) -> Visit page with hours, address, directions, and one clear action:
call to book. No online booking exists, so there is no "Book Now" anywhere.

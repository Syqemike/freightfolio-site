A family table on the bayou

Nathan's Restaurant is a family-run waterfront Creole, seafood, and steakhouse restaurant on Bonfouca Bayou in Slidell, opened in 2008 and rated AAA Three Diamond. Executive chef Ross Eirich is a third-generation chef. Private rooms seat 16, 12, and 28 guests.

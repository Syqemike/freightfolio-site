About

Mario's Pizza is a pizza and Italian-American takeout and delivery restaurant at 1000 Greentree Road in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, in Greentree. The menu covers traditional and white pizza, Sicilian pizza, specialty pies, calzones and strombolis, wings, hoagies, burgers and sandwiches, salads, appetizers, pasta, and a kids menu. Pickup or delivery runs to Greentree, Scott Township, Dormont, Beechview, Brookline, Carnegie, Ingram, Sheraden, Crafton, Mount Washington, Mount Lebanon, and Heidelberg. Hours are Monday, 4:00 PM to 11:00 PM; Tuesday through Thursday, 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM; Friday and Saturday, 10:00 AM to 12:00 AM; Sunday, 11:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Call (412) 921-8200 to place an order.

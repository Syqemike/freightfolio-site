About the truck

Sushi, the Sonoran way

Sonoran sushi is Tucson's own take on the roll: instead of raw fish, the rolls are fried and filled with things like buffalo chicken, carne asada, and bacon, loaded with cream cheese and finished with chipotle mayo and lime-soy style sauces.

Sushi-Lito works out of a red truck at 5422 S 12th Ave, and it has become a late-afternoon and evening stop for people across the south side. Regulars call ahead to skip the wait, then eat at the tables out front under the string lights.

The truck's own menu also covers boneless and traditional wings in buffalo and BBQ, chiles rellenos stuffed with shrimp or crab, California sandwiches, quesadillas, and aguas naturales.

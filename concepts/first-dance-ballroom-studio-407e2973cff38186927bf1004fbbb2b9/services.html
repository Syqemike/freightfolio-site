Lessons and classes

First Dance Ballroom Studio is a ballroom dance studio in Gulf Breeze, offering private lessons by appointment and group classes for all levels, and specialising in custom wedding dances with a study video and professionally cut music. The studio is owned by Margaret Takacs.

Private lessons

Private lessons by appointment, for all levels.

Group classes

Group classes for all levels.

Wedding dances

Custom wedding dances: first dance, father/daughter, mother/son, and wedding-party group dances, with a study video and professionally cut music.

Introductory class

A one-time introductory class at 35 dollars.

Group class pricing

Group classes at 10 dollars per person.

Practice parties and showcases

Practice parties and showcases twice a year. The average wedding takes 2 to 3 lessons.

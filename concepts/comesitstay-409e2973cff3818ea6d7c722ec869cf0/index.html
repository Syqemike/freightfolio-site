ComeSitStay

A small Omaha dog care facility where dogs are known by name, not number, with open play all day in a 3,500 sq ft custom facility.

About

ComeSitStay is a dog grooming, daycare, and boarding facility at 7001 S 181st St Ste 105 in Omaha, in a 3,500 sq ft custom facility with an open floor plan and a secured outdoor area. Dogs are out playing or snuggling with staff all day, not kenneled during business hours. Senior pets and dogs that do not get along with others are accommodated, a staff member makes a nightly after-hours visit, there are no add-on fees, and medication administration including insulin is included.

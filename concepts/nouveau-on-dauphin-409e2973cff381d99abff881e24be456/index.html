About

Nouveau on Dauphin is a salon and day spa in Mobile, serving guests since 1997. The menu covers hair transformations, color, and cuts, plus massage, skincare that includes HydraFacial and Dermaplaning, manicures and pedicures, and waxing. The salon works with Aveda, Kerastase, Matis, and L'Oreal Professionnel products, and holds Alabama Massage Therapy Establishment license E-2710.

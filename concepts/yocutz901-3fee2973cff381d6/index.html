The barber

Mario Askew

Mario, also known as DeMario Askew, cuts hair at Infamous Kutz Barbershop in Memphis, Tennessee. He has been booking through theCut since March 2022.

His menu keeps it simple with a standard cut and a haircut and shave combo. Teen and kids cuts have their own pricing. The haircut and shave includes a beard and mustache line up plus a shampoo wash and enhancements. Prices and hours are posted on his theCut booking page.

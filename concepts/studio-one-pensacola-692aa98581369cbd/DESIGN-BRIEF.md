## Aesthetic
Darkroom mood. Deep ink black + warm amber. Shot-list numbering like a call sheet. Technical and creative at once.

## Typography
"Space Grotesk" for display/body. "IBM Plex Mono" for numbering, meta labels, and session tags.

## Palette
- Ink: #151311 (background), coal #1f1d1a (bands)
- Amber: #e8a33d (accent, buttons)
- Paper: #f4f1ea (gallery band, contact card)

## Layout
1. Top bar: "Studio One" (amber) "Pensacola", "Design preview" chip, Call + Email buttons.
2. Sticky nav with mono numbers: 01 The space, 02 Sessions, 03 The studio, 04 Visit, 05 Contact.
3. Hero: two-column. Left: mono meta label, headline "A blank canvas with perfect light." (amber on last words), lede, rating line, Call + Email Michael. Right: spacious studio photo, stock caption.
4. Specs: coal band, 6-cell grid with amber numbers (1,500 sq ft; 10 ft ceilings; multiple backdrops; private dressing room; lounge area; run by a photographer).
5. Sessions: reversed two-column (photo left, copy right) with mono session tags (headshots, portraits, families, birthdays, maternity, music videos).
6. Gallery: light paper band, 3 stock studio photos with captions.
7. Visit: address, directions link, owner line with verified email; light contact card with Call + Email to book.
8. Footer: disclosure (labeled stock photos), "Concept by Freightfolio" + freightfolio.net.

## Customer journey
Photographer sees the space, scans specs, notes the session types, checks the address, calls or emails to check availability. Primary CTA: call/email (no rate claims made).

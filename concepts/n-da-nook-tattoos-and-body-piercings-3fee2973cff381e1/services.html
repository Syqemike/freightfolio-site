What the shop does

Services

Two trades under one roof. Walk in for something simple, or book an appointment when the piece needs real drawing time.

Custom tattoos

  • Custom design and application. Bring an idea and the artist draws it with you.
  • Script and lettering. Names, quotes, and hand-drawn lettering work.
  • Black and grey. Shaded work in black and grey tones.
  • Color. Full color pieces.
  • Tribal. Bold tribal designs.

Body piercing

  • Ear piercing.
  • Nose piercing.
  • Lip piercing.
  • Tongue piercing.
  • Navel piercing.
  • Dermal and surface piercing.

How a visit works

For simple designs, you can walk in during shop hours. For custom work, call ahead and book an appointment so the artist has time to draw your piece before you arrive.

Consultations are free. Talk through placement, size, and style with the artist, then decide. Aftercare guidance comes with every tattoo and piercing.

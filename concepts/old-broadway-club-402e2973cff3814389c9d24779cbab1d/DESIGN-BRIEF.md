# Design Brief - Old Broadway Club (Website Concept)

## Business in one line
A neighborhood dive bar and grill at 702 E Broadway St, Joplin, MO: cheap beer, inexpensive cocktails, and tasty pub grub. Pool, shuffleboard, jukebox, live music. Takeout available. Open Tue-Sat 11am-11pm, closed Sun-Mon.

## Concept: "The House Broadsheet"
The site is styled as a printed tavern handout, a house broadsheet tacked up by the door. Think of a menu board, a chalk special board, and a door hours-sign brought together into one page. This is deliberately not a photo-heavy hero slider and not a generic three-column card grid.

## Palette
- Paper: #f4ead6 (aged buttermilk, page background)
- Ink: #231a12 (dark roast brown, text and masthead)
- Copper rust: #b0612a (prices, rules, the CTA ticket border)
- Chalkboard green: #2c3a2a (menu-board panels, hours door-sign)
- Brick: #8f2d22 (small stamped labels, CTA fill)

Avoided: black-and-gold, navy-and-white, purple gradients, glowing blobs, pill buttons everywhere.

## Typography
- Headlines: Fraunces 700, warm old-style serif, sized like printed mastheads.
- Sign labels and ticket text: Bebas Neue, set in small uppercase sign boards.
- Body and menu copy: Zilla Slab 400/600, reads like printed menu copy.
No Archivo/Inter, no generic system serif. Pairing chosen to feel like ink on paper.

## Layout devices (the deliberate, non-generic parts)
- A disclosure strip at the very top: "Website Concept / Design Preview", set like a printer's slug line.
- Masthead: business logo image beside a large Fraunces masthead, address slug under it, navigation as a single ruled line of links.
- Menu sections use dotted leader lines between dish name and price, like a printed menu, inside a chalkboard-green panel with a light inner frame.
- Hours shown as a "door sign" panel: bordered box with day rows in Bebas Neue.
- CTAs styled as rectangular admission-ticket stubs ("Call to book", "Call for takeout") with tel:+14176210601, not pill buttons.
- Games (pool, shuffleboard, jukebox, live music) as a two-column broadsheet list with the one stock photo placed as a captioned print.

## Imagery
- Real: the business's own logo artwork (visitjoplinmo directory listing) and two photos of its printed menu (restaurantji listing).
- Stock: one Pexels pool-hall photo, captioned explicitly as a stock photo.
- Zero generated illustrations, zero SVG art, zero CSS-drawn graphics.

## Customer journey
1. Land on home: disclosure, who and where this is, what they serve, one rating with exact source.
2. Skim the house favorites board, jump to the full menu page.
3. Photos page: actual menu-board photos for proof of the real place.
4. About: neighborhood context (Route 66 East Town), what the room offers.
5. Visit: hours door-sign, address, phone, takeout, call CTAs.

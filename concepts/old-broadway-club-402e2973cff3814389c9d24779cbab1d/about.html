The Story

About the bar

Old Broadway Club is a neighborhood dive bar and grill on East Broadway in Joplin. It is the kind of room with regulars at the bar and the jukebox playing in the corner.

The draw is simple. Cheap beer, inexpensive cocktails, and tasty pub grub: burgers like the Fatty Patty and the Venom, beer-battered Fried Fungi, Philly Fries, a Philly cheesesteak and a Rueben.

The room keeps you busy between rounds. There are pool tables, a shuffleboard, a jukebox, and live music.

4.6 from 88 Restaurantji reviews.

What You Get

Cheap beer, honest cocktails

The bar is known for cheap beer and inexpensive cocktails, with a menu board of house pours like the Jalapeno Margarita and the Old Fashioned.

Pub grub worth the trip

Hand-cut fries, beer-battered munchies, and burgers with their own names, like the John Wayne, the Venom, and the Fatty Patty. See the full menu.

Games and music

Pool, shuffleboard, and the jukebox are always on, with live music too.

What Capitol Bistro offers

Capitol Bistro is a neighborhood cafe and deli steps from the Arkansas Capitol in the Victory Building, Plaza D, run by owners Tom and Lisa per listings and the own site. Breakfast and lunch include sandwiches and wraps, soups, salads including the Chef Salad, an avocado burger, a farmhouse burger, coffee, and desserts. Catering is available, and delivery runs via Uber Eats and Bite Squad.

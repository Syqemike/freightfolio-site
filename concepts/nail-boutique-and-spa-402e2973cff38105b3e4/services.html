What we do

Services

Five services, each given the same careful attention. Call (205) 407-4180 if you are unsure which one fits; the staff will point you the right way.

Dip Powder Nails

Colored powder applied over the natural nail and sealed for a smooth, durable finish. A solid choice when you want color that holds up to daily life.

Acrylic Nails

Length and shape sculpted to your preference, then finished with polish or art. Good for a defined shape or added length.

Pedicure

A warm soak, nail shaping, cuticle care, callus attention, and polish. The full reset for tired feet.

Gel Nails

Gel color set under light for a high-gloss finish that resists chips. Keeps its shine between appointments.

Nail Art

Hand-drawn details, accents, and seasonal designs added over your manicure. Bring a photo of what you have in mind.

A note on timing: the studio is open Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM, and closed on Sundays. Calling ahead is the surest way to get the time you want.

A family-owned auto repair shop in Huntsville, in business since 1990.

Kevin's Auto Repair is a family-owned Christian auto repair shop in Huntsville. It started in 1990 as a mobile lube service and has been a full-service shop at its present location since about 2000. Work covers tune-ups, oil and filter changes, tire rotations, wheel alignments, radiator and engine flushes, battery service, timing belt replacement, cooling system service, hybrid service and maintenance, and Ford Power Stroke diesel maintenance. Most repairs carry a 2-year, 24,000-mile nationwide labor warranty.

## Aesthetic
Playful retro, bold and fun. Sticker-bomb energy: chunky sticker-like section headers with thick outlines and hard offset shadows, slight rotations, dashed ticket borders, playful menu-card styling for the pedicure menu. Distinct from batch-28 siblings (Crystal Nails = editorial plum, Posh = coastal bright, Polished = forest-green classic, Jireh = spa zen).

## Typography
- Display: Bricolage Grotesque (bold, quirky character for headlines and sticker headers).
- Body: Nunito (round, friendly).

## Palette
- Coral #ff6b6b (primary, playful energy)
- Cream #fff8e7 (page background)
- Teal #0f766e (accents, footer, secondary buttons)
- Warm white #fffdf8 (cards)
- Ink #2b2118 (text/outlines)
- Sun yellow #ffc94d (sparingly, for sticker accents and stars)

No gradients, no glowing blobs. Flat retro color blocks.

## Layout
- Slim "Design Preview" disclosure bar above a sticky nav with business name + Home / Services / Gallery / Visit links + "Call to book" button.
- Home: hero with sticker headline, rating line (once), photo strip, pedicure menu teaser (4 specialty pedicures as menu cards), hours preview, chunky CTA section.
- Services: full pedicure menu centerpiece (menu cards with ticket/dashed borders), then nail care / acrylics / silk wrapping / waxing blocks. No prices (none researched). No invented service details.
- Gallery: photo grid, every caption labeled "Stock photo".
- Visit: hours table rendered exactly as researched, address + Google Maps directions link, "Call to book" CTA. No online booking claim, no "Book Now" button.

## Customer journey
Land -> see this is a fun nail salon in Dundalk -> scan the pedicure menu (the specialty) -> check hours -> tap "Call to book".

## Copy rules
No em dashes, no banned AI phrases, no invented testimonials/awards/prices/credentials, no invented service details, no urgency language, plain direct language.

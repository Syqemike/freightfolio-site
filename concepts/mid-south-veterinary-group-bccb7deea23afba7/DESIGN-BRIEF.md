## Aesthetic
Warm trustworthy neighborhood vet. Soft, rounded, pet-forward. Arch-shaped hero photo. Feels caring, not clinical.

## Typography
Display: "Fraunces" (warm serif). Body: "Nunito" (friendly rounded sans).

## Palette
- Sage green: #5f7a63, deep #42584a (topbar, hours band, contact card)
- Deep brown: #4a3826 (headlines, footer)
- Warm cream: #faf6ee, white sections, hairline #e6ddcc

## Layout
1. Top bar: business name, "Design preview" chip, Call button.
2. Sticky nav: Services, Our care, Hours, Visit, Contact.
3. Hero: two-column. Left: eyebrow, Fraunces headline "Good care for the pets you love.", lede, rating line, Call to book + See services. Right: arch-topped photo of vet embracing a dog, stock caption.
4. Services: white band, centered 3-col grid of 5 verified services (Vaccinations, Spay and neuter, Flea and tick treatment, Grooming, Emergency services with "Call first" note).
5. Our care: editorial two-column on the visit experience + vets photo.
6. Hours: sage band with verified hours table + checkup photo.
7. Visit: address, Google Maps link, dental-checkup photo; sage "Book a visit" contact card (call only).
8. Footer: disclosure (labeled stock photos), "Concept by Freightfolio" + freightfolio.net.

## Customer journey
Visitor sees warmth and the verified service list, checks hours, calls to book. Primary CTA: Call (228) 385-1008.

Southern comfort food, Moss Point

Plate after plate of food for the soul.

Kurbside Kitchen is a Southern comfort food spot in Moss Point serving the kind of food people drive across town for: sauced wings, loaded fries, open-face sandwiches piled high, fried green tomatoes, hush puppies, okra, gumbo, and banana pudding to finish.

Open Wednesday through Saturday, 11 AM to 6 PM. Closed Sunday through Tuesday. Takeout available, plus delivery through DoorDash and Caviar.

Wings

Battered or naked, sauced to order. Honey BBQ, Lemon Pepper, Honey Sriracha, Mild or Hot, Garlic Parm, Honey Gold, and more.

Loaded Fries

Shrimp, steak, or chicken over golden fries with the trimmings. Cheese fries if you want to keep it simple.

Open-Face Sandwiches

Pork chop, fish, shrimp, or wings, served as a full plate with all the fixings.

Burgers & Philly Cheesesteak

Dressed burgers, chicken and pork chop burgers, and the Philly cheesesteak customers call the best they have had.

Sides & Sweets

Fried green tomatoes, hush puppies, okra, potato salad, gumbo when available. Banana pudding and pineapple lemonade to finish.

To drink

Pineapple lemonade in regular, large, and XL. Sweet tea, Arnold Palmer, and canned sodas, too.

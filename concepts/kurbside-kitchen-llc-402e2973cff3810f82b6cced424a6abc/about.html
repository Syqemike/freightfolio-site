Our story

Food for the soul, served with a smile.

Kurbside Kitchen LLC is a Southern comfort food spot in Moss Point, Mississippi. The tagline says it plain: food for the soul. The menu covers sauced wings, loaded fries, open-face sandwiches, burgers and the Philly cheesesteak, plus fried green tomatoes, hush puppies, okra, gumbo, and banana pudding to finish.

It is a sit-down spot as much as a food counter: an outdoor patio for the nice days and air-conditioned seating inside. It is easy to find on Elvira Granderson Road.

Order at the counter, call ahead for takeout, or have it delivered through DoorDash. Questions about the day’s specials? Call (228) 355-4638.

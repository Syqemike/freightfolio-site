A muffler shop on South Broadway

What the storefront and the listings tell you about this Edmond shop.

Sooner Mufflers of Edmond is a muffler and exhaust shop at 1016 South Broadway in Edmond, Oklahoma.

The storefront says what the shop does. The window advertises catalytic converters installed, and the shop truck parked outside carries an oversized muffler on its roof.

The business is listed as an automotive repair shop and automotive service, so general auto repair is part of the picture along with the exhaust work.

Hours are Monday to Friday 9 AM to 5 PM, Saturday 9 AM to noon, closed Sundays. There is no online booking. Call (405) 348-9996 to book.

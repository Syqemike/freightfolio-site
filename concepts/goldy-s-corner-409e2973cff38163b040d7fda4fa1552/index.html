Goldy's Corner

Downtown Boise's corner coffeehouse and cafe, with a full coffee menu, quick eats, sweet treats, and a take-a-book, leave-a-book shelf.

About

Goldy's Corner is a corner bakery cafe and coffeehouse at 625 W Main St in Boise, described in its own branding as downtown Boise's favorite corner coffeehouse and cafe. The shop serves a full coffee menu, breakfast and sandwiches, bakery items, and sweet treats, with local artists work and gift shop vendors on display and an outdoor patio in warmer seasons. It is a partner to Goldy's Breakfast Bistro, a separate business at a different address with a different phone number.

Rated 4.6 from 962 Google reviews.

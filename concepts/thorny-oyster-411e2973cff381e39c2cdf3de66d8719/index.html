Inside the Pearl Hotel

Thorny Oyster is an upscale coastal seafood and raw bar inside the Pearl Hotel at 104 N Beach Blvd in downtown Bay St. Louis, Mississippi. The menu covers raw and grilled oysters, a seafood plateau, lobster rolls, marinated crab claws, royal red shrimp, the Thorny Burger, shrimp and grits, and Sunday brunch. Chefs Jeffrey Hansell and Lauren Joffrion run the kitchen. Reservations are offered. Hours are Monday closed; Tuesday and Wednesday, 5:00 PM to 9:00 PM; Thursday, 11:00 AM to 9:00 PM; Friday and Saturday, 11:00 AM to 10:00 PM; Sunday, 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM. Call (228) 688-0401 to book.

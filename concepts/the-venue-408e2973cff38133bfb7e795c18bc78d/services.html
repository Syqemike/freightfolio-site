Spaces and services

The Venue in downtown Fairhope offers more than 8,000 square feet of indoor and outdoor event space for up to 300 guests, per the venue's own site. The space includes a tented outdoor patio of 2,400 square feet, tables and chairs, an on-site projector and TVs, bar packages, and an open catering policy. The venue hosts weddings, rehearsal dinners, corporate parties, and bridal showers.

About the studio

A team that builds the shoot with you.

UA Creative Studios describes itself as a full-service photography company serving the greater Columbus area. Its photographers are college graduates who specialize in different areas of photography, and they work together to support each client's project.

The studio's approach is collaborative: clients take part in the creative process, from planning the shoot to the finished images.

You own your images. The studio states that clients truly own their images. Ask the studio when you call if you want the details of how delivery and ownership work for your project.

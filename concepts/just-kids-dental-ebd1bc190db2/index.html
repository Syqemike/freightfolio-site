Built around kids. Clear for parents.

Just Kids Dental is a pediatric dental practice serving children from age 1 through 21. Visits are structured around how kids actually feel about the dentist, and everything a parent needs to know about coverage, hours, and locations is spelled out in plain terms.

The practice accepts Medicaid, LaChip, and most private insurances, so coverage is rarely a guessing game. The Kenner office is open Monday through Friday, 8am to 4pm, and with offices in Baton Rouge and New Orleans as well, care is close wherever your family is.

What care includes

Pediatric plus general dental care for children ages 1 to 21.

Preventive visits

Regular checkups that keep an eye on your child's teeth as they grow, starting as early as age 1.

Everyday dental care

General dentistry for kids, teens, and young adults, from cleanings to treatment when teeth need attention.

Growing smiles

Care that follows your child through every stage, from first tooth to age 21.

Services for ages 1 to 21

Just Kids Dental provides pediatric plus general dental care for children, teens, and young adults. One practice, one team, for the whole stretch from toddler to 21.

First visits and preventive care

Care starts at age 1. Early visits build comfort with the dentist and keep small teeth on track as they come in.

Routine checkups and cleanings

Ongoing visits that keep teeth clean and give the dentists a chance to spot changes early.

General dental treatment

When a tooth needs attention, the team handles everyday treatment right in the practice.

Care through the teen years

Teens and young adults up to 21 keep seeing the same team, with care that fits each stage.

Ask about your child's needs

Every child is different. Call and talk with the team about what your child needs at this stage.

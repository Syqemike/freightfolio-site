An Acadian-style waterfront restaurant and event venue on Bayou Bonfouca, open since 2002.

Palmettos On The Bayou is an Acadian-style waterfront restaurant and event venue on Bayou Bonfouca near Old Town Slidell, open since 2002. The kitchen cooks classic Creole dishes with fresh Louisiana ingredients, including gumbo, shrimp and grits, chargrilled oysters, shrimp etouffee dumplings, cane syrup lacquered pork belly, and limoncello creme brulee. There is indoor and outdoor seating and a 5,000 square foot covered deck, with live jazz at Saturday and Sunday brunch.

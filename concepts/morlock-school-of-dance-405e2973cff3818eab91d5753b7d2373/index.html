A long history on Pitkin Street

Morlock School of Dance has been in business in East Hartford for 70 years. It was formerly known as the Mary Morlock School of Performing Arts, and today its owners and artistic directors are Christine Sessa and Elizabeth Sanderson.

The school's own description of itself is simple: dance is an art that should be enjoyed by everyone. That idea shapes a school built around children learning to dance in a welcoming room, not around pressure or performance.

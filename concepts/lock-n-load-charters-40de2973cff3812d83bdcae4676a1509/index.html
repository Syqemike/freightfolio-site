About

Lock N Load Charters is a family charter fishing business on Harbor Boulevard in Destin, with Capt. Steve Brown, the 2011 Destin Fishing Rodeo Captain of the Year. Trips run on a 28 ft Mako for up to six passengers, in 4, 6, and 8-hour lengths, with bait, tackle, and licenses included and the catch cleaned.

What you can catch

Trips target snapper, grouper, amberjack, wahoo, king mackerel, sheepshead, and shark. Call (850) 865-7838 or email info@locknloadcharters.com to ask about a trip.

# Design Brief: Virtue Barber Company (Portland, ME)

## Business personality
Bright, clean, spacious shop. 5.0/352 Google reviews. Team: Dillon (owner), Aidan, Hannah, Lance. Shop dog Waylon (wire-haired dachshund). Kid and pet friendly, free parking, free Wi-Fi. Warm and open, not dark and moody.

## Design direction
Bright light ground with a full-width hero photo and an overlapping white card: tag pill, bold Space Grotesk headline, star rating shown once, rounded CTA pills. Palette: paper (#faf8f3), denim blue (#245c7a), terracotta (#c96f4a), sand (#eee5d3). Space Grotesk + Inter.
Differentiated from Don's and Presidential Grooming by being the only bright/light design, card-overlap hero, and rounded modern components.

## Section order
Disclosure > sticky topbar (name + nav) > full-bleed hero photo > overlapping hero card (h1, rating once, Book Now) > team section (sand panel, Waylon mention) > services (numbered card grid) > gallery (masonry) > hours + visit panels > footer.

## Imagery
5 stock photos (Pexels): man receiving haircut, clippers side view, haircut and trim, bearded barber cutting boy's hair (kid-friendly), beard styling close-up. All labeled "Stock photo".

## Customer journey
Bright first impression, team intro builds trust, numbered service cards scan fast, hours and Vagaro Book Now (booking page verified: title matches business name) plus tel: fallback in the visit panel.

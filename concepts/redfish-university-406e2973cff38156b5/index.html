Fishing instruction and guided trips

Redfish University is a shallow-water fishing instruction and guided trip service at 2700 E Cervantes St in Pensacola, led by guide Eric Holstman. Trips and instruction serve beginner, intermediate, and advanced anglers: sight fishing for redfish with artificial baits is the specialty, plus speckled trout, pompano, flounder, and bull redfish. Instruction covers bait choice, line selection, casting presentation, and approaching the target. Call or email to ask about a trip.

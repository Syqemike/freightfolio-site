About

Belle's Bread is a family owned French inspired Japanese bakery and cafe. Pastries and cakes are made from scratch daily. The bakery is known for its crepes, matcha soft serve, and cream puffs, and Food and Wine quoted it as one of America's best Japanese bakeries. Its team counts 30 bakers.

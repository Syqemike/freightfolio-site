The Shop

Hooked on Castleberry Hill

Off The Hook sits in Atlanta's Castleberry Hill arts district, and the room matches the neighborhood. Laid back and colorful, with an energy that keeps things fun from the first cut to the last.

The shop has built a name for itself with a celebrity clientele, and every client in the chair gets the same serious attention to detail.

All hair types and textures welcome. Come as you are. Leave hooked on a great cut.

Solo chiropractor: Dr. A Peter Pingalore Jr., DC. 30 Park Ave Ste 4, Worcester, MA 01605. Phone (508) 799-0768.
Hours: Mon 9-5; Tue 1-7:30; Wed closed; Thu 1-7:30; Fri 9-5; Sat/Sun closed. Google 4.9 from 19 reviews (shown once).
WebMD review quote: "Very possibly the best chiropractor of all of Massachusetts!" (attributed to a WebMD reviewer, not presented as our claim).
No standalone website, no socials, no verified online booking: all CTAs are "Call to book" tel:+15087990768. Claim-free contact copy.

## Aesthetic
Calm clinical professionalism. Generous whitespace, restrained composition, photographic. Feels like a quiet, competent solo practice, not a spa and not a clinic chain.

## Typography
Display: "Newsreader" (calm editorial serif). Body: "Inter". Small-caps eyebrow kickers in muted teal.

## Palette
- Slate: #31424f (headers, hours band), deep slate #22303b (topbar/footer)
- Muted teal: #3e7c74 (accents, buttons, quote rule), deep #2c5d57
- Paper: #f7f9f8, white cards, hairline #dde5e2
- No gradients, no blobs, no pill-heavy buttons

## Layout
1. Top bar: practitioner name + specialty, "Design preview" chip, Call button.
2. Sticky section nav: About, Hours, Reviews, Visit, Contact.
3. Hero: editorial two-column. Left: eyebrow, serif headline "Personal chiropractic care on Park Ave.", lede with address, rating line "4.9 from 19 Google reviews", Call to book + Find the office. Right: treatment photo with stock caption.
4. About: two-column. Bio limited to verified facts (solo chiropractor, DC, Worcester address, listed in directories). WebMD quote card with attribution.
5. Hours: dark slate band, table of verified hours, "Call ahead to confirm appointment availability."
6. Visit: address block, Google Maps directions link, "Book an appointment" contact card (call only, states online booking is not available).
7. Footer: design-preview disclosure (notes labeled stock photos), "Concept by Freightfolio" + freightfolio.net link.

## Customer journey
Visitor confirms the practitioner and address, checks hours, reads the review quote, calls to book. Primary CTA: Call (508) 799-0768.

No invented services, prices, credentials, or history. No em dashes. No "No X, no Y" triples. No banned AI phrases. No demand/urgency invention. Rating shown exactly once with source. Deliberately avoided: confusing with Dr. Evin Pingalore / Top Spine Chiropractic (never mentioned).

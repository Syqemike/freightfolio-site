Trips

Knot EZ Charters runs inshore and nearshore fishing charters plus non-fishing boat charters and flounder gigging out of Pass Christian near Gulfport, for speckled trout, redfish, Spanish mackerel, and flounder. Bait and tackle are provided, and the catch is cleaned and bagged. Capt. John Powell fishes a 2021 Crevalle 26HBW, per FishingBooker.

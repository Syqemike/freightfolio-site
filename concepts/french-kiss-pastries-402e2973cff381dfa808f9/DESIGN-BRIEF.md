## Aesthetic
French patisserie romance meets Gulf Coast warmth. Warm cream paper background,
deep chocolate text, raspberry/rose accents, thin gold rules. Nothing sleek or
corporate; nothing barber-shop dark.

## Typography
- Display: Cormorant Garamond (600/700) — high-contrast Didone-style serif with
  a French patisserie voice. Used for business name, section headings, quotes.
- Body/UI: Jost (400/500) — clean geometric sans for readability on mobile.
- Letterspaced uppercase eyebrows for section labels.

## Palette
- cream #FBF4EA (page), cream-deep #F3E7D3 (bands)
- ink #33241A (chocolate, body text)
- raspberry #B44A5E (accents, CTAs), rose #E8B4B8 (soft washes)
- gold #A67C3A (rules, eyebrow marks)
- deep green #2E4B3F (footer band, contrast section)

## Layout (single page, mobile-first)
1. Preview banner: "Design Preview — Website Concept by Freightfolio"
2. Sticky nav: name + Pastries / Cafe / Visit + Call CTA
3. Hero: full-bleed case photo, name in large Cormorant, one-line promise,
   TripAdvisor rating chip (once per page), two CTAs: Call (228) 215-1160 and
   "See the case"
4. Welcome: owner quote + two short paragraphs (breakfast/lunch/dessert/coffee;
   custom cakes; seasonal menu). Interior photo, side-by-side.
5. The Case (signature lineup): photo-led cards — 3-inch cakes, macarons,
   eclairs & cannoli, croissants; plus text menu columns from the real menu
   board (Sweet treats / Cafe drinks / Lunches). No prices shown.
6. Cafe band (deep green): espresso bar photo + specialty drink names from the
   real board, coffee line, gelato.
7. Visit: hours table, "downtown Ocean Springs, MS" (no street number),
   big Call CTA. "Questions? Call (228) 215-1160."
8. Footer: disclosure (concept design, not affiliated), phone, noindex in head.

## Customer journey
Land on the case photo, feel the shop, read the real menu, call to order.
Every CTA is a tel: call; there is no online booking anywhere on the site.

## Explicitly avoided
- Em dashes, invented reviews/testimonials, awards, pricing, hours guesses
- Stock photos, illustrations, SVG art, gradients
- "Book Now" without a booking destination
- Reusing barber/salon layouts; TailWaggers/Studio 601 referenced for quality
  bar only

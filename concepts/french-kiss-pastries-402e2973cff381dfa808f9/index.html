Bienvenue

Baked with French craft, served with Southern warmth

French Kiss Pastries is an artisan bakery and cafe in downtown Ocean Springs, Mississippi. Owner and pastry chef Jacqueline Ladnier fills the case daily with butter and almond croissants, macarons, eclairs, cannoli, blueberry scones with lemon drizzle, cinnamon rolls, and custom cakes made to order.

Baking is the passion, and the proof is in every single bite.

Jacqueline Ladnier, owner and pastry chef

The menu rotates with the seasons, so the case changes through the year. A full line of coffee, gelato, and a gift shop in the back of the cafe round out the visit.

The cafe room at French Kiss Pastries

The pastry case

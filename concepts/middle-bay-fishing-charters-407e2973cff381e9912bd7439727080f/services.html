Trips

Middle Bay Fishing Charters runs custom inshore fishing charters on Mobile Bay, Weeks Bay, Fort Morgan, Orange Beach, and Gulf Shores, with light-tackle fishing for redfish, speckled trout, flounder, and tripletail, plus scenic non-fishing boat tours. Capt. David Rice has 25 plus years of local experience and is USCG-licensed.

Inshore charters

Custom inshore fishing charters on Mobile Bay, Weeks Bay, Fort Morgan, Orange Beach, and Gulf Shores.

Light-tackle fishing

Light-tackle fishing for redfish, speckled trout, flounder, and tripletail.

Scenic boat tours

Scenic non-fishing boat tours.

The boat

A 24 foot Pathfinder center console, up to 4 anglers.

What is provided

Bait, tackle, ice, and fish cleaning are provided.

Trip pricing

Trips from 400 dollars for 3 hours and 500 dollars for 4 hours, per FishingBooker.

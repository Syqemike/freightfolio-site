About

Pizzeria Di Giovanni is a New York-style pizza shop in the French Quarter of Charleston, South Carolina, at Rainbow Market on North Market Street. The shop sells pizza by the slice and whole pies up to 28 inches, with cheese at 47 dollars for a 28 inch pie per a delivery menu. Flavors include pepperoni, margherita, Hawaiian, and meat lovers with anchovies. Stromboli, calzones, and Greek salad are also on the menu, and takeout and delivery are available.

A Gulf Breeze boutique with a rack for every occasion.

Jen’s Designer Deals is a designer boutique on Gulf Breeze Parkway, run by owner Jennifer Wells, with a storefront since 2019. The shop carries designer apparel, shoes, handbags, and accessories for all occasions, and Jen offers personal styling and personal shopping in the store.

The shop is a community first boutique, featured as a success story by the Florida SBDC at the University of West Florida. Jen has lived in Gulf Breeze since 2001.

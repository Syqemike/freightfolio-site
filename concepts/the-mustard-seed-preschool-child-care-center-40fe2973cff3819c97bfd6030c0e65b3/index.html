About

The Mustard Seed Preschool and Child Care Center is a Christian preschool and child care center in D'Iberville, Mississippi, at 4247 Popp's Ferry Road. Founded January 3, 2005 by owner Nancy Wielgosz Koon, the center serves ages 1 to 4 in four classes: Seedlings, Sprouts, Shoots, and Vines. The program follows a set curriculum with a music program and kindergarten readiness, and children learn through play. The center is licensed by the Mississippi State Department of Health. Hours are Monday to Friday 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Call (228) 392-7405 or email themustardseedps@aol.com to ask about enrollment.

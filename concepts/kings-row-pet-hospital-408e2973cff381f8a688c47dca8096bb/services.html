What Kings Row Pet Hospital offers

Kings Row Pet Hospital is a full-service animal hospital in Reno seeing dogs, cats, birds, turtles, and tortoises. Services include dental care with dental x-ray, surgery including orthopedic surgery, vaccinations, microchipping, exotic pet care, hospice and palliative care, and nutritional counseling. It is not a 24-hour facility: overnight stays are unstaffed, and pets are transferred to an emergency center if needed.

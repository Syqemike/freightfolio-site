# DESIGN BRIEF: Nails Sensation (Mobile, AL) — Concept Website

## Aesthetic
BOLD GLAM, gallery-forward and modern. Dark, dramatic, editorial. High contrast
near-black base with vivid fuchsia accent bands; blush for breathing room.
Feels like a late-night runway showroom, not a soft spa. Distinct from the
other batch-28 nail salons (editorial plum, coastal bright, forest-green
classic, spa zen, playful retro) by being the dark, loud one.

## Typography
- Display: Syne (700/800), oversized uppercase hero lines, tight leading.
- Body: Manrope (400/500/600), small caps kickers with wide tracking.

## Palette
- Near-black #141114 (page base, hero)
- Vivid fuchsia/magenta #c026d3 (accent bands, CTA fills, rule lines)
- Soft blush #f7e3ee (light section breaks, card surfaces)
- White #ffffff type on dark; dark type #141114 on blush
- No gradients, no glowing blobs, no illustration.

## Imagery plan
- Hero: stock-01.jpg (dark glam manicure, black/tortoise nails on grey) —
  full-bleed right side / edge-to-edge band. Labeled "Stock photo".
- Gallery centerpiece: full-bleed horizontal band, 5 tiles:
  real-01.jpg (REAL: customer gel nails from the business's Google listing
  photo gallery via salondiscover.com) — hero tile, captioned as real.
  stock-02 (nude manicure), stock-03 (pink manicure application),
  stock-05 (polish bottles), plus stock-01 reuse.
  Every stock tile captioned "Stock photo". The real one captioned with
  source, never presented as stock.
- Services page: stock-03. Visit page: stock-05.
- >= 3 photos on every page via header-adjacent gallery strip.
- Zero generated/drawn/SVG/CSS illustrations. Photos only.

## Layout
1. index.html — disclosure banner; header (wordmark + nav + call CTA);
   dark dramatic hero with giant Syne type over the hero photo edge;
   fuchsia accent band (services strip); full-bleed gallery centerpiece;
   blush review/hours card band; footer.
2. services.html — blush editorial list of the 7 researched services, each
   with a plain one-line description; photo headers; call CTA band.
3. gallery.html — full-bleed grid of all 6 photos with honest captions.
4. visit.html — address (Westwood Square), hours table exactly as researched,
   directions link, call to book band, photo strip.

## Customer journey
Arrive -> see the name and the look in one glance -> skim services ->
browse the gallery -> check hours -> call to book. One CTA everywhere:
"Call to book" (tel:+12514784620). No online booking exists, so no
"Book Now" button. Rating appears once: "4.8 from 22 Google reviews".

awards, prices, staff names, or claims about how the business operates.
Hours exactly: Mon-Sat 9:30 AM - 6:30 PM; Sun closed. No social links

"Design Preview" disclosure near the top of every page; never imply

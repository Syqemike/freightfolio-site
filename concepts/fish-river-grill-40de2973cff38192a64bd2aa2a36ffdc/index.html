About

Fish River Grill is a casual Southern seafood restaurant on the Gulf Shores Parkway in Gulf Shores. The menu runs from Gulf seafood platters and shrimp po-boys to gumbo and gator bites, with a complimentary Swamp Soup for dine-in guests and an award-winning Bloody Mary.

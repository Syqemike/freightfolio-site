Breakfast all day in the warehouse district

Warehouse Bakery and Donuts is a neighborhood bakery and eatery in the warehouse district of Fairhope. It serves donuts, king cake, cinnamon roll French toast, biscuits and gravy, breakfast all day, soups, salads, and sandwiches, with Fairhope Roasting coffee, cold brew on tap, and beer and wine. Outdoor seating and takeout are available.

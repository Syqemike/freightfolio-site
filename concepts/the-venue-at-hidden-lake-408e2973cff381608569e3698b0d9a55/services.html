Spaces and services

The Venue at Hidden Lake in Silverhill is a lakeside wedding and event venue on more than 6 acres. It offers a vintage barn for up to 125 guests, a handcrafted cedar chapel, a glass-front Overlook room for up to 200, and an outdoor area for up to 250, plus a bridal cottage and groom's quarters, tables, chairs and linens, an event kitchen, and a decor room. Tours are booked by form, with a response within 48 hours.

# DESIGN BRIEF - Dennis' Small Engine Repair (Batch 28)

## Aesthetic
HONEST WORKSHOP. A plainspoken, blue-collar tradesman site. Solid flat colors, sturdy
serif headlines, generous whitespace. No gradients, no glow, no gimmicks. The design
should feel like a well-kept shop: clean counter, legible price board, nothing hidden.

## Typography
- Display: Roboto Slab (headlines, price figures, nav wordmark) - slab serif, workshop feel
- Body: Open Sans (paragraphs, lists, captions) - highly legible at small sizes

## Palette
- Navy #1f2a44 - header, footer, headline text on light, primary dark
- Barn red #b3392e - CTAs, price figures, warranty band, accent rules
- Kraft paper #e8dcc8 - alternating section backgrounds, disclosure bar, cards
- Off-white #faf7f0 - page base and light sections
- Ink #2b2b2b - body text

## Imagery plan
No real photos of the business could be verified (Facebook page is login-gated; no Yelp
or GBP listing exists). All six images are freely licensed stock from Wikimedia Commons,
each captioned "Stock photo":
1. images/hero-mower.jpg - closeup of a walk-behind mower on grass (hero, index)
2. images/repair-man.jpg - man repairing a mower outdoors (ethos section, index)
3. images/walkbehind.jpg - walk-behind mower (services page, "what we service")
4. images/blade.jpg - mower blade (gallery + $40 service card)
5. images/sparkplugs.jpg - spark plugs (gallery + tune-up card)
6. images/engine.jpg - small engine closeup (gallery)
Gallery page shows all six with captions; index/services reuse three contextually.

## Layout
- Slim kraft "Design Preview" disclosure bar at the very top of every page
- Navy header: business name (Roboto Slab), tagline, simple nav, red Call button
- Full-width hero photo with navy overlay panel carrying the headline
- Sections alternate off-white / kraft; red rules and price cards for the pricing board
- Red warranty band; two-column "we service / we no longer service" panel
- Navy footer with address, hours, phone, disclosures

## Customer journey
1. Land: see the name, the "repair shop not a replace shop" ethos, and the Call button.
2. Scan pricing: the tune-up and $40 service prices are on the homepage price board;
   full detail on Services & Pricing.
3. Check fit: "walk-behind mowers only" and the no-longer-serviced list set
   expectations before anyone picks up the phone.
4. Act: every page ends with a Call CTA (tel:+18507485681); Visit page gives the
   address, exact hours, and a directions link. No online booking exists, so no
   booking button appears anywhere.

## Copy notes
- Headline ethos uses the shop's own words: "We are a repair shop not a replace shop."
- Hours rendered exactly as researched: Mon-Fri 10:15 AM - 4:00 PM; Sat-Sun closed.
- Rating appears once, on the homepage: "4.6 from 22 Google reviews".
- No em dashes, no banned AI phrases, no invented facts, no urgency language.

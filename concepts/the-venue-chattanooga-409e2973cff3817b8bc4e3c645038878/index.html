The Venue Chattanooga

Chattanooga's newest upscale event destination, between Raccoon and Lookout Mountain, 7 minutes from downtown, with a 9,000 sq ft painted-brick hall and an outdoor Garden patio.

About

The Venue Chattanooga is a wedding and event venue at 4119 Cummings Hwy in Chattanooga, run by husband-and-wife team Russell and Kate and welcoming weddings since 2015. The indoor hall is a 9,000 sq ft painted-brick space with a balcony and a walk-up bar, and the outdoor Garden patio measures 40 by 60 ft with a stacked-stone fireplace. The venue has a bridal suite and a groom's room, allows any licensed caterer or bartender with no extra charge for outside beverages, and offers free parking.

Shady Lady Charters runs deep-sea fishing charters out of Zeke's Marina, Orange Beach, on the 60-foot sportfisherman Shady Lady, Captain Brian Daniels. The boat carries up to 40 passengers per the Alabama charter association directory. Target species include tuna including yellowfin, marlin, mahi-mahi, wahoo, amberjack, swordfish, sailfish, triggerfish, grouper, and snapper. The air-conditioned boat has 2 restrooms, TV, VCR and DVD, microwave, fridge, satellite stereo, shower, grill, and ice maker. FishingBooker trips are listed from 2,400 to 3,000 dollars, per the association directory, listing, and FishingBooker.

Pet Planet Grooming is a family owned and operated dog grooming shop in the Flower Valley Shopping Center. The team brings 50+ years of combined experience to every dog in the chair.

Baths, breed-specific haircuts, nail clipping, ear cleaning, coat care, and a little boutique of pet apparel and accessories, all in one friendly shop.

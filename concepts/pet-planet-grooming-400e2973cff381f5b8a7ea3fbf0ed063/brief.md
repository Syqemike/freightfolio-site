# Design Brief — Pet Planet Grooming (Florissant, MO)

## Design personality
Family legacy storefront. Sunshine yellow + teal mid-century warmth, retro
badge-style TYPOGRAPHIC header (no clip art, no drawn graphics, just big
lettering and rules). "Since 2002" heritage thread throughout. Cheerful,
neighborhoody, warm. Clearly different from the broadsheet barbershop and the
atelier grooming salon.

## Typography
- Display: "Alfa Slab One" (slab-serif, badge/poster feel) for the big
  typographic header and section titles.
- Body: "Karla" for warm readability; letterspaced small caps for labels.
- Hierarchy: giant badge headline > banner labels > story cards.

## Palette
- Sunshine yellow: #ffc83d
- Teal: #0e7c7b
- Cream: #fff8e7
- Deep ink: #21333b
Yellow bands with teal type alternate with cream and teal sections. No gradients.

## Imagery (all stock, every one captioned "Stock photo")
- pp_dogs.jpg — two happy dogs running at sunset (hero)
- pp_bath.jpg — golden retriever being bathed (services)
- pp_sweater.jpg — puppy in a sweater (boutique section: pet apparel)
- pp_corgis.jpg — two smiling corgis (our story)
No public photos of the business exist; all photos are stock, labeled as stock.

## Layout
- Disclosure bar at top (teal strip).
- Typographic badge header: PET PLANET / GROOMING letterspaced, "Serving
  Florissant since 2002" ribbon line, sticky section nav below.
- Hero: yellow band, headline "Family grooming, Florissant style.", photo right.
- Grooming services: teal section, card list (nail clipping, ear cleaning,
  breed-specific haircuts, baths with quality shampoos and conditioners, coat
  care and skin treatment).
- Boutique: cream section, pet apparel and accessories, sweater photo.
- Our story: family owned and operated since 2002, 50+ years combined team
  experience, corgi photo.
- Visit: hours Tue-Sat 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Sun/Mon closed; Flower Valley
  Shopping Center address; phone; rating once.
- Footer with disclaimer, provenance, rating source, prepared October 2026.

## Customer journey
Land on the cheerful badge, read the heritage story, browse grooming and the
boutique, check hours, then Call to book (tel: link). Every booking CTA is
"Call to book"; no online booking is verified.

## Copy notes
- Facts used only from research: address, phone, hours, full service list,
  boutique (pet apparel and accessories), family owned and operated since 2002,
  50+ years combined experience, 4.6 from 32 Google reviews.
- Rating appears exactly once on the page.
- Pricing not published: honest note to call for pricing.
- No invented facts, no em dashes, no banned AI phrases, no No-X-no-Y triples.

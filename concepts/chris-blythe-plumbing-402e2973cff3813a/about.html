About Chris

One plumber, full accountability.

Chris Blythe runs an owner-operator plumbing service in Pascagoula, Mississippi. There is no office staff, no dispatch board, and no crew rotation. The person who answers the phone is the person who does the work.

That is the whole business model. When one person owns every job from the first call to the last fitting, there is nobody to pass a problem to. It gets fixed right because the name on the truck is the name on the invoice.

Chris handles plumbing repair, drain and sewer cleaning, gas leak repair, and sewer line video inspection for homes around Pascagoula.

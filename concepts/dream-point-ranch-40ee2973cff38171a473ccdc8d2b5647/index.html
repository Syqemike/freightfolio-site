About

Dream Point Ranch is a wedding and event venue on a 191-acre private estate in Bixby, Oklahoma. The Mountain Crest Venue is 5,400 square feet and seats up to 350 guests, and the Riverbend Chapel hosts indoor or outdoor ceremonies. The full estate is available to rent, and elopement weddings are held Monday to Thursday for 75 guests or fewer. Call (918) 900-4850 to ask about a date.

Plan your visit

Dream Point Ranch is at 17400 East 167th Street South in Bixby, Oklahoma. Call (918) 900-4850 to ask about a tour or a date.

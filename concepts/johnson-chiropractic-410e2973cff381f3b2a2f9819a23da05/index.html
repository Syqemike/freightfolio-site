About

Johnson Chiropractic is a chiropractic clinic at 3169 Professional Plaza Drive in Germantown, Tennessee. Dr. Reed Johnson, a Life University 1995 graduate and member of the Gonstead Clinical Studies Society, was the former official chiropractor of the Mississippi Riverkings. Dr. Chancellor Johnson also practices at the clinic, along with a licensed massage therapist. Call (901) 767-6727 to ask about an appointment.

Conditions treated

Back pain, sciatica, neck and shoulder tension, headaches and migraines, joint dysfunction, sports injuries, work-related pain, and postural imbalance. Call (901) 767-6727 to ask about an appointment.

The Shop

A collective, not a franchise

Lowbrow Saints opened in 2013 and runs as an artist collective, with each tattooer working in a style of their own.

Jason Del Castillo founded Lowbrow Saints Tattoo Collective in Minneapolis in 2013. The shop has kept a lowbrow, gallery-minded identity ever since, and walk-ins are part of how it runs.

The artists take on custom work across the shop's core styles. Flash and gumball designs hang on the wall for same-day pieces.

How booking works

The shop books by phone. There is no verified online booking page, so the fastest route is to call and talk through your idea with the shop.

Walk-ins are welcome. Calling ahead is the reliable way to confirm an artist is in and taking same-day work.

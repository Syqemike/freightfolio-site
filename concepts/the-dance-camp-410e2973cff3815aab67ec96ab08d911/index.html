About

The Dance Camp is a children's dance school at 1325 Jefferson Avenue in Buffalo, New York, in the Coldspring neighborhood. The school teaches tap, jazz, ballet, modern and contemporary, lyrical, and praise dance, with Tots and Preschoolers classes, weekday and Saturday schedules, tuition and monthly billing, a June showcase, and costumes. Teachers are Karen Camp, known as Miss K, with more than 29 years of experience, Michelle Nirelli Stewart, and Amber Wyatt. Hours are Sunday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM; Monday through Thursday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM; Friday and Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Call (716) 881-5474 to ask about a class.

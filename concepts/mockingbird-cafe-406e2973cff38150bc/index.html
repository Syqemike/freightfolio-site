A community cafe since 2006

Mockingbird Cafe is a community cafe since 2006 in an 1868 cottage in Bay St. Louis. Breakfast and lunch are served, with specialty coffee and cocktails. Scratch-made dishes named in research include house biscuits, the Mockingburger, avocado toast, a hash brown bowl, the MS Honey Latte, and the Tequila Mockingbird. An outdoor patio is available, the cafe is dog friendly, and Tree House Yoga is upstairs. Call to ask about a visit.

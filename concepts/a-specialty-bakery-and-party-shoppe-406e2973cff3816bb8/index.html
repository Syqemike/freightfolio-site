Custom cakes in Gulf Shores

A Specialty Bakery and Party Shoppe is a custom cake bakery in Gulf Shores. It makes custom wedding and birthday cakes, petit fours, cupcakes, cookies, brownies, and cake truffles. Cake flavors include White, Butter, Yellow, Chocolate, German Chocolate, Strawberry, Red Velvet, Carrot, Marble, Almond, and Lemon, and icings include Buttercream, Cream Cheese, Chocolate Buttercream, Peanut Butter Buttercream, Caramel, and Coconut Pecan. Orders are by phone, visit, or Facebook message, and the bakery's site notes 2 weeks advance is recommended for custom cakes. No prices are published in research, so call with questions.

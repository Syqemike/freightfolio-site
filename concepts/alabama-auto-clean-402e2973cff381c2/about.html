Detailing the Gulf Coast since 1977.

Alabama Auto Clean has been an auto detailing shop on the Gulf Coast for nearly five decades, serving drivers from Gulfport, MS to the Florida Panhandle.

“You Ain't Gotta Drive a Dirty Car No More!!”

The shop at 665 Merwina Ave in Mobile details cars, SUVs, pick-up trucks, vans, boats, RVs, 18-wheelers, jet skis, and off-road utility vehicles. Full auto details and ceramic coatings are the core of the work.

The shop serves the Gulf Coast, from Gulfport, Mississippi to the Florida Panhandle.

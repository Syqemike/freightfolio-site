About

Griffin Fishing Charters runs inshore guided fishing trips in the Barataria Basin and Little Lake out of Jean Lafitte, with a waterfront lodge for groups that want an all-inclusive stay. Trips run on 24 to 26 foot center consoles with a captain and up to four anglers, guided by a team of seven or more guides.

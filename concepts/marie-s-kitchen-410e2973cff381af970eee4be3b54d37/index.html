About

Marie's Kitchen is a mom-and-pop Filipino restaurant and deli at 11910 Greenville Avenue, Suite 103, in Dallas, Texas, in the Forest Green Building on the first floor. Owners Marie and Jay Gerson opened it in 2019. The menu covers all-day Filipino breakfast silog plates for $12, Ube Pancakes and Coconut Pandan Pancakes for $8, omelets, a breakfast burrito, specialty sandwiches, salads, fried rice, mozzarella sticks, and quesadillas, with daily and weekly specials that rotate. Takeout and delivery are available. Hours are Monday through Friday, 7:30 AM to 4:30 PM; Saturday closed; Sunday, 8:30 AM to 2:00 PM. Call (972) 234-8383 or email inquiry@marieskitchentx.com to ask about a visit.

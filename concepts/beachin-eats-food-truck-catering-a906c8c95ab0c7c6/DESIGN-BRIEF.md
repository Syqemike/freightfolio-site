## Creative direction applied
Playful coastal food truck. Palette: teal/seafoam with sand and a coral accent. Bebas Neue condensed display type with Inter body text. Asymmetrical tilted hero photo, editorial two-column menu (menu list on one side, staggered tilted photo stack on the other) rather than uniform card grids. Sections: hero, menu, wedding catering (colored band), find the truck, visit/contact.

## Rules compliance
- No em dashes anywhere in copy. No banned AI phrases or constructions.
- No invented facts: no prices, no hours, no testimonials, no awards, no credentials. Menu descriptions are generic ("Local favorite from the Gulf Coast menu" style) and do not assert flavors or preparations not in the research.
- Rating shown exactly once with full source and breakdown.
- All CTAs say "Call to book" with tel:+12519798238. No booking buttons, no forms.
- Structure: business name prominent in header, "Design preview" disclosure bar at the very top, section nav, noindex meta in head, footer disclaimer crediting Freightfolio with a link to https://freightfolio.net.

The Atrium

An indoor event venue at Overton Square in Memphis, known as The Atrium. Steel arches and high ceilings set the room, with a catering kitchen, a private green room, upscale restrooms, and handicap access. The room seats 150 for a reception and holds up to 250 for a cocktail party. WiFi is available and the venue is pet friendly.

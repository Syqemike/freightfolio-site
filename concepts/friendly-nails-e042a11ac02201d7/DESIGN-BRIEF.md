NOTE: Instagram profile friendlynails_worcester was hit by an IG bot check during this shift, so no real business photos were harvestable; labeled stock is the documented fallback.

## Aesthetic
Playful, friendly, zero-fuss. Hot pink + peach + cream. Rounded bubbly display type. Warm, not fussy.

## Typography
Display: "Baloo 2" (rounded, playful). Body: "Nunito".

## Palette
- Pink: #e2558a, deep #c23e70 (buttons, headings, contact card)
- Deep plum: #3d2531 (topbar, hours band, footer)
- Cream #fff7f3, peach #ffe9df (services band), hairline #f3d9d2

## Layout
1. Top bar: "Friendly" (pink) "Nails", "Design preview" chip, Call button.
2. Sticky nav: Services, The shop, Hours, Book.
3. Hero: two-column. Left: eyebrow, Baloo headline "Great nails, zero fuss.", lede, rating line, Call to book + See services. Right: rounded filing photo with soft pink shadow, stock caption.
4. Services: peach band, 5 rounded cards (Dip powder nails; Acrylic nails; Gel nails; Pedicures; Call for pricing).
5. The shop: reversed two-column (acrylic photo left, copy right), review-driven neutral copy.
6. Hours: plum band with full verified hours table + red-polish manicure photo.
7. Visit: address, directions link, polish-application photo; pink "Book your nails" contact card (call only).
8. Footer: disclosure (labeled stock photos), "Concept by Freightfolio" + freightfolio.net.

## Customer journey
Visitor sees the friendly positioning and verified service list, checks hours, calls to book. Primary CTA: Call (508) 755-0200.

Overnight stays in a place your dog already knows

Vanity Furs is both a salon and a hotel, so boarding dogs stay with the same people who groom them. If your dog already comes here for baths and haircuts, a hotel stay means familiar faces and familiar routines.

Before the stay

What helps the first night go smoothly

When you call to book boarding, ask what to pack. Most owners send their dog’s regular food, a bed or blanket that smells like home, and one favorite toy.

Book by phone

Call (904) 387-9900 to check availability and reserve your dog’s stay. Boarding is booked directly, just like grooming appointments.

Combine with a groom

Many owners book a bath or haircut at the end of a boarding stay, so their dog comes home clean. Ask when you call.

Hours to know

Drop-off and pick-up follow salon hours: Tuesday through Friday 9 AM to 4 PM, Saturday 10 AM to 4 PM, closed Sunday and Monday.

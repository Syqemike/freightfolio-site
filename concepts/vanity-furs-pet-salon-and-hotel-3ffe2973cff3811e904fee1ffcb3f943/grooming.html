Grooming

The full salon treatment

Every groom starts with a shampoo and conditioner bath using all-natural shampoos, followed by a blow out. From there, pick the finish your dog needs.

Full-body haircut

A complete cut shaped to your dog’s coat and your preference, with nails and ears done and anal glands expressed as part of the service.

  • Shampoo and conditioner bath
  • Blow out
  • Full-body haircut
  • Nails and ears
  • Anal glands

De-shedding treatment

A 30 to 45 minute de-shedding session that works loose undercoat out of heavy-shedding breeds, finishing with nails, ears, and anal glands.

  • Shampoo and conditioner bath
  • Blow out
  • 30-45 minute de-shedding
  • Nails and ears
  • Anal glands

Bath and tidy

For dogs that do not need a haircut: a thorough bath, blow out, nail trim, and ear cleaning to keep them fresh between full grooms.

  • Shampoo and conditioner bath
  • Blow out
  • Nail trim
  • Ear cleaning

Extras

Small additions that make a difference. Ask about them when you call.

  • Tooth brushing
  • All-natural shampoos

A Manchester-district cafe

Cafe Zata is a neighborhood cafe in Richmond's Manchester district, serving breakfast and lunch all day. Its drinks include Ironclad Coffee, Carytown Teas, and Cupertino's NY Bagels, plus a dirty chai latte, a s'mores latte, and vanilla matcha. The cafe is remote-work friendly, with free WiFi and a chill patio.

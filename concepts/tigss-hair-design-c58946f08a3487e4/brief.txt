Tigg's Hair Design concept - design brief

Modern urban barbershop with 1920s art-deco stripe detailing. Warm charcoal (#2a2521) base with cream paper panels (#f6f0e2) and terracotta (#c2571f) accents, set in a strong grotesque system stack with tight-tracked headlines and wide-tracked eyebrow labels. The services menu is styled as perforated service tickets with rip-off stubs, and a dedicated downtown/community section carries only the verified facts (new 6th and Phillips Ave location, Black Hills Beauty College certification, 2018 Teen Night coverage, Black-owned business directory listing). Photo-free: no business-owned photos could be verified, so no stock or third-party imagery was used.

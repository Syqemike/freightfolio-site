Services

Five ways to sit down.

Everything below is done at the salon on Mt Pleasant Street. Prices are not published online, so call for current pricing.

01

Manicures

A manicure at Nail Asia covers shaping, cuticle care, and polish in the color you pick. Gel color and black gel color are on the options list.

Designs can be added to any set. Bring a reference or describe the mood and the technicians will work it out at the table.

02

Pedicures

Pedicures are a full sit-down service.

Seated, unhurried, and finished with polish.

03

Acrylic full sets

Full sets build length and shape from scratch. Coffin nails are on the options list.

The technicians shape each set to the look the client asked for, long or short, square, almond, or coffin.

04

Fills

Fills keep a set going. Growth is filled in, the shape is restored, and the set leaves looking new again.

A steady option for clients who keep their length week to week.

05

Nail art and designs

This is the design table. Ombre fades, rhinestones, holographic foils, and hand-drawn details, worked out one set at a time.

Appointments are recommended when you want something special. The lookbook page shows what has left the salon.

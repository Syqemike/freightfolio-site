New Bedford, Massachusetts · On Mt Pleasant St for over a decade

Nail Asia has been doing nails on Mt Pleasant Street for more than ten years.

Walk in and the menu is the easy part. Manicures, pedicures, acrylic full sets, fills, and hand-drawn nail art. Ombre fades, coffin shapes, rhinestones, holographic foils. The options list is long and the color wall is longer.

The salon is staffed by licensed manicurists, pedicurists, and technicians, and the room is kept clean and sanitary. Each set is shaped to the look the client asked for, one sitting at a time.

Designs come from a sense of fashion or a mood, or from an idea the client brings in the door.

Call ahead when you want something special. The design table rewards a little planning. Call to book: (508) 984-5935.

The salon

Over a decade of practice, in one room on Mt Pleasant Street.

Nail Asia has devoted more than ten years to cosmetology for hands and feet. The team is made up of licensed manicurists, pedicurists, and technicians, and the salon keeps a clean, sanitary room.

The work is straightforward. The technicians shape each set to the look the client asked for, and they are known for recreating designs a client brings in.

Clean. Professional. Nails. That is how the salon signs its own description, and the room backs it up.

The options list runs long: ombre nails, coffin nails, rhinestones, holographic foils, and black gel color, among others. Manicures and pedicures round out the menu.

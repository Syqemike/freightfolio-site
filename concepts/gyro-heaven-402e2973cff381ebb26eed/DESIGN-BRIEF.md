# Design Brief: Gyro Heaven (Batch 27)

Business: Gyro Heaven. Greek gyro food truck, 232 Coming St, Charleston, SC 29403.
Hours: Friday and Saturday 9:00 PM to 2:30 AM only. Phone: (646) 678-1824.
Verified facts only: menu = Lamb Gyro, Chicken Gyro, Gyro Plate, All-star special, Philly Cheesesteaks, Beef Burger, Beef Sausage, Beef Hotdog. Rating: 5.0 / 11 (Google-aggregated listing), shown once. Facebook page only, no real site.

## Aesthetic
Late-night street food, night-owl energy. Dark navy city-night background, warm golden amber like spit-roasted meat and warm pita, a neon teal accent like a street-sign glow. Chunky hand-painted-sign energy through typography alone. Zero illustrations, zero decorative graphics, no CSS art: the photos carry the visuals. Nothing fine-dining about it.

## Typography
Display: Anton (bold condensed, loud street-sign headlines). Body: Inter (readable on phones in the dark). Small caps eyebrow labels for section kicker lines.

## Palette
- Night navy: #0b0e17 (page background)
- Deep blue-black: #141a2b (cards, alternating sections)
- Amber/gold: #f2a93b (headlines, highlights, buttons)
- Pita cream: #f6ead3 (light text on dark)
- Tzatziki white: #fffdf8 (bright text)
- Neon teal: #35e0c8 (accent: hours chip glow, borders)
- Muted: #8b93a7 (secondary text)

## Imagery
- Hero: REAL night photo of Gyro Heaven's tent on Coming St (Yelp user photo), darkened with gradient so headline sits on it.
- Gallery: 1 real photo + 4 stock photos, each captioned; stock ones labeled "Stock photo" in the caption. No photo is presented as the business when it is stock.
- All images compressed JPG under 5 MB.

## Layout (single page, mobile-first)
1. Sticky top bar: GYRO HEAVEN name, "Call" button (tel:).
2. Hero: name, tagline "Greek gyros, late night on Coming St.", hours chip "Open Fri + Sat, 9 PM to 2:30 AM", rating chip "5.0 / 11 Google reviews", Call + Facebook buttons. Design-preview disclosure directly under hero.
3. Menu: bold list of the 8 verified items with short plain descriptions derived only from verified facts (lamb gyro, chicken gyro, gyro plate, all-star special, Philly cheesesteak, beef burger, beef sausage, beef hotdog). No prices invented.
4. Hours and Find Us: Fri/Sat 9:00 PM-2:30 AM table, "Closed the rest of the week", address 232 Coming St, Charleston SC, phone, Facebook page link.
5. Photos: gallery grid with captions and provenance labels.
6. Footer: name, address, phone, Facebook, "Website Concept. Design Preview. Not affiliated with Gyro Heaven."

## Customer journey (phone in hand at 1 AM)
Hero answers the three questions in seconds: open right now? where? how do I order? Call button is always one tap away (sticky bar + hero + find-us section). Menu is a short scan, no prices to mislead.

## Copy rules honored
No em dashes. No invented reviews, testimonials, prices, awards, or hours. Rating appears once with source and count. No online ordering destination exists, so every CTA is Call (tel:), never "Book Now".

## Quality benchmarks
Distinct from TailWaggers / Studio 601: dark night palette, single-page truck layout, menu-centered structure built for food trucks, not salons.

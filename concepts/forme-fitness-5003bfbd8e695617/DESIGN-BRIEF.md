# Design Brief — Formé Fitness (forme-fitness-5003bfbd8e695617)

Boutique group-fitness studio: barre, yoga, HIIT, pilates. 714 Delmas Ave, Pascagoula, MS 39567. Phone (228) 218-5569.
Hours: Mon-Fri 7:00 AM - 7:00 PM; weekend hours not published (stated honestly). Rating: "4.88 stars from 34 Mindbody ratings" (shown once, exact source).
No standalone website; no verified online booking: all CTAs "Call to book" tel:+12282185569. Claim-free contact copy.

## Aesthetic
Modern boutique studio. Bold, dark, energetic. Black hero with coral punch. Feels like a premium class studio, not a big-box gym.

## Typography
Display: "Oswald" (bold condensed, uppercase). Body: "Inter". Eyebrow kickers in coral caps.

## Palette
- Ink black: #141316 (hero, schedule band, footer), soft #232227
- Warm paper: #faf8f4 (body), white cards, hairline #e8e2d8
- Electric coral: #ff5a48 (accents, buttons), deep #d63f30

## Layout
1. Top bar: "formé" wordmark styling (lowercase, accent on e), "Design preview" chip, Call button.
2. Sticky dark nav: Classes, Schedule, Visit, Contact.
3. Hero: split layout. Left (black): eyebrow listing the four disciplines, big Oswald headline "Train your way in Pascagoula.", lede with address, Mindbody rating line, Call to book + See classes. Right: full-bleed barre class photo with stock caption.
4. Classes: 2x2 card grid (Barre / Yoga / HIIT / Pilates), each with photo, tag line, short description, stock caption. No invented class durations or prices.
5. Schedule: black band. Verified Mon-Fri 7-7 table; honest note that weekend hours are not published and to call.
6. Visit: address, Google Maps directions link, "Ready to start?" contact card (call only).
7. Footer: design-preview disclosure (labeled stock photos), "Concept by Freightfolio" + freightfolio.net.

## Customer journey
Visitor sees the four disciplines, picks a class, checks hours, calls to book. Primary CTA: Call (228) 218-5569.

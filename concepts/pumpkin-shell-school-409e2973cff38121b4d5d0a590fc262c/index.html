About

Pumpkin Shell School is a preschool and childcare center at 1037 N University Blvd in Norman. The teacher to student ratio is about 1 to 8, with hands-on centers plus group time. Programs run for ages 2 and a half to 3, 3 to 4, and 4 to 5, with a pre-reading and phonics emphasis in the oldest class. Tours are available Monday through Friday, 8:30 to 11 AM, by phone, and tuition is given by phone only.

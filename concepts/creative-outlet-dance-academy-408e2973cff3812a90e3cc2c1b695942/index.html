About

Creative Outlet Dance Academy is a dance academy in Fairhope for children and adults, with three studios with sprung floors. Classes cover ballet, jazz, hip hop, tap, lyrical, modern, acro, and adult classes, and the academy stages themed productions including A Fairhope Nutcracker.

About

Bloom Brigade is a florist on the North Side of Pittsburgh, in the Allegheny West neighborhood near PNC Park. The studio builds custom flower arrangements for weddings, holidays, parties, showers, and funerals, plus wedding and event flowers, table centerpieces, and everyday arrangements. Work is by appointment, arranged by phone or email.

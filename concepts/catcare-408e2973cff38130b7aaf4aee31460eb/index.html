Eugene's only cat-exclusive veterinary clinic

Care built around cats

The clinic's own site describes personal, expert care for feline patients in a friendly, comfortable setting. Educating clients matters here: the team shares information and skills so owners can care for their feline companions with more confidence.

Dr. Patricia Shea is named on the clinic's own site as one of its veterinarians. The clinic stays current with advances in medicine and technology, and treats cats with loving care at every check-up, procedure, and surgery.

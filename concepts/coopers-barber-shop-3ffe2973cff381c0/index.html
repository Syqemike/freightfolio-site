The man behind the chair

Meet Coop

Gary Cooper is the master barber behind Cooper's. Reviewers call him the best barber in town, and not just for the cuts: they write about the conversation, the laugh on the phone, and the way he treats every kid who climbs into the chair.

One reviewer drove an hour each way and said they would be back. Another called it the most welcoming shop in the Charleston metro area.

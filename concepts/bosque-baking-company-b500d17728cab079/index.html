Artisan Sourdough Bakery · Albuquerque, New Mexico

Naturally leavened sourdough, baked fresh in Albuquerque.

Bosque Baking Company is an artisan sourdough bakery in Albuquerque. The loaves are naturally leavened and baked in small batches through the week, alongside pastries, empanadas, and pies.

At the Growers' Markets

Find Us · 03

Bosque Baking Company sells at the Downtown and Corrales Growers' Markets. The same breads and pastries from the bakery, sold at the market table.

Market days The bakery is open Wednesday through Friday, 11am to 6pm, and Saturday, 9am to 3pm. Closed Sunday through Tuesday.

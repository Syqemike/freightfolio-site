About

Cadence Coffee Company is a direct-trade coffee shop at 11 East 7th Street in Chattanooga, Tennessee. The menu covers specialty espresso drinks, pastries, local and seasonal quiche, in-house cream cheeses, and bagels and breakfast sandwiches. The shop is mission-driven: nonprofit partnerships, pay-it-forward coffees, and employment for people facing homelessness. This record is the downtown 7th Street shop only; the brand's other locations are not covered here.

Menu

Specialty espresso drinks

Iced caramel latte, iced vanilla latte, cold brew, brown sugar latte, and chai, plus signature pun-named lattes including the Chattahooligan and the Blueberry Moc-iatto.

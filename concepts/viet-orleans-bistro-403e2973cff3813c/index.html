Vietnamese cooking and a sushi bar

The menu pairs Vietnamese cooking with a full sushi bar. Pho comes with chicken, rare eye round, brisket, meatball, shrimp and crab, or tofu. Banh mi, spring rolls, egg rolls and crab rangoon sit alongside vermicelli and rice plates, fried rice and stir-fried egg noodles.

At the sushi bar: nigiri and sashimi, plus rolls including the Crunchy, Crunchy Dynamite, Tuna Avocado and California rolls, and the house New Orleans, Baronne and Black and Gold rolls. Thai tea, Vietnamese iced coffee, limeade and draft beer round out the drinks.

About

Barkmore In The Country is a dog daycare, boarding, and spa facility in Ooltewah. Boarding asks a deposit equal to the first night's stay, daycare starts with a temperament test, and spa add-ons are available. Vaccinations required are Bordetella, Rabies, and DHPP, and spay or neuter is required after 9 months for daycare.

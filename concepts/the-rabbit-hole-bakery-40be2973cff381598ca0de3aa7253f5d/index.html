About

The Rabbit Hole Bakery is a small-batch bakery in Lincoln, Nebraska, with a Haymarket shop at 800 Q Street and a second counter in the Gateway Mall Food Court. Desserts are made from scratch in small batches, with 20 to 25 types or flavors on offer each day plus beverages. Google reviewers rate it 4.8 stars from 146 reviews. The bakery's own site is a thin single page with no menu or ordering page, so this concept shows what a full site could look like.

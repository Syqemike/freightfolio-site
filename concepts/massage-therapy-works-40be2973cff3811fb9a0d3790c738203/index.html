About

Massage Therapy Works is a massage practice at 3501 Severn Avenue in Metairie, Louisiana. Owner Lydia opened the practice in 2013. Sessions are therapeutic massage tailored to the client, aimed at injury prevention, increased range of motion, decreased fascial binding, and recovery after intense exercise. Clients include bodybuilders, cyclists, students, desk workers, and pregnant clients.

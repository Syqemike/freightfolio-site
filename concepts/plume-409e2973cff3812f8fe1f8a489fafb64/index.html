About

Plume is a shop at 5751 S Rte K in Columbia, a gathering place for more than 90 local artists and vendors. The range covers handmade gifts and home decor, including baby and wedding gifts, handmade and vintage jewelry and accessories, creams, soaps, and scrubs, and handcrafted and upcycled furniture and decor. The in-store Bake Shoppe sells cupcakes, gourmet popcorn, macarons, and cookies, and the Gathering Room is rentable for private events.

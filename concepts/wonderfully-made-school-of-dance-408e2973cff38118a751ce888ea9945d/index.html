About the school

Dance, taught with faith at the center

Wonderfully Made School of Dance is a Christian dance school for all ages, with a Kenner studio and a second studio in Slidell. Classes run from Babies and Tiny Tots through Teens, in ballet, tap, jazz, lyrical, contemporary, hip hop, liturgical dance, and mime. The school also teaches choreography, holds dance workshops, and puts on a recital each season.

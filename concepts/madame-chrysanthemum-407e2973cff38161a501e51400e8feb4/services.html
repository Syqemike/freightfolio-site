Services

Madame Chrysanthemum is a floral studio and boutique in Savannah, making unique and unexpected floral designs for any occasion: weddings and special events, individual arrangements, wedding bouquets, ikebana-inspired sympathy arrangements, mini bouquets, and boutonnieres, plus a build-your-own bouquet bar and a boutique of gifts, jewelry, greeting cards, and local art.

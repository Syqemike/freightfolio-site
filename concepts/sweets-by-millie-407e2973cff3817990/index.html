Sweets by Millie

A boutique bakery in Louisville run by owner Maggie Hoy, in business for more than 11 years. The bakery makes custom celebration cakes, cupcakes, signature Millie Cookies, and wedding cakes, with takeout and delivery and consultation by email. Signature flavors include chocolate cake with raspberry buttercream, red velvet with cream cheese icing, and brown sugar pound cake with bourbon buttercream.

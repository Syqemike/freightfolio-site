# Design Brief — Shave Barbershop (St. Louis, MO)

## Design personality
Downtown classic, editorial. A broadsheet-newspaper treatment: cream paper background,
deep navy ink, silver rules and column borders. Light-on-dark-inverse (the opposite of
the typical dark-espresso/amber barbershop site). Timeless, orderly, dignified.

## Typography
- Display: "Old Standard TT" (serif with newspaper lineage), centered masthead nameplate.
- Body: "Source Serif 4" for reading text; small-caps eyebrows for section labels.
- Hierarchy: masthead rule-set nameplate > broadsheet section headers with flanking rules > ledger rows.

## Palette
- Paper: #f7f1e5 (cream)
- Ink: #17233d (deep navy)
- Rules/silver: #b8b1a1
- Accent: muted navy wash for CTA blocks; no amber, no brown, no gradients.

## Imagery
3 stock photos, all labeled "Stock photo":
- shave_shop.jpg — classic barbershop interior (hero side)
- shave_shave.jpg — straight-razor shave (services)
- shave_tools.jpg — barber tools flatlay (shop section)
No public photos of the business exist; all photos are stock, labeled as stock.

## Layout
- Disclosure bar (thin navy) at very top.
- Centered masthead with address/phone under nameplate, sticky section nav below.
- Hero: two-column broadsheet (headline + copy left, shop photo right).
- Services: ledger with dotted price leaders; note "Haircuts about $40, per reviews."
- Barbers: two-column typographic introductions, Erik R. ("Big E") and Hank, monogram initials.
- Shop: photo pair with captions.
- Visit: hours table, address, accessibility notes, hours caveat, rating (once).
- Navy footer with full disclaimer and provenance.

## Customer journey
Land, read the masthead promise ("Downtown's classic barbershop"), see the
services ledger and the two barbers, check hours, then Call to book (tel: link).
Every booking CTA is "Call to book"; no online booking is verified.

## Copy notes
- Facts used only from research: address, phone, email, hours, services list,
  $40 haircuts per reviews, walk-ins welcome/appointments recommended,
  barbers' names, 4.8 from 36 Google reviews, wheelchair-accessible, parking,
  LGBTQ+ friendly.
- Hours caveat reproduced honestly: one reviewer reported the shop was not open
  at the listed time, so the page says hours can shift and to call ahead.
- No invented facts, no em dashes, no banned AI phrases, no No-X-no-Y triples.
- Rating appears exactly once on the page.

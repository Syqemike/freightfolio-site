## Creative direction
Energetic, stage-ready dance studio. Deep plum/black + hot magenta + gold spotlight accents.
Anton (bold display) for headlines + Archivo (clean sans) for body. Dark dramatic layout:
full-bleed hero over a stage photo, skewed gold kicker badge, magenta marquee strip with
class names, angular cards with magenta borders, spotlight-gold recital section.
Copy is plain and specific, no urgency, no invented registration promises. CTA copy:
"Questions about classes? Call (850) 932-5555."
Deliberately NOTHING like the daycare concept: dark vs light, angular vs rounded,
display type vs serif, magenta/gold vs sky blue/cream.

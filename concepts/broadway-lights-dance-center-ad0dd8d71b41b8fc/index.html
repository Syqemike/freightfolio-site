01 · Classes

Four styles to train in

Training for young beginners through adults, in ballet, hip hop, contemporary, and musical theater.

Ages 3 and up

Stock photo: young girls in a ballet class.

Ballet

The foundation of it all. Technique, balance, and grace taught step by step, starting with our youngest dancers.

High energy

Stock photo: teens in a hip hop rehearsal.

Hip Hop

Big beats, sharp moves, and real confidence. A fast favorite with dancers who want to move with power.

Contemporary

Fluid, expressive movement that blends ballet technique with modern style. A place to dance what you feel.

Musical Theater

Dance built for the stage. Performance style, character, and showmanship for dancers who love the spotlight.

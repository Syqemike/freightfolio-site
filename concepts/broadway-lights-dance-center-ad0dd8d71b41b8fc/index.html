Act I · Classes

Four styles to train in, for young beginners through adults. Each class builds technique week by week.

i.

Ballet

The foundation of it all. The youngest dancers start here and build skills one class at a time.

ii.

Hip Hop

A high-energy class set to big beats. Dancers learn sharp, powerful moves.

iii.

Contemporary

Fluid, expressive movement that blends ballet technique with modern style.

iv.

Musical Theater

Dance built for the stage, with a focus on character and showmanship.

Act II · Programs

From a first class to the competition floor, there is a way to dance here that fits.

Group classes

Train alongside other dancers at your level, from age 3 to adult.

Private lessons

One-on-one coaching focused entirely on your dancing.

Workshops

Short, focused sessions to sharpen skills and try new styles.

Summer camps

Keep dancing through the summer with camp programs.

Competition team

For dancers ready to take their training to the competition stage.

Act III · Recital

The annual recital at the Saenger Theatre.

Every year, Broadway Lights dancers take the stage in Pensacola for the annual recital. It is the performance the whole season builds toward.

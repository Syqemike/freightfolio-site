Bakery by day, pizzeria by night

Union Loafers is an artisan bakery, cafe, and pizzeria in St. Louis's Botanical Heights. Its hearth-baked, naturally leavened sourdough breads are sold by the loaf. Its food includes sandwiches, salads, and soups, plus chocolate chip cookies, and its evening pizzas include pepperoni, spinach-bacon, Provel, and mushroom with pickled shallot, served with buttermilk dipping sauce. The bakery-cafe offers craft beers and wine, outdoor seating, and takeout.

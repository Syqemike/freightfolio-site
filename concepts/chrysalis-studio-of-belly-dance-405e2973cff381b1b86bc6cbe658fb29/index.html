Muscles are for movement. Belly dance them.

Chrysalis Studio of Belly Dance teaches beginner and mix-level belly dance in small classes in Omaha. The studio's own words describe belly dance as a celebration of the feminine and the cycles of life, and a class as time to nurture body, mind, and spirit.

What the studio teaches

Classes built around the dance itself

Beginner classes

Belly dance classes for students starting out, taught in small classes so instruction stays close.

Mix-level classes

Classes that bring dancers of different levels together, covering drills and choreography.

Drills and choreography

Class work includes drills and choreographed pieces, building technique step by step.

Props: canes, veils, fan veils

Students work with canes, veils, and fan veils as part of the studio's classes.

Private and group sessions

Private and group sessions are available on request. Call the studio to ask about arranging one.

A Tuesday or Wednesday workout class

The studio's own site describes a Tuesday or Wednesday 5:30 belly dance workout, with a focus on low impact aerobic movement.

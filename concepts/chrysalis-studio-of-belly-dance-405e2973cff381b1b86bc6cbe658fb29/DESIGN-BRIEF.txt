DESIGN BRIEF - Chrysalis Studio of Belly Dance (Omaha, NE)
Single-page concept, batch 45, 2026-10-27.

Aesthetic: warm, stage-lit, editorial. The page leans on the studio's own performance
photography rather than decoration: deep plum panels frame the class list and visit
sections, warm cream carries the hero and gallery, and a slight tilt on the hero photo
echoes a dancer mid-movement. No illustrations, no decorative graphics of any kind.

Typography: Marcellus (display serif, elegant and dance-like) for headings and the brand
mark; Karla (clean, readable sans) for body copy, navigation, and captions.

Palette: deep plum #2e1a2e and darker plum #1f1120 for bands and buttons; copper
#b97e4b and soft copper #d9ac7c for eyebrows, accents, and the call link; warm cream
#faf3ea page background with cream-dark dividers. The palette is drawn from the
costumes in the studio's own photos (plum, copper, warm stage tones).

Imagery: three real photos from the studio's own website delladancing.com (see
PROVENANCE.txt): veil work as the hero, finger cymbals and stage costumes in the
gallery. Captions name each photo as from the studio's own website.

Layout: asymmetric hero (text left, tilted photo right on desktop, stacked on mobile);
a full-width plum band for the six class cards in a 3-column grid; a two-photo gallery;
a dark visit section split between address/call and listed hours. Simple top navigation
(Classes, Photos, Visit, Call) anchors to page sections.

Copy rules honored: only verified facts from the CRM research notes and the studio's
own site. No invented class times, prices, age ranges, instructors, or credentials.
Schedule and pricing are explicitly marked as not listed, with a call prompt. Rating
appears exactly once ("4.9 from 15 Google reviews"). No Book Now button (no verified
online booking destination); all CTAs are Call links to the verified phone
(402) 658-4978. No em dashes, no banned AI phrases, no "No X, no Y" triples, no
invented urgency or operating claims.

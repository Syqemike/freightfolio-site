Services at East Village Bohemian Pizzeria

Family-owned Neapolitan wood-fired pizza in downtown Tulsa, with an eclectic dining room and a patio garden.

Services

Wood-fired Neapolitan pizzas

Pizzas cooked in an 800 degree wood-fired oven. Named pies include Margherita Bohemian, Tre P, Cacio e Pepe, Craigie's Angry Bee, Mojo, Queen Margherita, and Bacchus alla Vodka.

Handmade pastas

Salads, appetizers, and desserts

The menu also includes salads, appetizers, and desserts.

Bar

Craft cocktails, Italian wines, local beers, and mocktails.

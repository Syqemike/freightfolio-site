About

East Village Bohemian Pizzeria is a family-owned restaurant in downtown Tulsa, run by Pat Johnson and Amy Johnson McMillin. Pizzas are cooked Neapolitan style in a wood-fired oven at 800 degrees, using imported Italian ingredients and local sausage. The dining room is eclectic, the patio garden is outdoors, and the bar serves craft cocktails, Italian wines, local beers, and mocktails.

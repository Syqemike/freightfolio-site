The Service Index

Cuts

Men’s and women’s haircuts

A good cut starts with a conversation. Tell us how you wear your hair day to day, what has worked, and what has not. We cut to match the person, not a chart.

Color

Full color services

From subtle shifts to complete changes, we formulate color for your hair and your skin tone. Our specialty is custom European color with creative placement, built for lightening in some areas and depth in others.

Smoothing

Keratin straighteners and Brazilian blowout

For hair that fights frizz or resists styling, we offer keratin straighteners and Brazilian blowout treatments. Ask us which one fits your hair and your routine.

Texture

Perms

Perms for curl, wave, and body. We talk through the look you want before anything starts, so the result suits how you actually style your hair.

02

The Color Studio

Our specialty

Custom European color, placed for your hair.

Color is not one formula applied the same way to everyone. Our custom European color work starts with your natural base, then adds creative placement that brings lightening where it flatters and depth where it grounds.

The result is color with dimension instead of a flat, single tone. It grows out naturally and keeps looking intentional between visits. Bring a photo of what you like, or come with an open mind, and we will plan the placement together.

If you have been thinking about changing your color, call and ask what is possible for your hair.

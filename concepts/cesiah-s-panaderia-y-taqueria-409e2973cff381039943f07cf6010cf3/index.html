About

Cesiah's Panaderia y Taqueria is a panaderia and pasteleria in Lubbock serving Mexican food with sazón, alongside Mexican brand articles. The menu covers chilaquiles, Catalina's special plates, pancakes, a huevos rancheros plate, and street tacos, and the bakery is known for Marranitas and tres leches cakes to order in quarter, half, and full sheet sizes.

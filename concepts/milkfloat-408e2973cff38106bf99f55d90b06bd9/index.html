Why the name

A milk float was a vehicle built to deliver fresh milk to neighborhoods. Milkfloat takes its name from those vehicles and from the connection to farms and communities that the name stands for.

The business is owned and operated by Bellwether LLC. Its own site describes a spacious, welcoming room for conversations, with seasonal flavors, baking fresh, a cup of coffee or tea, a hand-mixed soda, and an old-fashioned milkshake.

Programs built on play

Daycare and preschool programs for Savannah families, centered on the HighScope Curriculum and shared meals. Here is what shapes every day here.

The curriculum

HighScope: children learn by doing

HighScope is a research-based early learning approach built on a simple loop: children plan what they will do, carry it out, and then talk about what happened. Teachers guide, observe, and ask questions that stretch thinking.

In practice, that means the classroom is full of choices: building corners, art tables, dress-up bins, books within small reach, and sensory play. Kids direct their own play within a structured, caring routine, which builds confidence, language, and early problem-solving.

It is preschool the way childhood is supposed to feel: busy hands, big imaginations, and teachers who know when to step in and when to let the discovery happen.

The setting

Room to roam, inside and out

The center has dedicated classroom space plus a fenced outdoor playground with slides and climbing equipment, where kids burn energy and practice the big social lessons: taking turns, making friends, and inventing games.

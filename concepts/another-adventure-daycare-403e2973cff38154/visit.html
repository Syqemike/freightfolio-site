About us

Locally owned, kid approved

Another Adventure Daycare is locally owned and operated right here in Savannah, Georgia. We run daycare and preschool programs built around the HighScope Curriculum, which means children learn through play, hands-on exploration, and plenty of time outdoors.

Breakfast, lunch, and a snack are provided in all programs. Tours are offered, and we are happy to walk you through the classrooms and answer your questions in person.

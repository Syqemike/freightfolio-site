The day's journey

One day, five little adventures

Every morning starts a new expedition. Here is how a typical day unfolds for the children in our care.

  1. 1

    Welcome aboard

    Doors open at 7:30 in the morning. Children arrive, settle in, and the day's adventure begins with familiar faces and a warm hello.

  2. 2

    Plan, play, reflect

    Guided by the HighScope approach, children choose what to explore, dive into hands-on play, and talk through what they discovered. Learning through play, every single day.

  3. 3

    Meals together

    Breakfast, lunch, and a snack are provided in all programs, shared around the table with friends. Nobody packs a lunchbox unless they want to.

  4. 4

    Time outside

    The adventure heads outdoors to our fenced playground with slides and room to run. Fresh air is part of the daily route.

  5. 5

    Head home

    The day winds down with quieter play until pickup at 5:30. Pickup time is the best part of the day.

Learning through play, the HighScope way

We use the HighScope Curriculum, an approach built on the idea that children learn best by doing. It is not worksheets and sitting still. It is curiosity with a plan.

Plan

Children decide what they want to do and where they want to do it, building early decision-making skills.

Do

Hands-on exploration with real materials: blocks, art supplies, sensory bins, and make-believe.

Review

Kids talk about what they made and what happened, turning play into language and reasoning.

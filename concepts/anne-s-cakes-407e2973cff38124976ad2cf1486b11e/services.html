Anne's Cakes is a bakery and cake shop in Nashville, known for red velvet, chocolate, lemon chiffon, caramel, coconut creme, strawberry, and German chocolate cakes, plus muffins and other baked goods. Advance ordering is required and cakes sell out. Cakes are also sold at Sperrys Mercantile in Belle Meade. Take-out and delivery are listed.

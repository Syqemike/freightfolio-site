Design brief: TNT Electric, LLC (Baytown, TX electrical contractor) — Website Dev batch 31 wave 2

AESTHETIC: Job-site work order. A tradesman's clipboard translated to the web: flat paper, heavy ink,
stenciled amber highlights, hairline rules, numbered ledger rows. No gradients, no glow, no illustrations.
Photography carries the color; everything else is flat and typographic.

PALETTE (flat, no gradients):
- Paper #f5f1e6 (warm off-white page ground)
- Ink #18191d (near-black text, mastheads, footer band)
- Wire amber #e09112 (accents, numbers, rules, CTAs)
- Steel #555a63 (secondary text)
- White #ffffff for the work-order ticket panel
All surfaces flat. Buttons are solid rectangles with a hard offset shadow (industrial tag feel), never pills.

TYPOGRAPHY:
- Display: "Oswald" (Google Fonts), uppercase, tight tracking — condensed industrial signage feel.
- Body: "Inter" (Google Fonts), regular/medium. System fallback stacks.
- Phone numbers and ledger numerals set in tabular figures, oversized.

IMAGERY: 6 labelled stock photos of electrical panel/breaker work (no real business photos exist).
Hero uses one large photo beside a "work order ticket" fact panel. A full-bleed photo band and a
6-up gallery complete the set. Every caption says "Stock photo".

LAYOUT (deliberately non-generic; no hero + 3-column cards):
- Thin top disclosure strip (ink, amber text): "Website Concept — Design Preview".
- Masthead: giant stacked condensed business name left; phone set huge right; thick amber rule under.
- Hero: asymmetric split. Photo with caption bar left. Right: a white "work order" ticket panel with
  hairline borders listing verified facts as line items (trade, estimates, warranties, location),
  then the rating as its own standalone typographic line (exactly once per page), then the Call to book button.
- Services: numbered ledger rows (01 / 02 / 03) with hairline dividers instead of cards.
- Full-bleed ink photo band mid-page with amber headline type.
- Gallery: editorial 2-col masonry-ish grid, all captioned stock.
- Contact: giant phone number, address, "Call to book" — no online booking anywhere (none verified).
- Footer: ink band, name, phone, address, disclosure repeat, copyright.

JOURNEY: Land → read the work-order facts and rating → scan the numbered services → see the work
in the gallery → call to book. Every page ends with the same call block.

FACTS (only these, never beyond):
TNT Electric, LLC, electrician / general electrical contracting, 606 Rollingbrook Dr Ste 214, Baytown, TX.
Phone +1 281-819-2222. Free estimates. Warranties. Listed Active / Open Now.
Rating: 4.9 stars from 127 reviews on HomeAdvisor (exactly once per page, source + breakdown).
NOT verified: service list, service area beyond Baytown TX area, hours, emergency availability,
online booking, reviews content, awards, team. None of it appears anywhere on the site.

About

White Oak Estate and Gardens is a plantation-style wedding and event venue on 22 acres in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, operated by Chef John Folse's company. The estate offers a grand French ballroom, a New Orleans-style courtyard with a covered pavilion, and an oak-lined lawn, with seating for up to 350 guests.

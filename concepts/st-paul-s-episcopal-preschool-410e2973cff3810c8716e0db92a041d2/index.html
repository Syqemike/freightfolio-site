About

St. Paul's Episcopal Preschool is a Christian preschool on the St. Paul's Episcopal Church campus at 28788 North Main Street in Daphne, Alabama, in operation for more than 15 years. The preschool serves children ages 1 year through Pre-K 4. The core program runs 9 AM to 1 PM. Morning care runs 7 to 9 AM and after-school care runs 1 to 5:30 PM for working parents. The interim director is Elizabeth Faust. Hours are Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 5:30 PM. Call (251) 626-2774 to ask about enrollment.

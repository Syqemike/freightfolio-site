No Limit Charter And Guide

Fishing charters out of Pensacola with Capt. Jacob Miller, aboard a 24-foot Robalo with twin 150HP Yamaha engines.

Trips on the water, not a booking maze

No Limit runs half-day, full-day, and overnight trips for inshore, offshore, and deep-sea fishing. Rods, tackle, bait, and licenses are included, and the catch is cleaned and filleted. Trips are listed from $350. Google 5.0 from 30 reviews.

Call to book: (850) 723-4200

The boat

The charter fishes from a 24-foot Robalo 242 with twin 150HP Yamaha engines. That is the boat shown on this page, underway with its captain at the helm.

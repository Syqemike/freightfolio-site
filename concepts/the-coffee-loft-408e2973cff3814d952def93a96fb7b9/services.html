What The Coffee Loft offers

The Coffee Loft is an eclectic coffeehouse in Fairhope. The menu includes banana bread cold brew, avocado toast, a white chocolate frappe, white mocha butter pecan, a salmon bagel, cinnamon rolls made in house, quiche, smoothies, and pastries. The shop offers outdoor seating, takeout, local art for sale, and cozy couches.

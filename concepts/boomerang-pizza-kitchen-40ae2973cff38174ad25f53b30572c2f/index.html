About

Boomerang Pizza Kitchen is a locally owned New York-style brick-oven pizza restaurant in Milton, in a historic downtown building since 2014. The dining room has a retro 1940s and 1950s feel with vintage memorabilia, and the restaurant is kid-friendly, offering dine-in, takeout, and delivery, plus a front gift shop.

Straightforward auto repair on Ingalls Ave.

Gulf Coast Automotive at 1115 Ingalls Ave in Pascagoula handles oil changes, tune-ups, brake jobs, and general maintenance. The shop describes itself as Pascagoula's newest auto repair shop.

Maintenance, plus local pick-up

Alongside everyday maintenance work, Gulf Coast Automotive offers local vehicle pick-up for customers who are unable to bring the vehicle in. Call the shop to ask about pick-up for your visit.

The shop is open Monday to Thursday from 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM, and Friday from 8:30 AM to 2:00 PM. Questions? Call (228) 218-4326.

A coastal bakery in Long Beach

Dolce Bakeshop is a coastal bakery at 200 Jeff Davis Ave Suite 2 in Long Beach. Products include king cakes in traditional and premium filled flavors including peach cobbler, cookie butter, Nutella, s'mores, and praline, plus custom cakes, gourmet cupcakes, hand-crafted Italian macarons, petit fours, cookies, cinnamon rolls, and sticky buns. Takeout and sunny outdoor seating are available. Call to order.

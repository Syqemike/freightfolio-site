Heritage

A century at the bench.

The story of Edward E. Petri Co. Jewelers, from a master platinum fabricator at Tiffany & Co. to the jeweler Indianapolis calls the Cartier of the Midwest.

1922

A Tiffany craftsman opens his own shop

Edward Petri had been a master platinum fabricator for Tiffany & Co. in New York. He brought that bench craft to Indianapolis and opened his own jeweler-manufacturer: Edward E. Petri Co. Jewelers, "Creations in Platinum."

The craft

The Cartier of the Midwest

The shop earned its nickname the honest way: fine platinum work, custom design, and repair done by hand at the bench. Generations of Indianapolis families have trusted the shop with pieces that matter.

Today

1890s-era tools, still in use

The third owner still works with 1890s-era tools, keeping century-old techniques alive at the bench. The shop is now led by proprietor Charles F. Walker at 20 N. Meridian St., just south of Monument Circle.

About the shop

Flowers and More is a family-owned florist and boutique in Evansville at 1000 W Franklin St. Designers make fresh and permanent flower arrangements, wedding bouquets, funeral remembrance designs, and seasonal and potted plants. The showroom carries boutique gifts, and the shop offers wedding rentals, local and nationwide delivery, and in-store and curbside pickup.

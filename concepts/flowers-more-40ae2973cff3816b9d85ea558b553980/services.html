Services at Flowers & More

A family-owned Evansville florist and boutique for more than 30 years, making fresh and permanent arrangements for weddings, remembrance, and everyday occasions.

What the shop makes and carries

Fresh and permanent arrangements

Fresh arrangements made to order for weddings, remembrance, and everyday occasions, and permanent arrangements for displays that stay up longer. Orders can be picked up in store or at the curb, or delivered locally.

Wedding bouquets and rentals

Bouquets made for the couple, with rental pieces available for weddings and receptions. Talk through the order by phone and arrange delivery or pickup.

Funeral remembrance designs

Remembrance designs for services and memorials, made for friends and loved ones. Local delivery and nationwide delivery are both available.

Seasonal and potted plants

Seasonal plants and potted greens kept in the showroom. Stop in during shop hours or use curbside pickup.

Boutique gifts

The boutique side of the shop carries jewelry, apparel, and home decor, open during the same shop hours as the flower counter.

Delivery and pickup

Local delivery across Evansville and nationwide delivery beyond it, with in-store and curbside pickup at 1000 W Franklin St.

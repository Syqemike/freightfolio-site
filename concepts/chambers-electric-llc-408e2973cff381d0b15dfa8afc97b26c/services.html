What Chambers Electric LLC offers

Chambers Electric LLC is a licensed and insured electrical contractor in Amarillo, founded in 1998 by Robert and Theresa Chambers. Services cover commercial, residential, and industrial electrical work, including new construction, repairs, maintenance, troubleshooting, remodels, and alterations. The service department provides rapid-response emergency service.

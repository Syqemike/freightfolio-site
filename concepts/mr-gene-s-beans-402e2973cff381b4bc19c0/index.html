The signature

The Fairhope Float

The signature drink. Their menu calls it the Official Dessert of Fairhope.

It starts with frozen yogurt, blended with coffee or an iced mochaccino, then crowned with whipped cream and a dusting of your pick: chocolate, vanilla, cinnamon, or nutmeg.

Prefer a classic? They also pour the Pirate Float and the old-fashioned Coke Float.

Order your own at the counter.

  • Frozen yogurt
  • Coffee or iced mochaccino
  • Whipped cream
  • Chocolate, vanilla, cinnamon, or nutmeg

DRINKS + RULES

Cheap drinks, cash only, and a short list of rules that have worked since 1948.

The Short Version

01

Cash Only

The Flight Deck does not take cards and does not run tabs. Bring cash and you are set.

02

Cheap Drinks

Generous pours at low prices. Ask the bartender what is cold.

03

No Food Served

There is no kitchen here. Outside food is allowed, so bring dinner or order in.

04

Good Company

The room has a Cheers-style feel: regulars, conversation, and a stool with your name on it.

Order simple

This is a beer-and-a-shot kind of bar. The menu is short, the pours are honest, and the prices have stayed low for a reason: regulars.

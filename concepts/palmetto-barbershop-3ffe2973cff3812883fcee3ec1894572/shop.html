The shop

On Market Street, between King and Meeting

Palmetto Barbershop is a classic downtown Charleston barbershop run by owner Terry Wrenn. The shop sits at 129-B Market Street, between King Street and Meeting Street, in the middle of the city’s busiest corridor.

The format is traditional: walk in during open hours, take the next available chair, and get a men’s haircut finished with a hot lather neck shave. Cash only keeps things simple, and the shop has built a loyal local following.

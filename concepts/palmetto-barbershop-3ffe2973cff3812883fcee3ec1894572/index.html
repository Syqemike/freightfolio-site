How it works

No appointments. No fuss.

Palmetto is a walk-in shop. There is no online booking and no appointment book to manage. You come by during open hours, and the barbers fit you in. It keeps the day simple and the chairs turning.

Bring cash: the shop is cash only, and keeps prices friendly. That is part of why locals keep coming back.

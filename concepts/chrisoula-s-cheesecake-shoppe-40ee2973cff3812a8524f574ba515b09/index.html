About

Chrisoula's Cheesecake Shoppe is a cheesecake bakery on West Garden Street in Pensacola. The shop makes handcrafted cheesecakes in dozens of flavors, in mini, personal, and whole sizes, plus wedding orders and a gluten-free option. The shop has been locally owned and operated since 2007 by a husband and wife team.

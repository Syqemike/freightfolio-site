Music and lineup

Tonight's bill is on the wall.

Original Fat Cats runs as a live-music venue. Bands play here, the room keeps a hip-hop soundtrack, and the night runs till 4 in the morning. For this week's bands and set times, check the bar's Facebook page. That is where the lineup lives.

On stage

Live bands.
Real volume.

This is a room with a stage, not a corner with a speaker. The crowd stands close, the sound hits hard, and the night keeps going long after most bars close.

In the room

Skate decks.
Hip-hop. Dive bar.

Old-school dive-bar bones with skate-deck decor on the walls and a hip-hop soundtrack in the air. Unpretentious by design. The 4am closing time is the whole business model.

At the bar

Beer bar by trade.

Alongside the music, it is a proper beer bar and grill. Pub food comes off the kitchen, the taps stay busy, and nobody is checking a guest list at the door.

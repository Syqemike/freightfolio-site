DESIGN BRIEF - Duh for Garden and Home (website concept)
Business: home and garden emporium, downtown Pensacola, FL.
Research basis: CRM Research Complete record (research shift 2026-10-26)
plus the store's own website, duhpensacola.com, fetched 2026-10-27.

AESTHETIC
Editorial and warm, like a magazine spread about a store you wander
through. The design leans on the store's own look: white painted brick,
warm wood, terracotta pots, and green planting. No generic hero template,
no gradient panels, no decorative illustration of any kind.

TYPOGRAPHY
Georgia serif for headings (classic, bookish, fits a store that sells
books and antiques) against a clean system sans for body copy. Large,
quiet headlines; generous line height; small uppercase section labels
in terracotta.

PALETTE
Warm plaster background (#faf6ee), sand (#efe7d8), deep garden green
(#2e4a38) for the cafe band and headings, terracotta (#b85c38) for
accents and calls to action, ink (#23281f) for body text.

IMAGERY
Four real photographs of this business only: the showroom, Jo's cafe,
the garden courtyard with fountain, and a planted pot outside the white
brick building. Photos are the visual argument; no stock, no artwork.

LAYOUT
Single page with anchor navigation (Inside the Store, The Garden,
Jo's Cafe, Visit). Hero pairs the showroom photo with a short
introduction. The shop section is a six-item category grid drawn only
from verified services. The garden section uses an offset grid: copy
left, tall courtyard photo right, wide planter photo below. The cafe
section inverts to dark green. The visit section carries address,
verified hours, phone, current-website link, and the single rating
line. Design-preview disclosure sits at the very top and in the footer.

CUSTOMER JOURNEY
Land on the disclosure and name, see the showroom, browse what the
store carries, step outside into the garden, learn about Jo's cafe,
then find address, hours, and the call button. Every call to action is
a tel: link to the verified store phone (850) 439-0640. No booking
button: no online booking destination was verified for this business.
No prices: the store's published prices are from 2023 and stale.

WHAT WAS DELIBERATELY LEFT OUT
Online ordering (the store's site has no catalog or shop), a booking
button (no verified booking destination), an Instagram link (handle
confirmed but profile link not verified as a destination), prices,
square footage, and any claim about service speed or quality.

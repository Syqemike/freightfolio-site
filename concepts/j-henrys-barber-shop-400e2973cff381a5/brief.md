## Story (verified, told factually)
- J. (John) Henry, Orlando native, Jones High School graduate, licensed as a barber in 1984, started working in this shop in 1991 and bought it about three years later (1994).
- Served on the Orange County Economic Recovery Task Force; appointed to the state Board of Barbers by Gov. DeSantis (Dec 2020).
- Shop ethos: "Thirty years of good cuts and good advice." Atmosphere: no profanity, no swearing, no drug activities, no alcohol, no smoking.
- Electrical (faulty-wiring) fire destroyed the shop on Nov 13, 2021 during EDC weekend. Rebuilt with City of Orlando / CRA Facade Program support plus community crowdfunding. Ribbon-cutting March 2024; Mayor Buddy Dyer received the first haircut in the restored shop.
- Winner of the 2025 Downtown Orlando Partnership Golden Brick Award, Redesign & Renovation category.

## Design direction: heritage editorial newspaper
- Concept: a vintage Orlando newspaper front page, not a barber template. Cream paper (#f4ecdc), ink brown-black, deep green accent (#234034), oxblood (#7c2d26) for the disclosure/rules band.
- Display serif: Playfair Display via Google Fonts (fallback: Liberation Serif, Georgia, serif). Body prose in serif, utility labels in letterspaced sans small caps.
- Structure: disclosure bar, vintage masthead (dateline + giant nameplate + hairline rules), sticky section nav (Story, Services, Visit, Contact), hero photo with caption bar, story as editorial columns with timeline rule entries and pull-quote ethos line, services as a vintage price card with dotted leaders, photo gallery with archival captions, visit as hours/address card + directions link, contact as a plain call-to-book panel, footer with disclosure repeat, provenance, and rating once.
- Distinct from prior Freightfolio barber concepts (Razor Smooth, Shave Barbershop, Wally's, studio-601, batch-19 barbershops). No dark-and-gold template look, no cookie-cutter hero/3-column-services pattern.

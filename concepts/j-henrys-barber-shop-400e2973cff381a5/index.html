A Parramore landmark, cut by cut, since 1991.

J. Henry started working in this barbershop in 1991 and bought it about three years later. What he built on West Church Street became a fixture of historic Parramore: a shop where the cuts are sharp, the conversation is steady, and the welcome is the same whether it is your first visit or your five hundredth.

Fire took the building in 2021. The community helped bring it back. In March 2024 the doors opened again, and in 2025 the restored shop earned a Downtown Orlando Partnership award for its redesign. The chairs are new. The standard is not.

Our Story

Thirty Years on Church Street

1984

J. Henry earns his barber license. An Orlando native and Jones High School graduate, he trains for a trade he would practice for the rest of his life.

1991

He starts barbering in this shop. About three years later he buys the business and makes it his own.

2020

A voice for the trade. He serves on the Orange County Economic Recovery Task Force and is appointed to the state Board of Barbers by Gov. DeSantis in December 2020.

2021

Fire destroys the shop. Faulty wiring sparks an electrical fire on November 13, 2021, during EDC weekend. The building is lost.

2024

The comeback. Rebuilt with City of Orlando and Community Redevelopment Agency Facade Program support plus community crowdfunding, the shop holds its ribbon-cutting in March 2024. Mayor Buddy Dyer receives the first haircut in the restored shop.

2025

Recognized. J Henry's wins the 2025 Downtown Orlando Partnership Golden Brick Award in the Redesign and Renovation category.

A Long Beach cafe with a TV feature

Darwell’s Cafe is a Southern comfort cafe with Gulf Coast flair in Long Beach, featured on Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives. Its offerings include Cajun and Creole dishes, seafood gumbo, crawfish etouffee, shrimp creole, red beans and rice, a prime rib sandwich, a shrimp sandwich, and the Crawdaddy burger. The cafe is BYOB-friendly, with live music, an outdoor patio, and a party room available. The own site homepage shows only marketing blurbs and a gallery teaser with no menu text, prices, or ordering, so call with questions.

## Aesthetic
"Marquee dressing room": a loud boutique that performs. Near-black plum base
(#170a13), hot magenta (#e83a8c), champagne gold (#d9a94b), ivory (#f6efe3).
Flat colors only. No gradients, no glow blobs, no black-and-gold cliché.

## Typography (intentional pairing)
- Pinyon Script: the Gaudy Galz wordmark and select display accents (echoes the
  business's own script logo feel).
- Archivo Black: section titles, oversized display words, numbers.
- Archivo: body text, lists, captions.
Google Fonts CDN. Never use all three at once in the same line.

## Layout (deliberate, not template)
- Slim "DESIGN PREVIEW" disclosure bar pinned at the very top of every page.
- Sticky header: script wordmark left, plain text nav, rectangular "Call" button.
- Home hero: asymmetric split. Left column stacks an oversized outlined "GAUDY"
  word, an overlapping script "Galz", and a magenta kicker line. Right column
  holds a tall arch-framed photo. A text marquee ticker ribbon names the three
  product categories.
- "Three doors" navigation: three tall rectangular panels (Jewelry / Fashion
  accessories / Boutique clothing), each with a photo, a number, and a link.
  Not cards in a row; staggered heights.
- Hours as a dotted-leader schedule list ("stage schedule"), with open days.
- Collections page: alternating full-bleed rows, photo left/text right, then
  reversed. Big numbers 01/02/03.
- About page: the business's own "creating yourself" statement set large,
  followed by a two-column fact block (what they sell, where they are).
- Gallery: mixed-size framed photo wall, six photos, one-line captions.
- Visit page: schedule list, address, phone, big Call button, map link.
- Footer on every page: business name, address, phone, hours, and the rating
  shown exactly once as "4.9 from 102 reviews".
- Rectangular hard-edge buttons everywhere. Arch top on gallery/hero frames
  (border-radius only, not illustration). Mobile stacks to single column.

## Customer journey
Land and feel the boutique in 3 seconds (wordmark, hero photo, ticker).
Skim the three doors. See real photos and the rating. Visit page answers
when/where/how: Thu-Sat hours, address, tap-to-call. Every page ends with the
Call CTA.

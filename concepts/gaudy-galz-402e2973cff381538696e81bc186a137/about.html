The idea behind the shop

About Gaudy Galz

The idea for Gaudy Galz is you. There is nothing quite as fabulous as being a female. Fashion is not about finding yourself. Fashion is about creating yourself.

That is how the shop describes itself, and the racks back it up: bold jewelry, fashion accessories, and boutique women's clothing that let a shopper build her own look.

WHAT THEY SELL

Jewelry, fashion accessories, and boutique women's clothing. Statement pieces that are meant to be seen, plus the accessories and clothing to wear with them.

WHERE THEY ARE

1510 Dawes Rd, Mobile, Alabama. A jewelry store listed in the Mobile area, open Thursday through Saturday. Call (251) 379-1762 with questions.

HOW TO SHOP

In person. There is no online storefront. Walk the displays and try pieces on to see what fits your style.

FOLLOW ALONG

The shop posts on its Facebook page and has an Instagram location page where shoppers tag their visits and their finds.

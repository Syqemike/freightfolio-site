A family kitchen

El Punto Familiar Restaurant is a family-run Dominican and Latin Caribbean restaurant at 1903 Denny Ave in Pascagoula, Mississippi, open since 2009. The kitchen serves mofongo, including stuffed with conch, chivo guisado on Saturdays, Cuban sandwiches, ropa vieja, churrasco, fried whole red snapper, and tres leches and flan for dessert, with daily specials. Delivery is available through DoorDash. Hours are Monday through Thursday, 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM; Friday, 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM; Saturday, 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM; Sunday closed. Call (228) 205-4360 to order.

About

Jim Ludwig's Blumengarten Florist is a family-owned florist in Pittsburgh's Strip District, in business since 1929, near downtown and the convention center. The shop offers flowers and floral arrangements, plants, balloons, gourmet fruit and gift baskets, wedding bouquets, bridesmaids bouquets, boutonnieres, and centerpieces, with conventions and trade shows as a specialty.

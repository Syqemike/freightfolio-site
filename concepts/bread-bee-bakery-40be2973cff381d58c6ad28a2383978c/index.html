About

Bread and Bee Bakery is a family-run cottage bakery in Savannah, Georgia, baking with organic real-food ingredients. The menu includes sourdough bread in classic, cinnamon raisin, and jalapeno cheddar, plus sourdough bagels, donuts, pretzels, pepperoni rolls, cakes, cookies, and pastries. All items are pre-order only, with porch pickup and limited 5 dollar delivery. The bakery also appears at markets and pop-ups including the Savannah Sunset Market.

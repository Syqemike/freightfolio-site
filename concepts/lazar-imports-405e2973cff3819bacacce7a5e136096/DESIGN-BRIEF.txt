DESIGN BRIEF: Lazar Imports (Tulsa, OK) - Website Concept
Batch 45, 2026-10-27

AESTHETIC: Workshop editorial. Cream paper background, deep green-charcoal ink,
burnt-orange accent, heavy rules and a serif headline voice. It reads like a
printed shop broadsheet, not a generic template card grid.

TYPOGRAPHY: Georgia serif for the business name and section headlines; plain
system sans for body copy and navigation. Large, tight headlines; short
paragraphs; no decorative type effects.

PALETTE: Paper #f3efe4, ink #1e2b23, accent burnt orange #c24e1c, sage
#dfe5d8. No black-and-gold, no purple gradient, no glowing blobs.

IMAGERY: Three labeled stock photos only (engine bay, oil service, wrench on
engine). No real photos of this business were obtainable from verified public
pages (no verified Facebook/Instagram profile found in research), so every
photo is captioned as a stock photo and is not presented as the shop, its
staff, or its work. No illustrations, no SVG art, no drawn graphics anywhere.

LAYOUT: Disclosure strip on top, name and nav header, asymmetric hero
(copy left, engine photo right), numbered section rules, a bordered makes
grid, and a dark visit panel with hours and contact. Single page, anchor nav.

CUSTOMER JOURNEY: Visitor sees the import focus and since-1985 line first,
checks whether their make is serviced, then finds hours, address, and the
call action. No Book Now button: no online booking destination is verified
for this shop, so the only CTA is Call (918) 587-5009.

FACTS USED (all verified): 1330 E 11th St, Tulsa, OK; phone (918) 587-5009;
Mon-Fri 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM, Sat-Sun not listed; servicing Asian imports
since 1985; makes list from the shop's own website; rating 4.7 stars from
12 reviews (public listing), shown once. No prices, warranties,
certifications, service durations, or service-area claims are made because
none are verified.

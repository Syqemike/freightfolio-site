01 · The School

A small school with a full curriculum.

The school focuses on Chun Kuk Do, a karate style with a long training tradition. Every student trains across five areas of the curriculum: self defense, competition, weapons, grappling, and fitness.

Classes meet three days a week. Instruction is led by Mr. Matthew Carlozzi, a 4th degree black belt. Training happens inside PowerFlex Gym, so the school has a real training floor and a steady schedule.

03 · Affiliation

Part of the federation.

Flex Studio Martial Arts is affiliated with the United Fighting Arts Federation (UFAF), the membership organization for the Chun Kuk Do style.

The school trains inside PowerFlex Gym at 1806 E Main St, Waterbury, CT. Call ahead if you want to watch a class or ask about joining.

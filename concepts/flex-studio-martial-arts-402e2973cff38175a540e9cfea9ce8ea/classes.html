01 · Classes

Chun Kuk Do karate, one room, five disciplines.

Flex Studio Martial Arts teaches Chun Kuk Do karate. The style gives every student a wide base: defense skills, competition preparation, weapons, grappling, and fitness all sit in the same program.

Classes meet three days a week inside PowerFlex Gym, led by Mr. Matthew Carlozzi. The school keeps its focus on the training itself. If you want to know whether the classes fit you or your child, the direct answer is a phone call away.

02 · The Five Areas

One curriculum, covered completely.

  1. 01

    Self Defense

    Defense techniques are taught as a core part of the program, not as a separate add-on.

  2. 02

    Competition

    Students who want to compete can train for tournaments through the school.

  3. 03

    Weapons

    Weapons training, including the long staff, is part of the Chun Kuk Do curriculum.

  4. 04

    Grappling

    Grappling skills are taught alongside the striking curriculum.

  5. 05

    Fitness

    Conditioning is built into training, so class time doubles as exercise.

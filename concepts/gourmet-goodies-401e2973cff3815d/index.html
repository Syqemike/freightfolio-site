About

Hospitality is the whole business

Gourmet Goodies is run by Laura Stafford, whose roots are in hospitality and ministry. The shop bakes for birthdays, Mardi Gras, and ordinary Tuesdays, and caters events across the Daphne area.

One kitchen, many tables: a bakery case up front, custom cakes for celebrations, and takeout meals and charcuterie for nights you do not want to cook.

Owner Laura Stafford, hospitality-ministry roots. Google 3.8 from 52 reviews (shown once, exactly). Hours: Mon-Fri 10:00 AM - 5:30 PM, Sat 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM, Sun closed.

## Aesthetic
Warm bakery indulgence. Cream paper background, golden-brown headers and accents, berry-red callouts. Subtle Mardi Gras thread (purple, gold, green) used only in the king cake feature band, thin border stripes and the flavor chips, never as the base palette. Feels like stepping into a sweet-smelling bakery, not a cafe template.

## Typography
Display: "Fraunces" (soft serif with character) for headlines, price-free menu headings, and the king cake feature. Body: "Karla" for readable menu lists and hours. Small-caps eyebrow labels for section kickers.

## Palette
- Cream paper: #fbf6ec (page background)
- Deep cocoa: #4a2c17 (headlines, footer)
- Golden crust: #c98a2d (accents, buttons, rules)
- Berry: #a63a4a (eyebrow labels, king cake callouts)
- Mardi Gras accents: purple #6b3fa0, gold #d4a017, green #2e7d46 (king cake band only)
- Sage note: no gradients, no glowing blobs, no pill-heavy button soup

## Layout
1. Top bar: business name left, "Design preview" disclosure chip, phone CTA.
2. Hero: editorial two-column. Left: eyebrow "Bakery and Caterer in Daphne, AL", big Fraunces headline about king cakes, short line naming the award and the Crawfish King Cake, Call button. Right: king cake slice photo with honest stock caption. Below hero: thin Mardi Gras tri-color rule.
3. King cake feature band (deep cocoa background): 5 flavors as chips (Crawfish + 4 named as "Classic", "Cream Cheese", "Cinnamon", "Chocolate"? NO — only verified flavors: only "Crawfish" is named in research. Flavor chips: "Crawfish King Cake" plus "four more house flavors — call for this season's list"). Award callout: "Top New Contender" and "#1 in Alabama" as stated by the business.
4. Menu: grouped list, no prices. Bakery (cookies, chocolate cake, cake pops, custom cakes, pastries), King Cakes, Catering and To-Go (prepared/takeout meals, charcuterie boards), Gifts (flowers, home decor). Menu cards carry small photos.
5. About: Laura Stafford, hospitality-ministry roots, bakery and caterer. (No invented history.)
6. Visit: address, real hours table, Google Maps link, phone CTA. Rating shown once here: "3.8 from 52 Google reviews".
7. Footer: design-preview disclaimer, "Concept by Freightfolio", link https://freightfolio.net.

## Customer journey
Visitor learns the king cake story first (the differentiator), scans the full menu, checks hours and address, calls to ask about custom orders. Primary CTA: Call (251) 888-1152 (tel: link). Claim-free ordering copy: "Questions about custom orders? Call (251) 888-1152."

What the shop carries

Angel's Garden in Pensacola is a gift shop and boutique, in business since 1991 and run by owner Debbie Tracy. The shop carries work from over 100 local artisans: artwork, pottery and sculptures, jewelry, books, stationery, children's gifts, cards and postcards, souvenirs, crosses, decor, garden art, candles and soaps, seasonal ornaments, and keepsakes.

# Design Brief - Leather & Pearls

Boutique concept site for Leather & Pearls, 630-B Washington Avenue, Ocean Springs, MS 39564. Phone (228) 215-3596. Owner Alma.

## Business profile (research only)
- Women's clothing boutique plus fashion accessories and jewelry.
- Brand line: "simple, casual, beauty for girls of all ages."
- Hours: Mon 11:00 AM - 5:00 PM; Tue-Sat 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM; Sun closed.
- Google rating 4.9 from 47 reviews. Price level moderate.
- Web presence is a Facebook page; no standalone website.

## Aesthetic
Minimal elegant. A quiet gallery, not a loud storefront. Calm, refined, unhurried. Positioning: simple and casual clothing with a touch of polish (pearls as the signature accent).

## Palette
- Ivory #FAF7F1 (page ground)
- Warm white #FFFFFF (cards, breathing space)
- Charcoal #2A2A28 (text, header/footer)
- Soft gold #C6A15B (accents, rules, hairlines, CTA borders)
- Stone #EDE8DC (section washes)

## Typography
- Display: Cormorant Garamond (light/regular, large sizes, generous tracking on eyebrows). Romantic but restrained.
- Body/UI: Jost or system sans at comfortable sizes. Eyebrow labels in letterspaced uppercase small caps.
- Strong hierarchy: one large serif statement per section, short plain sentences under it.

## Imagery
Bright, airy boutique and pearl photography. Light-toned images that sit naturally on ivory. Photo captions label every image as a stock photo.

## Layout (single page, mobile-first)
1. Slim disclosure bar: "Design preview" notice.
2. Header: LEATHER & PEARLS wordmark prominent; simple nav (Collection, Jewelry, Visit, Call); sticky.
3. Hero: full-bleed bright boutique rack photo; headline "Simple, casual, beautiful." subline naming the boutique and Ocean Springs; Call CTA (tel:+12282153596).
4. Intro statement: short paragraph on the brand line (simple, casual, beauty for girls of all ages). Owner Alma named in visit section only.
5. "What you will find": three quiet columns - Women's clothing / Fashion accessories / Jewelry. No invented product specifics.
6. Gallery: editorial grid of 4-6 photos, each with visible "Stock photo:" caption.
7. Rating line, shown ONCE: "4.9 from 47 Google reviews."
8. Visit: address 630-B Washington Avenue, Ocean Springs, MS 39564; phone; hours (Mon 11-5, Tue-Sat 10-6, Sun closed); Google Maps link; Call CTA.
9. Footer: design-preview disclaimer, "Concept by Freightfolio" + https://freightfolio.net link.

## Customer journey
Land on calm hero → read the simple positioning → browse collection categories → see real-feel gallery → check hours/location → call the boutique.

## Copy rules
No em dashes. No "No X, no Y" triples. No banned AI phrases. Plain, direct, business-specific language. Never invent facts, reviews, hours, or history. Never imply online booking exists.

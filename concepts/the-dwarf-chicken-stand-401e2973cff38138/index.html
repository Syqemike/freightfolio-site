The story

A Pensacola landmark since 1963

1963

The Dwarf Chicken Stand was founded by Vera Rancifer in 1963 in the Belmont-DeVilliers neighborhood of Pensacola. For decades, this little stand has served soul-food fried chicken from a tiny 10-to-12-seat counter.

Regulars know the look: the lavender-purple sign and the Mardi Gras decor that have become part of the stand's identity. It is the kind of place people drive across town for.

Chicken is the star, but the sides and desserts get their own loyal following. Everything below is priced from the current menu board.

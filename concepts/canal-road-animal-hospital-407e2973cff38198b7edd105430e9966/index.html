A privately owned veterinary hospital in Orange Beach for dogs, cats, and other small animals.

Canal Road Animal Hospital is a privately owned veterinary hospital in Orange Beach for dogs, cats, and other small animals, with vets Julianna Taylor, D.V.M. and Teresa Russell, D.V.M. Services include heartworm testing, medication, and treatment, vaccinations, preventative care, spay and neuter surgeries, laser surgical procedures, pet food and supplies, emergency care, diagnostic testing, and digital radiography surgery.

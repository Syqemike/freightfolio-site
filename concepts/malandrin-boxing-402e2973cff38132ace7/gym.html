The Gym

A boxing gym in South OKC. Training sessions are led by the coaching team and cover the fundamentals: stance, footwork, bag work, and conditioning. Sparring clips from the gym are posted on TikTok at @malandrinboxing2012.

What a session looks like

Evening sessions run 6:00 to 8:00 PM Monday through Thursday. Saturday sessions run 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM. The gym is closed Friday and Sunday. First visit? Call (405) 979-0001 and ask the coaching team what to bring.

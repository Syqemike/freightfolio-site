# Design Brief: Pampered Pets by Patti (Camp Hill, PA)

## Business read (verified facts only)
- Solo operator pet grooming salon, 2005 Dartmouth St, Camp Hill, PA, (717) 649-7907.
- 5.0 from 14 Google reviews. No real website, no hours published anywhere (do not show an hours table; "Call for current hours").
- Brand tone from research: warm, calm, professional, clean. No menu or prices published.
- No real business photos exist; all imagery is labeled stock photography.

## Design personality
Warm, calm, personal. A solo groomer whose differentiator is patience and familiarity, so the design reads like a quiet, trustworthy letter, not a sales page.

## Art direction
- Palette: warm cream (#faf3e9), deep forest green (#2f4a3e), terracotta (#c96f4a). No gradients.
- Typography: Fraunces (soft, warm serif) for headlines; Karla (friendly humanist sans) for body.
- Hero: full-bleed photo with a bottom-weighted forest scrim and centered serif headline.
- Section order: Hero / About Patti / Services (forest band, 3 cards) / Gallery / Visit (address + phone + "Call for current hours").
- Texture: hairline borders, soft shadows, rounded corners. No illustrations, no SVG art.

## Differentiation
Does not resemble the reference barber concepts (no ticket stubs, no dashed price tables, no mono type). Compared to the other four pet sites in this batch: the only full-bleed centered hero, the only forest/terracotta palette, the only Fraunces pairing.

## Copy rules honored
No em/en dashes. No banned AI phrases. Rating shown exactly once (About section). Services describe only verified facts: full pet grooming by one groomer in a calm, professional, clean space. No hours invented. CTAs: "Call to book" to tel:+17176497907 (no verified online booking).

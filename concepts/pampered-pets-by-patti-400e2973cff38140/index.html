About Patti

One groomer who knows your dog by name.

Patti runs Pampered Pets herself. Your dog is groomed by the same calm hands each time, in a professional, clean space designed to keep nervous pets relaxed.

Owners across Camp Hill trust Patti with their dogs, and the business holds a perfect 5.0 from 14 Google reviews.

About

Buckeye Donuts is a family-owned donut shop at 1998 N High St in Columbus, across from the Ohio State campus. The Barouxis family has run it since 1969, now in its third generation, and the shop's own line is Open Always, Closed Never. The shop takes takeout and delivery orders.

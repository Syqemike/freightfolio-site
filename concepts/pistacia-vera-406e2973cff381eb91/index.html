A French pastry bakery and cafe

Pistacia Vera is a French pastry bakery and cafe in German Village, Columbus. Its offerings include macarons in 12 varieties per Wanderlog, croissants, eclairs, tarts, cookies, teacakes, and coffee including a pistachio latte. The own site carries hours, address, and Square ordering links only, with no menu, prices, or product info, so call with questions.

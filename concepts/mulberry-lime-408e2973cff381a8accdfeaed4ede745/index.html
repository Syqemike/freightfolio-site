The house

A shop in a family home

Mulberry & Lime opened in 2001 in the Matthew Kennedy House, the childhood home of owner Mary Ginocchio.

The house was built in the early 1800s by Matthew Kennedy and has been in the Ginocchio family for five generations.

Mary Ginocchio worked in retail for 15 years, much of it as a manager at Laura Ashley, before opening her own shop. She wanted to offer Lexington simple, timeless treasures to enhance daily life.

Inside

Rooms full of home and gifts

Inside, the rooms hold bedding, rugs, and furniture, and gifts from soaps to handbags to jewelry. The shop gives customers a one-on-one experience to help them create an ambience for their house.

## Creative direction executed
- Editorial magazine feel: asymmetric hero with oversized Zilla Slab headline, story section with drop cap, dark charcoal + burnt orange (#c9662a) + cream (#f6f0e3) palette, Zilla Slab display + Source Sans 3 body via Google Fonts.
- Menu: editorial two-column price-list layout with dotted leaders between item name and description, grouped into 5 groups (Loaded Potatoes, Sandwiches, From the Smoker, Loaded Fries & Nachos, Desserts).
- Sections: hero (tagline + rating once + Call to order), The Brothers story, Menu, Visit (address/phone/hours), Contact strip, dark smoky footer.
- All CTAs say "Call to order" with tel:+12514773289. No booking button, no forms.
- Responsive single-column layout under 860px. <meta name="robots" content="noindex"> in head. "Design preview" disclosure strip at the very top. Section nav in header. Footer disclaims this is a design concept by Freightfolio and links https://freightfolio.net.

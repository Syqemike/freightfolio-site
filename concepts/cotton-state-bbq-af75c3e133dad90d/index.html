Our story

Two brothers, one smoker, downtown Mobile.

Cotton State BBQ was founded by brothers Ryan and David Tillman, who set out to put their own spin on the Southern barbecue tradition in the heart of downtown Mobile.

The menu reads like a lunch plan built for regulars: brisket and pulled pork sandwiches, dry-rub chicken wings, red beans and rice, and loaded potatoes built on giant Idaho potatoes. Sweet finishes include the Moon Drop banana pudding and New Orleans sno balls.

It is a weekday lunch spot, closed on weekends, which makes every visit a downtown midday ritual. Questions about the day's menu or hours are welcome by phone.

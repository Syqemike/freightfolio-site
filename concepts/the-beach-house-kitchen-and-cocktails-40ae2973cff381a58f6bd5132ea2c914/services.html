Menu at The Beach House Kitchen and Cocktails

A Gulf seafood restaurant and cocktail bar on West Beach Boulevard, serving Gulf shrimp tacos, crawfish mac and cheese, and the Bushwacker.

Menu

From the Gulf

Gulf shrimp and fish tacos, coconut shrimp, and crawfish mac and cheese. Dishes can be eaten on the porch or packed for takeout.

Burgers

Burgers served with the same porch seating and takeout options as the seafood menu, and a gluten-free menu is available on request.

Signature cocktails and happy hour

Signature cocktails including the Bushwacker. Happy hour runs daily from 2:00 PM to 6:00 PM.

Good to know

Porch and outdoor seating is available. Takeout is offered. A gluten-free menu is available. No reservations are listed, so call ahead with questions about seating.

Burgers the old-fashioned way

Dubl-R Old Fashioned Hamburgers is a burger restaurant in Waco. Its menu includes the Dubl-R burger, cheeseburger, bacon cheeseburger, and jalapeno cheeseburger, plus curly fries, tots, onion rings, Texas Toothpicks, and chicken fried steak. Takeout and drive-through are offered, and no reservations are taken.

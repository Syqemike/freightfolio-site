About the Shop

A Neighborhood Shop, Run by Two People

Nina's Nails is a small walk-in shop run by Nina and her husband. It keeps things simple: come in, pick a service, and get seen the same day.

Reviewers single out the pedicures. Experiences with other services have been mixed, so we will keep it honest and say this: it is a modest shop with a personal touch, run by Nina and her husband.

About

Cafe Nola is a Cajun and Creole waterfront cafe at 400 Quietwater Beach Rd, Suite 14, on Pensacola Beach. The menu is Louisiana cookin': seafood gumbo, crawfish etouffee, shrimp po' boy, charbroiled oysters, red beans and rice, boudin balls, fried crab claws, alligator sausage po' boy, beignets, and bananas foster cheesecake. The deck overlooks Santa Rosa Sound, and the cafe offers takeout and delivery plus online ordering. The cafe was voted 2021 Best of the Bay for Best Gumbo, Best Poboy, Best Brunch, and Best Restaurant.

A boutique bakery and cafe since 2012

Antoinette Bakery & Cafe is a boutique bakery and cafe in Tulsa's Arts District since 2012. Products include wedding and party cakes, cookies, muffins, pies, tarts, and yeast breads. Signature items named in research include the kouign amann, the mushroom croissant, the Mile High Chocolate Pie, and macarons. Coffee, lattes, and the Ube Latte are served, along with vegan scones, breakfast burritos, and quiche. Call to ask about an order.

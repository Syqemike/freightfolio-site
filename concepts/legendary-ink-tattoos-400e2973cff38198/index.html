01 · The Shop

Built by artists

Legendary Ink has been tattooing Jackson since 2007. The shop is artist owned and run, which means the people behind the counter are the people behind the needle.

Pricing is moderate, walk-ins are welcome, and every piece starts with a conversation about what you want on your skin.

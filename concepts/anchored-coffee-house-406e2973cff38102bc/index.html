A coastal coffee house

Anchored Coffee House is a coastal coffee house at 1229 Gulf Shores Pkwy Ste 101 in Gulf Shores. The menu includes locally roasted coffee, specialty lattes such as the Banana Bread Latte and Cold Brew Shake, beignets, an apple fritter, a cinnamon roll, and breakfast and lunch items. Gluten-free options are available, and there is a complimentary small conference room.

# Design Brief: Thomas Hardy Hair Salon — Website Concept

## Aesthetic
"Historic 25th Street heritage": quiet art-deco restraint. Ivory paper background, near-black ink text, brass accents used sparingly for rules, eyebrows, and small caps. High contrast, generous whitespace, symmetrical classic layout that feels like a well-set invitation rather than a marketing funnel.

## Typography
- Display/headings: Playfair Display (Google Fonts), elegant high-contrast serif.
- Body: a clean sans (Inter via Google Fonts) for readability and contrast against the serif.
- Eyebrow labels: small caps, letterspaced, brass.

## Palette
- Ivory: #f7f3ea
- Near-black: #14120e
- Brass: #a8832f (accents only)
- Warm gray for secondary text: #5c564a

## Imagery
All stock photos (real salon Facebook photos not obtainable). Each captioned "Stock photo." Minimum 3 per page; planned 6:
- Hero-adjacent salon interior
- Haircut in progress
- Color application / styling
- Brow service detail
- Wash station
- Team-atmosphere salon shot

## Layout (original structure, section order)
1. Slim disclosure bar (design preview notice)
2. Business name masthead centered at top (large serif, no logo invention)
3. Sticky nav: Services, Stylists, Hours & Location, Contact
4. Hero: centered masthead treatment — business name, tagline-free direct line, street address, Call CTA
5. Services (two-column card list, brass rule separators)
6. Historic 25th Street note (short, grounded only in the verified fact of the location; no invented dates)
7. Stylists — centerpiece section: roster of 9 with owner/color-specialist titles only
8. Hours & Location (hours table + honest Tuesday-Saturday discrepancy note)
9. Contact (phone, address, call CTA)
10. Footer: disclaimer, photo provenance, rating source, concept date, address + phone

## Customer journey
Visitor sees the name and street immediately, scans services, meets the team by name, checks hours and the address, and calls. No online booking exists, so the single action is "Call to book" (tel:+18013939561).

## Copy notes
- Plain direct sentences. No em dashes. No banned phrases.
- Facts used: name, 248 25th St, Ogden, UT 84401; +1 801-393-9561; Mon-Fri 9-6, Sat-Sun closed (per Birdeye) + honest note that one local magazine lists Tuesday to Saturday; services haircuts, hair color (balayage, blonde, color correction, creative/vivid color), brow services; prices not published with call-for-pricing note; 9 stylists with Tom Hardy (owner) and Ashlee Knutson Graves (color specialist) titled, others untitled; 4.7 rating from 221 Birdeye reviews shown exactly once; only real web presence is Facebook.
- No invented history, no founding dates, no "since 19XX", no operating claims, no urgency language.

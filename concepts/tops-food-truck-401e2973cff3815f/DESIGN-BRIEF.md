## Creative direction
Family comfort diner on wheels. Palette: tomato red #b3402a, mustard #e0a92e, cream
#faf5e6. Typography: Archivo Black (display) + Inter (body). Layout rhythm: bold hero
with the truck's real food spread, a mustard Friday-special banner, a perforated
ticket-board menu (6 ticket cards), a dark wings-flavor row, a family-story section
anchored by the truck's real logo, and a find-the-truck section with Google hours.
Clearly different from the Zizi's build: different palette, different headline font
(Archivo Black vs Vollkorn), different layout rhythm (tickets vs editorial board).

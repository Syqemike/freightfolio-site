Our Story

Family-owned, Mobile proud

Tops is a family-owned comfort-food truck run right out of Mobile, Alabama. The truck has built its name on big portions, hot food, and fast service, with breakfast gumbo and loaded fries among the local favorites.

Regulars know to call their order in. The crew keeps the line moving and the food coming out hot.

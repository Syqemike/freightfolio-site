About

Angelo's Bakery is a third-generation family bakery on West Metairie Avenue in Metairie. The bakery makes king cakes including cannoli king cake, doberge, wedding and novelty cakes, eclairs, cream puffs, petit fours, rum cakes, napoleons, turtles, and Russian cakes, plus holiday fruitcakes, Christmas logs, and gingerbread houses. Vegan, gluten-free, and vegetarian options are available.

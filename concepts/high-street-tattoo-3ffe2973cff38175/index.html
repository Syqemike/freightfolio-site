About the Shop

307 High St

High Street Tattoo has been tattooing since 1987. The downtown Morgantown shop does custom work across styles, from portraits to traditional pieces, and also offers body piercings.

Sterilization techniques are kept up to date, and walk-ins are accepted alongside booked appointments. Call the shop to talk through your idea.

  • ●

    Custom tattoos - portraits, traditional, black and grey, and more

  • ●

    Body piercings - with experienced piercers on staff

  • ●

    Walk-ins welcome - stop by during shop hours

  • ●

    Up-to-date sterilization - stated on the shop's own page

Our story

Since 2005, our family has been grooming the dogs and cats of Hampton Roads.

Paws and Purrs Grooming is a small, family-owned salon in Virginia Beach. Every pet gets the same patient attention, from the nervous first-timer to the seasoned regular.

We believe a grooming visit should feel calm and familiar. That is how we run every appointment.

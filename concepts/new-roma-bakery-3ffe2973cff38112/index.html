Our story

Ninety years of bread

1934

The ovens fire up

New Roma Bakery opens in Sacramento's Boulevard Park neighborhood, baking bread in a real brick oven. The shop has been a midtown staple ever since.

Daily

Baked fresh every morning

French and Italian breads, bolillo, dinner rolls, glazed donuts, and pastries come out of the oven fresh each day the shop is open.

Today

Cakes for the big days

Alongside the daily bread, the bakery makes custom wedding and birthday cakes. Call ahead to talk through what you need.

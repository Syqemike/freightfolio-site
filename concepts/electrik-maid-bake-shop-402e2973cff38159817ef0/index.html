A century in Biloxi

Our story

One hundred years of fires, depressions and hurricanes, and the ovens never really stopped.

  • 1924

    Electrik Maid Bake Shop opens its doors on April 12 in downtown Biloxi, founded by Donald Entringer and A.W. Bissett with electric ovens that gave the shop its name.

  • 1950s

    Paul Balius, the head baker, buys the shop and keeps the family trade going from the Porter Avenue location.

  • 1981

    The bakery settles at the southwest corner of Veterans Avenue and Pass Road, 1833 Pass Rd, where it still stands today.

  • 2024

    The shop celebrates 100 years of baking, with coverage by WLOX and a city proclamation. Grandson Harrel Balius and family carry the tradition forward.

The family

Still family, still Biloxi

Electrik Maid has been family-owned since the beginning. Today the Balius family runs the ovens at 1833 Pass Rd, baking the same way the shop always has.

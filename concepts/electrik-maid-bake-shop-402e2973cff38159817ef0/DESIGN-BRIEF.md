# Design Brief — Electrik Maid Bake Shop (Biloxi, MS)

Slug: electrik-maid-bake-shop-402e2973cff38159817ef0
CRM page: 402e2973-cff3-8159-817e-f06970a13e5d
Business: family-owned bakery since 1924, 1833 Pass Rd, Biloxi MS. King cakes,
scratch-made glazed donuts, petit fours, eclairs, baklava, breads and rolls,
wedding and birthday cakes by pre-order. Slogan: "You Taste the Difference."
Phone (228) 388-1014. Google rating 4.8 / 31 reviews (research notes, 2026-10-22).

## Concept
"A century of sweet mornings." A heritage cream-and-brick design built around
the shop's real 1920s identity. The authentic Electrik Maid oval logo (real
asset, includes Since 1924, address, phone) anchors the hero; the 1924 opening
ad anchors the story section. Warm, nostalgic, confident, never generic.

## Palette
- Cream #f7f1e3 (page background), deeper cream #efe5cf (alt sections)
- Espresso #33241a (text, footer), warm brown #6b4a2f (subheads)
- Brick red #a8442f (accents, CTA hover), harvest gold #c99b3f (rules, badges)
- Muted green #4a6b4f reserved for the Mardi Gras king cake callout only

## Typography
- Display: Fraunces (Google Fonts), warm vintage serif, headlines only
- Body: system sans stack (clean, mobile-fast); letter-spaced small caps
  eyebrows for section labels

## Layout (single page, mobile-first)
1. Sticky top bar: name + "Website Concept" tag, nav (Story, Treats, Family,
   Visit), Call button
2. Hero: split. Left: concept disclosure eyebrow, H1 business name,
   "Baking in Biloxi since 1924", slogan line, rating line ONCE
   ("Rated 4.8 from 31 Google reviews"), Call + Treats CTAs.
   Right: authentic logo in a framed cream card.
3. Marquee-ish heritage strip: "1924 — 100 years of Biloxi mornings" with
   three quick facts (since 1924 / family-owned / scratch-made).
4. Treats grid (6 cards): King Cakes, Glazed Donuts, Petit Fours,
   Baklava (REAL photo, badge "Baked here at Electrik Maid"), Eclairs,
   Breads and Rolls. Plus a wide pre-order banner: wedding and birthday
   cakes (stock wedding cake photo, labeled).
5. Our Story: 1924 opening ad (REAL) beside a 4-step timeline:
   Apr 12 1924 opening by Donald Entringer and A.W. Bissett; Paul Balius
   head baker buys the shop early 1950s; 1981 move to 1833 Pass Rd;
   April 2024 centennial (WLOX, city proclamation).
6. Family: real photo of Harrel and Nora Balius in the shop.
7. Visit: address, phone Call CTA (tel:), hours Mon-Sat 5:30 AM-1 PM
   closed Sundays (per Facebook listing, verified in research notes),
   directions link.
8. Footer: concept disclosure, noindex note, Freightfolio credit.

## Customer journey
A neighbor or visitor lands, sees the century of history immediately, trusts
the real photos, browses treats, and calls to place a pre-order or asks when
to stop by in the morning. Every section answers "why this bakery" before
asking anything. No online booking exists, so CTAs are Call-only.

## Copy constraints observed
No em dashes. No invented facts, reviews, awards, hours, prices. Rating used
once with source and count. Plain direct sentences, no banned AI phrases.

About

A neighborhood nail salon on Elizabeth Avenue.

Princess Nails is a nail salon at 1151 Elizabeth Ave in Elizabeth, New Jersey. The work on the books is straightforward: acrylic nails, manicures, pedicures, fills, nail repairs, and custom nail decoration.

"Nuevas ideas, decoraciones personalizadas, cuidado garantizado y la mejor atencion. New ideas, custom nail decoration, guarantee the best attention." The salon's own description

The salon serves clients Monday through Saturday and takes appointments by phone. Everything on this site comes from the salon's public listings and real photos of the business.

Chapter II - What the salon does

Services

Six entries in the ledger. Call for details and booking.

01

Acrylic nails

The salon's signature work. Full acrylic sets are built for the length and shape you ask for, then finished with polish, French tips, or decoration. French finishes are a steady favorite here.

02

Manicures

Care and polish for your natural nails. Simple color, clean shaping, and the classic French manicure the salon is known for.

03

Pedicures

Pedicures are done in the salon's spa chairs. A full pedicure service with polish to finish.

04

Fills

Acrylic sets grow out. Fills keep them looking new without starting over, done on your existing set.

05

Nail repairs

Broken, lifted, or damaged nails get fixed. Bring the problem nail in and the salon will take care of it.

06

Custom nail decoration

Hand-applied designs and details: charms, butterflies, 3D flowers, rhinestones, and color work made to order. Bring an idea and the salon will work it into your set.

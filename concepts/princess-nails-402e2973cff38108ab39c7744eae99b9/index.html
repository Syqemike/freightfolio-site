Princess Nails

A nail salon on Elizabeth Avenue. Acrylics, manicures, pedicures, and hand-drawn detail.

Princess Nails sits at 1151 Elizabeth Ave in Elizabeth, New Jersey, and keeps its focus where it belongs: on the nails. The salon works in acrylics and natural nails alike, with fills, repairs, and custom decoration done by hand.

In the salon's own words: "New ideas, custom nail decoration, guarantee the best attention." That is the whole pitch, and it has carried the shop for years.

A shop by Whitney and Wolfgang Schaefer

Orange and Blue Co. is a lifestyle and boutique shop in Milwaukee, in Historic Brewer's Hill, run by owners Whitney and Wolfgang Schaefer. The building also houses the owners' Uncle Wolfie's Breakfast Tavern. The shop carries curated vintage and modern goods for home and body, from local designers, plus custom curated gifts.

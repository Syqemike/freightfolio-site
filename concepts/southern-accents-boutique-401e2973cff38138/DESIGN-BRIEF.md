## Aesthetic
Warm southern heritage. Think heirloom monogram stationery, a front-porch sitting
room, brass picture frames. Charming and inviting. Deliberately NOT a colorful
coastal maximalist gift-shop look: no coral, teal, or sunshine yellow; no playful
rounded cartoon styling. This is the distinct sister-shop to a beachy design,
carrying the same warmth through a quieter, more traditional register.

## Typography
- Script accent: "Great Vibes" (Google Fonts) for eyebrow lines, the brand name,
  and one or two headline words. Used sparingly so it reads as signature, not noise.
- Headings: "Cormorant Garamond" 600, a high-contrast serif with a heritage feel.
- Body: "Nunito Sans", clean and highly readable at small sizes.

## Palette
- Cream #FAF5EA (page background), warm cream deep #F1E8D4 (section bands)
- Blush #F0D9CE (soft panels, card accents)
- Sage #8A9B7C, deep sage #5F7052 (nav, footer, buttons)
- Brass #B08D57 (rules, monogram frames, dividers)
- Ink #3D3831 (body text, warm near-black)

## Layout (single page, mobile-first)
1. Disclosure strip: "Design preview" notice at the very top
2. Sticky header: brand name in script, simple section nav, Call button
3. Hero: boutique interior photo with overlapping cream panel, script headline,
   tagline, Call CTA, address line
4. Collections: grid of photo cards built from the services list, each with a
   monogram-style double border and stock caption
5. "More to love": monogram-framed text list of remaining services
   (monogrammed goods, frames, local pepper jelly, gifts and art)
6. About: owner Becky (Jenny) Shubert, Old Town Bay St. Louis, plain copy
7. Visit: address, phone, hours table, Google rating (one place only),
   Google Maps link, Facebook link
8. Footer: design-preview disclaimer, "Concept by Freightfolio",
   link https://freightfolio.net

Monogram frames are plain CSS (1px brass border plus offset outline). No SVG
illustrations, no CSS-drawn graphics, no clipart, no fake maps anywhere.

## Customer journey
Land on hero, see the name, the tagline, and a Call button immediately. Scroll
through collections to see what the shop carries. Read a short owner note.
Find hours, address, and phone in Visit, then call or get directions.

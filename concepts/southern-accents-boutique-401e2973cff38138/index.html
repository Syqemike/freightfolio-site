Our story

About the Shop

Southern Accents Boutique is owned by Becky (Jenny) Shubert and sits on Main Street in Old Town Bay St. Louis.

The shop carries women’s and plus-size clothing, southern fashion, and monogrammed goods, along with home decor, soy candles, frames, jewelry, baby clothes, hats, local pepper jelly, gifts, and art.

It’s a boutique and more, just like the sign says.

# Design Brief: Lenny's Tap (concept)

## Aesthetic
Vintage Wisconsin tavern letterpress. The site reads like the bar's own hand-painted
signage and chalkboards brought onto the web: brick storefront red, aged paper,
dark walnut wood, brass fittings, and chalkboard green. No neon glow, no gradients
of color, no hero stock imagery. Texture comes from real photos and typography.

## Typography
- Wordmark/display: Rye (tavern signboard feel, used for the name and big headlines)
- Section titles: Alfa Slab One (heavy slab, old billboard voice)
- Chalkboard notes: Caveat (handwritten specials, small doses)
- Body: Work Sans (plain and readable)

## Palette
- brick #7B2D1E (storefront sign band, header)
- cream #F5ECDA (page background, aged paper)
- walnut #2B1F16 (ink, footer, rules)
- brass #C9A24A (accents, buttons, double-rule trim)
- chalk #31473B (chalkboard panels)

## Layout
Not a hero-plus-cards template. The page opens with a full-width brick sign band
carrying the name like a hanging tavern sign, with a brass double-rule trim. Below,
sections alternate cream paper and chalkboard-green panels. Photos sit in thick
brass-framed "postcard" frames with captions set like photo credits. Hours are
presented on a green chalkboard panel in Caveat handwriting. Section dividers are
simple brass diamond rules, not gradients or blobs. Buttons are squared brass, not pills.

## Customer journey
1. Land: name, town, and one-line character statement; call CTA visible.
2. Skim the pour: Old Style draft, cheap beer, bloody marys.
3. Picture the night: jukebox, sports TVs, live music on game nights.
4. Check hours and the cash-only note; call or find the address.
5. Visit page: hours, address, phone, Facebook link. Call to book game-night plans.

## Notes
- Rating shown exactly once, on the home page, with source and breakdown.
- "Open every day of the year" used only in the quoted branding form, once.
- No invented prices, reviews, testimonials, or history beyond the research brief.

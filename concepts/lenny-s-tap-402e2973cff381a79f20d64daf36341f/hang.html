Game night

This is a Green Bay sports bar the way it used to be done: every TV on the game, a jukebox when it isn't, and live music when it matters.

Live music on game nights

On game nights the tavern clears a corner and brings in live music. A band in the middle of the room, the game on the TVs, and the bar three deep is the standard picture.

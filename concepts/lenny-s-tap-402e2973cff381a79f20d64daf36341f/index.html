An old-time tavern on North Broadway

Lenny's Tap is a historic neighborhood dive bar in Green Bay. Several generations of customers have pulled up a stool here for cheap beer, a jukebox, and a crowd that knows your name.

The Pour

Cheap beer, poured right

This is a cash-only tavern and proud of it. The chalkboard draft list says it plain: Old Style is on tap, and cold beer is poured today and tomorrow too.

Old Style draft leads the board

The tap list keeps to the classics. Old Style draft is the house pour, joined by a short row of familiar bottles and cans written up on the wall in chalk.

  • Old Style on draft
  • Bloody marys for the morning crowd
  • Cash only, prices kept low
◆

Game night is the main event

Sports TVs stay on around the room, and on game nights the tavern brings in live music. The jukebox fills in the rest of the time.

  • Sports TVs throughout the room
  • Live music on game nights
  • Jukebox for everything in between

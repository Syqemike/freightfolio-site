The drinks

Nothing fancy on the tap wall. The beer is cold, the prices are low, and the chalkboard tells you everything you need to know.

The house pour is Old Style on draft. It shares the chalkboard with a short list of familiar bottles and cans, written up in chalk and poured cold.

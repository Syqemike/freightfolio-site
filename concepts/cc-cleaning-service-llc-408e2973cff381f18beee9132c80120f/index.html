About

CC Cleaning Service, LLC is a Reno cleaning company founded in 2014. It handles janitorial work and commercial sites including offices, restaurants, gyms, spas, and clinics, plus new construction and post-renovation cleaning, move-in and move-out cleaning, and property and student housing turnover. The company is WMBE and DBE certified and a member of the Nevada Builders Alliance.

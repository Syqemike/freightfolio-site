The practice

Chiropractic Works is the practice of Dr. Francis J. Smith, DC, at 48 Colchester Avenue in Burlington. The office is a house on Colchester Avenue, near the University of Vermont campus.

Dr. Smith is the author of Back to a Pain-Free Life. His care covers back pain, neck pain, sciatica, shoulder pain, whiplash, and headaches and migraines, along with Class IV laser therapy, spinal decompression, massage therapy, and X-rays in the office.

East Shore Cafe sits at 1506 Main St in Daphne. Larry Mickelsen and John Sibley have owned it since 1998. The cafe serves breakfast and lunch daily except Monday, dinner Friday and Saturday evenings, and welcomes dogs on the porch. BYOB is offered.

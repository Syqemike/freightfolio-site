Dance classes for ages 3 to 18 in Des Moines

Dmi Dance Force is a dance studio of almost 20 years, with a Main Season through the school year ending with a recital showcase in May and June, and a Summer Season including State Fair Dance Camps. Classes include Jazz, Tap, Ballet, Hip Hop, Tumbling, Pom, preschool, Lyrical, and the boys-only hip hop and breakdancing program DMI Boyz, for ages 3 to 18, built on Spirit, Passion, Teamwork, and Dedication.

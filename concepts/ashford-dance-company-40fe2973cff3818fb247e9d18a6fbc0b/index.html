About

Ashford Dance Company is an inclusive dance studio in Rochester, New York, in the Village Gate at 376 North Goodman Street. The studio teaches classical ballet, tap, jazz, and creative movement for ages 1.5 and up, beginner to advanced, with a Dancers With Diverse Abilities program, Parent-and-Me toddler classes, and free trial classes. The director is a New York State certified teacher in Special Education and Dance. Recitals are short, 45 minutes. Hours are Monday 4:00 PM to 9:00 PM, Tuesday and Wednesday 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM, Thursday 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM, and Saturday 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Call (585) 417-6580 to ask about a class.

Dallas longest-running pole and aerial studio

Extend Fitness Studio was founded in 2008 as The Girls Room and rebranded in 2015. Owner Samantha Coley runs the studio at 136 Glass St in Dallas, after the move from the old Greenville Ave address.

Private lessons and private parties are available, including birthday, bachelorette, ladies night, and kids parties. Booking is through MindBody and Momence; call (214) 735-3233 or email extendfitnessstudio@gmail.com to ask about a class.

A woodland estate venue

Belforest Pointe is a woodland wedding and event venue in Daphne on 10 acres, an Eastern Shore event space. Its 6,000 square foot air-conditioned reception hall and 3,000 square foot covered patio host ceremonies, receptions, rehearsal dinners, bridal showers, engagement parties, and elopements. An event planner is available, with setup and cleanup staff, lighting and sound equipment, and event items from altar and arch to chairs, dance floor, decor, drapery, furniture, lighting, tables, tents, barware, china, flatware, glassware, and linens. Outside vendors are welcome.

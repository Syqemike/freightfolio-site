# DESIGN-BRIEF — Lavish Nail Gulf Shores
Concept slug: lavish-nail-gulf-shores-402e2973cff381c492a3eb50b2d9e544

## Aesthetic
ART-DECO OPULENT, refined and lavish. Deep burgundy (#5c1a24) fields against warm cream
(#fdf6ee) sections, rose (#e8b4b8) used as soft accent, hairline gold (#c9a25f) rules and
thin double frames only. No gradients, no glowing blobs, no illustrations. The luxury
comes from symmetry, generous whitespace, thin rules, and the real photography.

## Typography
Cinzel (display, uppercase, letter-spaced) for name, headlines, section titles.
Quicksand (body) for prose, hours, lists. Google Fonts CDN.

## Palette
- Burgundy #5c1a24 (hero, footer, bands)
- Cream #fdf6ee (page background, text on burgundy is cream)
- Rose #e8b4b8 (walk-in band background, accents)
- Gold hairline #c9a25f (rules, double frames, seal ring)
- Ink #2a1216 for body text on cream

## Imagery plan
8 real salon photos from the business's own Yelp page (fetched via Giftly's listing):
interior manicure station (hero), reception desk, customer pedicure, 5 nail-art
close-ups (several carrying the salon's own "Lavish" watermark + phone number).
Every photo captioned "Salon photo". No stock, no generated art.

## Layout
- Header: centered Cinzel business name on cream with gold double rules; "Design Preview"
  disclosure directly under it; simple section nav below.
- Hero: symmetrical burgundy panel, gold hairline frame inset, headline + short line,
  Call CTA (gold outline button), hero image framed below the copy.
- Sections alternate cream and rose-tint bands, each with a centered Cinzel heading and
  a short gold rule.
- Gallery page is the centerpiece: framed grid of the 6 nail-art photos with captions.
- Walk-in policy rendered as a prominent rose band on every page (short block before footer).
- Rating seal: a circular framed seal "5.0 from 23 Google reviews" appears ONCE (home page).
- Footer: burgundy, hours summary, address, call, Yelp link, disclosure line, freightfolio.net credit.

## Pages
index.html (hero, intro, walk-in band, rating seal, visit teaser),
services.html (gel, gel extensions, dip powder/dip sets, acrylics, manicures, pedicures, custom nail art),
gallery.html (framed salon-photo gallery),
visit.html (hours table exact, address, directions link, call CTA, Yelp link).

## Customer journey
Land on a name-forward, clearly-marked design preview -> read the walk-in policy first
(no booking confusion) -> browse services and real nail-art work -> find hours and
directions -> call the salon. No booking button anywhere.

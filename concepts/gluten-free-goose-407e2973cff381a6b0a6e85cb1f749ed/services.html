Menu

Gluten Free Goose is a bakery in Nashville that is 100 percent gluten-free, peanut- and tree-nut-free, celiac-safe, and artificial-dye-free. The menu includes cinnamon rolls, French crullers, donuts, cupcakes, cookies, scones, cakes, a chicken sausage and cheddar breakfast sandwich, biscuits and gravy, eclairs, and apple fritters, plus vegan options.

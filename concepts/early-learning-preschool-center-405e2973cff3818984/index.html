Learning through play since 1996

Early Learning Preschool Center is a State of Vermont licensed child care center in downtown Burlington, on the city bus line, established in 1996. It serves ages 3 to 5 with half- or full-day preschool, full- or part-time openings, and year-round care with a play-based summer camp. Its program pairs hands-on early academic activities with a social and emotional focus, and includes a school garden and Burlington's original Giving Tree.

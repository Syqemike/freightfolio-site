01 - Services

The service menu

Seven massage styles offered at the practice. Call to ask which is right for you and to schedule a session.

Swedish

Long, flowing strokes meant to ease tension and help the body relax.

Deep Tissue

Slower, focused pressure aimed at deeper layers of muscle and connective tissue.

Chair

A focused massage performed seated in a massage chair.

Hot Stones

Warmed stones placed and worked along the body as part of the massage.

Medical

Goal-oriented bodywork tied to a specific treatment need.

NMT

Neuromuscular therapy: targeted work on trigger points and patterns of tension.

Sports

Bodywork built around activity, training, and recovery.

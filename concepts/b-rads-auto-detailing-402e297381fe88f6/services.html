The work.

Auto detailing and automotive restoration in Wichita, Kansas. What is listed here is what the shop does.

Exterior detailing

A proper hand wash is the foundation. Wheels, tires, and body panels cleaned by hand, then finished and dressed.

  • Wheels, tires, and wheel wells

Interior detailing

The inside gets the same effort as the outside. Seats, carpets, and every surface you touch, deep cleaned.

Correction & restoration

For paint and headlights that have seen better days. Machine polishing to bring back gloss, and restoration work that makes tired surfaces look right again.

  • Machine polishing
  • Headlight restoration
  • Restoration of worn exterior surfaces

About

A neighborhood bakery on East Pass Road

La Panaderia is a Latino bakery at 1909 E Pass Rd in Gulfport, Mississippi. The bakery serves pan dulce such as conchas and hojaldras, special occasion cakes, drinks, and Latin American restaurant fare. It is open every day of the week, and customers rate it 4.7 stars on Google across 49 reviews.

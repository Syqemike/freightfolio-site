The shop

A barbershop the way they used to make them

Corey runs the shop solo, the way his grandfather Don started it. It is a small room, a single chair, and a steady line of regulars who have been coming for years. The reviews mention the vintage interior, the easy conversation, and cuts that hold their shape.

There is no online booking and no waiting room crowd. You call, you walk in, and Corey takes care of you.

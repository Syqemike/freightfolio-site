About

Sycamore House is a family run French American and globally inspired dinner restaurant at 210 Main Street in Bay St. Louis, Mississippi, run since 2002 by owners and chefs Michael Eastham and Stella LeGardeur. Dinner is served Wednesday through Saturday evenings, with reservations, dine in, and takeout offered.

Dinner menu

Appetizers

Appetizers including BBQ shrimp remoulade and crabmeat cheesecake.

Soups and salads

Soups and salads including sunchoke soup, French onion soup, and artichoke soup.

Entrees

Entrees including fish of the day almondine, Thai curry with chicken, shrimp, or tofu, BBQ shrimp and grits, and Cuban braised brisket.

Grill and cafe items

Grill items plus cafe sandwiches and po boys.

Children's menu

A children's menu for younger diners.

Desserts

Desserts including creme brulee, key lime pie, bread pudding, and homemade ice cream.

Dinner, Wednesday to Saturday

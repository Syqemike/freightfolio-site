About

Lilah's Bakery is a Shreveport bakery at 1718 Centenary Blvd, a king cake institution since 2002 named after owner Lisa Tike's daughter Lila. King cakes come in more than 20 flavors, including cinnamon, cream cheese, strawberry Bavarian, triple berry, and maple bacon twist, with holiday-themed cakes for Easter, Halloween, Thanksgiving, and Christmas. After its October 1, 2026 grand reopening, the bakery is open year-round with cookies, brownies, and dessert bars, plus special-order king cakes outside Mardi Gras season and delivery in Shreveport and Bossier.

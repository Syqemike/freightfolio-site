# Design Brief: The Ultimate Experience Wilmington (Wilmington, DE)

## Business personality
Blank-slate barbershop at 1200 Northeast Blvd, Wilmington DE. No retrievable branding, logo, or photos. Services: hot towel shaves, beard trims, classic haircuts, modern styles. Moderate price level. Self-description: "aim to be the best of the best in providing quality, affordable grooming to todays busy men". 4.9 from 19 places reviews. Phone, address, and hours are all single-sourced (IG location page); hours shown with a "call to confirm" flag. 6 labeled stock photos used.

## Creative direction
Minimal, modern, confident. Maximum whitespace, oversized grotesk type, numbered service index (01-04), thin hairline rules, one cobalt accent against near-white. The opposite of the heritage and luxury-dark barber concepts built earlier in this wave.

## Typography
Space Grotesk (display) + Inter (body). Distinct from Don's (Bitter/Plex Mono), Presidential (Cormorant/Jost), and Razor Sharp (Zilla Slab/Karla).

## Palette
White #fbfbf9, ink #101014, cobalt #2438d8, hairline gray #e4e4e1.

## Layout
Thin disclosure bar, minimal topbar with wordmark + nav, split hero with giant stacked h1 left and rounded-corner photo right, numbered services list, "The work" photo grid, two-column hours (flagged as listed, call to confirm) + visit section, footer disclaimer. One h1. All CTAs are "Call to book" with tel:+13024339235. No online booking verified. No social links (only an IG location page exists; not linked since it is not the business's own page).

## Photo provenance
Pexels stock IDs: 12074386 (fade from behind), 33461079 (clippers and comb), 7781852 (haircut + beard side view), 4625626 (modern barbershop), 34702982 (classic haircut), 10775080 (b/w clipper close-up). Every photo carries a "Stock photo" figcaption; footer discloses stock photos.

## Unverifiable facts and handling
Phone/address/hours single-sourced; hours table labeled "Hours as listed. Call to confirm before you come by." No ZIP shown (unconfirmed). No prices or service details invented beyond the four verified services. The shop's self-description was lightly paraphrased ("Quality, affordable grooming for today's busy men").

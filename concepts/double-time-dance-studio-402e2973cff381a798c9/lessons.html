Lessons Your Way

Group social nights run nightly, and private instruction is available for anyone who wants one-on-one attention. Owner John Henry Moore has led the studio since 1999, and the teaching team works with absolute beginners through experienced social dancers.

Private Lessons

Single lessons or packages, scheduled at your pace. Work on footwork, lead and follow, or a specific dance before a trip or event. Call to ask about current availability and package options.

Wedding Lessons

First-dance choreography built for your song, your timeline, and your comfort on the floor. Couples usually start a few months out. Call to ask about current availability.

Kids Hip Hop

Saturdays 10 to 11 AM. A high-energy weekly class for younger dancers who want to move.

Start Tonight

Social nights welcome newcomers every night of the week. If you have never danced a step, instructors walk the floor and keep new dancers moving.

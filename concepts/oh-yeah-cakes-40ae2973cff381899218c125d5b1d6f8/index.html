Oh Yeah Cakes

A San Antonio cake and cupcake shop, baking signature cupcakes fresh daily since 2012.

About

Oh Yeah Cakes is a cake and cupcake shop in San Antonio, Texas, at 713 S St Mary's St, owned and run by San Antonio native Lori Hernandez. The shop has been in business since 2012 and bakes its signature cupcakes fresh daily, with 15 flavors including 8 signature flavors and a cupcake of the month.

Flavors

Customer favorites include Lemon Raspberry, Red Velvet, and Mexican Chocolate cupcakes.

Services

Signature cupcakes

15 flavors baked fresh daily, including 8 signature flavors and a cupcake of the month.

Custom cakes

Custom cakes for weddings, birthdays, and other life events. Bring a picture, a drawing, or an idea, per the shop description.

Custom cupcakes

Custom cupcakes, made to order. Custom orders are taken at least 3 to 4 weeks ahead, per the shop description.

About

Maverick Training Center is a martial arts academy in Huntsville. The academy offers Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu for adults and kids ages 4 and up, Muay Thai, MMA, kickboxing, and Tai Chi, plus one-on-one competition training and coaching. The first class is free.

About

It is a Ruff Life offers dog daycare and boarding in Clive, Iowa, with more than 10 years of experience. Care runs at 1 staff to 15 dogs, with indoor and outdoor play, a midday nap, and no kennels. New dogs complete a 10 to 15 minute temperament evaluation before joining the group.

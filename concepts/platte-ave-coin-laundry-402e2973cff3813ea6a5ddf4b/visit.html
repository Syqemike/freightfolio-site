What to know

Before you come.

  • Self-service only. This is a coin-op laundromat you run yourself. There is no wash-and-fold, no drop-off, and no commercial service.
  • Card and coin. Every machine takes cards or quarters, whichever you prefer.
  • Free Wi-Fi. Get online while your load runs.
  • Big loads welcome. The 40 lb super-size washers take comforters, blankets, and sleeping bags.
  • Soap on site. Single-use detergent available from $1.00 if you forget yours.
  • Plan your timing. Start your last wash with enough time to wash and dry before 10:00 PM closing.

Locally roasted coffee, homemade pastries

BuzzCatz Coffee and Sweets is a cat-themed coffee shop and bakery in Orange Beach, with a purple door. It serves locally roasted coffee from Fairhope Roasting Company, homemade pastries, cinnamon rolls, Kitten Head Biscuits, the Buzz Bowl, avocado toast, waffles, omelettes, cookies, ice cream, and cheesecake. Outdoor seating and a pet-friendly patio are available, with takeout.

01  ·  The boutique

Upscale fashion, chosen for the Coast

Gina's is an upscale women's boutique in the heart of Ocean Springs, built around designer clothing with a polished, put-together feel. The racks hold recognized designer labels, and the cases hold handcrafted jewelry and one-of-a-kind antiques. It is the kind of shop where an afternoon of browsing turns into the outfit for the season.

Designer labels, handcrafted jewelry, and antiques under one roof on Washington Avenue.

# Design Brief - Gina's Clothing & Jewelry Boutique

Boutique concept site for Gina's Clothing & Jewelry Boutique, 902 Washington Ave, Ocean Springs, MS 39564. Phone (228) 818-3333.

## Business profile (research only)
- Upscale women's fashion boutique. Designer labels: Joseph Ribkoff, Habitat, Johnny Was, Nic + Zoe, Betty Carre, Michele Busch, Brighton.
- Also carries handcrafted jewelry, antiques, accessories and gifts, tees.
- Hours: Mon-Sat 11:00 AM - 4:00 PM; Sun closed.
- Google rating 4.7 from 17 reviews. Price tier: expensive.
- Web presence is a Facebook page (1,661 followers); no standalone website.

## Aesthetic
Luxury boutique editorial. A fashion magazine spread, not a quiet gallery. Rich, dramatic, distinctive. Must be visually DISTINCT from the Leather & Pearls concept (quiet ivory minimal vs. this dark, opulent editorial).

## Palette
- Deep plum #2E1424 (page ground, hero washes)
- Rich plum #4A2138 (section accents)
- Ivory #F6F0E4 (text on dark, light sections)
- Champagne #D3A96B (accents, rules, brand names)
- Black-plum #1D0C17 (footer, deep contrast)

## Typography
- Display: Playfair Display (bold, large, editorial). Magazine headline voice.
- Body/UI: a clean grotesque sans. Drop-cap style lead paragraphs, pull-quote styling for the brand statement, oversized section numbers.
- Deliberate asymmetry: overlapping text over imagery, full-bleed crops, staggered grids.

## Imagery
Dramatic, richly toned fashion editorial photography: burgundy gown portrait, black velvet portrait, evening dress with pearls, vintage pearl-and-gold jewelry set. Dark, saturated images that sit on plum. Every caption labeled as stock photo.

## Layout (single page, mobile-first)
1. Slim disclosure bar: "Design preview" notice.
2. Header: GINA'S wordmark prominent; simple nav (The Labels, Jewelry, Visit, Call); sticky.
3. Hero: full-bleed burgundy gown portrait; oversized serif headline naming the boutique; "Ocean Springs" location line; Call CTA (tel:+12288183333).
4. Editorial intro: magazine-style lead with drop cap. Designer boutique positioning using research words only.
5. "The Labels": brand list as text names only (Joseph Ribkoff, Habitat, Johnny Was, Nic + Zoe, Betty Carre, Michele Busch, Brighton). No logos.
6. "Jewelry, antiques & gifts": handcrafted jewelry, antiques, accessories and gifts, tees - research categories only.
7. Gallery: staggered editorial grid with visible "Stock photo:" captions.
8. Rating line, shown ONCE: "4.7 from 17 Google reviews."
9. Visit: 902 Washington Ave, Ocean Springs, MS 39564; phone; hours (Mon-Sat 11-4, Sun closed); Google Maps link; Call CTA.
10. Footer: design-preview disclaimer, "Concept by Freightfolio" + https://freightfolio.net link.

## Customer journey
Land on dramatic editorial hero → read the designer positioning → scan the label list → browse jewelry/antiques → check hours/location → call the boutique.

## Copy rules
No em dashes. No "No X, no Y" triples. No banned AI phrases. Plain, direct, business-specific language. Never invent facts, reviews, hours, awards, or history. Never imply online booking exists. Do not invent designer lines beyond the verified list.

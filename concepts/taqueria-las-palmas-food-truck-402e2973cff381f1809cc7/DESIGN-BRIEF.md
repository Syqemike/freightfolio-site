## Aesthetic
Late-night taqueria street energy. Char-grilled dark charcoal base, warm cream panels, chile red for accents, agave green pulled from the actual truck paint, golden tortilla yellow for highlights. Chunky hand-painted-sign vibe via typography alone, no illustrations, no clipart, no icons.

## Typography
- Display: "Anton" (bold condensed, street-sign weight) for the name, section headers, marquee
- Body: "Archivo" (clean, readable) for everything else
- Ticket-stub details (dotted borders, dashed dividers) instead of graphic ornaments

## Customer journey
Truck rolls past in the day or the name comes up after hours -> name + open-until-1AM is the first thing seen -> menu answers "what do they serve" -> gallery proves the food is real -> rating proves it is good -> visit block has address, hours, phone, map link in one glance -> Call CTA everywhere.

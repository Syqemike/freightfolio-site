OPEN UNTIL 1 AM, EVERY NIGHT

Most kitchens are closed by 10. The truck is still firing the plancha at midnight. Tacos, birria, and burritos stay on the menu straight through to 1 AM, seven days a week.

There is covered seating with a fan on the screened porch, so rain or shine you can sit down and eat.

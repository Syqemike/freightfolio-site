# Design Brief: Murphy's Mutts and Cuts (St. Louis, MO)

## Research facts (only facts used)
- Murphy's Mutts and Cuts, 2307 Cherokee St, St Louis, MO; +1 314-771-3338
- Hours: Tue-Sat 9:00 AM-5:00 PM; Sun/Mon Closed
- Services: full-service grooming (baths, trims, and more); also sells pet essentials (treats, leashes)
- Groomer Missy named in reviews; women-owned business
- Rating: 4.9 from 46 Google reviews
- Real storefront photo exists on cherokeestreet.com/neighbor (photo by R.J. Hartbeck): downloaded and verified as the actual Murphy's Mutts & Cuts storefront (blue storefront, "Baths-Cuts-Nails" signage, 314.771.3338). Used as the anchor photo. Remaining photos stock fallback, all labeled.

## Design direction
"Cherokee Street corner shop": brick red + botanical green + cream, warm neighborhood feel, Missy the groomer as a human touch, a "visit the block" section with the Cherokee Street address and directions link. Friendly storefront energy, not corporate.

## Typography
- Display: Zilla Slab (sign-painter slab, storefront feel)
- Body: Nunito Sans (round, friendly)

## Palette
- Brick #B0492F (headers, accents), Botanical green #2E5B3E (bands, buttons), Cream #F8F1E3 (page), Ink #2B2018

## Layout / customer journey
Disclosure bar > sign-style name header + sticky nav > hero (real storefront photo + welcome + CTAs + rating) > green welcome band with murphy-hero > grooming services > Missy + women-owned note > pet essentials > photo strip > "Visit the block" (address, directions link, hours) > footer. Customer journey: see the real shop, feel the neighborhood, confirm services, get directions and call.

## Copy notes
- "Call to book" only (tel:+13147713338); no online booking verified.
- Rating exactly once (hero); footer cites source only.
- Missy: "Reviews name Missy, one of the groomers" (plain, no invented title).

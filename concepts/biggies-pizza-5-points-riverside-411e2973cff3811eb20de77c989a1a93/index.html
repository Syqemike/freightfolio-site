About

Biggies Pizza 5 Points Riverside is a pizza shop at 1053 Park St in Jacksonville, Florida, in the 5 Points Riverside neighborhood. The shop serves NY-style pizza by the slice and whole pies: pepperoni, Sicilian, veggie, chicken bacon ranch, and buffalo chicken, plus garlic knots, heroes, salads, wings, and desserts. Signature items are the Buffalo Soldier Slice and the Benny Blanco Pie. Dine-in and takeout are available. The Jax Beach and Southside locations are closed; this 5 Points location is active. Hours are Monday through Thursday, 11:00 AM to 9:00 PM; Friday and Saturday, 11:00 AM to 10:00 PM; Sunday, 11:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Call (904) 580-5879 or email biggiespizzajaxbeach@gmail.com to place an order.

About

Revel Movement is a pole and aerial arts studio in Boise, the original pole and aerial studio in the city, formerly Ophidia Studio. Classes cover pole dance, aerial silks, lyra, and aerial hammock, for all levels, ages 18 and up.

Classes

Pole dance

Pole dance classes for all levels, ages 18 and up.

Aerial silks

Aerial silks classes for all levels, ages 18 and up.

Lyra

Lyra, or aerial hoop, classes for all levels, ages 18 and up.

Aerial hammock

Aerial hammock classes for all levels, ages 18 and up.

All levels

Classes are open to all levels, from first-timers to experienced movers.

Ages 18 and up

Classes are for adults, ages 18 and up.

Merritt's Bakery is a family bakery in Tulsa since 1979. Products include cakes, pastries, and donuts, plus custom celebration and wedding cakes. Items named in research include yeast donuts, tres leches, king cake, and strawberry cake. The Midtown location shown here is at 3202 E 15th St, with Broken Arrow and Riverside locations also listed.

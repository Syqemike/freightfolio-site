On the wall

A studio that hangs art

City of Ink works as both a tattoo studio and an art gallery. Custom work is the standard here, and the walls carry the proof.

The founder

Miya Bailey

Artist Miya Bailey founded City of Ink, which opened around 2006 in Castleberry Hill.

It is a Black-owned tattoo studio that also works as an art gallery and a cultural hub for the neighborhood. The shop is known for its brick and turquoise walls, and for treating tattooing as fine art.

The work

Custom tattoos, drawn for you

Every piece starts with a consultation. The artists draw custom designs in black and grey realism, traditional American, Japanese, fine line, and color, and they take on cover-ups. Consultations include prep and aftercare guidance.

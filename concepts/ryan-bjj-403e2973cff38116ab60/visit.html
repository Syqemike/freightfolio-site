Your First Visit

Show up about fifteen minutes before the session starts. Wear comfortable athletic clothes and bring water. If you have a gi, bring it. If not, you are fine without one for a trial.

  • First class is free for beginners
  • Call ahead so the team expects you
  • Parents are welcome to watch youth classes

Common Questions

Do I need experience to start?

No. Beginners start with fundamentals, and the free trial class is built for people who have never trained.

What should I wear?

Comfortable athletic clothes for your trial class. If you join and need a gi or rash guard, the team will tell you what to get.

Can my child train here?

Yes. RYAN BJJ runs youth BJJ classes in addition to adult classes. Parents are welcome to watch.

How do I sign up for the free trial?

Call (918) 510-8745 and tell the team you want the free trial class. There is no online signup.

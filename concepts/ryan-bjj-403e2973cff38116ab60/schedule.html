Two Sessions Most Days

Monday through Thursday the academy runs a midday block from 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM and an evening block from 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM. Friday evenings wrap at 7:30 PM, and Saturday is a single morning session from 10:00 AM to 11:00 AM.

New students should call ahead. The team will point you to the right session for a trial class.

Bark N Bubbles - Design Brief (batch 7, concept 1)

Playful premium pet brand. Warm cream base with teal/aqua and coral accents, reflecting a clean bath-time feel rather than rugged workwear. CSS radial-gradient bubble-dot motifs used restrainedly in the hero and art panels, plus glossy bubble "photo" stand-ins since no real photos exist. Typography pairs Avenir Next/Segoe UI system stack in heavy 900-weight headlines with big size contrast against soft gray body copy. Layout is airy and asymmetrical: editorial hero with bubble cluster art, two-column service cards, a dark-teal Meet Chrissy panel with an initials monogram, and a three-card visit section. Copy is plain and direct, grounded only in verified facts (Chrissy's 13+ years, former Clive Petco salon manager, inside Family Pet Vet Clinic, low-stress focus, Mon/Wed hours, Google 5.0 from 21 reviews). No photos used anywhere.

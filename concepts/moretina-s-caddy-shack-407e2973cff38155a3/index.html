Moretina's Caddy Shack

A family-owned dive bar and sports bar in Kansas City since 1993. The kitchen serves Sicilian-style thin-crust pizza, wings, toasted ravioli and other appetizers, sandwiches, and burgers, with food served late. Twenty big-screen TVs cover game days, and the patio is dog friendly.

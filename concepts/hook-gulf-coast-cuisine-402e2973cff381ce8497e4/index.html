On the plate

Dishes people drive across the Coast for

A few of the plates that built this restaurant's name in Pass Christian.

Thai-ger Shrimp

Crispy Gulf shrimp tossed in a sweet Thai chili sauce with pickled jalapenos. A starter with a kick.

Andouille-Crusted Triple Tail

Fresh catch triple tail with an andouille crust. Order it grilled, blackened, or fried, with the sauce of your choice.

Seafood Gumbo

A chunky, seafood-loaded gumbo that regulars call the real thing. Ask for a bowl next to your po-boy.

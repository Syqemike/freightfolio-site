A downtown deli with a Deco room

Deco Deli is a breakfast and lunch deli in the Deco District. The room keeps its Art Deco identity: original 1922 granite countertops and tables, framed Tulsa Historical Society photos, and an Atlas mural. The deli is run by Gary and FaDawn Kesler.

Seasonal Highlight

King Cake Season

When carnival season rolls around, Crazy B's is known for its king cakes. Owner Susan Kendrick bakes them fresh in season, and they have become a local favorite for parties, showers, and office gatherings.

King cake bites are a smaller way to get the same flavor. They are great for bridal and baby showers, or just for sharing at home.

~1,000 king cakes baked and sold each season by Susan Kendrick

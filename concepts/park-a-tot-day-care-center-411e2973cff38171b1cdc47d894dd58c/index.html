About

Park A Tot Day Care Center is a daycare and preschool at 3809 W Fig St in Tampa, Florida. Owners Mike and Olivia run a licensed center with capacity for 88 children, offering full-time and part-time infant, toddler, and preschool care, plus before- and after-school care. Food is served, and transportation is available. Hours are Monday through Friday, 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Call (813) 877-3478 to ask about enrollment.

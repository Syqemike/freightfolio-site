About

Fashion Nails is a nail salon in Metairie, Louisiana, at 3101 North Causeway Boulevard. Services cover gel manicures, acrylic nails, dip powder, manicures and pedicures, nail art, eyelash extensions, waxing, and nail repair, plus microblading with tech May. Hours are Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, and Saturday 9:30 AM to 7:00 PM and Tuesday 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

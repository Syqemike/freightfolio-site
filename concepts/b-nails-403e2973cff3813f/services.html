Services

Nails, brows, lashes. All in one chair.

Every service below is offered at B Nails on SW 9th St. Walk-ins are welcome, and a beverage is offered while you sit. Call ahead at (515) 280-3120 to reserve your time.

Color & length

Dip powder sets & extensions

Dip powder color built for staying power, with extensions when you want extra length. From the salon's own photos: bold orange coffin sets, blush tones, and glitter finishes.

Hands

Manicures

Classic manicure care and polish, including white French tips for a crisp, timeless finish. Shape, color, and detail are finished exactly the way you ask.

Feet

Pedicures

Full pedicure care, plus the hot-stone pedicure: a warm, relaxing treatment for feet that have had a long week. A calm seat, a beverage, and unhurried time.

Finishing details

Eyebrow waxing & lash lift

Eyebrow waxing for a clean shape, and the lash lift for open, defined eyes without daily mascara. Easy add-ons to any nail appointment.

Walk-ins welcome.

B Nails is open Monday through Saturday, 9:30 AM to 5:00 PM. Call to book ahead or just come by 520 SW 9th St Unit 112.

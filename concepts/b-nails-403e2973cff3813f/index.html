The studio

A clean, modern spot for your nails

B Nails describes itself as a full service nail salon located in downtown Des Moines, and that is exactly the point. One visit covers it: nails, brows, lashes, and the finishing details.

Dip powder sets and extensions sit alongside classic manicures and pedicures, with white French tips, eyebrow waxing, a lash lift, and a hot-stone pedicure on the menu. Everything happens in a clean, modern setting with a beverage in hand.

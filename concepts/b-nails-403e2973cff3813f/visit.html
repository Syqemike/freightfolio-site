About the salon

A clean, modern salon in downtown Des Moines.

B Nails describes itself as a full service nail salon located in downtown Des Moines, with a clean, modern vibe. Manicures, pedicures, dip powder sets and extensions, eyebrow waxing, white French tips, the lash lift, and the hot-stone pedicure are all offered under one roof.

Walk-ins are welcome, and beverages are offered while you sit. The salon is also on Facebook, where its own photos are shared.

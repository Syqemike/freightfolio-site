Our Story

A Family-Run Shop Built on Strong Foundations

Bonnie Sue's is a family-run coffee shop and cafe in Pearl, Mississippi. The tagline says it plainly: coffee brewed as strong as our foundations.

Regulars come for the signature drinks and the homemade baked goods, and they stay because it feels like home. Bring your laptop, bring a friend, or just bring an appetite.

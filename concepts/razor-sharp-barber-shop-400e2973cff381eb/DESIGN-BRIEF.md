# Design Brief: Razor Sharp Barber Shop (Trenton, NJ)

## Business personality
Neighborhood barbershop at 20 Passaic St, Trenton NJ, opened 2001, owner William Hacket. Community-hub reputation ("sharp cuts and community vibes"). Services: men's haircut (~$35, verified), men's beard trim, taper fade, "big chop", mullet, kids' cuts. Appointments required. Free Wi-Fi. Hours not found anywhere: no hours table shown; "Call for hours" banner instead. Phone single-sourced and listing stale-risk, but used with tel: per instructions. 4.5 from 30 place reviews. 6 labeled stock photos used.

## Creative direction
Heritage community-board: cream paper, double rules, tape-edged tilted photo frames, stamped "Est. 2001" badge. Hunter green and mustard against kraft tones. Deliberately different from Don's heritage take (oxblood/brass, ticket stubs, pole stripes): this one is a small-town bulletin board with a hunter/mustard palette and polaroid-style gallery.

## Typography
Zilla Slab (display) + Karla (body). Distinct from Don's (Bitter/Plex Mono), Presidential (Cormorant/Jost), and Wilmington (Space Grotesk/Inter).

## Palette
Paper #f6f0dd, hunter #26402c, mustard #d9a62e, brick #9a3b26, ink #231e14.

## Layout
Disclosure bar, masthead with wordmark + est. badge + boxed nav, hero (copy left, tilted framed photo right), "The shop" story section, services grid with verified ~$35 price on the men's haircut, tilted polaroid gallery, full-width "Call for hours" banner (no hours table), visit section, footer disclaimer. One h1. All CTAs "Call to book" / "Call for hours" with tel:+16096720560. No social links (no verified pages; the same-named Trenton shop is a different business).

## Photo provenance
Pexels stock IDs: 6487911 (clipper close-up), 4625632 (back view clipper cut), 27467943 (fashionable haircut profile), 9971240 (trimmer and comb), 7781848 (stylish haircut profile), 18503604 (getting a haircut). Every photo carries a "Stock photo" figcaption; footer discloses stock photos.

## Unverifiable facts and handling

Only price shown is the verified ~$35 men's haircut. Community-hub copy stays within the verified reputation ("sharp cuts and community vibes", "neighborhood hub").

The shop

A barbershop with a quarter century of roots on Passaic Street.

Razor Sharp opened its doors in 2001 and has been part of the Trenton neighborhood ever since. Owner William Hacket built the shop around a simple idea: a place where regulars get a great cut and feel at home.

The shop doubles as a community hub, with free Wi-Fi, a chair for every age, and appointments so your time is respected.

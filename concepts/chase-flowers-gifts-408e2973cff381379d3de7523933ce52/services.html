What Chase Flowers & Gifts offers

Chase Flowers and Gifts is a florist and gift shop in Springfield, in business since 1921 and now in Paramount Plaza. The shop makes floral arrangements for any occasion, plus gift bouquets, weddings, boutonnieres, potted hydrangea, and agate street roses. Same-day delivery is available by phone.

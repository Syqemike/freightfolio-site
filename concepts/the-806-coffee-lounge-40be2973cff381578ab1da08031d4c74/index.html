About

The 806 Coffee + Lounge is a coffee house and lounge in Amarillo's Historic Route 66 District, open since 2007. The shop calls itself Coffee + Culture + Community, and the room doubles as a gathering place for local artists, musicians, and neighbors.

Menu

Fair-trade and organic coffee

Coffee, espresso, and tea made with fair-trade and organic beans.

Chai

Chai tea and chai lattes, including the year-round apple cider chai called Spider and the seasonal Pumpkinhead spiced pumpkin latte.

Vegetarian and vegan food

Vegan chili, quinoa salad, hummus, bagels, pizza bagels, blue corn nachos, pastries, and espresso brownies. The menu is vegetarian and vegan only.

Beer and wine

Beer and wine served alongside the coffee menu.

Live music, art shows, and open mic

Live music and art shows through the week, plus Amarillo's longest-running open mic night. Art on the walls is by local artists and is for sale.

Weekend brunch and happy hours

Weekend brunch runs Saturday 11am to 2pm and Sunday 10am to 2pm. Happy hours run daily from 3pm to 7pm.

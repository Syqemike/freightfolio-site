# Design Brief: Perrine Barber Shop (Twin Falls, ID)

## Research facts (only facts used)
- Perrine Barber Shop, 115 Shoshone St N, Twin Falls, ID 83301; +1 208-731-7895
- Hours: Mon Closed; Tue/Wed/Fri 7:00 AM-6:00 PM; Thu 8:00 AM-6:00 PM; Sat 8:00 AM-1:00 PM; Sun Closed
- Services: men's cuts, shaves, beard trims, fades, hot lather neck shave; locally owned 60+ years; appointments preferred over walk-ins
- Reviews describe it as traditional, recently remodeled, clean
- Rating: 4.9 from 30 Google reviews. No socials, no public photos.

## Design direction
"60-year mid-century Americana": cream + barn red + navy. Vintage badge-style TYPOGRAPHIC header (borders + stacked type, zero clip art). Light, airy, morning-clean. Deliberately NOT a dark-espresso barbershop. Restrained longevity note: "locally owned 60+ years" only, no invented founding dates.

## Typography
- Display: Oswald (uppercase, letterspaced; badge and section heads)
- Body: Libre Franklin
- Accent: Special Elite (typewriter, used once for the 60+ years stamp)

## Palette
- Cream #FAF5EA (page), Barn red #A63A30 (band, accents), Navy #20304F (frames, footer), Ink #2A2118

## Layout / customer journey
Disclosure bar > typographic badge header > sticky nav > hero photo + headline + rating + CTAs > red longevity band > services (with appointments-preferred note) > photo row > hours + visit > footer. Customer journey: recognize a classic shop, confirm it is clean and long-standing, see services, call to book.

## Copy notes
- "Call to book" only (tel:+12087317895); no online booking verified.
- Rating exactly once (hero); footer cites source only.
- "Appointments are preferred over walk-ins" is a verified fact, stated plainly.
- Hours with "to". No em dashes, no invented history, no urgency.

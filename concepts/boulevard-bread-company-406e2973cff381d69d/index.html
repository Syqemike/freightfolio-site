A Little Rock bakery and bistro

Boulevard Bread Company is an artisan bakery and bistro group in Little Rock. Its offerings include artisan bread, breakfast and lunch menus, sweets, catering, and venue rental. This page covers the SOMA location at 1417 S Main St, open Monday to Saturday 7:00 AM to 2:00 PM and closed Sunday. The own site is a news and Instagram-style page with no menu, no prices except one dinner special, and no online ordering, so call with questions.

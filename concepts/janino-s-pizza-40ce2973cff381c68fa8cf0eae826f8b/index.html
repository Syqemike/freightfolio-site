About

Janino's Pizza is a NY-style beachside pizzeria in Gulf Shores, for pickup and delivery only, with no indoor seating. The menu covers NY-style pizzas named for the Bronx, Brooklyn, and Manhattan, plus stromboli, calzones, wings, and salads, with a 12-inch gluten-free crust.

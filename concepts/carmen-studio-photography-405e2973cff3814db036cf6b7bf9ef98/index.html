A portrait studio in downtown Akron, built around the people in front of the camera.

Carmen Studio Photography works from its studio at 72 E Mill St in Akron. The studio photographs high school seniors, working professionals, sports teams, families, and weddings, and offers online proofs so clients can review their portraits.

The studio's own site describes more than 60 years behind the camera. Sessions are by appointment, and the studio asks visitors to call before coming, since staff are often out on shoots.

# Design Brief: Klik'S Deli & Bar (Columbia, MO)

## Concept: "The Menu Board"
A dive bar does not need a polished corporate site. The design is built around
the one thing Klik's already does: a kitchen chalkboard / printed menu board
pinned to the wall of a dark, wood-and-neon room. The site should read like
walking in and reading the board: what's cooking, what's on tap, when they're
open, where to find them. Nothing slick, nothing invented.

## Aesthetic
Dark bar-room base (warm charcoal, #17120d) like the interior at night, with
cream "ticket" panels (#f5eedb) that read as pinned menu slips. Hairline
dashed chalk-style rules separate sections. No gradients, no glowing blobs, no
cards grid in columns, no illustration of any kind. Photos carry all imagery.
The real Klik's logo photo is used once as the authentic sign-off mark.

## Palette
- Barroom charcoal #17120d (base)
- Warm cream #f5eedb (menu-ticket panels, body text on dark)
- Bottle amber #f0a832 (Klik's sign yellow, headline accents, rules)
- PBR red #c33a2b (call-to-action, dividers, stamps) 
- Pale mustard #d9b36a (secondary accents on dark)

## Typography
- Display: "Bebas Neue" (Google Fonts) — tall condensed bar-sign capitals for
  page titles and section heads. Fallback: 'Arial Narrow', sans-serif.
- Handwritten notes: "Caveat" (Google Fonts) — used sparingly for ticket-pin
  asides like the rating stamp. Fallback: cursive.
- Body: system stack (-apple-system, 'Segoe UI', Georgia serif for pull quotes)
  for honest, plain reading.

## Layout (deliberately NOT hero + 3 cards)
- Sticky narrow top bar: real logo mark small at left, business name,
  simple text nav, one red "Call to book" button.
- Index: full-bleed interior photo (the real bar, verified) with a chalk-board
  title plate laid over it; below, a wide "menu board" strip of menu items on
  cream tickets; a horizontal hours ticker (plain strip, no animation); a
  "good to know" section as a pinned list (patio, pool, darts, cheap PBR);
  rating stamp exactly once; big footer CTA band with address + phone.
- Menu: single-column ticket list, each item with stock food photo labeled
  honestly, no prices (none published). 
- About: two-column prose + real logo photo; only verified facts.
- Photos: simple masonry-ish gallery; captions distinguish real Klik's shots
  from stock food photos.
- Visit: hours table, address, phone, Facebook link, call CTA.

## Customer journey
1. Land: see the room (real photo), know it's a dive bar + deli, get the hours
   at a glance.
2. Browse menu items (name + one verified line each).
3. See where it is and when it's open, then call: tel:+15734496692.

## Imagery
Real Klik's photos: interior bar shot (Wanderlog place page), logo sign
(Columbia CVB directory). Stock food photos (reuben, cheese curds, burger,
tacos), each captioned "Stock photo". Captions match depicted content.

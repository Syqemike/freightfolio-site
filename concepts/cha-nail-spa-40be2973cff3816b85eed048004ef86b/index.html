About

CHA Nail Spa is a nail salon and spa at 313 Manufacturers Road, Suite 117, in Chattanooga, Tennessee. The salon offers a full range of nail and spa services, with booking by appointment. Services run from gels and acrylics to facials, waxing, and eyelash work.

Services

Gels, acrylics, and dip

Gel manicures, acrylics, sculpting gel, and dip powders.

Manicures and pedicures

Manicures and pedicures, including the Signature OPI Spa Pedicure, the Deluxe house spa pedicure, and hot stone massage.

Freehand nail art

Freehand nail art drawn by hand.

Hard gel manicure

Hard gel manicures for longer lasting wear.

Facials and waxing

Facials, waxing with organic wax, eyelash extensions, threading, tinting, and hair removal.

Kids services

Kids services, styled Mini Me, for younger clients.

What the day holds
What a day here looks like

Tiny Tots Academy is an Alabama state licensed child care center. Children are grouped by age in six classrooms, and the day mixes classroom time, art, meals together, and time outside.

The littlest ones

The infant rooms care for children from 6 weeks old. Cribs and cots sit in a quiet room with soft mats, toys, and pictures on the walls. Infant teachers are Ann, Hailey, and Nadia.

Classroom days

Classrooms run an age-based curriculum built around art and exploration. The classrooms hold dress-up days, like pajama day in October 2026. Toddler teachers are Kahla, Lauren, Anna P., Maddie, and Brit. K3 and K4 teachers are Autumn, Destiny, and Kat.

Time outside

The fenced play yard has a swing set, a climbing frame, and ride-on cars, with room for the children to run. The center held a trunk-or-treat outside in October 2026.

Families stay in the loop

Parents get updates, photos, and direct communication through the Brightwheel app. Meals are part of the day too: the center serves a nutritious breakfast, lunch, and snacks daily.

Led by Director Katie

The center is family owned and operated. The center's teachers also turn out for community events, like this 5K at the USS Alabama in November 2026.

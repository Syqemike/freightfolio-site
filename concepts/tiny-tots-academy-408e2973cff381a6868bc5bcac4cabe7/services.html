What Tiny Tots Academy offers

Tiny Tots Academy is an Alabama state licensed child care center in Daphne founded in 2010 by Robert and Cindy Turnage, for children aged 6 weeks to 5 years. The center has 6 classrooms divided by age, serves nutritious breakfast, lunch, and snacks daily, and uses art and an age-based curriculum encouraging exploration and discovery. Families get updates, photos, and direct communication through the Brightwheel app.

Programs and tuition

Three programs, by age

Each program has its own classroom and teachers. Tuition is billed weekly through the Brightwheel app, by debit, credit, ACH, check, or cash. The full-time rate has no hourly restrictions.

Infants

6 weeks to 18 months

$225 per week

Care for the youngest children in the nursery rooms, with cribs, cots, and quiet space. Infant teachers are Ann, Hailey, and Nadia.

Toddlers

18 months to 4 years

$195 per week

Classroom days built around art, exploration, and play. The classrooms hold dress-up days, like pajama day in October 2026. Toddler teachers are Kahla, Lauren, Anna P., Maddie, and Brit.

Preschool

4 to 5 years

$190 per week

Pre-kindergarten classrooms for K3 and K4, with an age-based curriculum built around art and exploration. K3 and K4 teachers are Autumn, Destiny, and Kat.

What tuition covers

  • Nutritious breakfast, lunch, and snacks every day.
  • Basic school supplies.
  • Updates, photos, and direct communication through the Brightwheel app.
  • Outdoor play in the fenced play yard.

The center accepts Child Care South subsidy payments. A one-time registration fee of $150 applies.

Sibling rates, per week

  • Two infants: $405
  • Infant and toddler: $378
  • Infant and preschool: $353.50
  • Two toddlers: $351
  • Toddler and preschool: $346.50
  • Two preschool children: $342

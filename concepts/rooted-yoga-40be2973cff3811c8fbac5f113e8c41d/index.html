About

Rooted Yoga is an inclusive yoga and wellness studio in the Shandon neighborhood of Columbia, South Carolina. Owner Anne Miller leads a studio offering warm power vinyasa, all-level vinyasa, gentle yoga with guided meditation, Tai Chi, dynamic flows, and Pilates, plus class passes, a 200-hour teacher training program, and Saturday outdoor sessions at Hollywood Park.

Our Story

Seventy years in the chair

State Barber Shop has stood on South Warren Street since the 1950s, and celebrated its 60th anniversary in January 2021. The man behind the chair is owner Joe Festa, now 85, known up and down the street as the "Mayor of South Warren Street."

The walls carry the proof of a life well cut: rows of photographs, a portrait of Lincoln, and a Christmas tree that stays up all year. Governors, legislators, and Trenton mayors have all sat in these chairs. In 2016, even Reggie Jackson walked in off the street for a cut.

This is a cash-only shop with early morning hours and no appointment apps. You walk in, you get a proper cut or shave, and you leave looking the part. It has worked since the fifties, and it still does.

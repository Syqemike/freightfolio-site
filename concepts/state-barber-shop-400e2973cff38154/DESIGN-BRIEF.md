# Design Brief: State Barber Shop (Trenton, NJ)

## Business personality
Heritage shop since the 1950s. Owner Joe Festa, 85, the "Mayor of South Warren Street." 60th anniversary Jan 2021. Governors, legislators, Trenton mayors among clientele; Reggie Jackson walk-in 2016. Walls of photos, Lincoln portrait, year-round Christmas tree. Cash only, early-morning hours.

## Design direction
Vintage newspaper/masthead style: centered giant Barlow Condensed name, dateline "Since the 1950s", double rules, drop-cap story columns, framed services menu, sepia-toned facts strip. Palette: ink (#171310), aged paper (#f2e9d5), deep red (#8c2f2f), bronze. Barlow Condensed + Libre Baskerville.
Differentiated from Don's (dark mono/slab paper) and Presidential Grooming (charcoal/gold Jost) by the newspaper masthead structure and red-on-paper heritage register.

## Section order
Disclosure > centered masthead (h1, rating once) > double rules > nav > story (drop cap, 60th anniversary, governors, Reggie Jackson) + facts strip > framed services menu (cash only) > gallery > hours + visit card > footer.

## Imagery
5 stock photos (Pexels): B&W straight-razor shave (vintage register), elderly man getting trimmed, straight razor on towel, clippers close-ups x2. All labeled "Stock photo".

## Customer journey
Heritage story sells the shop's credibility, services menu is honest (cash only, no invented prices), hours and "Call to book" tel: card. No online booking exists (cash only shop), so no fake Book Now.

5.0/46 place-listing reviews. Address 116 South Warren St, Trenton NJ (ZIP unconfirmed, omitted). Phone +1 609-392-2211. Hours Wed-Fri 6 AM-6 PM, Sat 6 AM-5 PM, Sun-Tue closed. Cash only. Joe Festa story details per research. No invented facts.

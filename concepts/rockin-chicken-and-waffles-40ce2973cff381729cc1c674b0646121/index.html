About

Rockin Chicken and Waffles is a Southern diner in D'Iberville. The restaurant won Gold for Best Chicken and Waffles in both 2026 and 2025 and Silver for Best Fried Chicken in 2025 in the Sun Herald Best of Coastal Mississippi. Takeout and online ordering are available, and the restaurant does not take reservations.

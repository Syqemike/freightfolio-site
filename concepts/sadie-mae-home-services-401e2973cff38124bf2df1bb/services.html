Line-by-Line Detail

LINE 1 Carpentry Framing Repair

Damaged or worn framing gets repaired or replaced so the structure stays sound. Typical framing repair work covers:

  • Inspection of the damaged framing members before work starts
  • Repair or replacement of rot- or pest-damaged studs, plates, and blocking
  • Straight, plumb reassembly so the framing is ready for finish work

LINE 2 Interior Trim and Decorative Moldings

Trim is the finish line of a room. Work covers:

  • Baseboards, casings, crown molding, and chair rail
  • Replacement of damaged or outdated trim pieces
  • Caulking and finishing so joints sit clean against the wall
Call for a free estimate

LINE 3 Wood and Fiber-Cement Siding Repair

Storm wear, rot, and impact damage on siding gets addressed before it spreads. Work covers:

  • Removal of damaged wood or fiber-cement siding sections
  • Replacement with matching sections fitted to the existing wall
  • Sealing and prep so the repaired area is ready for paint
Call for a free estimate

LINE 4 Painting

Interior painting done with proper prep so the finish lasts. Work covers:

  • Surface prep: patching, sanding, and priming before paint
  • Clean cut-in lines at ceilings, trim, and corners
  • Even roller coverage on walls
Call for a free estimate

LINE 5 Ceilings

Ceiling repair and refinishing, from single-room fixes to full refinishes. Work covers:

  • Patching of cracks, holes, and water-stained sections
  • Texture matching where the ceiling has a finish to blend
  • Priming and painting to finish the repaired area
Call for a free estimate

LINE 6 Doors

Doors that stick, drag, or will not latch get repaired or replaced. Work covers:

  • Planing, shimming, and hardware adjustment for fit and swing
  • Replacement of damaged doors and hardware
  • Weatherstripping and threshold checks for exterior doors

LINE 7 Windows

Window repair and replacement work, fitted and sealed. Work covers:

  • Repair of damaged sashes, frames, and trim
  • Replacement of worn or broken windows
  • Caulking and sealing so the unit sits weather-tight

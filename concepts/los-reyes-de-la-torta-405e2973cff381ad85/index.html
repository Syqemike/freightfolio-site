Family-owned since January 2021

Los Reyes De La Torta is family-owned by Mariela and Javier Reyes. It opened January 30, 2021, and moved to its current Fairview Avenue location in April 2023. The menu centers on Mexico City-style tortas, including the Reyes and Queen varieties, with indoor seating and foosball.

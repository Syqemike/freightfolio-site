About

SoMa Animal Clinic and Housecalls is a small, fear-free veterinary clinic in downtown Little Rock, Arkansas, led by Dr. Ball. The clinic offers full service veterinary care in the office and at home, and it is accepting new patients.

Services

Annual and general exams

General exams and annual checkups for dogs and cats.

Vaccinations

Routine vaccinations as part of regular care.

Geriatric workups

Checkups and testing for older pets.

Microchipping

Microchip identification for pets.

Surgical services

Surgical care at the clinic.

Housecalls

In-home visits for pets that do better at home.

End-of-life care

In-home euthanasia and cremation support, handled with care.

Care at home, too

Housecalls bring veterinary care to your home for pets that travel poorly or do better in familiar surroundings. Call (501) 916-2246 to ask about a clinic visit or a housecall.

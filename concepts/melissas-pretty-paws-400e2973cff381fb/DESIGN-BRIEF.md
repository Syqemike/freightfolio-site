# Design Brief: Melissa's Pretty Paws (New Albany, IN)

## Business personality
Small-team personal dog groomer at 1615 E Market St, New Albany IN. "Loving quality dog grooming" positioning. Services: bathing, drying, brushing, styling/coat cuts to client specs for various breeds/sizes. Team: Melissa (owner), Sydney, James, Ricki, Megan (names only, no invented roles). Hours Tue-Sat 8:30 AM-5 PM, Sun/Mon closed (FB listing, single source; "call to confirm"). 5.0 from 89 Facebook reviews. Facebook page verified live via browser fetch (facebook.com/melissasprettypawsgrooming, professional account, 1700 followers) and linked. Phone single-sourced from FB. 6 labeled stock photos used.

## Creative direction
Warm, friendly, rounded. Cream and cocoa with coral accents and sage for trust. Soft rounded cards everywhere, centered section heads, team name chips. No illustrations; all decoration is borders and rounded shapes.

## Typography
Bree Serif (display) + Nunito Sans (body). Distinct from the bakery (Fraunces/Work Sans) and both barbershops.

## Palette
Cream #faf4ea, cream2 #f3e9d5, cocoa #4b3226, coral #e2703a, sage #87a08b.

## Layout
Disclosure bar, topbar wordmark + pill nav, friendly split hero with badge and rounded photo, rounded services cards, sage "team" band with name chips, rounded gallery grid, two-column hours (dark cocoa card with confirm flag) + visit card with FB link, footer disclaimer. One h1. All CTAs "Call to book" with tel:+18129446367. No prices listed (none verified). No online booking (none verified).

## Photo provenance
Pexels stock IDs: 6130997 (groomer styling Yorkie), 6131580 (blow-drying Yorkie), 1436139 (puppy bath), 39609542 (groomed poodle on grass), 6131003 (Yorkie haircut), 19145894 (trimming large dog). Every photo carries a "Stock photo" figcaption; footer discloses stock photos.

## Unverifiable facts and handling
Phone and hours single-sourced from the shop's own Facebook listing; hours flagged "call to confirm". ZIP unconfirmed, omitted. Team roles not invented: only Melissa is labeled Owner (verified); others are names only. No service promises or prices invented.

Gulf Shores Steamer is a coastal seafood restaurant on the marina in Orange Beach. The menu covers Royal Red Shrimp, Snow Crab Legs, crawfish, mussels, oysters, grilled fish, hand-cut steaks, grilled chicken, and the Island's Best Cheeseburger, plus combination platters for two, four or more. There is an outdoor patio overlooking the marina, a children's menu, and takeout.

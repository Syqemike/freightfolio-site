What Aztecas Restaurant & Cantina offers

Aztecas Restaurant and Cantina is an authentic Mexican cantina in Gautier. The menu includes tacos al pastor, birria tacos and a birria bowl, steak fajitas, chips and queso, quesadillas, and margarita flights. The cantina has live music and outdoor seating, and offers takeout and delivery.

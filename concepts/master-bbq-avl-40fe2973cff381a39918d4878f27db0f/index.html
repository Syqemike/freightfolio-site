About

Master BBQ AVL is a Filipino comfort food truck in Woodfin, North Carolina, voted Best of WNC 2026 Best Food Truck, first place. The truck's primary spot is 870 Riverside Drive at Zillicoah Beer Co in Woodfin, with a second spot at 13 Mystery Street at RAD Brew Co in Asheville. The menu is built on family recipes from Lola Rosa's kitchen since 1941, and the truck offers catering and DoorDash delivery. Hours at the Woodfin spot are Sunday 12:00 PM to 7:00 PM, Monday, Wednesday, and Thursday 3:00 PM to 8:00 PM, and Friday and Saturday 12:00 PM to 9:00 PM; Tuesday closed. Call (828) 808-5622 to ask where the truck is today.

Guncles Gluten Free is a dedicated gluten-free bakery in Mobile, the only dedicated gluten-free bakery in Southern Alabama per its own site. The bakery makes cinnamon rolls, cheddar herb buttermilk biscuits, oatmeal cream pies, bundt and layer cakes, cookies, breads, and a gluten-free king cake. The shop offers nationwide shipping and local on-demand delivery, and supplies 23 retail partners.

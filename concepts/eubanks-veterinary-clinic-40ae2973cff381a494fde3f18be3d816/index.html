About

Eubanks Veterinary Clinic has served North Little Rock pets since 1972. Dr. Rion Weiner has practiced at the clinic since 1995. The clinic offers wellness care, vaccinations, dental care, X-ray imaging, and microchipping, plus grooming and boarding, all from its 35th Street location.

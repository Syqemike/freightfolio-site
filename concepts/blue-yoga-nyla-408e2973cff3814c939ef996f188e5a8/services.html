What Blue Yoga Nyla offers

Blue Yoga Nyla is a yoga studio in North Little Rock, owned by Stacey Reynolds, a Licensed Yoga Therapist teaching since 2000. The studio offers all-levels classes, yoga therapy, a yoga school, chair classes, senior classes, mental-health and addiction-recovery programs, classes for children and teens, and corporate yoga. Drop-ins are 14 dollars, packages carry a 15 percent discount, and unlimited monthly auto-renew is 75 dollars with a 6-month contract.

Services

Locs are the specialty. Healthy hair is the point.

Deborah is a licensed cosmetologist with 32 years of experience, and she works with all hair textures. Every service starts with a conversation about what your hair needs.

Instant Locs

Start your loc journey in one appointment. Deborah works with your natural texture to form locs that are ready to grow from day one.

Loc Extensions

Add length and fullness to new or existing locs with extensions matched to your hair.

Loc Repair

Thin spots, breakage, and locs coming undone can be fixed. Bring damaged locs in and get an honest read on what they need.

Loc Maintenance

Regular retwists and upkeep that keep locs neat, strong, and growing. Good maintenance is what separates locs that last from locs that struggle.

Kinky Locs, Interloc, and Wic Locs

Specialized techniques for different loc looks and hair types, including kinky locs, the Interloc method, and Wic locs.

Hair Color and Highlights

Color and highlights done with loc health in mind, so the style looks good and the hair stays strong.

Cuts and Trims

Short natural cuts and trims for all hair types, shaped around how you actually wear your hair.

Natural Hair and Skin Care Products

Deborah makes her own natural hair and skin care products, and she teaches clients how to care for their hair between visits.

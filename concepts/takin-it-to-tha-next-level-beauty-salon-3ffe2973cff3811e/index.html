The loc specialist

Deborah Thompson Bey is a licensed cosmetologist who has spent 32 years working with hair. Her specialty is locs: starting them, repairing them, extending them, and keeping them healthy for every hair texture.

Clients come to her for instant locs, loc maintenance, color, and honest guidance about their hair. She makes her own natural hair and skin care products, and she takes the time to talk through what your hair needs. People describe the chair as a place where you feel like family.

The wedding lookbook

Bouquets and ceremony florals

Coastal Flowers And Design builds wedding flowers around your plans: bridal bouquets, ceremony pieces, and the small details in between, made by a full-service florist in Gulf Shores.

Bouquets

Made around you

Tell the shop your colors, your dress, and the shape you love, and they arrange a bridal bouquet to match. Bridesmaid bouquets, boutonnieres, and corsages follow the same palette so the whole wedding party reads as one design.

Bring photos, fabric swatches, or just a feeling. The designers work with what you bring them.

Ceremony

Where the day happens

Ceremony florals set the tone of the aisle: altar pieces, aisle markers, and arrangements that frame the moment you walk in. The shop can deliver and set up at your venue.

  • Altar and aisle arrangements
  • Entrance and reception accents
  • Local delivery and setup

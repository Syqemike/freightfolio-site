A studio built around the sitting, not the walk-in line.

Relentless Ink runs as a tattoo parlour and lounge, and it is appointment-only. That means your time in the chair is planned around you: a real consultation first, then a private setting for the session itself.

The studio handles custom tattoo work and piercings. Reviews say the artists listen and take time with each client. Prices are fair and the atmosphere is easy and unhurried.

How booking works

Call the shop to talk through your idea and set a consultation. Sessions are scheduled by appointment, so the artist's full attention is on your piece when you arrive.

## Aesthetic direction
A craftsman's atelier, not a mall jeweler. The shop's identity is making: wax molds, flask casting, hand-set stones. Design should feel like a workbench under warm light: warm ivory paper tones, deep espresso ink, aged brass accents. Refined and quiet. No neon, no gradients, no flashy luxury cliches.

## Typography
- Display serif: Georgia / "Iowan Old Style" / "Times New Roman" stack for headings. Classic, bookish, suits estate jewelry.
- Body: system sans (-apple-system, Segoe UI, Helvetica). Clean contrast.
- Eyebrow labels: small caps with letterspacing for section kickers.

## Palette
- Ivory/parchment background: #f6f1e6 (page)
- Deep espresso ink: #1c150e (text on light, dark section bg)
- Aged brass: #a9823f (accent, hairlines, CTAs on dark)
- Soft bronze line: #e2d5bc (dividers on light)
- Muted taupe: #8a7c64 (secondary text)

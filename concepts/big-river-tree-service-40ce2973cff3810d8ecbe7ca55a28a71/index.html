About

Big River Tree Service is a tree service based in Davenport, serving the Quad Cities within about an hour of Davenport, including Bettendorf, Eldridge, LeClaire, Muscatine, Clinton, Rock Island, Moline, and East Moline. The company started after the 2020 derecho and opened for good in April 2021. Work is insured for residential and commercial jobs, estimates are free, and most requests are looked at within 48 hours.

About

Michelle Miller

Michelle Miller owns Simply Anointed Massage Therapy and has practiced massage for more than 15 years.

Her approach is personal. Every session starts with what you are carrying in the door, and the work is shaped around that. Deep tissue for stubborn tension. Gentle prenatal care. Warm stones for muscles that will not soften on their own. Targeted work for athletes and active bodies.

The studio sits at 951 NW Broad St in Murfreesboro, a quiet room set up for one thing: helping you slow down, release tension, and feel renewed through the healing power of touch.

Michelle's work has been featured in a Nashville Voyager interview.

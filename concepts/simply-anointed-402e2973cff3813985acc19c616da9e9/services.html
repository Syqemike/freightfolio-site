Services

Massage, the way your body asks for it.

Four specialties, each with its own character. If you are not sure which one fits, book the session and talk it through with Michelle. The work is always tailored to you.

Deep tissue massageTherapeutic

Slower strokes and focused pressure that reach past the surface, into the deeper layers of muscle and connective tissue. This is the style most people ask for when tension has settled in and will not move on its own.

Prenatal massageGentle

Careful, supportive bodywork for mothers to be. Positioning and pressure are adjusted for comfort and safety at every stage, so you can rest and let the work happen.

Hot stone massageWarmth

Smooth stones, warmed and placed along the body, combined with massage strokes. The heat loosens tight muscle and lets the therapist work deeper with less effort.

Sports massageActive

Targeted work for athletes and anyone who trains hard. It is built around movement: easing overworked areas, supporting recovery, and keeping the body ready for what is next.

Why people come in

Real reasons, real relief.

  • Sports injuries and recovery
  • Circulation
  • Diabetes related discomfort
  • Stress and everyday tension

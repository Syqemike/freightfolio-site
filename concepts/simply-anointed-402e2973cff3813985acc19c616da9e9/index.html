Treatments

Four ways to be cared for.

Massage is the whole practice here. Choose the style that fits what your body is asking for, or talk it through with Michelle before your session.

Deep tissue massageTherapeutic

Slower, focused pressure that works into deeper layers of muscle and connective tissue.

 Prenatal massageGentle

Supportive, careful bodywork designed for mothers to be.

 Hot stone massageWarmth

Smooth, warmed stones used with massage to coax tight muscles into letting go.

 Sports massageActive

Targeted work for athletes and active bodies, before and after the hard effort.

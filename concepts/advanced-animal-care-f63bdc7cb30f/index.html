Our approach

A clinic that feels like home, on purpose

Pets notice everything. The smells, the sounds, the way they are handled. Advanced Animal Care built its name around one simple promise: give every patient a homelike environment, and give every client the same courtesy.

That idea shapes the whole visit. Calm handling, plain talk about what your pet needs, and a team that treats cats, dogs, and small animals as individuals rather than appointments. It is veterinary care at a human pace, in Foley, from people who chose this work because animals matter to them.

A homelike environment is not decor. It is how your pet is handled, how you are spoken to, and how your visit unfolds.

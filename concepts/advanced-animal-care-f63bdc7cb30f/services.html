Services

Plain, complete veterinary care

Five service lines, each one done carefully. The clinic serves cats, dogs, and small animals at 625 E Laurel Ave in Foley. Call ahead and the team will tell you what a visit involves and what to bring.

  • 01Diagnostics & X-Rays

    In-house diagnostics and x-rays, so the vet can see what is going on and explain it to you in the same visit. No referral runaround for the basics.

  • 02Spay & Neuter

    Routine spaying and neutering with careful monitoring through recovery. Ask about scheduling when you call.

  • 03Vaccinations

    Core vaccinations for puppies, kittens, and adults, on a schedule the vet sets for your pet's age and lifestyle.

  • 04Surgeries

    Surgical care for common procedures, planned with you in advance. The team will walk you through preparation and aftercare.

  • 05Microchipping

    A permanent form of identification for your pet, placed quickly during a regular visit.

Questions? Call (251) 943-7507. A real person at the clinic can tell you which service fits your pet's situation.

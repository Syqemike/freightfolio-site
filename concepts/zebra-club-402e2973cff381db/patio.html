The Patio

A tropical escape off Dauphin Island Pkwy

The patio is the Zebra Club’s calling card: an outdoor tropical space with room to spread out, strung with lights after dark.

Daylight under the green

Afternoons on the patio are the slow kind: a cold drink, shade from the tropical plantings, and no reason to hurry. It is the easy answer to a long Mobile day.

The patio is the reason first-timers become regulars.

After dark, under the lights

When the sun drops, the patio strings up and the night moves outside. Live music drifts out from the bar, and the dancing starts where the floor feels like it.

Benefit events and gatherings use the patio too. If you are planning one, call the bar and talk it through.

Same-day delivery in Colorado Springs is usually possible for orders placed by 10 in the morning on a weekday; for weekend delivery, order by 3 on Friday afternoon. Deliveries run from 9 AM to 5 PM daily, including to area hospitals. The shop also delivers to local funeral homes and cemeteries.

A woman-owned florist in downtown Colorado Springs.

Springs In Bloom is a woman-owned and operated florist in downtown Colorado Springs. The shop carries anniversary and birthday flowers, funeral and sympathy flowers, succulents and plants, orchids and lilies, seasonal and holiday arrangements, and gift baskets. It offers same-day delivery in Colorado Springs, works with 1-800-Flowers and Teleflora wire services, and ships nationwide.

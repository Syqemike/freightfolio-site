Occasions and delivery

Everything the shop makes is arranged to order in the downtown Colorado Springs shop. Call to talk through the occasion and the shop will put the arrangement together.

Call to order: +1 719-596-4806

What the shop makes

Flowers for the occasions the shop is known for, and for an ordinary day.

  1. 01

    Anniversary and birthday flowers

    Made to order for anniversaries and birthdays, and available for same-day delivery in Colorado Springs when ordered by 10 in the morning on a weekday.

  2. 02

    Funeral and sympathy flowers

    Funeral arrangements and sympathy flowers, delivered to local funeral homes and cemeteries.

  3. 03

    Succulents and plants

    Succulents and potted plants for the home, alongside cut flowers.

  4. 04

    Orchids and lilies

    Orchids and lilies from the everyday range, for the home or as a gift.

  5. 05

    Seasonal and holiday arrangements

    Arrangements built around the season, with spring, summer, fall, and winter flowers, plus centerpieces for Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, and the holidays.

  6. 06

    Gift baskets

    Gift baskets, including get well baskets, made up with flowers for delivery.

  7. 07

    Same-day delivery in Colorado Springs

    Order by 10 in the morning in the recipient's time zone and the shop can usually deliver the same day, Monday through Friday. For delivery on the weekend, order by 3 on Friday afternoon. Deliveries run from 9 AM to 5 PM daily. The shop delivers to area hospitals.

  8. 08

    Nationwide shipping

    The shop works with 1-800-Flowers and Teleflora wire services, so orders can be sent outside Colorado Springs too.

Good to know before you order

The shop is closed on New Year's Day, Easter, Memorial Day, Father's Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas. Email changes or cancellations are not accepted; call the shop to make a change. The shop cannot deliver to a P.O. Box or an A.P.O. address.

The shop delivers across Colorado Springs, including ZIP codes 80903, 80904, 80905, 80906, 80907, 80909, 80910, 80911, 80917, 80918, 80919, 80920, 80923, and 80829.

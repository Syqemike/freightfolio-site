# DESIGN BRIEF — Senses Nails (Gulf Shores, AL)

## Aesthetic
Coastal minimalist: airy, calm, lots of white space. The page should feel like
a quiet morning at the beach, not a busy salon. Restrained use of color, thin
rules, one accent at a time. Must look distinct from the other batch-28 nail
salon directions (editorial plum, coastal bright teal, forest-green classic,
spa zen, playful retro, bold dark glam). This one is bluer and calmer than
Posh's playful coastal teal, closer to a clean resort aesthetic.

## Typography
- Display: DM Serif Display (headlines, name). Calm authority, editorial but
  light.
- Body: Lato (paragraphs, nav, captions). Clean, readable, unobtrusive.

## Palette
- White `#ffffff` — base
- Ocean blue `#0369a1` — headings accents, buttons, footer
- Soft coral `#fb7185` — single accent (eyebrow labels, hairline accents, small
  highlights). Used sparingly.
- Light gray-blue `#eef4f8` / `#f4f8fb` — alternating section backgrounds
- Ink `#1e293b` — body text
- No gradients. No glowing blobs. No decorative shadows.

## Imagery plan (all Pexels stock, labeled "Stock photo" in captions)
- hero.jpg — index hero right panel (airy polish application)
- manicure.jpg — index service row: gel extensions and acrylics; services page
- polish.jpg — index service row: dip powder; services page
- pedicure.jpg — index service row: pedicures and spa pedicures; services page
- spa.jpg — visit page side image; gallery
- detail.jpg — gallery
No real photos of the business exist (directory stubs only; no GBP, Yelp,
Facebook, or Instagram photos found). Stock is used as neutral nail-care
imagery, never presented as the business's own work.

## Layout
- Sticky minimal white nav: wordmark left, section links, coral-accented
  "Call to book" button.
- Index: split-screen hero (type left, photo right), "Design Preview" badge
  near the top, airy alternating service rows with generous whitespace, quiet
  hours/address band, ocean-blue footer.
- Services: one airy row per service, image alternating with text, no prices.
- Gallery: wide, calm grid with visible breathing room, "Stock photo" captions.
- Visit: two-column info layout (hours, address, phone, directions link).

## Customer journey
A Gulf Shores visitor or local opens the page, sees the name, services, and
rating immediately, scrolls calmly through services, and ends at the visit page
where the address, exact hours (Mon-Sat 9:30 AM - 6:30 PM; Sunday not listed),
and a "Call to book" button (tel:+12519438028) are one tap away. No online
booking is offered by the business, so no booking button anywhere.

## Copy constraints honored
No em dashes. No banned AI phrases. No invented testimonials, prices, awards,
or credentials. Services only from research: pedicures, spa pedicures, dip
powder nails, gel nail extensions, acrylic nails. Rating shown once as
"4.5 from 35 Google reviews". No social links (none verified).

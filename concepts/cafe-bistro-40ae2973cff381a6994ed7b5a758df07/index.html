About

Cafe Bistro is a coastal cafe, bar and restaurant at 39 Via de Luna Dr on Pensacola Beach. The menu covers breakfast, lunch and dinner, from fluffy pancakes, eggs benedict, chicken and waffles, and shrimp and grits to burgers, Philly cheesesteaks, salads, handmade pizzas, gourmet pasta, and Mediterranean plates. The bar serves signature cocktails, with happy hour from 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM, and the dessert case holds 24 ice cream flavors.

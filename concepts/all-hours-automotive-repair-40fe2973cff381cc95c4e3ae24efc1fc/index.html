About

All Hours Automotive Repair is a full-service auto repair shop in Columbus, Ohio, at 2386 Wood Avenue. Owner Justin Rothenhauser leads a team of ASE-certified technicians. The shop handles maintenance and repairs, entertainment systems, custom work, engine and transmission repair, and electrical diagnostics, with flexible scheduling. Hours are Monday to Wednesday 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM and Thursday 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM. Friday, Saturday, and Sunday hours are not published, so call (614) 419-2395 to ask.

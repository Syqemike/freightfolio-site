About

HoQ Restaurant is a farm-to-table New American restaurant at 303 E 5th Street in Des Moines' East Village. About 90 percent of its ingredients come from local farms, and the menu changes almost daily, so no fixed dish list is printed here. The restaurant offers reservations, takeout, and outdoor seating, with vegetarian and gluten-free options.

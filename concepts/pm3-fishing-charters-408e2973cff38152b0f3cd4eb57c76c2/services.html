Trips and rates

PM3 Fishing Charters runs Pensacola and Navarre inshore light-tackle charters for redfish and speckled trout, with Capt. Shawn Mistovich, owner and operator, aboard an 18-foot Beavertail Vengeance skiff. Tackle, bait, gear, and license are included.

Inshore light-tackle charters

Pensacola and Navarre charters for redfish and speckled trout.

The boat

An 18-foot Beavertail Vengeance skiff, with Capt. Shawn Mistovich, owner and operator.

Included in every trip

Tackle, bait, gear, and license are included.

Rates

400 dollars and 600 dollars for 1 to 2 anglers, with a 100 dollar deposit. Booking by phone.

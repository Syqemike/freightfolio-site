Trips and rates.

PM3 Fishing Charters runs inshore light-tackle charters in Pensacola and Navarre waters for redfish and speckled trout. Capt. Shawn Mistovich, owner and operator, runs each trip aboard an 18-foot Beavertail Vengeance skiff.

Rates

Rate one

$400
For 1 to 2 anglers.

Rate two

$600
For 1 to 2 anglers.

Deposit

A $100 deposit is part of booking.

Call to book and to confirm which rate applies to the trip you want.

Included in every trip

Tackle, bait, gear, and license are included. Booking is by phone.

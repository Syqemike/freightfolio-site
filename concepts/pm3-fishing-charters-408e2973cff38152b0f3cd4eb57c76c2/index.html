Inshore light-tackle charters out of Pensacola and Navarre.

PM3 Fishing Charters runs Pensacola and Navarre inshore light-tackle charters for redfish and speckled trout, with Capt. Shawn Mistovich, owner and operator, aboard an 18-foot Beavertail Vengeance skiff. Tackle, bait, gear, and license are included. Booking is by phone.

The trip, in the log

Where

Pensacola and Navarre inshore waters.

What you fish for

Redfish and speckled trout, on light tackle.

The boat

An 18-foot Beavertail Vengeance skiff.

The captain

Capt. Shawn Mistovich, owner and operator.

Included

Tackle, bait, gear, and license are included in every trip.

Rates

PM3 publishes two rates for 1 to 2 anglers: $400 and $600. A $100 deposit is part of booking. Booking is by phone.

Call to book: (850) 232-7333

# DESIGN BRIEF: Crystal Nails (Dundalk MD): Website Concept

## Design direction (batch-28 assigned)
EDITORIAL LUXURY, magazine style. Must look distinct from other batch-28 nail salons: deep plum + champagne on warm ivory, Playfair Display + Inter, asymmetric editorial layouts, eyebrows, hairline dividers, pull-quote rating callout. NOT a 3-column card grid. NO black-and-gold, NO gradients, NO glowing blobs, NO pill buttons.

## Aesthetic
A fashion-magazine spread for a neighborhood nail salon. Generous white space, thin hairline rules separating editorial sections, large italic serif pull quotes, small-caps eyebrows in champagne-on-plum. Quiet, confident, feminine luxury.

## Typography
- Display: Playfair Display (Google Fonts), used for oversized headlines, the masthead, pull quotes, and page titles.
- Body: Inter (Google Fonts), used for nav, paragraphs, captions, and fine print.
- No other fonts.

## Palette
- Warm ivory #faf6ef (page background)
- Deep plum #3d1f3d (text accents, masthead bar, eyebrows on light, section fills)
- Champagne #e8d5a8 (eyebrows on dark, rules accents, callout backgrounds)
- Supporting: plum-ink #2a142a for body text (near-plum for contrast), hairline rgba(61,31,61,.18)

## Layout (magazine grammar, shared across pages)
- Slim top disclosure ribbon (plum): "Design Preview" notice with link to Freightfolio.
- Masthead header: small-caps eyebrow "Dundalk, Maryland" above the Crystal Nails serif wordmark; simple text nav (Home, Services, Gallery, Visit) plus a text "Call to book" link, all separated by hairline rules. No pill buttons.
- Hero (home): asymmetric two-column spread. Left column: eyebrow "Nail Salon on Holabird Ave", oversized Playfair headline "A beautiful set, from careful hands.", short paragraph, hairline, call links. Right column: collage of 2 photos at different sizes with offset margins and a small captioned credit line.
- Section rhythm: small-caps eyebrow, Playfair section title, hairline divider, then an asymmetric two-column or numbered editorial list. Never a 3-up card grid.
- Rating: shown exactly ONCE (home page) as a pull-quote callout: "4.5 from 34 Google reviews."
- Visit details: address, phone, hours table, directions link. Sunday line reads "Sunday: hours not posted."
- Footer: hairline, business name, disclosure, contact line, "Concept built by Freightfolio."

## Customer journey
1. Land: see the salon's name, neighborhood, and phone in the masthead; read the editorial hero; see the rating pull quote.
2. Browse: numbered service list invites a read of the Services page for detail on acrylics, manicures, pedicures, facials, waxing.
3. Gallery: browse nail-style photography with honest stock captions.
4. Visit: find hours, address, directions, and the "Call to book" phone CTA. No online booking is offered, so no "Book Now" button exists anywhere.

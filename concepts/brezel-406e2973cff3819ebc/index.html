A Bavarian-style pretzel shop

Brezel is a Bavarian-style soft pretzel shop at the North Market in Columbus. Its pretzels include Original, Cinnamon Sugar, Everything, Jalapeno Cheddar, Spinach Asiago, and Margherita Pizza, with dips of beer cheese, garlic herb butter, and spicy brown mustard. The own site is an Instagram-feed style page with no menu, prices, or online ordering, so call with questions.

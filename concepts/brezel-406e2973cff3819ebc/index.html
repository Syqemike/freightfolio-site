Brezel is a Bavarian-style soft pretzel shop at the North Market in Columbus. Call to ask about Original, Cinnamon Sugar, Everything, Jalapeno Cheddar, Spinach Asiago, or Margherita Pizza pretzels, plus dips.

A Bavarian-style pretzel shop

Brezel's pretzels are hand-rolled in the Bavarian style. The shop's own site is an Instagram-feed style page with no menu, prices, or online ordering, so call with questions about flavors and dips.

Menu

  • Original pretzelThe classic Bavarian-style pretzel, salted.
  • Cinnamon Sugar pretzelRolled in cinnamon sugar.
  • Everything pretzelTopped with sesame seeds, poppy seeds, caraway seeds, garlic, onion, and salt.
  • Jalapeno Cheddar pretzelChopped jalapenos baked in, topped with shredded cheddar.
  • Spinach Asiago pretzelFresh spinach and garlic in the dough, topped with Asiago cheese.
  • Margherita Pizza pretzelFresh basil and garlic in the dough, topped with mozzarella.
  • DipsBeer cheese, garlic herb butter, and spicy brown mustard.

About

MOXIE coffee and espresso is a women-owned specialty coffee shop in Old Metairie, next to Glow Luxury Salon under the same owners. The shop pours espresso drinks, lattes, cold brew, and chai lattes, plus a matcha latte made with house-made ube vanilla syrup and the signature Iced Moxie. Kouign amann pastries round out the counter. Seating includes outdoor tables, the shop is pet-friendly, and free Wi-Fi and grab-and-go options are available.

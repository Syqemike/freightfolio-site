A downtown kitchen with Lao roots.

Ban Moon brings authentic Lao and Thai cooking to West Avenue N in San Angelo. It is a casual spot where the food does the talking: bold chilies, fresh herbs, and recipes from two cuisines.

The restaurant is wheelchair accessible, and the dining room keeps things simple so the kitchen can focus on the plate in front of you.

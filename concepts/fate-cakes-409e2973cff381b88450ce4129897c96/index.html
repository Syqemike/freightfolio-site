About

Fate Cakes is a woman-owned, one-woman custom cake bakery at 1360 Cherry Bottom Road in Gahanna, run by owner Marie Johns. Founded in 2011 as a home bakery, it has run its storefront since 2019, and it delivers across central Columbus.

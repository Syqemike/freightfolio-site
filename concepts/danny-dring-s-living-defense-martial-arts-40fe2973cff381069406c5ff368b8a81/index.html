About

Danny Dring's Living Defense Martial Arts is a martial arts school in Sherwood, Arkansas, at 308 East Kiehl Avenue, Suite A-4, in the same location about 25 to 40 years. Master Danny Dring is a Pulaski County reserve deputy. The school teaches Taekwondo, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, boxing, kickboxing, MMA, and Weeping Style Jujutsu for adults and children, with Little Dragons classes for ages 3 to 5, youth classes for ages 5 to 12, fitness bootcamps, and concealed carry pistol classes. The first class is free. Call (501) 834-3537 to ask about a class time.

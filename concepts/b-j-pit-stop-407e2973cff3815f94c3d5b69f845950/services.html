Services

B and J Pit Stop is a full-service auto repair shop in Bay St Louis, in business 19 years per its own site, on the corner of Bouslog Street and Highway 90, across from the US Post Office. Services include oil changes, tune-ups, brake service, tire rotation, and belts and hoses, plus Mississippi state vehicle inspections and tint stickers.

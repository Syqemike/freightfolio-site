A bakery built around dietary needs

Dempsey Bakery is a dedicated gluten-free bakery and cafe in downtown Little Rock. The bakery is also soy free and nut free, and many items have vegan options. Offerings named in research include cupcakes, cookies, sandwiches, pies, birthday cakes, and a coffee bar. Online ordering is coming soon per the own site, and no items are listed online yet, so phone orders only for now. No prices were published in public listings, so call with questions.

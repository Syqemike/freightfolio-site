# Design Brief: Fetch on Franklin (pet grooming, Evansville, IN)

## Research facts (only facts used)
- Fetch on Franklin, 1732 W Franklin St, Evansville, IN 47712; +1 812-303-3066
- Hours: Mon 8:00 AM-3:00 PM; Tue-Wed 8:00 AM-5:00 PM; Thu 8:00 AM-3:00 PM; Fri 8:00 AM-4:00 PM; Sat-Sun Closed
- Full-service pet grooming for cats and dogs: bathing, haircuts, nail trimming, ear cleaning, de-shedding, teeth cleaning, flea/tick prevention; add-ons aromatherapy and massage
- Head groomer: Lindsey Fulton
- Published pricing: full groom Small $55+, Medium $55-75+, Large $75-100+, XL $100+; bath and brush Small $25+, Medium $35+, Large $40+, XL $45+; flea treatment $10
- Rating: 4.9 from 81 Google reviews. No socials, no public photos.

## Design direction
"Honest price-board": warm forest green + cream, pricing as the hero element (chalkboard menu-board panel with big tabular mono numerals), apothecary-calm layout, Lindsey Fulton introduced as head groomer. No em dashes, no invented facts, no urgency.

## Typography
- Display: Fraunces (72pt optical, soft serif with character)
- Body: Karla (clean, warm grotesque)
- Prices/labels: IBM Plex Mono (tabular numerals for the board)

## Palette
- Forest #1E3B2C (board, footer), Deep ink #122419, Cream #F7F2E7 (page), Warm gold #C9A24B (accents/rules)

## Layout / customer journey
Disclosure bar > name header + sticky nav > hero (headline + photo + CTAs + rating) > PRICE BOARD (chalkboard, the centerpiece) > services list > Lindsey section > photo gallery > hours + visit > footer. Customer journey: see honest prices first, confirm services, meet the groomer, call to book.

## Copy notes

- Rating appears exactly once (hero); footer cites the source only.
- Hours written with "to" (no dashes). Prices exactly as published.

Vestige

A tasting-menu restaurant in a cozy historic house in downtown Ocean Springs, from owner chefs Alex Perry and Kumi Omori.

About

Vestige is a small, locally owned tasting-menu restaurant in a cozy historic house in downtown Ocean Springs. Owner chefs Alex Perry and Kumi Omori build an ever-changing, chef's choice, multi-course tasting menu from the seasons, preservation, and what sounds tasty each day, with sustainable seafood, premium proteins, and locally grown food. The restaurant is Michelin recommended and multi James Beard nominated, and reservations are required.

Rated 4.8 from 160 Google reviews.

Services

Chef's Choice tasting menu

An ever-changing, chef's choice, multi-course tasting menu; the seasonal five-course prix fixe tasting menu is $105 per person.

Seasonal dishes

Dishes from the changing menu such as sakura-cured wagyu nigiri, Gulf red snapper, foie gras tartlet with gooseberry jam, beef cheek hobamiso, Louisiana strawberry mousse, and tournedos rossini.

Sustainable seafood and local food

Fresh seasonal preparation with sustainable seafood, premium quality proteins, and locally grown food.

Wine and cocktails

A full bar with wine and cocktails alongside the tasting menu.

An intimate room

A cozy historic house converted into a small restaurant, serving a maximum group size of 4 at this time.

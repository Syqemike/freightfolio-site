What Diamond Doghouse offers

Diamond Doghouse is a doggie day care, boarding, baths, and nails business in Richmond in an open-cage environment, opened August 20, 2006, by owner Dodie Lynch. It also carries its own line of collars and leashes. Boarding drop-off and pick-up on Sundays is listed separately from weekday hours, and Saturday hours vary across sources, so call to confirm weekend times.

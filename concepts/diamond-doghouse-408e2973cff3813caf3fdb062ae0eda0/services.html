Services

Every service below comes from the shop itself. Prices are the prices listed on the shop's own site. To schedule, call the office at +1 804-254-4101. There is no online booking form.

Doggie day care

Full day $28. Half day $18. 10-day package $250. 20-day package $450.

Half days run 7 AM to 12 PM or 1 PM to 6 PM. Dogs spend the day in the open room and out in the fenced yard, grouped by size, with outdoor play all day.

Day care dogs must be spayed or neutered at 6 months. Vaccinations are required: Rabies, Distemper and Parvo, and Bordetella every 6 months.

Boarding

$50 a night per dog. 10% multi-dog discount.

Boarding includes a full day of day care, and a bath before the dog goes home. Pickup after 6 PM costs $10 for every 15 minutes late.

New boarding dogs get a temperament test first, and the shop asks new clients to fill out a Client Information Sheet. Dogs must be social and non-aggressive.

Baths

Call to ask about bath prices.

Baths are offered with day care and boarding. Every boarding stay ends with a bath before the dog goes home. No bath prices are published on the shop's site.

Nails

Call to ask about nail prices.

Nail care is offered with day care and boarding. No nail prices are published on the shop's site.

Collars and leashes

In the shop.

The shop carries its own line of collars and leashes, sold in the shop.

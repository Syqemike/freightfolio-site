01

The Studio

Custom ink, gallery roots.

77 Art Collective LLC is a custom tattoo studio in downtown Indianapolis. Around town it is also known as Studio 77 Tattoos.

It is a Black-owned studio with real ties to the city's hip-hop and music scene. The room has more of a gallery feel than a typical shop. Art on the walls, music in the air, and artists who take the work seriously.

Custom pieces, flash, and cover-ups. Bring your idea or browse the flash wall. Every session starts with a conversation.

Artist Spotlight

Featured artist

Brian Winston

Brian Winston is one of the artists at 77 Art Collective. He works out of the downtown Indianapolis shop.

03

Collective Ethos

  • A1

    Custom first

    The shop's name is 77 Art Collective for a reason. Pieces are drawn up for the person in the chair, not pulled off a shelf.

  • A2

    Conversation before the needle

    Good tattoos come from good communication. You talk it through first: design, placement, size. No surprises.

  • A3

    A room that feels like Indy

    Downtown Indianapolis, Black-owned, connected to the city's music scene. The shop carries that energy into the work.

  • A4

    Cover-ups welcome

    Old work you do not love anymore is fair game. Bring it in and talk through what a cover-up could look like.

## Creative direction
Warm Southern celebration cakes. Buttercream/blush (#f9ece1) + deep chocolate (#3a2718) + gold accent (#c69a2e). NOT the terracotta/Southwestern look of the Bosque site. Playfair Display refined serif headlines + Pinyon Script celebration accents + Jost clean sans. Centered joyful compositions: script eyebrow hero with framed caramel-cake photo and rotating "famous caramel cake" seal, dark signature-cake feature panel, 11-flavor grid, occasion cards, gold-accent order band (pickup/delivery/takeout-only), visit cards. Rounded shapes and gold hairlines throughout.

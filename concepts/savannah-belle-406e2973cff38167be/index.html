A lifestyle boutique since 2009

Savannah Belle is a lifestyle boutique in downtown Savannah, in business since 2009. Three former Bay Street shops, Savannah Rose, Savannah Belle, and Matter of Factors, consolidated to Broughton Street as Savannah Belle. Products include women's clothing, jewelry, shoes, hats, gifts, and art and home decor. Personalized travel advisory services are also offered. Call to ask about an item.

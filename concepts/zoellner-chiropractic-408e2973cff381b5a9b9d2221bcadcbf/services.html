What Zoellner Chiropractic offers

Zoellner Chiropractic is a Tulsa chiropractic clinic of Dr. Charles Zoellner, DC, welcoming new patients with a free consultation. Care covers traditional chiropractic techniques, physical therapy modalities, advanced neuromusculoskeletal treatments, chiropractic manipulation, Active Release Technique (ART), exercises and stretching, acupuncture, interferential current therapy, rehabilitative conditioning, biomechanical analysis, and auto accident care. The clinic works with patients' existing medical professionals. The clinic states it accepts most insurance programs, including Blue Cross and Blue Shield, Aetna, Medicare, workers compensation, and personal injury, per its own new-patients page.

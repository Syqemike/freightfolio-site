Since 2010

A Canal District shop with a following

Owner Michaelangelo Mangual opened his first shop in 2010 and moved into the Crompton building in Worcester’s Canal District, directly across from Smokestack BBQ. The shop’s own words: traditional barbering with modern artistry, where the haircuts are never rushed.

Walk-ins are welcome, and appointments are also taken. Call (508) 209-8800 to set up a visit.

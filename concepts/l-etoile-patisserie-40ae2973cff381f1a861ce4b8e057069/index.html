About

L'Etoile Patisserie is a French bakery and cafe on Meridian Street North in Huntsville, open since 2021 and run by Chef Kevin Zurmuehlen and his wife Kristen. The shop is veteran-owned and bakes croissants, kouign-amann, pain au chocolat, baguettes, choux a la creme, and quiche, with espresso, retail and wholesale sales, and gluten-free and vegan options.

About

Pain Relief Massage and Wellness is a massage therapy practice in Rochester, New York, at 150 Linden Oaks, Suite A, run by Shawn Belles, LMT. The practice offers pain relief massage for neck and shoulder pain and low back pain, myofascial release, and MediCupping therapy. Hours are Monday and Tuesday 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM, Wednesday 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Thursday 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM, Friday and Saturday closed, and Sunday 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

# Design Brief: Charleston Bread (Charleston, WV)

## Business personality
Downtown artisan bakery on Capitol Street, Charleston WV. Old-school community feel, about 10 local employees, takeout and pickup only. Verified facts: artisan breads (Charleston sourdough, bagels, brioche, baguette, challah, ciabatta, cinnamon raisin swirl, multigrain, olive bread; most loaves vegan), laminated pastries (croissants, raspberry cheese Danish, almond Danish), cookies, scones, brownies, almond torte, fresh coffee. Daily-rotating menu. Call-ahead orders accepted; large pastry or cake orders need two weeks notice (from the shop's own Facebook description). Hours Tue-Fri 7 AM-6 PM, Sat 7 AM-3 PM, Sun/Mon closed. Own small free parking lot, wheelchair accessible. 5.0 from 11 places reviews. No real photos available; 6 labeled stock photos used.

## Creative direction
Editorial, food-first, menu-led. Warm cream background, deep forest green, terracotta accents. The menu is the hero section, styled like a printed bakery menu with category columns and a call-ahead ordering note. Rounded-arch hero photo (sourdough) against a split editorial layout.

## Typography
Fraunces (display serif, italic accents) + Work Sans (body). Distinct from Don's (Bitter/Plex Mono) and Presidential (Cormorant/Jost).

## Palette
Cream #faf5ea, forest #33502e, forest deep #22391f, terracotta #c4622d.

## Layout
Disclosure bar, masthead wordmark + tagline + nav, asymmetric hero (copy left, arch-framed photo right), two-column printed menu, dark forest gallery band, hours card + visit list two-column, footer disclaimer. One h1. CTAs are all "Call ahead to order" with tel:+13047203022. No social links (Facebook page is an auto-generated category page, not the business's own page, so it was omitted).

## Photo provenance
Pexels stock IDs: 8633662 (sourdough), 1387075 (baguettes), 2253636 (bakery shelves), 30926962 (kneading dough), 13425794 (croissants), 7405059 (display case). Every photo carries a "Stock photo" figcaption; footer discloses stock photos.

## Unverifiable facts and handling
Rating 5.0/11 is from a single places listing source, shown once with exact source. Menu items are all from verified research; no prices invented. Logo/brand colors unknown, so a type-driven wordmark was used instead of inventing a logo.

Downtown Charleston artisan bakery

Baked fresh in the morning. Gone by the afternoon.

Charleston Bread is an artisan bakery on Capitol Street specializing in artisan bread, laminated pastries, and cookies, all made from scratch. Call ahead to reserve your loaves, or stop by the counter for whatever is warm out of the oven.

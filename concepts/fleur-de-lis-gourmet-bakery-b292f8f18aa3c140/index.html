02 · Cakes

Cakes for Every Occasion

Custom cakes, wedding cakes, and special-event cakes, designed and baked to order. Call ahead so the bakery can plan your date.

Custom and Wedding Cakes

Made-to-order cakes for weddings, anniversaries, and celebrations. Bring your ideas and the bakers will shape the design around them. To start an order, call (228) 896-0005.

King Cakes and Cheesecakes

King cakes in praline pecan and strawberry cream cheese, plus cheesecakes in Chantilly, cookie butter, peach cobbler, and black and white. Seasonal favorites sell out, so ordering early is smart.

03 · The Cafe

Stay for Coffee

A quiet table, a proper espresso

The bakery doubles as a dine-in cafe with tables for lingering. Order a coffee or espresso drink and enjoy a pastry on the spot.

It is an easy stop on Courthouse Road, and a calm place to start the morning or take an afternoon break.

04 · Our Story

A Gulf Coast Patisserie

Fleur de Lis Gourmet Bakery opened in the fall of 2010, founded by Jason Brewer and Allen Weger. The fleur-de-lis emblem reflects both the French baking tradition and Gulf Coast pride.

More than a decade later, the bakery still bakes everything by hand, from morning croissants to wedding cakes, for the community that made it a local favorite.

Jason Brewer and Allen Weger, founders

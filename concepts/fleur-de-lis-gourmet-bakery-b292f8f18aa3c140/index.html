03 · Our Story

A Gulf Coast patisserie

Fleur de Lis Gourmet Bakery opened in the fall of 2010, founded by Jason Brewer and Allen Weger. The fleur-de-lis emblem is part of the bakery's European-inspired identity.

The bakery still bakes everything by hand, from morning croissants to wedding cakes, for the customers who come through the door.

Jason Brewer and Allen Weger, founders

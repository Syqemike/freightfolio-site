A small-animal clinic in Knoxville

Riverside Veterinary Clinic is a small-animal veterinary clinic at 1111 Sevier Ave in Knoxville, owned by Dr. Leah Wulforst. The clinic cares for dogs and cats. Services include wellness care, vaccinations, spay and neuter, dental cleaning, microchipping, deworming, FIV and FeLV testing, and microscopy for ear mite detection, skin scraping, and cytology.

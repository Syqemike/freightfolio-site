The Truck

A Family Lot in Laveen

Comiendo Con memo is a family-owned taco truck parked at 3112 W. Dobbins Rd. in Laveen. Nights here run on mesquite fire, outdoor tables, string lights and a drive-thru lane for quick pickups.

  • Family owned. Memo and family run the grill and the lot themselves.
  • First come, first served. Walk up, drive thru, or grab a table.
  • Keto friendly. Cheese-shell tacos for low-carb eaters.
  • Delivery. Also listed on DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub.

The truck’s flame-lettered banner in Laveen.

Fresh toppings ready for your tacos.

Made Fresh, Every Night

Meats go on the mesquite grill each evening. Toppings are prepped fresh: cabbage, cucumber, radish, pickled onion, lime, and the truck’s salsas.

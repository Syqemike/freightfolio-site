## Business truth (verified)
Family-owned mesquite-grill Mexican street food truck at 3112 W. Dobbins Rd., Laveen, AZ 85339.
Phone (602) 826-8652. Hours: Tue-Thu 5:00-11:00 PM, Fri-Sat 5:00 PM-12:00 AM, Sun-Mon closed.
Menu and prices verified against the business's own printed menu board (photographed from Instagram):
Tacos $4.00 (Asada, Al pastor, Cabeza, Lengua, Chorizo, Tripa, Birria, Pollo);
Vampiros $5.00; Cheese Quesadilla $4.00; Quesadilla con Carne $5.00; Quesabirria $4.00;

Business posts that they are also on DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub, with a drive-thru
and outdoor tables. Rating: 4.6 from 198 Restaurantji reviews (shown once).

## Aesthetic: "Noche de Brasas"
A night-market ember look that matches the truck itself: black painted walls, hand-lettered
flame lettering, string lights. Deep charcoal base, ember orange and flame yellow accents,
warm cream panels that echo the truck's wooden menu board, avocado-lime green as a small
freshness accent. Nothing corporate, nothing salon-like.

## Typography
- Display: Bebas Neue (tall condensed caps, evokes the truck's hand-painted signage)
- Body: Manrope (clean, readable on mobile)
- Prices and day labels in Bebas for a menu-board feel.

## Palette
- Charcoal #14100C (base), ember #E8631C (primary accent), flame yellow #F5A623 (highlights/prices),
- cream #F6EAD3 (menu panel), avocado lime #A9C83F (small accents), off-black text on cream.

## Imagery
10 real photos from the business's own Instagram (@comiendoconmemo_), identity-verified
against branded menu boards and truck signage (see img/PROVENANCE.txt). Hero: the crew
photo at night in front of the painted truck. Menu board photo shows the real printed menu.
Food shots for the signature-dish cards. No stock, no illustrations, no generated art.

## Layout
1. Thin disclosure ribbon: DESIGN PREVIEW, not the business's site.
2. Sticky nav: Menu, Favorites, The Truck, Find Us. Name prominent left, Call button right.
3. Hero: full-bleed night crew photo, name in big Bebas caps, subline
   "Mesquite-grilled Mexican street food in Laveen, AZ", hours line, CTAs: Call / Find Us.
4. Ember strip: family-owned / keto-friendly / drive-thru facts as plain chips.
5. Menu: cream "menu board" panel with categories and verified prices, photo of the real
   printed menu beside it. Footnote: sales tax and a card processing fee apply (from their menu).
6. Favorites: photo cards for tacos (asada/pastor/chorizo), quesabirria, vampiros, chile
   relleno, costillas, keto tacos, aguas frescas. Short plain descriptions, no hype.
7. The Truck: crew photo + story copy + facts (family owned, first come first served,
   tables + drive-thru + delivery apps). Truck banner photo.
8. Find Us: address, hours table, phone CTA "Questions? Call (602) 826-8652.", Google Maps link,
   Instagram/Facebook links. Rating line once: "4.6 from 198 Restaurantji reviews."
9. Footer: design-preview disclosure, Freightfolio credit.

## Customer journey
A hungry visitor sees what the food looks like, where the truck is, when it is open, and
how to call, within seconds. Menu answers the price question immediately. No booking
flow exists (no online booking verified); the site drives calls and drive-by visits.

## Compliance notes
- noindex meta tag. Disclosure near top and in footer.
- No invented reviews, awards, or claims. Rating carries source and count, appears once.
- CTAs: Call (tel:) and Find Us only. No "Book Now."
- No em dashes, no banned AI phrases, no "No X, no Y" constructions.
- Images all under 1 MB JPGs, compressed with PIL at quality 82.

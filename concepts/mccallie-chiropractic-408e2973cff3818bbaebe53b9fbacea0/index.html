Care at the practice

McCallie Chiropractic and Rehabilitation Center offers chiropractic care and massage therapy. Here is what a visit can cover.

01

Chiropractic adjustments

Dr. Adair adjusts by hand, working with the joints of the body, particularly the spine, to reduce pain and restore joint function. His training covers Diversified, Gonstead, Thompson, Activator, flexion-distraction, and Webster technique, among others.

Stage 1. Reduction of pain

Stage 2. Recovery of function

Stage 3. Rehabilitation

Stage 4. Reinforcement

02

Low back pain

Basic management of low back pain often involves a four-stage plan. The aim is a spine with healthier, stronger, and more stable nerves, discs, and muscles.

03

Massage therapy

Melody Karm and Harmony Sovia are licensed massage therapists at the practice. Sessions run from trigger point and deep tissue work to Swedish, sports, prenatal, reflexology, cranial sacral, and aromatherapy massage.

The people at the practice

Dr. L. Doug Adair, Doctor of Chiropractic

Dr. Adair earned his doctorate of chiropractic from Logan College of Chiropractic in St. Louis. Before that he worked as an underground mine foreman, and back pain of his own led him to chiropractic. He has worked in radiology since 1991.

Melody Karm, Licensed Massage and Neuromuscular Therapist

Melody has over 22 years of experience as a massage therapist. She is also a certified medical assistant and chiropractic assistant.

Harmony Sovia, Licensed Massage Therapist

Harmony has been a licensed massage therapist since 2007 and a certified medical massage practitioner since 2017.

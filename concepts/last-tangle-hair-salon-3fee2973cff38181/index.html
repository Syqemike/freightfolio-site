The Last Tangle Hair Salon and Fabulouz Findz Accessoriez

A Black-owned Detroit institution on Clifford Street. The salon offers natural styling and silk and ceramic press work. Relaxers and color are on the menu, along with weaves. A barber is on staff, and a boutique full of fashion accessories is next door.

Our story

Three decades of Detroit hair

1990

The salon opens its doors

The Last Tangle Hair Salon is established in Detroit. The salon focuses on healthy hair.

2008

Fabulouz Findz Accessoriez joins the family

The boutique opens alongside the salon, giving clients a curated shelf of fashion accessories to finish the look before they leave.

2026

Named a Detroit Legacy Business

The salon receives a $2,500 Detroit Legacy Business microgrant (DEGC, September 2026), recognizing its place in the city’s story.

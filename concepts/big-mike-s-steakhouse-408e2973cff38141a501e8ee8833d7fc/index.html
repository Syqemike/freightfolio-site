Big Mike's Steakhouse is a wood-fired steakhouse in Orange Beach, voted Alabama's Best Steak by the Alabama Cattlemen's Association. The menu covers hand-cut Certified Angus Beef steaks including the 24-ounce Big Mike ribeye, filet mignon, chargrilled oysters, crab cakes, and bread pudding. Seating is first-come, first-served with no reservations; private parties and catering are available, and there is bar seating while waiting. This is one of 8 Alabama locations; this concept is for the Orange Beach location.

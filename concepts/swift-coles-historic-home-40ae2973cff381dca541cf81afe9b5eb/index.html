About

Swift-Coles Historic Home is an 1882 tidewater historic home at 17424 Swift Coles Ln in Bon Secour, Alabama, on the Bon Secour River and owned by the Baldwin County Historic Commission. The home is a museum open for tours and a wedding and event venue. The own site swiftcoleshistorichome.com is a thin single page, so it is not linked as a full site.

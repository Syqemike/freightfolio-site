The studio

Built for custom work

Fountain City Tattoo is a custom tattoo studio. Bring your own idea or pick from the artists' designs. Walk-ins are welcome and the shop runs flash events for ready-to-go pieces.

Custom tattoos

One-of-one pieces designed with you before the needle moves. Send your idea through Instagram DM.

Walk-ins

The shop takes walk-ins. Show up, talk to whoever is free, and see what can happen that day.

Flash events

Ready-made flash designs at set prices during shop events. Follow the Instagram for event posts.

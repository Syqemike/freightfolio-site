What We Do

K9 Necessities provides professional full-service grooming for dogs. The shop is a small operation, and the work is done by a caring stylist with many years of experience in grooming and kennel management.

Owners in Urbandale and the surrounding area bring their dogs in for dependable grooming from someone who has done the job a long time.

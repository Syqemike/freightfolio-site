The shop

Built on two decades of trust

The Ink Spot is one of Casper's established names in body art. Where many shops come and go, this one has stayed, building its reputation one client at a time over roughly twenty years.

It is a full-service shop: custom tattooing and body piercing sit alongside laser tattoo and hair removal and permanent cosmetics. Whatever brings you in, you are dealing with people who have done this a long time.

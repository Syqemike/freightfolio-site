Manci's Antique Club is a laid-back dive-bar diner in Daphne's Old Town, in a 1924 building, known as the Bloody Mary Capital of the Eastern Shore. The menu covers burgers, seafood, po'boys, brunch, and Indian frybread, with live music and an outdoor patio. The club has been featured on Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives, Southern Living, and Coastal Living.

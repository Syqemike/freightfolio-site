A country boarding resort for dogs and cats

All Pets Boarding Resort sits in Loomis, about 20 miles from Sacramento, and cares for both dogs and cats. Dogs board in extra-large runs. Cats board in spacious condos built for comfort. The resort also runs a daycare play area and its own grooming shop.

The people who work here describe themselves simply: all staff are TLC specialists. The property feels like a ranch, not a kennel row, and every guest gets individual attention through the day.

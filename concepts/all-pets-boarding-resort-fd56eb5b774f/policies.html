Policies & details

A few things to know before your pet's stay.

Stock photo

Hours: call to confirm

Hours vary by day, and the listings online do not all agree. Before you drive out, call to confirm current hours: (916) 652-3687.

What to bring

Every pet is different, so the resort will tell you exactly what to bring when you book. A few things to ask about on your call:

  • Your pet's regular food, so meals stay on routine
  • Any medication your pet takes, with clear instructions
  • Your vet's contact information
  • A favorite blanket or toy from home

Call ahead and the staff will walk you through what your pet needs for their stay, including any paperwork or vaccination records to bring along.

Good to know

  • Dogs and cats board in separate areas of the resort.
  • Daycare and grooming can be added to a boarding stay. Ask about it when you call.
  • All staff are TLC specialists. If your pet has special needs, tell the staff when you book so they can plan for them.

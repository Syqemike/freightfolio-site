Daycare: the play area

The resort has a daycare play area where dogs spend the day playing, running, and resting between games. Staff are on hand for the whole session, and since all staff are TLC specialists, shy dogs get patience and the playful ones get a game of fetch.

Daycare works well for a dog that needs company and exercise while you are at work, or as an add-on to an overnight boarding stay. Call to ask about daycare days and pricing.

Grooming: the on-site shop

All Pets Boarding Resort has its own grooming shop, so a boarding stay can end with a bath and a trim. Your dog comes home clean and smelling good.

Call the resort to ask what grooming services are offered and to set up an appointment. Grooming can be booked with a stay or on its own.

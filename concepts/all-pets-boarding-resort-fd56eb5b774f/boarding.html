Boarding at the resort

Dogs get extra-large runs. Cats get spacious condos.

Stock photo

Dogs: extra-large runs

The dog boarding runs at All Pets Boarding Resort are built extra-large, so big dogs have room to stretch out and small dogs have space to explore. Each dog has its own run, and staff members check on every guest through the day.

Dogs boarding here can also spend time in the daycare play area. Ask about daycare add-ons when you call to book.

Cats: spacious condos

Cats stay in their own part of the resort, away from the barking. The condos are spacious, with room for a cat to perch, nap, and watch the room. Litter, bedding, and fresh water are part of every stay.

If your cat needs a quiet routine, the staff will keep to the schedule you set at check-in. Call ahead and tell them what your cat is used to.

Booking a stay

Call to book a stay: (916) 652-3687. The staff will ask about your pet, your dates, and anything special your pet needs while you are away.

What the studio offers

Therapeutic massage

Professional, relaxing therapeutic massage in Huntsville. This is the studio's main service, offered in its treatment room on Rhett Avenue. It includes relaxation, deep tissue, sports, prenatal, hot stone, cupping, aromatherapy, and hot towel treatment at no extra charge, plus facial massage using jade rollers.

Prices: 30 minutes $60, 60 minutes $85, 90 minutes $125.

Sessions are scheduled by phone. Call to ask about a time that works for you.

Ashiatsu Barefoot Massage

Barefoot massage using the overhead bars mounted above the treatment table. Review coverage names Amanda Peoples and Ashiatsu Barefoot Massage at this studio. Amanda Peoples is an Ashiatsu Barefoot Massage instructor.

Prices: 60 minutes $90, 90 minutes $125.

Medi-Cupping Therapy

Massage cupping and Medi-Cupping, included in all massages at no additional cost. Additional time is $20 per 30 minutes.

Medical Massage

Medical massage at the studio requires a Letter of Medical Necessity. The studio notes that some insurance companies may pay for medical massage.

Gift certificates

Gift certificates are available from the studio. Call to ask about one.

The studio

Ambiance Massage Therapy is a professional relaxing therapeutic massage business in Huntsville. Sessions are scheduled by phone, at the studio on Rhett Avenue.

The treatment room is a small, lamplit space: a massage table under an owl tapestry and string lights, trigger point posters and certificates on the wall, and the overhead bars Amanda uses for Ashiatsu Barefoot Massage.

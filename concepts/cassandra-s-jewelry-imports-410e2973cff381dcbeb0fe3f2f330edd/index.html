About

Cassandra's Jewelry Imports is a family-owned jewelry store at 115 East River Street in Savannah, Georgia, in business for more than 30 years. The shop carries hand-selected sterling silver artisan jewelry, semi-precious stones, fossils, and shells, cast in sterling silver, stainless steel, copper, and gold, plus local Savannah vintage and estate jewelry. Hours are Sunday, 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM; Monday through Friday, 10:30 AM to 6:00 PM; Saturday, 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Call (912) 651-1094 to ask about a piece.

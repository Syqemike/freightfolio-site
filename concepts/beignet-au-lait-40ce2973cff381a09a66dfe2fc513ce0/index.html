About

Beignet Au Lait is a cafe and bakery in Slidell, Louisiana. The cafe serves beignets, classic powdered-sugar plus caramel, chocolate, raspberry, and blackberry glazes, alongside cafe au lait, with breakfast, outdoor seating, and takeout.

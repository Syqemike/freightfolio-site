## Creative direction
GULF CHARTER. Deep sea navy (#0d2b45) + sand + rust orange. Oswald condensed uppercase display + Source Serif 4 body. Full-bleed photo hero with gradient scrim, tide-fact strip, 5 trip cards, dark species section with fish-tag chips, split boat-spec panel, sand pricing band, rust booking band. Nautical without clipart; nothing like the restaurant or garage concepts.

## Structure
Disclosure bar -> navy sticky nav -> full-bleed hero (kicker, h1, lede, rating once, CTAs) -> tide strip (1979, 5 guests, gear included, $450-$850) -> trips (5 cards) -> dark species section (8 species chips + photo) -> boat spec panel + pricing band -> 3-photo gallery (labeled stock) -> hours/dock cards -> rust booking band -> footer disclaimer.

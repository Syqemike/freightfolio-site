About

Events at Haven is a contemporary industrial event venue at 2515 6th Ave S in Birmingham's Lakeview District, in a restored 1924 building. The space covers 20,000 square feet with capacity up to 1,100 guests, with exposed brick, concrete floors, large windows, a bridal suite, in-house audio and video, and a large stage. The venue hosts weddings, receptions, corporate events, and private events.

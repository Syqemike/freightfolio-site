About

The Camellia is a rustic barn wedding venue on the 6W cattle farm in Perkinston, set on 420 acres with a 30-acre pond. The 6,400 sq ft barn hosts weddings, receptions, showers, parties, rehearsal dinners, portrait sessions, birthdays and proms for up to 200 guests. Saturday weddings include all-day Friday for decorating and setup. Couples choose their own vendors, except a preselected bartending service when alcohol is served. Owners Lance and Charlee Wedgeworth hosted the first wedding in September 2019.

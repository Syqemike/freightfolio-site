PELICAN TATTOO & BODY PIERCING — DESIGN BRIEF
Hartford, CT · Concept site by Freightfolio (unsolicited design preview)

Creative direction: heritage letterpress / neighborhood broadsheet. Aged newsprint paper, dark green-black ink, and an oxblood red accent, set in old-style Georgia serif with a newspaper-style dateline masthead, double-rule borders, an original "Est. 1970" seal motif, and shop-ticket service boards.

The layout is editorial rather than template-driven: a front-page lead story, a two-ticket services board, a history column with a timeline rail of verified facts, and a plain-spoken visit section. This avoids the black-and-gold flash cliché common to tattoo sites and keeps the identity distinct from other Freightfolio concepts.

Content sources: the shop's public directory listings only. No photos are used: directory gallery photos could not be verified as business-owned, WNPR article photos belong to the news outlet, and the shop has no verified website or socials. All facts are verified (address, phone, email, hours, services, heritage since 1970, Connecticut's first licensed tattoo shop, Frog Hollow/Park Street, WNPR profile, 4.6 from 116 Google reviews). The single price mention is attributed to a review, not presented as a shop price list. No reviews, testimonials, awards, staff names, or facts were invented.

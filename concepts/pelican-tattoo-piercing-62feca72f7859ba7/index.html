The Shop

A Park Street institution since 1970.

Pelican Tattoo & Body Piercing has served Hartford from 577 Park Street since 1970. The shop offers custom tattoos and body piercings, and walk-ins are welcomed.

The work covers a wide range of tattoo styles and piercing placements, and the shop also sells body jewelry, including 14K gold pieces.

History

Connecticut’s first licensed tattoo shop.

Pelican Tattoo & Body Piercing has operated on Park Street in Hartford’s Frog Hollow neighborhood since 1970. It is described as Connecticut’s first licensed tattoo shop.

Connecticut Public (WNPR) profiled the shop as a Park Street institution, a fixture of the neighborhood for more than five decades.

  1. 1970

    The shop opens on Park Street in Hartford’s Frog Hollow neighborhood.

  2. First of its kind

    Pelican is described as Connecticut’s first licensed tattoo shop.

  3. Profiled

    Connecticut Public (WNPR) profiles the shop as a Park Street institution.

  4. Today

    The shop continues to offer custom tattoos, body piercings, and body jewelry from the same Park Street address.

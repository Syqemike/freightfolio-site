About

My Hot Yoga is a yoga studio in the Southern Hills Shopping Center in Springfield, with two studios, one heated and one non-heated. Classes run from Hot 60 and Power Yoga to Yoga Sculpt, Deep Stretch, Gentle Vinyasa, Slow 60, aerial yoga, and barre. The studio has full-service locker rooms, and classes are booked through Mindbody.

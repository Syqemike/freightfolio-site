About

Revel Event Center is an event venue on Stone Avenue in Greenville. The venue is an 8,500 square foot historic space between downtown and the Historic Earle District, with a 4,500 square foot Grande Ballroom, a whiskey lounge, a bridal suite, grooms quarters, and an outside promenade. Capacity is up to 500 guests. Catering comes through partner companies.

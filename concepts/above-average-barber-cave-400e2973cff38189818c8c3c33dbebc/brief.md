# Design Brief: Above Average Barber Cave (Website Concept)

## Aesthetic
"The Cave, bold industrial." A dark slate and hazard-amber identity that reads as a barber shop built like a garage or clubhouse, not a spa. Heavy condensed display type, oversized statements, hard edges, generous whitespace between big structural bands. No wood textures, no espresso tones, no script fonts, no ornamental filigree.

## Typography
- Display: Oswald (Google Fonts), weights 500/600/700, uppercase, tight letter-spacing for eyebrow labels, wide for headlines.
- Body/UI: Archivo (Google Fonts), 400/500/600.
- No other fonts. Numbers and hours set in Oswald for an industrial ticket-board feel.

## Palette
- Background: #14181f (dark slate)
- Panel: #1d232c (raised slate)
- Line/border: #2e3642 (slate hairlines)
- Accent: #f5a524 (hazard amber), used sparingly for CTAs, eyebrows, dividers, ticket perforations
- Text: #f2f4f7 (off-white), secondary #aeb6c2 (slate gray)
- Danger/none. Distinct from espresso-and-amber template builds by leaning fully into cool slate grays.

## Layout (original structure)
1. Slim disclosure bar: "WEBSITE CONCEPT / DESIGN PREVIEW" + business name.
2. Sticky nav: wordmark left, links right (Services, Barbers, Hours & Location, Contact), amber Book Now button.
3. Split-screen hero: left text column on slate (eyebrow, oversized "AVERAGE AIN'T ACCEPTABLE." headline quoting the verified tagline, short sub copy, Book Now + Call to book buttons, hours snapshot line); right full-height photo panel.
4. Slim rating band: "4.9 from 19 Google reviews" shown exactly once, with exact source.
5. Services: five ticket-stub cards in a row (Haircuts, Fades, Lineups / Edge-ups, Beard Shaping, Kids' Cuts). Each card has a perforated stub edge made with a dashed CSS border divider and punched notches. No prices published anywhere; each card says prices are not listed online and to call or book.
6. Gallery: "Fresh off the chair." Four photos with factual captions.
7. Family-friendly block: quote of the verified tagline + cut3 photo.
8. Barbers: two typographic cards, David Turner (Dave) and Abdul. No invented titles or bios. Book with Dave links to the verified theCut booking page; shop phone for Abdul.
9. Hours & Location: two-column block. Day-by-day hours table (verified: Mon Closed; Tue 9-7; Wed 9-5; Thu-Sat 9-7; Sun Closed). Address 1017 E Pontiac St, Fort Wayne, IN 46806, phone, directions link.
10. Contact CTA: big booking band with Book Now and Call to book.
11. Footer: design-preview disclaimer, photo provenance line naming the theCut profile, rating source line, "Concept prepared October 2026", address and phone.

## Customer journey
Arrive -> see the disclosure and the shop name immediately -> read the tagline headline and know this is a no-average barber shop on E Pontiac St -> book online with Dave or call the shop from the hero -> scan ticket-stub services -> see the 4.9 Google rating -> browse real client cuts -> read the family-friendly tagline -> meet the barbers -> check hours and address -> book.

## Copy notes
- Plain direct sentences only. No em dashes anywhere.
- Banned phrases and constructions checked: no "where passion meets precision", no "elevate your experience", no "more than just a", no "No X, no Y" triples, no comma triples, no period fragments.
- No invented facts. No prices (not published). No claims about how the shop operates beyond walk-ins welcome / appointments recommended and family-friendly, which are verified.
- Rating "4.9 from 19 Google reviews" appears exactly once. The theCut 4.96/227 figure appears nowhere.
- CTAs: primary "Book Now" links to https://book.thecut.co/DavidTurner (verified page: "david turner, Barber in Ft. Wayne, IN"). Secondary "Call to book" links to tel:+12604427937.

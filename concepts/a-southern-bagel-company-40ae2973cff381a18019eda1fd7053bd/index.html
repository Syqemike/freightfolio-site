About

A Southern Bagel Company is a small family-run bagel shop in Long Beach, run by husband and wife team David and Anne Guille. The shop opened February 3, 2019, after starting at the local Farmers Market in 2018. Bagels are hand-rolled, boiled, and baked, served buttered, smeared, or dressed, with house-whipped cream cheeses and homemade jellies, jams, and caramel. The storefront has a front-porch entrance and service window with outdoor seating, and offers dine-in, takeout, and delivery.

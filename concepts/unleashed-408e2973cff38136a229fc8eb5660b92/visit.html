Good to know before you call

  • First day of daycare is free
  • Bathing and nail trims are by appointment
  • Penthouse suites are available for boarding, and familiar bedding from home is welcome
  • Play areas are set by dog size, with a small-dog area for dogs 35 lbs and under and a large-dog area for dogs over 50 lbs

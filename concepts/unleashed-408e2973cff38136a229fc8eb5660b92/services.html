Services

Dog daycare

Open play during the day, with social time and exercise under staff supervision. Personalized evaluations help match each dog to safe, comfortable play. Every dog's first day of daycare is free.

Boarding

Overnight stays in a boarding facility built so dogs feel cozy and content through the night. Penthouse suites are an option, and familiar bedding from home is welcome.

Bathing and nail trims

Gentle baths and nail trims by appointment, using top-grade products suited for sensitive skin and allergies.

Training

Training is listed among the services at Unleashed. Call to ask about current training options.

Booking

Reserve through the Gingr app, or call (251) 375-1600 to ask about booking. Stop by for a tour of the facility.

What Unleashed offers

Services

Daycare, boarding, and bathing in one place, so a dog owner can see what a day here looks like before calling. Booking runs through the Gingr app.

01

Dog daycare

Open play with staff supervision. Dogs socialize and play with well-trained staff and burn off energy, and personalized evaluations help keep every pet safe and comfortable. The first day of daycare is free.

02

Boarding

Overnight stays in a home-style boarding setting. Penthouse suites are available, and familiar bedding from home is welcome.

03

Grooming and bathing

Gentle baths by appointment, using top-grade products suited for sensitive skin and allergies.

04

Nail trims

Nail trims by appointment, alongside bathing.

05

Training

Training is listed among the services offered. Call to ask about current training options.

Unleashed

Dog daycare and boarding in Daphne, with open play during the day and restful overnight stays.

A day at Unleashed

Daycare at Unleashed is open play. Dogs socialize and play with well-trained staff and burn off energy, and personalized evaluations help keep every pet safe and comfortable. Whether your dog visits for the day or for an extended stay, the setting is an open-play environment with home-style boarding.

Room to play, sized by dog

The facility has two large outdoor play areas with pet-friendly turf and beach sand, plus dog-friendly playground equipment and cots and chairs for lounging. Play areas inside and outside are set by dog size. The small-dog area is for dogs 35 lbs and under. The large-dog area is for dogs over 50 lbs.

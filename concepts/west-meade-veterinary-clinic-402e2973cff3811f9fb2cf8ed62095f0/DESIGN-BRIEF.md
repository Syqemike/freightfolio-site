# DESIGN BRIEF — West Meade Veterinary Clinic concept (batch 29)

## Aesthetic
Calm, trustworthy medical-editorial. A neighborhood clinic that has been there
since 1992 deserves a design that reads like a well-made annual report or a
quiet magazine feature: generous whitespace, hairline rules, restrained color,
photography given room to breathe. No hero banners with buttons, no cards, no
playful pet-store energy.

## Typography
- Headlines: elegant serif — Georgia, "Palatino Linotype", Palatino, serif.
  Large sizes, tight line-height, sentence case. Italic used sparingly for
  emphasis words.
- Body/UI: clean humanist sans — system-ui stack. Small caps eyebrow labels
  with wide letter-spacing for section kickers.
- Numerals for the services index set in serif, muted.

## Palette
- Soft white background: #FBFAF6
- Warm stone neutral: #EFE9DD (section bands, rules)
- Sage green: #5C7052 (primary), deep sage #3F4F38 (text accents, footer)
- Ink: #2E2C26 (body text)
- All color is flat. No gradients, no glows, no shadows heavier than a soft
  1px rule or a whisper of a drop shadow under framed photos.

## Imagery
Photography-forward. Portrait-orientation vet photos are framed like editorial
plates: thin stone border, small caption beneath, "Stock photo" tag. No
cropping into circles, no duotone overlays, no icon illustrations anywhere.

## Layout (deliberately non-template)
- Index: a magazine cover composition. Masthead row (wordmark left, nav
  right). Then an asymmetric two-part opening: a large framed photograph on
  the left (about 7 of 12 columns) and a sage-tinted column on the right
  carrying the eyebrow, headline, lede, and the single call CTA. Below it, a
  ruled "practice facts" strip (Est. 1992 / rating / species seen) divided by
  hairlines instead of cards.
- Services are presented as a numbered editorial index (01, 02, ...) with
  hairline dividers, one service per row on mobile, two-column rows on
  desktop. No 3-column card grid.
- An avian/exotics feature band: sage background, bird photo at left, Dr.
  Lutz mention at right.
- Visit page: address block and hours presented as a simple ruled ledger.
- Footer: deep sage, three columns (visit / hours / contact), disclosure line.

## Customer journey
1. Land: disclosure + clinic name + what they are (full-service vet, West
   Meade, since 1992). One clear action: call the clinic.
2. Trust: facts strip (est. 1992, 4.8/149 reviews, dogs/cats/exotics),
   photography of real exam moments, the clinic's own positioning language.
3. Explore: numbered services index, avian/exotic note with Dr. Lutz.
4. Act: visit page with address, conservative hours, directions link, and the
   tel: CTA. Payment note (cash, check) set plainly.

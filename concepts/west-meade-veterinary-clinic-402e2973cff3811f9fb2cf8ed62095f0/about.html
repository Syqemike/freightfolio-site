About the clinic

A neighborhood clinic since 1992.

West Meade Veterinary Clinic is a full-service animal healthcare provider in the heart of West Meade, offering compassionate and comprehensive medical care for pets.

How they practice

The same care and respect we would our own.

The clinic describes its standard of care as personalized to each pet's needs, from routine wellness exams to surgery. Experienced veterinarians and skilled support staff see dogs, cats, and exotic pets through every stage of life.

The clinic accepts payment by cash or check.

Avian and exotic expertise

Dr. Michael Lutz and exotic pets.

Dr. Michael Lutz is known for his work with avian and exotic pets, from birds to other species that need a veterinarian who understands them.

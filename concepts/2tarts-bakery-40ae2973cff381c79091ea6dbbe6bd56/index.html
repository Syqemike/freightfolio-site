About

2tarts Bakery is a sister-owned downtown bakery and coffee shop at 139 N Castell Ave Ste 300 in New Braunfels, run by sisters April and Ashley. The bakery makes its treats from scratch every day, serves coffee every morning and fine desserts late into the evening, and makes wedding and birthday cakes.

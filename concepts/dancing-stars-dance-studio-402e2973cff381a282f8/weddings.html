Your First Dance

Dancing Stars is a wedding choreography studio as well as a dance school. Owner and head choreographer Brittney Garcia creates personalized first dances and wedding-party routines, named Best of Weddings in both 2024 and 2025.

Couples Choreography

A routine built around your song, your timeline, and how you move together. Brittney works with couples at every level, including two left feet.

Wedding Party Dances

Group routines for the whole wedding party, plus help with reception walk-ins.

Music You Love

Latin, classical, country, hip-hop, R&B. Your first dance fits your taste, not a template.

Start Planning

Couples usually begin choreography a few months before the wedding. Call to ask about current availability and how many sessions your routine might need.

Programs

Classes for ages 3 and up, from recreational movers to pre-professional competitors. Call for the current class schedule.

Recreational Classes

Weekly classes in Jazz, Hip Hop, Lyrical, Contemporary, Ballet, Tumbling, Heels, and Pom. A welcoming entry point for dancers ages 3 and up, with an emphasis on confidence and fundamentals.

Competition & Pre-Professional

For dancers ready to train harder and perform more. Competition teams have placed at Albuquerque competitions, including 2025 events, under nationally and regionally recognized choreographers.

Tumbling & Technique

Focused training in acrobatic skills, flexibility, and clean technique that supports every style a dancer studies.

Wedding First Dances

Personalized first-dance choreography for couples and wedding parties. Best of Weddings 2024 and 2025. Details on the Weddings page.

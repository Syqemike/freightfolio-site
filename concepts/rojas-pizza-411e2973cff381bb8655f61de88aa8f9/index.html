About

Rojas Pizza is a family-owned NY-style pizza shop at 440 W Adams St in downtown Jacksonville, Florida, in the Duval County Courthouse area, in business since 2014. The shop is veteran-owned and serves pizza by the slice and whole pies: cheese, pepperoni, pepperoni sausage, and Margherita, plus garlic knots, wings in 7 flavors including bourbon and mango habanero, salads, and Italian dishes. A two-slices-and-a-drink special runs about $7.99 to $8.99. Takeout and delivery are available, and the shop also runs a food truck. Hours are Monday, 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM; Tuesday through Friday, 9:30 AM to 5:00 PM; weekends not listed. Call (904) 525-0432 to place an order.

Our Story

Run by the Carvers, built for the Coast

Bay Strength and Fitness is owned and operated by the Carver family: Zach, Jackie, and Jordan. It is an independent gym, which means decisions are made by the people behind the front desk, not a corporate office three states away.

The idea is simple. A clean, well-equipped place to train where beginners are welcome, experienced lifters are at home, and nobody is trying to upsell you on things you do not need.

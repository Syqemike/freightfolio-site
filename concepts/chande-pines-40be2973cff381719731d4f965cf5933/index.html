About

Chande Pines is an outdoor wedding and event venue in Pembroke, Georgia, off I-16, about 35 minutes from downtown Savannah, set on 50 acres of Georgia pines. The grounds include a meadow with a pond view, a 3,200 square foot pole barn with chandeliers, a smaller 800 square foot Baby Chande pavilion, and a custom steel photo bridge. Packages include Basic Elopement, Day-Only Wedding, and Grande Chande Weekend, with one couple per weekend. Floral design, decor, rentals, and design and labor setup come through Chande Design and Rentals. Owner Tammie Ricks Hall brings more than 10 years of event planning, and preferred vendors cover catering, photography, officiants, and planning through Rooted Gatherings. Capacity is 200 maximum, with 150 recommended. Contact Tammie by text at the number on this page.

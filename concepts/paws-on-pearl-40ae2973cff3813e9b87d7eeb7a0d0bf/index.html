About

Paws On Pearl is a locally owned, family-operated dog daycare, boarding, grooming, and training center in Tulsa's Historic Pearl District, run by Joy and Michael Toburen, with more than 18 years of daycare experience. Daycare is open-play and kennel-free, boarding includes five premium private suites, and the center stays open on all major holidays at normal hours. New guests need two daycare visits before boarding, and the first visit is free.

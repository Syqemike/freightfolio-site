A faith-based dance studio in Colorado Springs for ages 2 through adult.

Turning Pointe Dance is a faith-based dance studio in Colorado Springs for ages 2 through adult, offering ballet, tap, jazz, hip-hop, musical theater, and lyrical and worship dance. The studio has five indoor studios with professionally installed sprung flooring and viewing windows, a training division through pre-collegiate levels, adult classes, birthday parties, Girl Scout badge events, and workshops.

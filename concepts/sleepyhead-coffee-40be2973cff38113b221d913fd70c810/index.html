About

Sleepyhead Coffee is a multi-roaster coffee shop in the historic James Building at 735 Broad Street, Suite 104, in downtown Chattanooga, Tennessee. The food and pastry menu is 100 percent vegan, with gluten-free options. This record is the downtown Broad Street location only; the second location on East Main Street has its own phone and is not covered here.

Menu

Multi-roaster coffee

Drip, cold brew, and espresso drinks from multiple roasters.

Signature lattes

Cereal, banana bread iced, maple brown sugar, strawberry matcha, and raspberry cold foam lattes, plus turmeric, cinnamon, and beetroot lattes.

Matcha and chai

Matcha and chai alongside the coffee menu.

Vegan pastries

Vegan donuts and cinnamon rolls, with gluten-free options.

Vegan food

Avocado toast, bagel breakfast sandwiches, vegan quesadillas, and sandwiches including chickpea salad and pimento cheese.

Events and mobile cart

Order-ahead is offered on the shop's own site, and a mobile cart and event service are offered. The email shown here is the shop's published events contact; email events@sleepyheadcoffee.com about a private party or event.

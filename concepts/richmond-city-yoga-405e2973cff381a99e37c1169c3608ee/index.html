The Mysore way of learning

In a Mysore class, the teacher works with each student one at a time while the room practices together. The sequence is learned step by step, so the practice fits the person doing it.

People of widely varying ages and abilities practice together in the early morning class, each at their own pace and according to their own capabilities. That shared room, with individual attention, is the traditional way Ashtanga Yoga is taught and practiced.

Initial sessions typically last 45 minutes to an hour. Questions are welcome: call or email Robbie Norris directly.

DESIGN BRIEF: Richmond City Yoga (Website Concept)
Business: Richmond City Yoga, Ashtanga yoga studio, 212 E Clay St, Richmond, VA.
Phone 804-928-6677. Email Robbie@RichmondCityYoga.com. Studio site richmondcityyoga.com.

AESTHETIC: Quiet, traditional, editorial. A calm morning-practice feel that matches
Mysore teaching: individual attention inside a shared room. No loud gym energy,
no decorative illustration, no icons, no gradients.

TYPOGRAPHY: Fraunces serif for headlines (warm, bookish, traditional) with Inter
for body text. Large headline, generous line spacing, short paragraphs.

PALETTE: Warm paper background (#f6f1e7), deep pine ink (#1f2e26), terracotta
accent (#a84e2b), muted sage for secondary labels. Terracotta is used only for
the headline accent, the call button, and the card top rules.

IMAGERY: Four stock yoga photographs, each captioned "Stock photo" and none
presented as the studio, its teacher, or its students. No real photos of this
business were obtainable from its public pages (its site is a text welcome
letter with no usable photos), so stock is the labeled fallback. See PROVENANCE.txt.

LAYOUT: Disclosure bar first, masthead with business name and text navigation,
split hero (headline left, tall photo right), a dark class-window band,
editorial practice section, three class cards, teacher section, single-photo
gallery strip, and a visit/contact panel. Mobile stacks every section to one
column. Distinct from recent Freightfolio concepts by its editorial split hero,
dark band, and restrained serif system rather than a card-grid hero.

CONTENT RULES APPLIED: Only verified classes are listed (Mysore, Led Primary
Series, private instruction). Class windows are labeled as class windows, not
full open hours. No prices (none verified), no online booking (none verified),
no Book Now button. Contact CTA is Call with the verified phone plus email.
Rating appears once only: 4.7 from 33 reviews on a public listing. No health
claims, no invented credentials, no urgency copy.

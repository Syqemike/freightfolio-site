About

The Barn at Shady Lane is a country wedding venue on 8 acres by the Robert Trent Oxmoor Valley Golf Course, 1 mile from the Renaissance Ross Bridge Marriott. The fully air-conditioned barn seats 300, with 28 dining tables and 250 Chiavari chairs, plus a vintage Bride's Loft, a Groom's Cottage, indoor and outdoor ceremony space, and Wi-Fi. Rental is full-day, with access from 9:00 AM on the day of the event and a typical close at 11:00 PM.

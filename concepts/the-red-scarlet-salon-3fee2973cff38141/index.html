The salon sits on East Washington Street in the Thornton Park neighborhood of downtown Orlando. Its menu centers on dimensional color and blonding, balayage, highlights, and colormelt, alongside precision cuts and styling services such as curtain bangs and long layers.

Hair extensions, eyelash service, permanent makeup, nail care, makeup, threading, and full-body waxing round out the menu.

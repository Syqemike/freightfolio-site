What Hollis Funeral Home offers

Hollis Funeral Home is a family-owned funeral home in Syracuse, serving families for more than 45 years. Services include traditional funerals, cremation, direct cremation, embalming, grief support, and veteran assistance. Obituaries are published on the funeral home's own site.

Services

Traditional funeralsA traditional service planned with the family at the funeral home, which has served Syracuse families for more than 45 years.
CremationCremation arranged through the funeral home, with the service details planned with the family.
Direct cremationCremation without a preceding funeral service, arranged directly with the funeral home.
EmbalmingEmbalming care provided by the funeral home as part of funeral arrangements.
Grief supportSupport for families after the service, offered by the funeral home.
Veteran assistanceAssistance for veteran families arranging a funeral.
ObituariesObituaries published on the funeral home's own site.

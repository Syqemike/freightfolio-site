The captain

Captain Chad Jay

Three decades. One coast.

Three Jays Charters is a family-owned fishing charter out of Biloxi, Mississippi, run by Captain Chad Jay. According to the business listing, the operation has been running trips for more than 30 years.

That kind of tenure shows up in the small things: knowing which wreck holds snapper this month, reading the water before the rods are even out, and keeping kids engaged when the bite slows down. Families are welcome aboard, and the captain's reviews go back years, many from anglers who came back for a second trip.

Every trip includes bait, tackle, rods and reels, fuel, fishing licenses, ice, and catch cleaning and filleting. You bring sunscreen. The captain handles the rest.

## Creative direction
Premium dark forest green + gold, matching the business's own brand identity (their flyer: deep green + gold serif on black). Gold serif display (Cinzel) + clean sans body (Inter). Editorial composition: offset portrait hero with hairline gold frame, salon-menu price list with dotted leaders, membership card panel for Mal's Club, section numbers for rhythm. Mobile-first. Visually distinct from TailWaggers (coral/teal on cream) and Vanity Furs (beige/teal).

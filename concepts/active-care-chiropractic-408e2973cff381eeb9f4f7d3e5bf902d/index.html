Care that starts with listening

Dr. Kurt Steffensmeier believes you know your body best. At Active Care Chiropractic, attentive patient care comes first. Treatments and adjustments are determined by what best meets your needs and your comfort level.

Call to book: (515) 528-8225

A small practice in the skywalk

Active Care Chiropractic is a small business owned and operated by Dr. Kurt. For more than 10 years he has practiced from the second floor of the skywalk at the corner of 6th and Walnut in downtown Des Moines. A native of southeast Iowa, Dr. Kurt graduated from the University of Iowa and Palmer College of Chiropractic.

Walk-ins are welcome. New patients are welcome.

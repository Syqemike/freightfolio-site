Services

01

Individual chiropractic care

Adjustments are determined by your needs and your comfort level, after Dr. Kurt listens to your description of how you feel. Care is built around the whole patient, not a set routine.

02

New patient visits

New patients are welcome. If you are unsure whether chiropractic care fits your situation, call or visit the office. Dr. Kurt is happy to explain chiropractic care and its natural, healing benefits.

03

Walk-in visits

Walk-ins are welcome at the skywalk office during open hours. The office is open Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm.

04

Advocare products

Advocare products are kept in stock at the office. Ask about them when you visit.

How a visit works

Your visit starts with a conversation about how you feel. From there, treatment is set by what best meets your needs and your comfort level. Dr. Kurt takes the time to listen first, because you know your body best.

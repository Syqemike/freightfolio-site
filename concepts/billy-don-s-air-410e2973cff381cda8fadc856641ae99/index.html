About

Billy Don's Air is a family-owned heating and air conditioning contractor in Elmore, Alabama, in business since 2001, with more than 46 years of experience. The owner is on site for all jobs. Services include heating and air conditioning, AC repair, furnaces, and air duct systems. Financing is available through Alabama Power Company and the Gas Company and Microf lease-to-own. A military discount is available, and estimates are free. The service area covers Autauga, Montgomery, Elmore, and Chilton County. Call (334) 391-1522 to ask about an estimate.

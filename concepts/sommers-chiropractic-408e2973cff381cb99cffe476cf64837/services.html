Services

What the practice offers

The practice offers corrective chiropractic care, family health, preventive care, torque release technique, and pregnancy care. Each one is listed below with more detail.

Corrective chiropractic care

Corrective care looks for the underlying cause of a problem instead of only easing symptoms for a short time. Visits focus on the spine and the nervous system.

Family health

The practice cares for the whole family, from infants to grandparents. Pediatric care is one of the services the practice lists.

Preventive care

Preventive care is regular chiropractic care on a set schedule, for people who want to look after their spine before pain shows up.

Torque Release Technique

Torque Release Technique is a gentle, minimal-force technique. It uses a handheld instrument and is safe for children. The practice uses it to address imbalances in the nervous system.

Pregnancy care

The practice offers chiropractic care for expectant mothers, including care during pregnancy.

Also offered

  • Upper cervical care
  • Chiropractic Biophysics
  • Full spine and diversified technique
  • Spinal decompression
  • Pediatric care

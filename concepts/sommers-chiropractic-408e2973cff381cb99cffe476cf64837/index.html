About the practice

A family practice built around corrective care

Sommers Chiropractic is a family chiropractic practice on Market Street in South Burlington. Dr. Michael Sommers founded the practice and earned his doctorate from Life University.

The practice focuses on corrective care, which looks for the underlying cause of a problem instead of only easing symptoms for a short time. Visits focus on the spine and the nervous system.

Sommers Chiropractic is the official chiropractic partner of the Vermont Lake Monsters.

For the whole family

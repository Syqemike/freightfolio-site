About

Polonza Bistro is a family-owned bistro in downtown Pensacola, Florida, at 286 N Palafox St. The bistro serves breakfast, brunch, and lunch, making its dishes and sauces from scratch and smoking its own meats. The drink menu covers coffees and loose-leaf teas.

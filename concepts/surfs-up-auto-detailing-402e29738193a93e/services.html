Detailing services

Inside, outside, wherever you are.

Mobile interior and exterior detailing for cars, trucks, and SUVs. Call to talk through what your vehicle needs and set a time.

01 / Exterior

Exterior detailing

Coast living puts salt, sand, and road film on everything. A proper exterior detail gets the paint, wheels, glass, and trim clean and finished.

  • Hand wash of the full exterior
  • Wheels, tires, and wheel wells
  • Windows and mirrors, inside and out
  • Trim and exterior details finished
02 / Interior

Interior detailing

The inside of the car is where you actually live. Interior detailing covers the seats, carpets, dashboard, and every surface you touch daily.

  • Seats cleaned and treated
  • Carpets and floor mats deep cleaned
  • Dashboard, console, and door panels
  • Vents, cupholders, and small details
03 / Full detail

The full treatment

Interior plus exterior in one visit. For the daily driver that has not been detailed in a while, or the vehicle you want brought fully back.

  • Everything in the exterior detail
  • Everything in the interior detail
  • Cars, trucks, and SUVs
  • Done at your location, on your schedule

Lebanese and Mediterranean, in Fairhope

Sage Lebanese Cuisine and Cafe serves authentic Lebanese and Mediterranean food in Fairhope, with indoor and outdoor seating, takeout, and reservations. Its menu includes hummus, including a Hummus Trio and Traditional Hummus, baba ghanouj, garlic labneh, grape leaves, kibbeh, tabouli, falafel, and moussaka, plus shawarma wraps and plates, NYC Chicken, Chicken Kebab, Kafta Kebab, Shish Kebab, a Kebab Mixer, lamb chops, and salads, with baklava and baklava cheesecake, Turkish coffee, Lebanese iced tea, and Sage lemonade. No menu prices are published in research, so call with questions.

Cut like stone

Services

Every service is shaped with the same care Phil puts into his artwork on the shop walls. Clean lines and sharp detail in a finish that holds.

01

Fades and custom cuts

Skin fades, tapers, and the big chop, plus custom cuts designed around your head shape and hairline. Cuts for women, children, and men.

02

Beard trims and razor shaves

Beard sculpting and straight razor shaves with a hot lather finish. Sharp edges and clean necklines, with detail work that frames the face.

03

Hair design and color

Hair design etched with precision and hair coloring for a bold or natural change. Bring a reference or talk it through in the chair.

04

Eyebrow arching and facials

Eyebrow arching and facials to finish the look. Small details make a big difference and get the same steady hand as every cut.

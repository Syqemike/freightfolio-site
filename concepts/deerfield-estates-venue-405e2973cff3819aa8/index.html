A barn with history

Deerfield Estates Venue is a historic timber-frame barn wedding and event venue in Waco, on 11 acres, with a barn from the 1840s brought from Newtown, Pennsylvania, rose gardens, and park-like grounds. It hosts weddings, ceremonies, receptions, corporate events, family reunions, galas, and fundraisers.

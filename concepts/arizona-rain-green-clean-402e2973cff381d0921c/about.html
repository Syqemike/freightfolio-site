Why green

Natural products are the whole point

Arizona Rain Green Clean built its business on one commitment: every cleaning uses all-natural cleaning products. The floors your kids play on, the counters where you prep dinner, and the air your family breathes stay free of harsh chemical residue.

In a desert city where dust is constant and windows stay shut against the heat, what is in the air inside your home matters. A green clean keeps the indoors as healthy as it looks.

  • No harsh chemical residue on household surfaces
  • All-natural products on every clean
  • No lingering chemical smell after the clean

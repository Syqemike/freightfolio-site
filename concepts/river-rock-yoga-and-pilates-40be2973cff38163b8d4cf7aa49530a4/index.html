About

River Rock Yoga and Pilates is a yoga and Pilates studio in Ocean Springs, in business since 2006. The studio runs more than 25 classes a week with more than 15 certified instructors, for all levels.

Classes and offerings

All Levels Vinyasa Flow

Flowing yoga for all levels, part of a weekly schedule of more than 25 classes.

Gentle, Restorative, and Yin

Gentle and Gentle Flow classes, Restorative sessions, and Yin practice.

Heated Hatha

Heated Hatha in a far infrared hot studio.

PiYo and children's yoga

PiYo classes that mix Pilates and yoga, plus yoga for children.

Mat Pilates and hoop dancing

Mat Pilates classes and hoop dancing sessions.

Private lessons

Private lessons by arrangement.

Workshops and donation events

Concerts, workshops, and donation-based events such as Yoga for Recovery and Reiki shares.

Teacher training

A Yoga Alliance 200-hour teacher training run since 2007, led by Moira Anderson and Laura Tyree.

Counseling and wellness

Mental-health counseling with Moira Anderson, LCSW. Massage, sauna, salt lamp, and water therapy are listed in the studio's listing description.

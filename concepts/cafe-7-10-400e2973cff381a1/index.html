A mother-daughter kitchen.

Cafe 7-10 is run by a mother-daughter team who bake everything with the kind of care you can taste. Regulars know the weekly rotating menu by heart and plan their visits around it.

It is the rare downtown spot that feels like a family kitchen: warm, unhurried, and generous with portions.

Pie flights

Can't pick one? Don't.

A pie flight is four or six slices to go, packed up so you can taste across the case. It is the easy way to try the signatures, settle a flavor debate, or bring home dessert for the whole table.

Flights are available in the shop on Fridays and for Saturday pre-order pickup at Riverside Arts Market. Call ahead with 24 hours notice and a 4pm pre-order cutoff, and your flight will be boxed and ready.

Whole pies

Feed the whole table

Whole pies are made for pre-order: birthdays, holidays, office lunches, or just a Friday worth remembering. Pre-order cutoff is 4pm with 24 hours notice. Call the shop and tell them which pie you want.

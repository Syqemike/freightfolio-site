Signature class

"O.M.G. Becky Look At That Crust!"

The shop's famous crust class. You mix, roll, and crimp your own pie crust, and the bakers walk you through the tricks that keep their crusts flaky. Class dates and details change by session, so call the shop to ask about the next one.

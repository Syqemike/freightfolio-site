Slice Society

The pie-of-the-month club

Join the Slice Society and a pie of the month shows up at your door with local delivery. It is the shop's standing reservation with dessert, no remembering required.

How the club works

  • A pie of the month, picked by the shop's bakers
  • Delivered locally in the Jacksonville area
  • Call or email to ask how to join

Details like delivery days and current membership terms change, so call (904) 206-9932 or email Info@mixedfillingspieshop.com to ask how to join.

Woman-owned and Jacksonville grown

Mixed Fillings is woman-owned. Chef Natasha Burton is Le Cordon Bleu trained and a seventh-generation Floridian; she founded the shop with Curtis Giebler. The pies carry their names, their humor, and their standards.

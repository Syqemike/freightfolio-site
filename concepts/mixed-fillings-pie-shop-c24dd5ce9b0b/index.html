The shop

Pies that put Jacksonville on the map

Mixed Fillings started with one idea: pie should be fun. The shop is woman-owned, led by Chef Natasha Burton, a Le Cordon Bleu trained chef and seventh-generation Floridian, with Curtis Giebler. The pies are handmade, the names are playful, and the regulars plan their Fridays around the shop window.

Order a whole pie, pick up a flight of slices to go, take a baking class, or join the Slice Society and let pie of the month deliveries handle dessert.

Fridays are pie day

The shop opens one day a week: Fridays, 10am to 5pm. Pre-orders close at 4pm with 24 hours notice, and Saturday pre-orders pick up at Riverside Arts Market from 10am to 3pm. A quick call is all it takes.

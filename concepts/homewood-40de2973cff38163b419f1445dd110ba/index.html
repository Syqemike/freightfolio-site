About

Homewood is a restored 1920s stone manor in the Montford historic district of Asheville. The venue hosts weddings, receptions, rehearsal dinners, parties, reunions, and meetings. The Great Room seats 110 for dinner and 150 for cocktails, with 200 free parking spaces and handicap-accessible entry.

The Great Room

About

Guice Electric is a residential and commercial electrician at 613 First Avenue in Ocean Springs, in business since 1994. It was founded by John Guice Sr. and is now run by his son John Guice Jr. The company belongs to the Ocean Springs Chamber, the National Association of Home Builders, and the Southeast Mississippi Home Builders Association.

About

Gulf Coast Mobile Massage Spa offers mobile massage and an in-spa location in Biloxi. The business started mobile-only in 2014, added an Edgewater Mall kiosk in 2015, and opened a full-service spa in 2016 with 5 rooms and 6 tables, staffed by licensed massage therapists.

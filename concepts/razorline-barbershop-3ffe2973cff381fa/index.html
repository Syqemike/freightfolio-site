Around the shop

A Shop the Neighborhood Reads In

Razorline is built around family. The chair fits a first-timer and a grandfather the same way, and the shop opens its doors to the community beyond haircuts.

Books with Barbers

Razorline is the first Books with Barbers literacy pilot site in Macon, run with EveryLibrary. Kids can pick up a book while they wait and read in the chair.

  • Family-friendly shop: kids and seniors are welcome
  • Books available for young readers during visits
  • Community space, not just a waiting area

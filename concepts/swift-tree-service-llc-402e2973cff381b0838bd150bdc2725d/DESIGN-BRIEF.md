# Design Brief - Swift Tree Service LLC (Cleveland, OH)

## Aesthetic
OUTDOOR RUGGED: a working tree crew's site, not a boutique. Big honest
photography, thick type, wide open sections, solid earthy color blocks. No
gradients, no glow, no decoration beyond a rough bark-tone divider strip.

## Typography
Archivo (display, weights 800-900, tight tracking) for headlines and the
wordmark. Inter (400/500/600) for body and UI. No italics, no serif anywhere.

## Palette
- Pine green #14532d - header, footer, primary buttons
- Bark brown #6b4a2f - accent band, divider, quote CTA
- Sky #dbe9f0 - service-area section background, card tint
- Cream #faf8f2 - page background, content sections
- Ink #1c2420 - body text

## Imagery plan
Real photos of THIS business were not obtainable (no business photos on the
unclaimed Birdeye profile; Facebook 403-gated; no owned site). Five
freely-licensed tree-service photographs from Wikimedia Commons are used, each
captions-labeled "Stock photo", with a Commons credit line in the footer.
- Hero: arborist with climbing gear beside a tree (hero-climber.jpg)
- Tree removal: crane grapple dropping wood chips (removal-felling.jpg)
- Trimming: climber high in a bare tree (trim-surgeon.jpg)
- Cleanup: chipper truck and crew (cleanup-chipper.jpg)
- Gallery extra: climber atop tree with crew and chipper (felling-crew.jpg)

## Layout
Sticky dark-green header: wordmark + phone CTA. Pages: Home (full-bleed hero,
4 service cards, "Why Swift" trust band, service area, hours/location teaser,
final Call CTA), Services (detail sections per service), Gallery (5 photos with
stock-photo captions), Visit (address, hours table, directions link, phone).
Footer: address, phone, hours, disclosure, photo credits. No booking form.

## Customer journey
Land on hero: who, what, where. Scan trust band: family-owned since 2013,
4.9 from 267 Google reviews, free quotes, full cleanup. Pick a service or jump
to Visit for hours and directions. Every page ends with one action:
"Call for a free quote" (tel:+12169249978).

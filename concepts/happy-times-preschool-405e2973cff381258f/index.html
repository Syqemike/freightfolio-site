Happy Times Preschool is a half-day, play-based preschool in Huntsville for ages 3, 4, and 5. Its core values are social, emotional, cognitive, physical, and spiritual growth. Days include fun learning themes, creative snacks, puppet shows, and rainy-day gym fun. The 4-year-old class ends with individual kindergarten-readiness feedback from Mrs. Debbie.

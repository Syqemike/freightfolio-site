About the shop

A family shop on Avenue D

Amys Nails is a small, family owned nail salon in Katy, Texas. You will find it at 811 Avenue D, open six days a week starting at 8:45 in the morning.

Four techs work the shop. The menu is short on purpose: manicures, pedicures, dip powder, gel lacquer removal, and ingrown toenail care. Prices sit on the inexpensive side, and walk-ins are welcome any day the shop is open.

“Hello! We are a small family owned nail salon in Katy Tx w/ 4 techs who are more than happy & willing to assist/welcome anyone who’s in need of a mani/pedi”

The shop, in its own words, from its Google listing

That is the whole pitch. A neighborhood shop, run by family, glad to see you. If you need your nails done in Katy, this is a straightforward place to get it handled.

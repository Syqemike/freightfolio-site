Inspirations Dance Studio · Spokane

Where movement sparks inspiration.

Inspirations Dance Studio is a nonprofit dance studio in Spokane, founded in 2014 by Mikaela Ostrander and her mother Janet. Classes run for ages 2 to adult, from tiny stars to grown-ups, with an emphasis on creativity, community, and compassion.

Inside the studio

Classes in session at 3618 E Broadway Ave in Spokane.

A studio built on community

Mikaela Ostrander founded Inspirations Dance Studio with her mother Janet in 2014. Janet retired in June 2022, and the studio carries on as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, with Mikaela Ostrander and Marlina D’Angelo as co-directors and Josie D’Angelo as lead dance teacher.

The studio meets each student where they are. New students can try one class free before enrolling.

One free trial class

Call the studio to set up a first class for your dancer.

Private lessons

Private lessons are $40 an hour. Bungee Fitness is also offered at the studio.

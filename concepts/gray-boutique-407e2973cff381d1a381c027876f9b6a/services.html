What we carry

Gray Boutique is a women's boutique in downtown Pensacola, carrying curated, timeless, quality wardrobe pieces: oversized button-downs, linen dresses, chambray shorts, silk-velvet dresses, crochet sets, blazers, and mules. Owner and stylist Katy Nagel offers personal and private shopping appointments.

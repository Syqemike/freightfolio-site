About

A neighborhood bakery

Anna's Pies sits on Moeling Street in Lake Charles, a small bakery with a devoted local following. The menu is short on purpose: pies, cakes, and baked goods made to be remembered.

If you are picking up dessert for dinner or feeding a crowd, call ahead and they will tell you what is coming out of the oven.

About

FishEye Studios is a commercial photography studio in Greenville, run by owner John Brooker. The studio works on location and in a 5,000 square foot downtown studio, making photographs for marketing materials and websites. Work covers product, food, architectural, fashion and model, lifestyle, aerial, conceptual, corporate, and headshot photography.

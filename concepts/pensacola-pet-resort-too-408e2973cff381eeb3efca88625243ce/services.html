Services

A stay for cats and small dogs.

Pensacola Pet Resort Too takes cats and dogs 40 pounds and under. Bring your own food, treats, and toys from home. Every arrival gets a complimentary wellness exam.

01

Overnight dog boarding

Dogs stay overnight in large suites. Boarding is by phone, and the office keeps the hours listed on the Visit page.

02

Private Suites

Private Suites are an upgrade for more private accommodations, or for two dogs staying together.

03

Doggie day care

Day care for puppies and seniors, with play time on the fenced turf yard under the shade sail.

04

Cat boarding

Cat boarding is available at the resort. Call to ask about a stay for your cat.

05

Grooming

A grooming center staffed by five professional groomers. Call the grooming line at +1 850-434-1183.

06

Medication administration

Medication administration is available for an extra charge. Tell the resort what your pet needs when you call to reserve.

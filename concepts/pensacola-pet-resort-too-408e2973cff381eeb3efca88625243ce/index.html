East Hill Historic District, Pensacola

A pet resort for cats and small dogs under 40 pounds.

Pensacola Pet Resort Too sits in a green house in the East Hill Historic District, one block south of Cervantes. It is the first and only pet resort in the Pensacola area for cats and dogs 40 pounds and under. The resort is owned and operated by Dr. Hall of East Hill Animal Hospital, and it is part of the Pensacola Pets group, in business more than 20 years.

What Bright Eyed Brew Co. offers

Bright Eyed Brew Co. is a family-owned brew company on the Mississippi Gulf Coast, started as the Coast's first pop-up coffee cart. The shop serves nitro cold brew on tap and house-made syrups, including the banana foster latte, lavender cream latte, horchata latte, and maple bourbon latte, plus Belgian waffles. There is a pet-friendly patio.

The Pub

A Proper Dive Bar

The Ugly Pirate is a small pub and cafe on Demontluzin Avenue, dressed head to toe in pirate and nautical memorabilia. The bar is tiny. The kitchen turns out artisan pizzas, gyros, and subs. On Saturdays there is live music. On Fridays there are turtle races. That is the whole business plan, and it works.

# Design Brief: The Ugly Pirate

## Business in one line
Bay St. Louis' first official pirate pub and cafe: a tiny dive bar covered head to toe in pirate and nautical memorabilia, serving artisan pizzas, gyros, subs, and craft beer on tap, with Friday turtle races and Saturday live music.

## Aesthetic
Rugged nautical tavern. Aged ship's deck meets hand-lettered tavern signage. Deep sea navy backgrounds, aged wood browns, brass hardware, parchment ledgers. Playful pirate personality done premium: the restraint is in the typography and generous spacing, the playfulness is in the words and the real photos of the memorabilia walls.

## Typography
- **Pirata One** (display, sparing): the wordmark and big section titles. Blackletter pirate flavor; never body text.
- **IM Fell English** (subheads, menu ledger, quotes): weathered serif that reads like a ship's log.
- **Manrope** (small labels, nav, hours, captions): clean sans for legibility at small sizes.

## Palette
- Sea navy: #0B1A2B (page ground), #071221 (deep sections)
- Aged wood: #6B4A2F, #8A6238 (borders, masthead accents)
- Brass: #C9A35C (rules, buttons, dividers)
- Parchment: #EAD9B8 / #F2E8CF (ledger cards)
- Ink on parchment: #2A2118
- Foam white text: #EFE6D0

## Imagery
Four REAL photos of the business (provenance in PROVENANCE.txt): the bar counter with its memorabilia wall (hero), beer taps with the cash-only sign, interior seating with the tall-ship painting, bar corner with the ATM. Photos are presented as framed prints on the navy ground, with honest captions. Zero drawn illustrations anywhere, per the no-concept-art directive.

## Layout (deliberately NOT a template)
- Disclosure band, then a masthead styled like a carved wooden sign board: wordmark centered, brass nail dots, a nav row set like hand-painted planks.
- Hero: full-bleed real photo with a parchment "ship's notice" plaque pinned over it, torn paper edge below (CSS clip-path, no images).
- The menu is a two-column tavern ledger on parchment with dotted leaders, not cards.
- Events are two torn-edged parchment notices pinned on the navy ground.
- Hours live in a brass-ruled ledger table; footer repeats address, phone, hours.
- Texture comes from CSS only: subtle repeating grain, torn edges, brass rules. No gradients pretending to be art, no blobs, no pill buttons.

## Customer journey
1. Land: disclosure, wordmark, tagline, hero photo tells them instantly it is a real pirate dive bar.
2. Scroll: intro copy + rating, the galley (menu), the ship's notices (events), practical notes (cash only, ATM, pet-friendly patio, bar open later).
3. Act: Call the pub (tel: link), find the address and hours, see the full menu and events pages.
4. Sub-pages: menu.html (full ledger), events.html (turtle races + live music), visit.html (address, directions, hours, contact).

The Goings-On

Two standing traditions keep the pub lively every week, and the patio keeps the crew comfortable between them.

Friday Night Turtle Races

Every Friday night the turtles race. It is exactly as silly and fun as it sounds. Grab a pizza, grab a beer, and pick your turtle.

Every Friday Night

Live Music Saturdays

The Ugly Pirate is part of the Gulf Coast live-music scene. Local musicians play the pub every Saturday.

Pet-Friendly Patio

The outdoor tables are dog friendly. Bring the four-legged members of your crew and settle in outside.

The Memorabilia Walls

The walls are the main attraction: pirate flags, ship wheels, old signs, and nautical oddities collected over the years. Walk in and look around.

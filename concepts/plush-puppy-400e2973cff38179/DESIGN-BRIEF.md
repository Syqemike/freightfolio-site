# Design Brief: Plush Puppy (Lemoyne, PA)

## Business read (verified facts only)
- Pastel boutique dog salon, 702 State St, Lemoyne, PA 17043, (717) 975-5531.
- 4.6 from 13 Google reviews. Hours verified: Tue-Sat 8 AM to 1 PM, closed Sun/Mon; appointment-only, mornings-only.
- Verified services: luxury baths with hypoallergenic shampoos, breed-specific cuts, nail trims, ear cleaning, de-matting, teeth brushing; add-ons: flea treatment, pawdicures, spritzes; loyalty program. No prices.
- No real website, no socials found. No real business photos; all imagery is labeled stock photography.

## Design personality
Soft, playful, boutique. A pastel salon that should feel like a candy shop for dogs: round, cheerful, unpretentious.

## Art direction
- Palette: blush pink (#ffd9e3), mint (#d9f0e6), cream (#fffaf4), plum text (#7b4b5e), sunny yellow (#ffcf6e) sticker. No gradients.
- Typography: Baloo 2 (rounded, playful display) for headlines; Nunito for body.
- Hero: card composition (blush card with headline left, photo right with a rotated yellow rating sticker).
- Section order: Hero / Services (colored rounded tiles + dashed add-on cards + loyalty band) / Gallery / Hours (plum box) / Visit (blush card).
- Texture: pill buttons, dashed borders, rotated sticker, big border radii. No illustrations, no SVG art.

## Differentiation
The only playful/rounded design in the batch and the only one using a sticker treatment for the rating. Baloo 2 is unlike every other headline face. Pastel palette is unique. Tile-based services (6 tiles + 3 add-ons) differ from the list/menu treatments on the other sites.

## Copy rules honored
No em/en dashes. No banned AI phrases. Rating shown exactly once (hero sticker: "4.6 from 13 Google reviews"). Services use the exact verified item list; add-ons and loyalty program included as verified. Hours shown exactly as verified with appointment-only mornings-only note. No urgency or booking promises. CTAs: "Call to book" to tel:+17179755531.

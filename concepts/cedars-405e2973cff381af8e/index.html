A historic mansion venue

Cedars is a historic 1840s mansion event venue in Jackson, operated by the Fondren Renaissance Foundation. It hosts outdoor weddings beneath a magnolia tree, an art gallery, and Symphony at Sunset. Rental packages run 1-day $1,500, 2-day $2,500, and 3-day $3,500, per the foundation rental guide. Call to ask about dates.

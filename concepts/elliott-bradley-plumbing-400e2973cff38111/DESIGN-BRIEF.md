## Creative direction
LEGACY TRADESMAN. Deep navy (#16305b) + copper (#c06a2c) + cream. Zilla Slab display + Public Sans body. Heritage banner (family owned since 1959), copper emergency band, left-ruled service cards, dark legacy section with stat blocks. Sturdy and trustworthy; nothing like the darkroom studio or blush salon concepts.

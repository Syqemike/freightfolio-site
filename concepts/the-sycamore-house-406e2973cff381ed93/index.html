The Sycamore House is a family-run upscale French-American restaurant in Bay St. Louis since 2002. Chefs Michael Eastham and Stella LeGardeur are CIA trained, per the own site. Dishes named in research include shrimp and grits, poisson almondine, aged tenderloin, creme brulee, and chocolate pot de creme. A full bar and private dining are available, with reservations and takeout. Call to ask about a reservation.

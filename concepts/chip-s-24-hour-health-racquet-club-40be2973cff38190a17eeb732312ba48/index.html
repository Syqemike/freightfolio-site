About

Chip's 24 Hour Health and Racquet Club is a gym in Gulf Breeze, Florida, run by owner Chip Holston. The current 10,000 square foot facility opened in April 2000, and the club has been in business for 25 years. Members get 24-hour access, and the club offers racquetball, basketball, and volleyball, a powerlifting room, a ladies-only room, a cardio theater, classes, and a dry sauna. The club's own site is a short Squarespace page, so this concept lists the facilities plainly.

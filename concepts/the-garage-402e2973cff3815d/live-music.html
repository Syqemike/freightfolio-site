WHAT’S ON

The calendar runs on bands, bikes, and Mobile tradition.

Weekend Bands

Local bands take the corner most Friday and Saturday nights. No cover theatrics, just a loud room and cold beer.

Bike Nights

Bikers are part of the regular crowd. Bike nights bring the lot full of chrome and the patio full of people.

Mardi Gras Season

Downtown Mobile is parade central, and the Garage sits right in it. Expect packed nights and a rolled-up door.

The Patio Set

Good weather moves the night outside. String up the evening on the patio between sets.

The Room

Built like a garage. Runs like a venue.

The roll-up storefront is the whole personality: concrete, steel, and a room that opens to the street. Bands set up close, the crowd stands close, and nobody is checking their watch.

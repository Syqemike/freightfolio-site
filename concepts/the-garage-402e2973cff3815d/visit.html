What to expect

A dive bar crowd: regulars, bikers, and music fans. Pool tables up front, patio out back, and the garage door rolled open when the weather allows.

Kitchen runs bar bites like spam fries and crawfish. Happy hour keeps drinks cheap. Bands play most weekends; Mardi Gras season is the loudest time of year.

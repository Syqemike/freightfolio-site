Our story

A Mobile bakery since 2010.

Flour Girls Bakery has been baking custom cakes, cupcakes, and treats on Hillcrest Road for over 15 years. What started as a neighborhood bakery is now one of the most reviewed cake shops on the Gulf Coast.

We keep it simple: real ingredients, designs matched to your celebration, and prices that stay reasonable. Order ahead for custom work or stop by for something from the case.

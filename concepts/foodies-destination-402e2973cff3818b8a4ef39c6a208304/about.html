Foodies Destination · Metairie, LA

The Truck

A Korean food truck serving comfort food in Metairie, Louisiana. The side of the truck says it plainly: Korean Style Kitchen.

Black truck. Bold food.

The Foodies Destination truck is hard to miss: black paint, big yellow lettering, and order and pick-up windows built for a fast line. The menu sticks to six Korean staples done right: kalbi short ribs, bulgogi, spicy pork, chicken teriyaki, don katsu, and chicken katsu.

Everything is packed for takeaway. The truck runs a short weekly schedule, so check the hours before you go.

Order at the window.

Walk up to the order window, place your order, and pick it up when it is ready. For catering or large orders, call ahead at (504) 330-5545 so the crew can plan.

The truck also posts updates on Facebook at @koreanfoodnola.

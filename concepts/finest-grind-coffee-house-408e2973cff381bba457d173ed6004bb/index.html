A family-owned coffee house in Ocean Springs since 2008, run by the Triplett family.

Finest Grind Coffee House is a family-owned coffee house in Ocean Springs, opened in 2008 by Alan and Dori Triplett and now run by daughters Kelley and Kathy and son-in-law Aaron. The menu covers quality espresso, colorful energy drinks, dirty sodas, teas, refreshers, waffles, sandwiches, salads, ice cream, and baked items. The chicken caprese panini is a customer favorite. Pickup ordering is offered through Toast, with delivery through DoorDash and Uber Eats.

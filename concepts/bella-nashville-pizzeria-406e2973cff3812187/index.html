A pizzeria built around the oven

Bella Nashville Pizzeria is a wood-fired pizzeria in Nashville. Its dough is hand-kneaded with natural leaven, its oven is fired at 900 degrees, and its mozzarella is made in house. Pizzas named in research include prosciutto di parma, pepperoni, sausage, and Capricciosa. Weekly specials use local produce, and catering is noted.

Mississippi weather

Built for the long summer

On the Gulf Coast, the cooling season stretches across most of the year. Heat and humidity put long hours on every system, so the ones that get regular attention are the ones that hold up.

  • 01

    Summer arrives early and stays late

    Cooling season here runs far longer than it does up north, which means local systems log far more hours every year.

  • 02

    Humidity does its own damage

    Heavy Gulf air makes cooling harder work, and it strains a system that is already struggling.

  • 03

    A checkup before the heavy season

    A maintenance visit ahead of the hottest months catches small issues while they are still small.

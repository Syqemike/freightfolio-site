Our story
1942

A South Omaha institution

The bakery's roots go back to 1942, and the little shop on South 10th Street has been doing things the same careful way ever since. Family owned and operated, budget-friendly, and loved by word of mouth.

Step inside and it feels charmingly frozen in time: vintage display cases, familiar faces, and donuts that regulars drive across town for.

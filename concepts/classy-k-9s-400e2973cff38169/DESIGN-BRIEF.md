# Design Brief: Classy K-9'S (New Cumberland, PA)

## Business read (verified facts only)
- Full-service grooming salon, 113 Old York Rd, New Cumberland, PA, (717) 350-9513.
- 5.0 from 49 Google reviews. Hours verified: Tue-Sat 9 AM to 5 PM, closed Sun/Mon.
- Moderate pricing, no menu or prices published. No real website, no socials found.
- No real business photos exist; all imagery is labeled stock photography.

## Design personality
A classic, confident salon. "Classy" is in the name, so the design reads like a well-kept shopfront: traditional, orderly, proud.

## Art direction
- Palette: warm paper (#f8f4ec), burgundy (#5c1a1b), charcoal ink (#26221d), gold accent (#c99a3f). No gradients.
- Typography: DM Serif Display for headlines; Inter for body.
- Hero: editorial split (copy left, tall photo right), thin gold rule above the headline.
- Section order: Hero (with rating line) / Services (numbered white cards on sand band) / Gallery (asymmetric grid) / Hours (white table) / Visit (two-tone contact cards).
- Texture: double-rule masthead, serif numerals. No illustrations, no SVG art.

## Differentiation
Split editorial hero is unlike the full-bleed hero on Patti and Barkery, unlike the card-composition hero on Plush Puppy, and unlike the rounded hero frame on Doggie D'Tails. Burgundy/paper pairing is unique in this batch. Numbered service cards and a captioned hours table give it a classic-salon feel none of the others have.

## Copy rules honored
No em/en dashes. No banned AI phrases. Rating shown exactly once (hero line: "5.0 from 49 Google reviews"). Services stay within "full-service grooming salon" (complete grooms, bath and blow out, breed cuts and styling, nail care). Hours shown exactly as verified. CTAs: "Call to book" to tel:+17173509513.

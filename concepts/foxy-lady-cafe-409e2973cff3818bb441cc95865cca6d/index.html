About

Foxy Lady Cafe is a licensed caterer in Jacksonville's Historic Springfield neighborhood, serving non-traditional Southern comfort food. The kitchen is known for chicken salad, ribs, fried chicken, and waffles, and for desserts that include pound cake, sweet potato cheesecake pound cake, and red velvet banana pudding. Catering and specialty desserts are available alongside the cafe menu.

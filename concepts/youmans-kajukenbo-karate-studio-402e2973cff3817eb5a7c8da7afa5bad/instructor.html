Art: Kajukenbo

Grand Master Youmans holds a 9th degree black belt and has trained in the martial arts for more than 35 years. He teaches Kajukenbo at the studio on Main Street in Springfield, Massachusetts.

Kajukenbo demands an instructor who has lived inside all four of its roots. The karate striking, the judo throws, the kenpo hand work and the kung-fu movement each need a teacher who knows where they fit. That is the training the studio offers.

Specific questions about rank history, teaching experience or class format are best asked directly. Call the studio and talk to the instructor.

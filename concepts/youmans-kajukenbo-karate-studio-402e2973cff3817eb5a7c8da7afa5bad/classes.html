02 · Classes

Train all four roots.

Every session at Youmans Kajukenbo Karate Studio draws on the full hybrid: striking, grappling, hand technique and evasive movement. One art, studied as a whole.

Root one

Striking, from karate

Kajukenbo keeps karate's hard, powerful techniques as its foundation. Students build a solid base in kicks and strikes before the rest is layered on.

Root two

Throws and holds, from judo

The judo and ju-jitsu side of the art covers throwing, joint locks and sweeps. Training teaches what to do when a confrontation closes the distance.

Root three

Fluid hand work, from kenpo

Kenpo adds multiple, flowing hand techniques. It softens the hard karate base with speed and combination work at close range.

Root four

Movement, from kung-fu

Chinese boxing brings flexibility, agility, parrying and evasive movement. It keeps the system mobile and adaptable instead of rigid.

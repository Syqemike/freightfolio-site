The school

Kajukenbo was created when five black belts from different martial arts backgrounds trained together to build one practical fighting art. That idea still shapes every class at the studio: take what works, discard the rest, and keep training.

What the name carries

Four letters. Four arts.

Kajukenbo is a compound word. Each syllable names one of the arts that went into it.

KA

Long life · Karate

The karate root brings hard, powerful technique: the striking foundation of the system.

JU

Happiness · Judo and Ju-jitsu

The judo root adds throwing, joint locks and sweeps, the grappling side of the art.

KEN

Fist · Kenpo

The kenpo root contributes multiple, fluid hand techniques layered over the harder movements.

BO

Style · Chinese boxing

The Chinese boxing root, kung-fu, brings flexibility, agility, parrying and evasive movement that flows together.

Put together, the school teaches the Kajukenbo motto: through this fist style one gains long life and happiness.

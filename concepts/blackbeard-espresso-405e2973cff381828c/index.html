Locally roasted beans on West Main Street

A coffee shop on West Main Street in downtown Louisville's historic 7th block, serving locally roasted beans, Cuban cafe con leche, cold brew and nitro cold brew, iced lattes, and breakfast sandwiches and pastries.

From truck to Main Street

Blackbeard Espresso began as a coffee truck in 2013 and opened its brick-and-mortar shop at 718 W Main St in April 2018. The shop serves locally roasted beans with outdoor seating, free Wi-Fi, takeout, and delivery.

The model, stated plainly

Most studios hire one therapist and sell the brand. Here the structure is reversed: several independent therapists share one location, and each one practices in their own way. When you call, describe what you are dealing with and you will be matched with a therapist in the collective.

Meet the collective

Therapeutic massage and bodywork

The work here is therapeutic: deliberate, focused sessions rather than a hotel spa routine. The details of each session are set between you and the therapist, because no two bodies ask for the same thing.

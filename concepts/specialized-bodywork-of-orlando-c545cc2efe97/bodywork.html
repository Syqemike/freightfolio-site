Bodywork

Deliberate work, not a spa routine

The collective focuses on therapeutic massage and bodywork: sessions built around what your body actually needs, paced the way the therapist's training dictates.

What a session looks like

You arrive at the room on North Orange Avenue. The therapist asks what you are dealing with and what you want from the hour. Then the work begins: focused, unhurried, and adjusted as your tissue responds. There is no menu of add-ons and no script. The session ends when the work is done, and the therapist tells you plainly what they noticed.

The type list, kept honest

  • Therapeutic massage

    A full session of purposeful, hands-on work. Tell the therapist what hurts or what has been stiff, and the session is built around it.

  • Focused bodywork

    A session aimed at one area that needs sustained attention. Shoulders, back, hips, legs: one region, the whole hour or part of it.

Each therapist in the collective practices in their own way. Call, describe what you need, and you will be matched with a therapist in the collective.

Ready when you are

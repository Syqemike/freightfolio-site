The Collective

Several therapists. One address.

Specialized Bodywork of Orlando is a collective of independent massage therapists who share one calm location. Each therapist runs their own practice and their own schedule, under one roof.

Why it works this way

Independent therapists choose where they work. The ones who choose this room chose it because it is quiet, because the standards are high, and because clients come back. You get the care of a sole practitioner with the confidence that the address has been here a while.

Therapist spotlight

Mimi

Massage therapist, member of the collective

The name that keeps appearing in client reviews is Mimi. Reviewers mention her by name and describe sessions that stood out from anything they had booked before. She works from the same North Orange Avenue room as the rest of the collective.

First name only, as reviewers wrote it. When you call, ask for Mimi by name if you want to book with her.

# Design Brief — St Tammany Transmission

## Business facts (grounded in research)
- St Tammany Transmission, 2542 Front St, Slidell, LA 70458, (985) 641-5670
- Automotive Repair Shop / Auto Transmission; family-owned; "we are a family owned business that offers top quality work at competitive pricing"
- Hours: Mon-Fri 8:00 AM - 4:30 PM; closed weekends
- Rating: 5.0 from 10 Google reviews (shown once, index only)
- Stated service note: "FREE MOBILE SERVICE TO YOU"
- No logo, colors, website, or real photos publicly available. All page photos are labeled stock photography.

## Aesthetic
INDUSTRIAL RUGGED. Charcoal (#23262b) base, safety orange (#e8590c) accents, steel gray (#8a9199 / #3a3f45) structure, white text on dark sections, near-black footer. Flat colors only: no gradients, no glowing blobs, zero concept art.

## Typography
Oswald (condensed, uppercase display for headlines, CTAs, spec labels) + Source Sans 3 (body). Big condensed headlines, letterspaced eyebrow labels, spec-sheet numbers.

## Palette
- Charcoal #23262b (primary dark)
- Safety orange #e8590c (accents, CTA, dividers)
- Steel #3a3f45 (panels), steel light #8a9199 (secondary text)
- Off-white #f4f4f2 (light sections), white #ffffff

## Imagery plan
5 verified stock photos (all captioned "Stock photo"), all auto-shop imagery:
1. mechanic-hero.jpg — mechanic under a lifted SUV (index hero)
2. transmission-unit.webp — transmission gears close-up (index specialty + services)
3. repair-bay.jpg — mechanic under a lift in a repair bay (services)
4. mechanic-lift.webp — mechanic at service center (gallery)
5. repair-closeup.webp — mechanic working under a hood (gallery + visit)

## Layout
- Sticky dark header: business name, nav (Home / Services / Gallery / Visit), Call button.
- Hero: strong left-aligned headline block over dark photo with orange hazard-stripe divider; diagonal clip-path edge into the next section.
- Sections alternate charcoal and off-white with offset/diagonal edges (clip-path), never gradients.
- Services presented as a spec-sheet: numbered rows with orange index numbers, hairline dividers, honest descriptions from research only.
- "Free mobile service" as a full-width orange callout band.
- Service area: Slidell and surrounding St. Tammany Parish only.
- Footer: contact facts, disclosure, noindex.

## Customer journey
1. Land: business name + "Transmission and auto repair in Slidell" + Call CTA in one screen.
2. Trust: family-owned quote in their own words, single rating mention, free mobile service note.
3. Detail: spec-sheet services, gallery, visit page with hours/address/directions.
4. Action: Call CTA repeated on every page. No online booking offered (unverified), no Book Now button.

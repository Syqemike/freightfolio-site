About

Rhodes Club Fitness is a gym in Gulfport, Mississippi, at 145 Hardy Court Shopping Center, in business since 1979. Owner Doc Rhodes and manager Faith Jordan run a club with personal training, fitness classes, yoga, Jiu Jitsu, a sauna, a steam room, a hot tub, and a smoothie bar. Members have 24-hour access; staffed hours are Monday to Thursday 5:00 AM to 10:00 PM, Friday 5:00 AM to 8:00 PM, Saturday 5:00 AM to 7:00 PM, and Sunday 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Call (228) 868-0190 to ask about membership.

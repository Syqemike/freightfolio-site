Canadian Bakin

About

Canadian Bakin is an active bakery and bagel shop in downtown Huntsville. The shop hand-rolls its bagels, bakes artisan breads, builds bagel sandwiches, and serves coffee and lattes. Dine-in and takeout are available, and online ordering routes off-site to Toast.

Services

Hand-rolled bagels

Hand-rolled bagels: plain, everything, blueberry, garlic, sesame, poppyseed, cinnamon raisin, chocolate chip, plus a weekly specialty.

Artisan breads

Artisan breads: sourdough, brioche, baguettes and pretzels.

Bagel sandwiches

Bagel sandwiches including the Q-Becco (bacon, egg, cheddar, caramelized onion), Lox and Loaded, Notorious BLT, The Down Payment avocado toast and The Fathead.

Coffee and lattes

Coffee and lattes including the Miel Latte and Vanilla Bourbon Latte.

Dine-in and takeout

Dine-in and takeout at the downtown shop.

Online ordering through Toast

Online ordering routes off-site to Toast.

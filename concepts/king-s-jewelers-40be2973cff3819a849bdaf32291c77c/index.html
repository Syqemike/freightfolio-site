About

King's Jewelers is a jewelry store at 1611 Main Street in Columbia, South Carolina. The store carries GIA-certified diamonds, loose and mounted diamonds, and fine jewelry including diamond earrings, pendants, bracelets, watches, gold, and silver. Custom design and expert jewelry and watch repair are offered, and specialized appraisals are stated in the listing description. The store's own site is a thin, outdated page, so this concept shows what a fuller site could look like.

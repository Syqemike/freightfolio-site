About

Goodman Coffee Roasters is a small batch roastery and cafe at 1110 Market Street in the Warehouse Row area of Chattanooga, Tennessee. This concept covers the Warehouse Row location only. The roastery also has a second shop in St. Elmo, with its own address and phone number, which is not covered here.

Menu

Drip and roast of the day

The daily drip coffee, poured from the current roast.

Espresso drinks

Espresso, cappuccino, latte, and breve, made to order.

Cold brew and nitro cold brew

Cold brew served still or on nitro tap.

Seasonal lattes

Seasonal drinks such as peaches and cream, iced lavender white mocha, and spiced orange mocha.

Teas and pastries

Teas alongside scones, muffins, croissants, and bagel sandwiches.

Retail beans and wholesale

Beans to take home, plus wholesale for other businesses. Ask in the shop about current roasts.

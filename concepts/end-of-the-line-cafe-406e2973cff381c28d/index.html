A vegan cafe, downtown for over 20 years

End of the Line Cafe is a vegan cafe with a full coffee and espresso bar in downtown Pensacola for over 20 years. Its menu includes the Oyster Mushroom Gyro, Sriracha Mac & Cheese, Knuckle Sandwich, Muffuletta, Buddha Burger, falafel, and salads, with brunch, lunch, and dinner, patio seating, catering, and gluten-free options. The own site is a thin page with a short story and two dish names and has no prices or online ordering, so call with questions.

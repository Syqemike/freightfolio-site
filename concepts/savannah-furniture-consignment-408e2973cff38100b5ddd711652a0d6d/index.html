A showroom that started as a side hustle

Tracy Eckard started Savannah Furniture Consignment in 2010 from garage sales and auction hunting. What began as a side hustle grew into one of Savannah's furniture consignment destinations, with over 7,000 square feet of showroom on the second floor of 7501 Abercorn Street. The store carries high-quality, gently used furniture and home decor, and it is open seven days a week. Its website is updated with new inventory daily.

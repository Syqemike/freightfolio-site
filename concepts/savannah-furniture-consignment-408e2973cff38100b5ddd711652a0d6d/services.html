Services

The store carries high-quality, gently used furniture and home decor, and it takes consignments for downsizing or updating a house. New inventory arrives daily, so the floor changes as pieces come in.

01

Dining-room furniture

Dining tables and chairs arrive through consignment and join the floor as new inventory comes in daily.

02

Bedroom furniture

Bedroom pieces arrive through consignment, and the bedroom floor turns over as new inventory arrives daily.

03

Living-room furniture

Sofas, sectionals and armchairs fill the living-room floor, with new pieces arriving daily.

04

Home decor

Mirrors, lamps, vases, artwork and rugs finish a room, and new decor arrives with the daily intake.

05

Consignment for downsizing

When you downsize a house, the store takes your furniture and home decor on consignment.

06

Consignment for updating a house

When you update a house, the store takes the pieces you are replacing on consignment.

New inventory daily

New pieces reach the showroom floor every day, and the website is updated with new inventory daily.

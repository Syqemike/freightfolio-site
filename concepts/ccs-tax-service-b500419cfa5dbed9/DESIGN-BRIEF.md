# Design Brief — CC's TAX Service (ccs-tax-service-b500419cfa5dbed9)

Tax preparation since 1996 ("Tax Refund Specialist since 1996" tagline, per their Facebook). 3735 Pontchartrain Dr, Slidell, LA. Phone (985) 643-0909.
Services: personal and business returns, electronic filing and mail-in, multiple years' returns, amended returns.
Hours: Mon-Fri 9-6; Sat 10-3; Sun closed. Rating: "5.0 stars from 14 reviews on its business listing" (shown once).
No standalone website; no verified online booking: all CTAs "Call to book" tel:+19856430909.

## Aesthetic
Trustworthy professional. Classic, established, no gimmicks. "Since 1996" as the trust anchor throughout.

## Typography
Display: "Libre Baskerville" (classic serif). Body: "Source Sans 3". Gold "Serving Slidell since 1996" badge.

## Palette
- Forest green: #1e4d3f (services band), deep #143528 (topbar/footer)
- Gold: #c9a227 (buttons, accents), soft #e8d189
- Cream: #faf7ef (body), white cards, hairline #e3dccb

## Layout
1. Top bar: business name, "Design preview" chip, Call button.
2. Sticky nav: Services, Why us, Visit, Contact.
3. Hero: two-column. Left: "Serving Slidell since 1996" badge, serif headline, tagline, lede with address, rating line, Call to book + See services. Right: tax desk photo with stock caption.
4. Services: forest band, 2x2 cards (Personal returns, Business returns, Multiple years, Amended returns) with gold-bordered cards.
5. Trust: "Three decades of tax seasons" editorial section, "30" stat (2026-1996), photo of return review.
6. Visit: address, hours table, Google Maps link; "Talk to a tax preparer" contact card (call only).
7. Footer: disclosure (labeled stock photos), "Concept by Freightfolio" + freightfolio.net.

## Customer journey
Visitor sees longevity and services, checks hours, calls with questions or to book. Primary CTA: Call (985) 643-0909.

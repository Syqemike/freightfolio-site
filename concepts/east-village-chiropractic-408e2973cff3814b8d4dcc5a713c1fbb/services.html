Services

Consultations

Your first visit starts with a review of your health complaints and a discussion of methods and plans to get you back on track. If another provider or specialist would be a better fit, you get a recommendation for that instead.

Comprehensive chiropractic care

Several adjustment techniques for injuries, with every adjustment tailored to each patient, from more aggressive approaches to significantly lighter methods.

School physicals

Physicals for students, offered at the clinic. Call to ask about scheduling one.

Health education

Alongside adjustments, the clinic provides health education as part of its care, in the same warm and caring atmosphere it describes on its own site.

How visits work

Walk-ins are welcome for established patients. New patients need a scheduled appointment. Last appointments start 15 minutes before closing time.

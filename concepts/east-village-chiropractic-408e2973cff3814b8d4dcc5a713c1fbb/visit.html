Visit the clinic

300 E. Locust St., Suite 140, Des Moines, IA 50309. The clinic is in the Metro Waste Authority building, a red brick building across E. Locust St. from the Staybridge Hotel. There is no sign for the office on the exterior of the building. You will find the clinic right inside on the first floor, from either the north or south facing doors. Remember the East when you use a map app to find it.

Parking

The clinic has a free parking lot on the north side of its building, but spaces are limited. The spaces are labeled on the concrete within each space. Four visitor spots can be used if one is available, and the office spot is spot 11. Two handicapped spaces are available for visitors with the appropriate tags. Do not park in any other numbered spots. A towing company patrols the lot. If the lot spaces are unavailable, the other options are on-street parking or the parking ramp across the street.

# Design Brief — Delmar Doggie Design (St. Louis, MO)

## Design personality
Master-craft atelier. Warm charcoal + marigold + ivory editorial. Credential-led:
Christina Duncan's National Certified Master Groomer status and award-winning
competition record are the centerpiece. Refined, confident, gallery-forward.
No purple gradients, no clip art, no illustrations.

## Typography
- Display: "Fraunces" (expressive editorial serif), large ivory headlines on
  charcoal or charcoal on ivory.
- Body: "Karla" for clean, warm readability.
- Hierarchy: oversized display headline > marigold eyebrow labels > structured
  credential list with hairline dividers.

## Palette
- Charcoal: #26211c (warm, not black)
- Marigold: #e09a1c
- Ivory: #faf6ec
- Soft stone: #efe7d6 for panels

## Imagery (REAL business photos, downloaded from the public gallery)
- ddd_2.jpg — wiry-haired dog on the grooming table, tools behind (hero)
- ddd_1.jpg — brindle dog on the table, salon interior behind (gallery)
- ddd_4.jpg — Delmar Blvd storefront at night, business sign visible in window (visit/gallery)
Provenance: public gallery for Delmar Doggie Design on bestprosintown.com.
A fourth gallery image (a snowy yard) was unrelated to the business and excluded.
No stock photos on this page.

## Layout
- Disclosure bar (charcoal strip) at top, sticky section nav below.
- Hero: split editorial on ivory, real photo right, "Call to book" CTA.
- Pedigree section: full-bleed charcoal, Christina Duncan centerpiece with three
  credential lines (National Certified Master Groomer; award-winning competition
  groomer; operating since 2008).
- Hand-stripping specialty panel: stone background, honest description.
- Services: pet and show grooming for all breeds; hand-stripping; pricing not
  published, call for rates.
- Gallery: three real photos with plain, truthful captions.
- Visit: hours Tue-Sat 7:00 AM-3:30 PM, Sun/Mon closed; address; phone; rating once.
- Footer with disclaimer, provenance, rating source, prepared October 2026.

## Customer journey
Land on the craft story, see the credentials and the real work in the gallery,
check hours, then Call to book (tel: link). Every booking CTA is "Call to book";
no online booking is verified.

## Copy notes
- Facts used only from research: address, phone, hours, services, hand-stripping,
  since 2008, Christina Duncan name + credentials, 4.5 from 119 Google reviews.
- Rating appears exactly once on the page.
- No invented urgency, no appointment promises, no claims about how they operate.
- No invented facts, no em dashes, no banned AI phrases, no No-X-no-Y triples.

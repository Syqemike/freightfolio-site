Specialty

Hand-stripping, done by hand.

Hand-stripping is one of the salon’s specialties. It is the traditional technique for maintaining the coats of wire-coated breeds, done strand by strand instead of clipped.

Ask about it when you call if your dog’s breed calls for a stripped coat.

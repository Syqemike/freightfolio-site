A bakery on The Hill since 1924

Missouri Baking Co is a historic family-owned Sicilian and Italian bakery on The Hill in St. Louis, founded in 1924. It makes Italian cookies and pastries, cannoli including ricotta cannoli, gooey butter cake, chocolate drops, custard puffs and cream puffs, tiramisu, biscotti, amaretto cookies, and stollen, plus cakes and cupcakes that can be customized. It also offers a lunch menu, breakfast and lunch catering, and daily pastry and sandwich delivery. There is no standalone website and no online ordering portal; ordering is in person or by phone, so call with questions.

About

Concord Auto Spa is a family-owned auto detailing shop in Concord, New Hampshire, at 16 Water Street, run by owner Joey Seluk and established in 2005. The shop offers professional auto detailing, ceramic coating, paint protection film, paint correction, oxidation removal, wheels and glass coating, steam cleaning and leather care, dent and wheel repair, headlight restoration, and pet hair and odor removal. Concord Auto Spa is a 2025 member of the International Detailing Association. Hours are Monday to Friday 7:45 AM to 5:00 PM; Saturday and Sunday closed. Call (603) 225-8200 to ask about your car.

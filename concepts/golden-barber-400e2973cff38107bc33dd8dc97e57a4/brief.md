# Design Brief: Golden Barber (Twin Falls, ID) — Website Concept

## Aesthetic
"High-desert modern editorial": warm sand and paper tones with charcoal ink, a premium magazine-style identity. Warm, confident, masculine without dark-espresso cliches. Asymmetric layouts, oversized numbers, hairline rules, quiet confidence.

## Typography
- Display: Fraunces (Google Fonts), editorial serif, used at large sizes for the business name, section headers, and pull-quote typography.
- Body/labels: Manrope (Google Fonts), clean sans for body copy, hours table, nav, captions.
- No em dashes anywhere. No comma triples, no period fragments.

## Palette
- Sand paper: #f4ede1 (page background)
- Warm sand: #e6d9c3 (section washes, cards)
- Charcoal ink: #22201b (text, header bar)
- Muted clay accent: #b0703c (used sparingly: rule accents, small labels)
- No dark-espresso backgrounds; the site stays light and warm.

Every image carries a "Stock photo" caption. No public real photos of the business exist.

## Layout
1. Thin design-preview disclosure bar (Website Concept / Design Preview).
2. Header: "Golden Barber" nameplate, Twin Falls Idaho line, sticky nav (Services, Hours & Location, Contact).
3. Hero: oversized Fraunces headline with business name, location line, photo collage (one wide + one offset), CTA "Call to book" tel:+12083292486.
4. Numbered service index: 01 Fade cut, 02 Custom cut, 03 Beard maintenance, 04 Long haircut, 05 Shampoo and conditioning. Editorial list with hairline rules and one inset photo.
5. Price note callout: "clients mention fades around $25" + "call to confirm current prices."
6. Hours & location panel: asymmetric card grid with hours table (Monday Closed; Tuesday-Saturday 11:00 AM to 6:00 PM; Sunday 12:00 PM to 4:00 PM) and address 407 2nd Ave E, Twin Falls, ID 83301.
7. Pull quote section: typographic quote anchored on the only real customer fact available (the price mention), clearly framed as a client mention.
8. Contact footer: call CTA, address, phone, rating shown once ("5.0 from 21 Google reviews"), design-preview disclaimer, photo provenance line, "Concept prepared October 2026", robots noindex.

## Customer journey
Land on hero -> read services index -> check hours -> call to book. Every CTA is tel:+12083292486 labeled "Call to book". No online booking promised anywhere.

## Photo provenance
All photos are stock photos from images.unsplash.com, downloaded to img/ and verified as real image bytes. Every caption reads "Stock photo." Footer carries the provenance line.

## Copy notes
- Use only the given facts. Hours reproduced exactly.
- Price: "Clients mention fades around $25. Call to confirm current prices."
- Rating: "5.0 from 21 Google reviews" shown exactly once (footer), with exact source.
- No invented facts, no urgency, no booking promises, no invented team/services/stories.
- Banned phrases avoided; banned punctuation patterns avoided.

About

Peggy Ann Bakery is Huntsville's oldest bakery, in business since 1953. The bakery handcrafts cakes, including birthday, wedding, and specialty carrot cake, plus petit fours, cookies, cupcakes, cheese straws, and pastries. Orders are takeout.

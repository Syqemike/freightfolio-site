Poppy Seed Bakery + Cafe

A family-owned bakery and cafe in Boise, where happiness is homemade, serving house-made breakfast, lunch, and dinner at the Warehouse Food Hall.

About

Poppy Seed Bakery + Cafe is a family-owned bakery and cafe at 370 S 8th St Ste 112 in Boise, inside the Warehouse Food Hall. Owners Leanne and Mitch Meade started at a local bake sale before opening a cottage bakery. The menu runs from scones to soups, with sweet treats and savory eats, and the shop's own site is a single short paragraph plus a menu-card link, with no prices and no ordering.

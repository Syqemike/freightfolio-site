About

Lou's Jewelry is a family-owned store in Mobile, run by the Sherrer family since 1975. The shop specializes in custom design and engagement rings and does everything in between, with walk-ins welcome and no appointment needed.

What the shop does

1

Custom design and engagement rings

Custom design work and engagement rings, built in the store.

2

Pearls, diamonds, and color stones

Pearls, diamonds, and color stones in earrings, rings, bracelets, and necklaces.

3

In-store repair and manufacturing

A full-service repair shop in the store, where jewelry is manufactured and repaired, including in-house CAD and 3D printing.

Walk-ins welcome

No appointment is needed. Free jewelry cleaning and checks are offered while you visit.

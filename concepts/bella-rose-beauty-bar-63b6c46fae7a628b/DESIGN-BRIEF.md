## Aesthetic
Elegant salon menu. Deep plum + rose + gold on ivory. Menu cards with dotted price leaders. Calm, premium, feminine.

## Typography
Display: "Cormorant Garamond" (elegant serif, italic accents). Body: "Jost" (light, geometric).

## Palette
- Plum: #3b2334, deep #2a1825 (hero, booking band, footer)
- Rose: #b76e8a (buttons), pale #f6e9ee (about band)
- Gold: #c9a05a (eyebrow, secondary buttons)
- Ivory: #fbf8f4, hairline #e7d9de

## Layout
1. Top bar: business name, "Design preview" chip, Book Now.
2. Sticky nav: Services, About, Hours, Visit, Book.
3. Hero: plum. Left: eyebrow, headline "Beautiful, on your terms." (rose italic), lede, rating line, Book Now + View services (gold). Right: lash application photo, stock caption.
4. Services and pricing: ivory. Four menu cards (Lashes; Facials; Waxing; Body and makeup) with prices from the booking page; unpriced items say "book online" rather than invented prices.
5. About: blush band. Owner Nichelle Braden, review-driven copy + lash artist photo.
6. Hours: verified table + lash treatment photo.
7. Booking band: plum, centered "Reserve your appointment" + Book Now.
8. Visit: address, phone, directions link; extension procedure photos, all stock-labeled.
9. Footer: disclosure (labeled stock photos), "Concept by Freightfolio" + freightfolio.net.

## Customer journey
Visitor scans the menu and prices, checks hours, books through the verified booking page. Primary CTA: Book Now (real destination).

A historic East Hill studio

Emerald Coast Dance Conservatory is a dance school in Pensacola's historic East Hill community. Its studios have sprung marley and hardwood floors, floor-length mirrors, and portable barres. The school runs the ECDC Motion Company competitive team, plus summer programs, workshops, and master classes.

About

Sunvet Animal Wellness Clinic is a holistic veterinary clinic in Asheville. The clinic offers holistic and conventional veterinary care for dogs and cats, including cold laser therapy, dental cleanings, nutrition counseling, vaccinations, full laboratory bloodwork, QEST4 Meridian Stress Analysis, a pharmacy, and end-of-life check-ins.

Policies & Discounts

Plain terms, stated plainly.

No fine print. The practice keeps its policies short so everyone is on the same page before a session is booked.

A standing thank-you

$5 off for military and first responders.

Military members and first responders receive a $5 discount on services. Mention it when you book so the discount is applied.

Cancellation policy

What happens if plans change.

“A credit card is required to book. Cancel the same day and the charge is 50% of the service. If you do not show up, the charge is 100%.”

The practice’s cancellation policy

Life happens. If you need to move an appointment, call ahead of the day of your session and there is no charge. Same-day cancellations are charged half, and missed appointments without notice are charged in full.

Questions about a charge? Call (228) 596-9050 and ask.

Why it works this way

Your time is held for you.

When you book, that hour belongs to you and no one else. The policy keeps the schedule fair for the therapists and for every client waiting on an opening.

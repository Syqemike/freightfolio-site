Services

Customized therapeutic massage, plus esthetics.

Two offerings, done well. Every massage session is customized to the person receiving it. Esthetics services round out the practice for clients who want care for their skin too.

Therapeutic massage

Shaped around you.

This is not a one-size routine. The therapists at A Hand 2 Heal customize each session to the client: the areas that need attention, the pressure that feels right, and the pace that lets you settle in.

Many clients book on a regular rhythm and have for years. If you are new, tell the therapist what brings you in and they will take it from there.

Esthetics

Care for your skin, in the same calm place.

Alongside massage, the practice offers esthetics services. It is the same straightforward approach: professional, unhurried, and focused on what you asked for.

Call to ask about current esthetics offerings and availability.

Chapter I

The one-chair shop

Barber Brandi's Place is a one-chair barbershop on Shoshone Street, run by Brandi herself.

In October 2015, Brandi took over the shop and gave it her name. It has been her chair ever since. One barber, one chair, and the full attention that only a solo shop can give.

Men come in for traditional cuts. Kids come in too, and leave looking sharp.

Chapter II

The straight razor

Reviews praise her meticulous straight-razor shaves.

The hot towel shave is the centerpiece here. Hot towel, lather, and a straight razor handled with care. It is the old way of doing it, and reviewers say Brandi does it meticulously.

A beard trim gets the same treatment. Edges are lined up, the neck is finished with the razor, and nothing leaves the chair half done.

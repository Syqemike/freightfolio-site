# Design Brief: Barber Brandi's Place (Twin Falls, ID)

## Research facts (only facts used)
- Barber Brandi's Place, 147 Shoshone St N, Twin Falls, ID 83301; +1 208-420-4936
- Hours: Mon-Sat 9:00 AM-2:00 PM; Sun Closed
- Services: traditional haircut, hot towel shave, beard trim; solo operator Brandi (since Oct 2015)
- Reviews praise meticulous straight-razor shaves; serves men and kids
- Prices not published
- Rating: 5.0 from 16 Facebook reviews
- Real-photo attempt: Facebook page blocked for logged-out viewers; localstylist CDN photos on the listing page all belong to other nearby salons. Stock fallback used, all labeled.

## Design direction
"The one-chair craftsman": dark walnut + brass + ivory, intimate single-operator story, the straight-razor ritual as the centerpiece. Journal-like single-column flow (narrow measure, serif throughout) with full-bleed photo interludes. Women barber angle mentioned plainly, never editorialized. Quiet and confident, differentiated from Perrine's light badge layout by being dark, narrow, and chaptered.

## Typography
- Display: Cormorant Garamond (large, quiet)
- Body: EB Garamond
- Labels: letterspaced small caps (CSS), chapter numerals in Cormorant

## Palette
- Walnut #241811 (page), deeper #1A100A (interludes), Brass #C19A3F (rules, labels), Ivory #F1E8D5 (text), muted #B9A98C

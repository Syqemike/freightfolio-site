What we do

Tattoos and piercings

One shop, five artists, no rush jobs.

01Custom tattoos

Bring your idea and we will draw it up with you. Every piece is drawn for the person wearing it. Nothing off the wall unless you ask for it.

02Cover-ups

Got ink you do not love anymore? We do a lot of cover-ups. Bring it in and we will tell you straight what we can do with it.

03Walk-ins

Smaller piece in mind? Walk in Tuesday through Saturday. First come, first served.

04Consultations

Thinking about something bigger? Consults are free. We will talk placement, size, and price before anything touches skin.

05Piercings

We do piercings too. Same clean shop, same standard of care.

06Aftercare

You will leave knowing exactly how to heal it right. If something looks off while it heals, call us.

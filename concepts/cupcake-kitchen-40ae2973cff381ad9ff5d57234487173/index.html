About

Cupcake Kitchen is a family-owned bakery in downtown Chattanooga since 2011, run by owner Helene Grzybowski. Cupcakes are baked fresh on-site each morning, alongside custom cakes, cookies, brownies, and desserts. The bakery also caters corporate events, weddings, birthdays, dessert tables, and holiday gifting, and takes large orders. Custom orders are taken by phone.

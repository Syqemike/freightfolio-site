# Design Brief: La Frontera (batch 27)

Business: La Frontera, taco truck, 3501 W Lincoln St, Phoenix, AZ 85009. Open 7 days, 8 AM to midnight (1 AM Fri, 2 AM Sat). Phone (623) 326-6808. Google 4.6 / 85 reviews.
Separate business from "La Frontera #1" (209 N 16th St) and from Comiendo Con memo. Photos and facts come only from La Frontera #2 sources.

## Customer journey
Hungry Phoenix local or late-night visitor finds the page: sees the actual truck at night in the hero, instantly gets the hours ("open 7 days, until midnight and later on weekends"), reads the real menu board transcription, sees real taco photos, then calls from a sticky mobile CTA or taps the address for directions. One page, no friction, designed mobile-first because taco customers search on phones at night.

## Aesthetic
Late-night street taqueria. Dark charcoal-green background like the Phoenix night, amber trompo-fire orange as the primary accent, chili red and lime as supporting notes, warm cream type. Big condensed display typography (Anton) reads like hand-painted truck lettering. Photo-led: the actual truck and real customer taco photos carry the page. No illustrations, no icons-as-decoration, no clipart anywhere.

## Typography
- Display: Anton (uppercase, tight) for hero headline and section headers. Hand-painted-truck-sign feel.
- Body: Inter for readability on small screens.
- Labels/eyebrows: Inter bold, letterspaced uppercase, small.

## Palette

## Layout (single page)
1. Preview disclosure bar (top, thin): "Website Concept / Design Preview" text, honest framing.
2. Sticky nav: LA FRONTERA wordmark, links Menu / Food / Visit, big Call button (tel:).
3. Hero: truck night photo right/contained panel, huge "LA FRONTERA" headline, subline "Tacos y Birria. Phoenix, AZ.", hours pill "Open 7 days", one Google 4.6 / 85 reviews badge, Call + Get Directions CTAs.
4. Strip: meat list marquee-like row? Keep static grid instead (no motion gimmicks): Carnes row: Asada, Pastor, Tripa, Carnitas, Chicharron, Cabeza y Lengua.
5. Menu section (light cream card on dark): transcribed from the truck's own posted menu board (photo shown beside it): Tacos / Burritos / Quesadillas (Mixtas, Sencillas, harina o maiz) / Tortas / Tostadas / Vampiros / Birria / Tortas de Jamon / Tostadas de Ceviche / Coctel de Camaron / Burritos de Chile Verde. Cash-only note (verified on the truck's own signage). No prices listed (not verified).
6. Food gallery: 6 real photos, 3-col desktop / 2-col mobile, short honest captions.
7. Reviews: 2 short real public quotes with source attribution. No invented reviews.
8. Visit: address, full hours table (verified Sun-Thu 8-12, Fri 8-1, Sat 8-2), phone, outdoor seating / takeout note, Google Maps link. Cash-only callout again (practical).
9. Footer: design-preview disclosure repeated, honest line that this is a concept built by Freightfolio, no invented claims.

## Differentiation check
TailWaggers = cream/pastel, rounded, Baloo/Caveat, playful pet. Studio 601 = barber. This = dark night palette, Anton display, photo-led food truck, taqueria-menu feel. No shared layout, colors, or type.

## Copy rules enforced
No em dashes. No invented facts/prices/awards. Rating appears once (hero) with exact source and count. No banned AI phrases. "Book Now" never used; only "Call" CTAs via tel: links.

What we do

Exteriors that hold up, interiors that get finished.

T&M focuses on the parts of a house that take the weather, and the indoor projects that wait all summer. Here is the work, plainly listed.

01

Awnings, railings & posts

Aluminum awnings, porch railings, and posts repaired or replaced to stand straight and shed water.

Exterior · Aluminum

Entryways take a beating in Michigan weather. We straighten, secure, and replace the pieces that make a porch safe and presentable.

02

Siding, trim & gutters

Siding repair, trim replacement, and gutter work that keeps water moving away from the house.

Exterior · Water management

Small siding and gutter problems turn into big ones. We fix the damaged sections before they spread.

03

Doors & windows

Entry doors, storm doors, prime windows, and storm windows installed and adjusted to close tight.

Exterior · Entryways

Doors that stick and windows that draft are daily annoyances. We fit replacements that open, close, and seal the way they should.

04

Screen & glass repair

Torn screens re-screened and damaged glass replaced in windows and doors.

Repair · Windows & doors

A ripped screen or cracked pane does not need a whole new window. We repair the part that is broken.

05

Winter indoor handyman work

When the cold sets in, the work moves inside: repairs, installs, and the list that waited all year.

Seasonal · Indoor

Cold months are the right time for indoor projects. Call with your list and we will work through it.

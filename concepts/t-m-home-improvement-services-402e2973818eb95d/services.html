Services

Every service, spelled out.

T&M Home Improvement Services handles home exteriors and seasonal indoor work in St Clair Shores, Michigan. Free estimates on all of it.

01

Aluminum awnings, railings & posts

Entryways and porches take the worst of Michigan weather. We repair and replace aluminum awnings, porch railings, and posts so they stand straight, drain properly, and look cared for.

  • Aluminum awning repair and replacement
  • Porch and step railings
  • Posts secured, straightened, or replaced
02

Siding, trim & gutters

Damaged siding and failing gutters let water where it should never go. We fix the damaged sections and keep water moving away from the house.

  • Siding repair
  • Exterior trim replacement
  • Gutter repair and adjustment
03

Entry & storm doors

Doors that stick, draft, or will not latch are daily annoyances. We install entry doors and storm doors fitted to close tight and seal properly.

  • Entry door installation
  • Storm door installation and adjustment
  • Hardware, hinges, and weatherstripping
04

Prime & storm windows

Windows that draft in January cost money every month. We install prime windows and storm windows, and service the ones you already have.

  • Prime window installation
  • Storm window installation
  • Window adjustment and sealing
05

Screen & glass repairs

A torn screen or cracked pane does not require a whole new window. We re-screen frames and replace damaged glass in windows and doors.

  • Window screen re-screening
  • Glass replacement in windows and doors
06

Winter indoor handyman services

When the cold sets in, the work moves inside. The repair list that waited all summer gets handled during the winter months.

  • Indoor repairs and installs
  • Seasonal project lists worked through
  • Call with your list for an estimate

About

The Injury Treatment Center of Mobile is a chiropractic and injury treatment practice at 3101 Springhill Ave in Mobile, established in January 2011. Dr. Scott Walker earned a BS in Microbiology from Auburn in 1991, an MBA in 1992, and a Doctor of Chiropractic degree from Life College in 1996, and has practiced in Mobile for more than 20 years with a conservative therapeutic approach.

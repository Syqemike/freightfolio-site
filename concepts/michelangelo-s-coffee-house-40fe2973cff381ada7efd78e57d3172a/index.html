About

Michelangelo's Coffee House is a full-service coffee house in Madison, Wisconsin, at 114 State Street, locally owned since 1997 by Sam (Samir) and Ellen Chehade. The house serves Fair Trade coffee from Equal Exchange, with specialty drinks including the Monkey Mocha, the Chai Charger, and the Peanut Butter Mocha, plus panini grilled sandwiches, bakery, muffins and pastries, smoothies, and Italian sodas. Seating is indoors and on the patio, with a Capitol view, and the shop is dog friendly with free WiFi and takeout. The interior is art-filled and eclectic, under a black-and-white striped awning. Hours are 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM every day, and 7:00 AM on Farmers' Market Saturdays. Call (608) 251-5299 or email michelangeloscoffeehouse@gmail.com to ask about the menu.

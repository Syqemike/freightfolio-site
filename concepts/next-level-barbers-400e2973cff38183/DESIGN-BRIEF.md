# Design Brief: Next Level Barbers (Burlington, VT)

## Business personality
Urban, no-nonsense Burlington shop on Pearl Street. Owner Dani Diallo, 24-year hairstylist (Seven Days VT, Oct 2025). Barbers Dani and King. Hot towel shaves, scalp treatments, massage therapy. Walk-ins accepted, Booksy-centric.

## Design direction
Bold dark urban: near-black ground, signal red (#e5383b) accents, giant uppercase Archivo Black headlines, red CTA blocks, collage hero with staggered photos, numbered service rows with hover states. Inter body.
Differentiated from Don's (light paper, Bitter slab) and Presidential Grooming (charcoal/gold, serif elegance) by the brutalist type scale, red-on-black, and collage hero.

## Section order
Disclosure > sticky dark topbar (name + nav) > staggered hero (h1, lede, collage) > numbered service rows > barbers (Dani + King, walk-ins) > gallery grid > hours + visit cards > footer.

## Imagery
5 stock photos (Pexels): scissors/comb close-up, precision grooming on bearded client, hot towel application, precise haircut, warm-towel beard trim. All labeled "Stock photo".

## Customer journey
Big typography and collage set the urban tone, numbered services scan fast, barber bios build trust, hours table marks Sun/Mon as "call to confirm", "Call to book" tel: card. Booksy page could not be identity-verified (app launcher wall), so CTA is "Call to book", never a disguised phone link.

## Verified facts used
4.7/14 place-listing reviews. Address 163 Pearl St, Burlington VT 05401. Phone +1 802-324-5483. Hours Tue-Thu 10-5, Fri 11-7, Sat 11-5; Sun/Mon unlisted (shown as "call to confirm"). Dani Diallo 24-year hairstylist, Seven Days VT Oct 2025. Walk-ins accepted. No invented facts.

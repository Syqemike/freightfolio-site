What The Locale offers

The Locale is an event venue in Mobile owned and operated by Debbie and Tommy Hartley. It hosts weddings, wedding receptions, fundraisers, corporate parties, and Mardi Gras balls. The venue has a main ballroom, a bridal suite, a groom's lounge, an outdoor patio, and a secret garden, with capacity up to 400 per a venue profile.

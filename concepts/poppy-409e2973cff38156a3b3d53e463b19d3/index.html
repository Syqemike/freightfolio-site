About

Poppy is a handmade shop at 920 E Broadway #1 in Columbia, founded in 1981. About 200 makers, designers, and artists are represented in the shop, with handmade jewelry, greeting cards, prints, postcards, tea towels, and other artisan goods. It is a different business from Poppy Seed Bakery + Cafe and Poppy's Gift Shop.

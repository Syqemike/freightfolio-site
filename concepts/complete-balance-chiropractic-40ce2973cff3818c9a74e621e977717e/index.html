About

Complete Balance Chiropractic is a chiropractic clinic in La Vista. Dr. Cary Holl has more than 25 years of experience and is a Certified Strength and Conditioning Specialist. The clinic treats lower back and neck pain, sciatica, headaches and migraines, whiplash, sports injuries, poor posture, hip and knee pain, plantar fasciitis, and herniated discs.

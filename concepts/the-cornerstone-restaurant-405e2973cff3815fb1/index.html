On the Gulf Coast since 1997

The Cornerstone Restaurant opened in Pascagoula in 1997 and moved to Gautier in 2025. Its menu covers steaks, Gulf shrimp, Mississippi farm-raised catfish, coastal oysters, crab claws, chicken, po'boys, half-pound burgers, salads, and appetizers, with noted dishes including shrimp and grits, the crab burger, the greater tater, backlash shrimp, and butterfly pasta with shrimp and creole sauce.

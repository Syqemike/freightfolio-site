El barberoThe barber

Wally Estrella cuts hair at 140 Broadway in Somerville, and regulars come back to his chair week after week. Customers describe a comfortable shop, careful work, and a barber who knows your name when you walk in.

The shop is bilingual, English and Spanish, and the books are open seven days a week. Parking, card payments, and Wi-Fi are all available while you wait.

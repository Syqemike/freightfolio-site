Gulf seafood on Krebs Avenue

Fried or grilled, straight off the Gulf

OFF the Hook is a Gulf seafood restaurant in downtown Pascagoula. Trigger fish platters, fried crab claw baskets, shrimp and grits, and seafood gumbo come out of the kitchen lunch and Friday dinner. The whole fried menu is gluten free, cooked in a dedicated gluten-free fryer.

What to know

Three things the regulars already know

Daily board specials

The chalkboard on the wall changes every day. Check it before you order; the catch of the day lives there, not on a printed menu.

Fried means fried for everyone

A dedicated gluten-free fryer means the fried seafood is gluten free. Celiac diners eat here with the same options as everyone else.

Takeout and delivery

Call ahead and your food is boxed and ready. Dine in or carry out from 707 Krebs Ave in downtown Pascagoula.

The room

A chalkboard, a mural, and a fishing-village feel

Inside it’s a working seafood joint: the menu goes up in chalk, the walls carry a hand-painted underwater mural, and the dining room stays small and personal. Lunch runs Monday through Friday, with a Friday dinner service too.

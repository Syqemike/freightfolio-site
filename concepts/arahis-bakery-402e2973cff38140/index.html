Family-owned Cuban bakery · since about 1986

Pastelitos calientes, cafecito fuerte.

Arahis Bakery is a family-owned Cuban bakery and cafe on Calle Ocho in Little Havana. Guava pastelitos from the case, Cuban sandwiches pressed to order, and strong cafecito poured all morning, at prices the neighborhood can afford.

From the bakery case

Guava pastelitos

The signature. Flaky pastry around guava, or guava and cheese, baked through the day.

Croquetas

Ham, chicken, and cheese croquettes, crisp and hot, the way Little Havana likes them.

Cuban sandwiches

Pressed Cuban sandwiches, media noche, and pan con bistec, made to order.

Bakery y cafeteria

Two counters, one corner of Calle Ocho

Arahis works as a bakery and a cafeteria. Pick up pastelitos and pan cubano for the table, or sit with a Cuban breakfast plate and a cafecito. Take-out and delivery are available.

Coffee, the Cuban way

Cafecito strong enough to stand on. The morning crowd on 8th Street runs on it.

Hot from the press

Cuban sandwiches, media noche, pan con bistec, and vaca frita from the hot kitchen.

Cakes, flan, and more

Cakes, flan, tequenos, chivaritos, and loaves of pan cubano for the week.

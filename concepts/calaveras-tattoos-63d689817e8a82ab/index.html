Styles

Work that starts with your idea

The shop takes on custom work across all styles. Client reviews mention portraits and sleeves. Fine-line lettering comes up often as well. Every piece is drawn for the person wearing it.

Portraits

Detailed portrait work drawn from your reference photos. Bring a clear image and the artist will talk through size, placement, and how it will age.

Sleeves

Full and half sleeves planned as a single composition. The artists help you map the piece across sessions so it reads as one design.

Fine-line lettering

Script and lettering kept clean and legible. If you have a phrase in mind, the shop will help you choose a style that fits the spot.

Calaveras Tattoos sits in the Southtown Art District, a block from Hemisfair Park and near the River Walk. The studio has an open environment with lots of flash art on the walls.

Visitors describe the shop as clean and friendly. Reviews also call it professional. Walk-ins are welcome and appointments are available. Consults are offered for custom pieces.

The shop has been mentioned in SA Current for its flash tattoos at community events.

Planning a visit

Bring your reference images and an idea of size and placement. If you are not sure yet, a consult is a good first step.

Walk-ins and appointments both work. The best way to reach the shop is to call.

Birmingham Breadworks is an artisan bakery and cafe in Southside Birmingham. It offers sourdough and artisan breads, croissants, cinnamon rolls, bagels, breakfast and lunch, takeout, and outdoor seating. The space is also rented for parties and receptions after hours. The own site shows reviews plus address and phone, with no menu, prices, ordering, or service pages, so call with questions.

01 · The mission

Good clothes deserve a second act.

Revolution started with a simple mission: provide quality, name-brand, gently used clothing at amazing prices, all while saving the planet. Shopping sustainable fashion is just the right thing to do. By keeping great looks in circulation, the shop reduces demand and eases stress on the environment.

The racks hold on-trend clothing, shoes and accessories for men, women and juniors. The Downtown Fairhope visitors guide puts name-brand style here at 60 to 80 percent off retail.

Recognized 2019 Nappie award finalist, Best Clothing Consignment Store (Baldwin).

New pieces land on the floor all the time, and the mix runs from everyday staples to formal gowns. Questions about a piece? Call (251) 990-4859.

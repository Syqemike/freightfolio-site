Why we exist

The Mission.

In the boutique’s own words: provide quality, name-brand, gently used clothing at amazing prices, all while saving the planet.

Home The Floor The Mission Visit Call (251) 990-4859

Shopping sustainable fashion is just the right thing to do.

Revolution was started for the shopper who loves the name brands but doesn’t love the retail prices. The racks carry a wide selection of on-trend clothing, shoes and accessories for men, women and juniors, each piece checked for quality before it earns a spot.

The idea is simple. By keeping great looks in circulation, the shop reduces demand and eases stress on the environment. Every bag that walks out the door is a small win for the planet and a big one for your closet.

The house tagline says it best: “I can afford that!” The Downtown Fairhope visitors guide puts name-brand style here at 60 to 80 percent off retail.

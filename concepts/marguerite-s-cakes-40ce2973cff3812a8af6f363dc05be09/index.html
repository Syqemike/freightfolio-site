About

Marguerite's Cakes is a bakery in Slidell specializing in king cakes and wedding cakes, run by owner Marguerite Riehm. King cakes are baked from scratch since 1978, with traditional, cream cheese, and other fillings, praline, and almond icing. The bakery also makes roll fondant wedding cakes, 3D cakes, doberge cakes, cookies, pies, and cheesecakes, with nationwide shipping.

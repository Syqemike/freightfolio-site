Our story

A salon that feels like a deep breath.

We started as Savanah & Co. and grew into The Willow, but the feeling never changed. Eclectic, retro, and relaxed on purpose. This is the kind of chair where you settle in, not sit up straight.

Come as you are. Stay for the conversation. Leave with hair you cannot stop touching.

Relaxed chairs and easy conversation. Bring us a screenshot, a wild idea, or just a vague feeling, and we will figure out the hair together.

What we do

Hair is our thing.

Tell us what you want and what your hair can do. Here is where we shine.

Blonding & balayage

From bright, bold blonde to soft lived-in color, we build blondes that grow out gracefully instead of screaming for a touch-up in three weeks.

Vivid color

Pinks, purples, teals, and everything loud. We love a bold head of hair, and we know how to get you there while keeping it healthy.

Extensions

Hand-tied and clip-in extensions for length, fullness, or both. Matched to your color so they disappear right into your own hair.

Cuts & styling

Precision cuts, highlights, blowouts, and updos for the big days. The everyday work, done with the same care as the transformations.

Also on the menu: keratin treatments, glossing and glazing, deep conditioning treatments, and full color services.

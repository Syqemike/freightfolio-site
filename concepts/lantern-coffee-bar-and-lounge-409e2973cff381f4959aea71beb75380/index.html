About

Lantern Coffee Bar and Lounge is a specialty coffee bar in downtown Grand Rapids, on the ground floor of the old Grand Central Engineering Building, with the entrance on Oakes Street. Upstairs is the coffee bar; downstairs is the lounge. The bar serves espresso drinks and pour-over, lattes that include the lavender latte and cafe miel, house-made syrups and house-made oat milk, seasonal drinks, and pastries and local bakery items.

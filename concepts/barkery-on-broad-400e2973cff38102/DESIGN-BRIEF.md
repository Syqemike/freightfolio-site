# Design Brief: Barkery on Broad (Augusta, GA)

## Business read (verified facts only)
- Full-service personalized pet styling, 1159 Broad St, Augusta, GA, (706) 691-3242.
- 5.0 from 153 Google reviews (strongest social proof in the batch).
- Hours verified: Tue-Sat 9 AM to 3 PM, Sun/Mon closed. Phone/address/hours single-sourced from the business's own verified-active Facebook page; no conflicts found.
- No menu/prices. No real website; Facebook page facebook.com/barkeryonbroad is verified active and linked.
- No real business photos; all imagery is labeled stock photography.

## Design personality
A Broad Street salon with craft pride. Warm, editorial, a little rustic. Personalized styling deserves a design that feels bespoke, not franchised.

## Art direction
- Palette: warm paper (#f6f1e7), espresso (#3b2f2a), mustard (#d9a441), soft mustard (#f0d9a8). No gradients.
- Typography: Libre Caslon Text (literary serif) for headlines; Work Sans for body.
- Hero: full-bleed photo with a left-side angled dark veil, italic accent in the wordmark, mustard eyebrow tag.
- Section order: Hero / Intro (oversized serif numeral) / Styling (espresso band with lettered service rows) / Gallery (paper frames) / Hours + Visit (split panel: paper hours, espresso visit card).
- Texture: lettered service rows (a., b., c.), paper photo frames, double accents. No illustrations, no SVG art.

## Differentiation
The only site with a full-viewport hero and an angled veil; the only italic wordmark; the only lettered service list. Espresso/mustard palette is unique in the batch. It is also the only site that links a verified social profile (Facebook), because Barkery is the only business with one.

## Copy rules honored
No em/en dashes. No banned AI phrases. Rating shown exactly once (styling band: "5.0 from 153 Google reviews"). Services stay within "full-service personalized pet styling" (personalized styling, full grooms, baths and blow outs, nail trims). Hours shown exactly as verified. No invented facts about the shop's history or operations. CTAs: "Call to book" to tel:+17066913242; Facebook link only to the verified page.

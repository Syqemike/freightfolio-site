Training at Clockwork Performance covers strength, conditioning, mobility, pain management, and recovery. It is built for athletes, teams, young athletes, and people getting back from injury.

01

Functional Training

The core of the gym’s system: traditional strength training merged with prehab, rehab, mobility, and conditioning.

Functional Strength classes run Monday, Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday at 9:00 AM and Monday, Wednesday, and Thursday at 12:00 PM.

A member working a plank on a platform in the gym.

02

Sports Performance

Training built around the demands of an athlete’s sport, including work in and out of fight camp.

Performance Training runs Saturday at 10:00 AM and Monday and Wednesday at 5:30 PM.

Jump and medicine ball work on the outdoor turf lane.

03

Personal Training

One on one training with a coach. You start with an initial assessment, then follow a custom program written just for you, with each workout tracked across sessions.

A member training with a barbell on the gym floor.

04

Small Group

Small group conditioning with a coach, following the gym’s system of strength, prehab, rehab, mobility, and conditioning.

Members working on mobility on the turf.

05

Group Conditioning

Conditioning sessions on the green turf.

Conditioning runs Tuesday and Thursday at 7:00 AM.

Members working on the turf during a conditioning session.

06

Mobility Assessment and Movement

A mobility assessment and movement work, part of the system that merges mobility with strength training, prehab, and rehab.

A coach working a mobility drill at the rack.

07

Pain Management

Work on the problem areas behind pain, with a plan and correctives. One member came in with chronic lower back pain. Chris pinpointed the problem areas and built a proper plan, and she is now pain-free.

Members training at the squat rack.

08

Recovery

The gym’s recovery room has a sauna, and a cold plunge is also offered for recovery after training.

The recovery room at the gym.

09

Youth Training

Youth training runs Monday to Thursday at 4:30 PM. The gym notes the time changes seasonally, so call to confirm.

Young athletes with their coaches by the rack.

10

Team Training

Team training for a whole squad, coached together at the gym.

The Yale baseball team training at the gym.

Also offered

Remote training is also offered. One member trains remotely as well as in the gym with Chris, working on programming in and out of fight camp.

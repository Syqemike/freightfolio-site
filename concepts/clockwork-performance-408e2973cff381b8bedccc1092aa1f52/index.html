02 — The coaches

Meet the coaches

Chris Lockhart

Owner, Founder, and Head Coach

Chris started Clockwork Performance to help as many people as he can through the training that has been his passion since he was 14. He found jiu jitsu and combat sports in 2014, and he brings a train smarter, not harder attitude to the people he works with.

“I started Clockwork Performance because my main objective has always been to help as many people as I can through what has been my passion since I was 14 years old. When I found my love of jiu jitsu and combat sports in 2014, I was driven to adopt a ‘train smarter, not harder’ attitude, and that’s what I strive to bring to my clients.”Chris Lockhart

Certified FMS (Functional Movement Screen), Functional Strength Coach Levels 1 and 2, Daru Strong Performance Coach, Bio Force Conditioning Coach, Physical Preparedness Specialist, Functional Range Conditioning Specialist (Mobility), Westside Barbell Personal Training and Athletic Coaching, Landmine University Levels 1 and 2, Movement and Mobility Specialist, Jiu Jitsu Black Belt at Mid City MMA.

Jared Fryoux

Assistant Coach

A professional MMA fighter and BJJ Brown Belt at Mid City MMA. He has been in the fitness industry for a decade as a personal trainer, martial arts instructor, and athlete.

His goal is to help members learn in a way that aids injury prevention inside and outside the gym.

“At Clockwork, our members become family and we want to see our family thrive.”Jared Fryoux

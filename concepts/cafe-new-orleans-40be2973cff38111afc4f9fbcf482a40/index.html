About

Cafe New Orleans is a family-owned country Cajun restaurant in D'Iberville, Mississippi, serving Cajun cooking since 2000. The menu covers jambalaya, Bourbon Street gumbo, crawfish etouffee, red beans and rice, Catfish Chelsea, fried shrimp platters, po'boys, stuffed red snapper, and fried green tomatoes, and the restaurant has a banquet room for groups.

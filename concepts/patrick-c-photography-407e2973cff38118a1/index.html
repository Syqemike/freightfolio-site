Twenty years behind the camera in Memphis

Patrick C is a Memphis photographer with more than 20 years behind the camera. His work includes event photography and corporate headshots. It also includes weddings and real estate photography. Drone imagery and creative portraiture are part of the range, along with behind-the-scenes coverage and canvas prints.

His current homepage is two short intro paragraphs. It lists no service prices and no packages. This concept shows the full range of the work in one place, so a client can see what he does before calling.

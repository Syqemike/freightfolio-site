A piece of tattoo history

This is the address where Sailor Jerry built his name. The walls still carry original flash, and Polynesian tattoo tools are on display, a direct line to the craft’s roots in Hawaii.

The current crew keeps that tradition going: Harisumi, Clay, Sue, and Mark, plus guest artists passing through.

Dance Canvas Performing Arts Center

A Springfield dance school for ages 2 to 16 in ballet, tap, jazz, contemporary, hip hop, and acro, with small classes in two large mirrored studios.

About

Dance Canvas Performing Arts Center is a dance school at 3322 S Campbell Ave Ste G in Springfield, opened in September 2013 by owner and head instructor Whitney Copley. Classes run for ages 2 to 16 in two large, modern studios with mirrored walls, ballet barres, and professional-grade dance floors, and class sizes stay small, between 12 and 15 students.

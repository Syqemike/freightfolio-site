A New Orleans-style bakery-cafe with a full bar in Kenner's Rivertown district.

Debbie on the Levee is a New Orleans-style bakery-cafe with a full bar in Kenner's Rivertown district. The signature Doberge cake has seven thin cake layers with pudding layers under poured fondant. The menu also includes dobites, beignets, Foster French Toast, a breakfast burrito and sandwich, grilled caprese, scones, savory and sweet hand pies, cookies, pies, and craft cocktails including a tableside-smoked old fashioned. The bakery-cafe serves breakfast, brunch, lunch, and dessert, uses coffee from Pretty Coffee, and offers takeout and delivery, with no reservations.

# Design Brief — Inn Zone Bar and Grill (inn-zone-bar-grill-401e2973cff38167)

Neighborhood bar and grill, 1815 Bienville Blvd, Ocean Springs, MS 39564. Phone (228) 875-9908.
Services: bar and grill — Mrs Mae's wings, chili cheese fries, steak sandwich, hot dogs, game-night spaghetti; pool, darts, karaoke. Hours: daily 11:00 AM - 12:00 AM. Google 4.8 from 63 reviews (shown once, exactly). Facebook page is their web presence; no standalone website.

## Aesthetic
Rugged neighborhood bar. Dark walnut browns and near-black base, amber highlights, pool-hall green felt accents. Chalkboard-style specials board for the menu section (dark board, hand-lettered-feel type in chalk white and amber — done with type only, no drawn doodles). Feels like a real local hangout with regulars, not a polished restaurant template.

## Typography
Display: "Oswald" (condensed, bold, no-nonsense) for headlines and the specials board. Body: "Inter" for menu items, hours, and directions. The contrast of condensed display + plain body sells the dive-bar voice without cartoonishness.

## Palette
- Near-black: #14100c (backgrounds)
- Dark walnut: #241a12 (panels, header)
- Amber: #e09a2b (highlights, CTA buttons, board headers)
- Pool felt green: #1f5c3d (accents, felt strips, section dividers)
- Chalk white: #f3ead8 (text)
- No gradients, no glowing blobs, no purple anywhere on this site

## Layout
1. Top bar: name left, "Design preview" chip, Call button (amber).
2. Hero: full-bleed dark photo (pool hall) with headline in Oswald: "Your neighborhood bar on Bienville." Subline: bar and grill, open daily 11 to midnight. CTAs: Call, Get directions.
3. Chalkboard specials board: the verified menu items chalked up — Mrs Mae's wings, chili cheese fries, steak sandwich, hot dogs, game-night spaghetti. No prices (unverified). Copy line: "Cold drinks, hot food, no pretense."
4. Hangout section: three felt-green cards — Pool, Darts, Karaoke — each with a photo and one plain line of copy. Copy stays claim-free: no promises about leagues, nights, or schedules since those are unverified. (Darts: no darts photo available; use the pool balls close-up for Pool and the karaoke photo for Karaoke; for Darts describe it without a photo? Rule: every photo figure needs a caption; darts can be text-only card. I'll include darts as a text card with felt accent, no photo, to avoid a mismatched photo.)
5. Visit: address, daily hours, Google Maps link, phone CTA. Rating shown once here: "4.8 from 63 Google reviews".
6. Footer: design-preview disclaimer, "Concept by Freightfolio", link https://freightfolio.net.

## Customer journey
Regular-to-be checks the food list and hours, sees it's a real hangout (pool, darts, karaoke), calls or gets directions. Primary CTA: Call (228) 875-9908 (tel: link).

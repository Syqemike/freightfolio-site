About

Seva Yoga is a yoga studio in East Grand Rapids, Michigan, at 2237 Wealthy Street Southeast in Gaslight Village. Classes run daily, about 26 classes a week, in gentle yoga, Hatha, and Vinyasa for all levels. The studio runs donation-based community classes, including Free Friday, plus a Yoga Alliance-registered 200-hour teacher training, a retail shop, off-site and community classes, and retreats. Seva Yoga was founded by Michele Fife and has been owned since January 2013 by Melissa and Tobi Tungl. Call (616) 458-2541 to ask about the class schedule.

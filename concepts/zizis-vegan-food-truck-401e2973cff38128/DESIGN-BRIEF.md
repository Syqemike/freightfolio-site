## Creative direction
Verdant editorial. Palette: deep olive #3c5233, chili red #c1442e, warm cream #faf3e3,
plus mustard accent #f0b429 for prices and board highlights. Typography: Vollkorn
(display serif) + Inter (body). Layout: split hero (photo + rating + CTAs), scrolling
marquee ticker of priced menu items, dark price-board menu section, catering/events
section built around the business's own award photo, and a dark "find the truck"
section with the address and the hours honesty note. Distinct from other Freightfolio
food-truck builds: no charcoal/gold, no teal/coral.

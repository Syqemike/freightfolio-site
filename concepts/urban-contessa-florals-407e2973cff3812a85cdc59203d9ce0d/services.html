Services

Urban Contessa Florals is a wedding and event floral studio in Indianapolis, run by mother-daughter duo Asia and Olivia White, in business since 2017. The studio makes bridal and bridesmaid bouquets, boutonnieres, corsages, centerpieces, aisle decorations, wedding arches, and chuppahs, plus flower crowns, flower delivery and setup, retail arrangements, and creative floral workshops.

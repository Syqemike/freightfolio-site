A Broad Street barbershop with a real chair and a real blade.

Haircuts, fades, hot towel shaves, and beard trims in downtown Richmond. The shop opens at 7 in the morning, seven days a week, so you can get a cut before your day starts or after it ends.

The shop

Sitting in the chair since day one

McCormick's Progressive BarberShop sits on East Broad Street in Richmond, with a barber pole out front and a crew of barbers behind the chairs. Master stylist Melvin (Mel) McCormick Jr. leads the team.

What to expect

Take a seat, leave sharp

The shop keeps classic barbershop hours and classic barbershop services: precise fades, clean head shaves, hot towel shaves, and beard trims that keep their shape between visits.

Booking is simple: one phone call to the shop, and you are on the book.

About

Bobby's Hot and Cheesy is a fast-casual pizza restaurant on Colonial Avenue in Roanoke. The restaurant makes pizza from scratch, including the Hot and Cheesy Pizza, Cuban pizza, pizza salad, pizza flight, and mini pizzas, plus meatball subs, salads, wings, pasta, and charcuterie, with frozen sangria, beer, wine, and frozen lemonade. Takeout, delivery, dine-in, and outdoor seating are available.

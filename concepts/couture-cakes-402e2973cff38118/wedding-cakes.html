Custom Wedding Cakes

The centerpiece of your day

Each wedding cake is custom designed for the couple: the tiers, the finish, the flowers, and the flavors you choose together.

Tell us about your wedding and we will sketch a cake that belongs in it. Design details are arranged during your phone consultation.

Groom's Cakes

A cake with his name on it

Groom's cakes are a wedding day tradition, and one of our specialties. Chocolate, themed, or classic, each is baked to order and finished by hand.

Many couples order the wedding cake and the groom's cake together so both arrive ready for the reception.

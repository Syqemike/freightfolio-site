About

The Venue at Stonebrook Meadows is a barn wedding and event venue in Bauxite, built in 2018. The elevated barn sits on 41 secluded acres and holds a 5,600 square foot climate-controlled barn with vaulted ceilings and beam work, crystal chandeliers, and a massive window wall. Outside there are ceremony sites and a 1,000 square foot veranda, and the venue holds up to 250 guests.

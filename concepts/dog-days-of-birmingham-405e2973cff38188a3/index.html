Cage-free daycare and boarding

Dog Days of Birmingham offers cage-free dog daycare, overnight boarding, grooming, and retail. Overnight boarding includes daycare each day and private sleeping quarters. Grooming includes haircuts with one groomer on Tuesday and Thursday when scheduling allows, plus baths and nails most days. A temperament test is required before daycare enrollment. The business has operated since September 2010. Call to ask about daycare or boarding.

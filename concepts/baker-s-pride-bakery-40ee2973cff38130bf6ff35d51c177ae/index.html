About

Baker's Pride Bakery is Savannah's family bakery since 1982, at 840 East Derenne Avenue. The bakery makes donuts, cupcakes, cookies, pies, cheesecakes, tarts, breads, and petit fours, plus custom wedding, birthday, and specialty cakes. The shop serves wholesale and retail customers, with a dine-in area and takeout and delivery.

Wholesale, retail, dine-in, and takeout

Baker's Pride serves wholesale and retail customers, with a dine-in area and takeout and delivery. The bakery is at 840 East Derenne Avenue. Hours are Monday to Friday 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Saturday 6:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and Sunday closed. Call (912) 355-1155 to ask about the menu or a custom cake.

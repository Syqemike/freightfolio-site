What families find here

Everything at T and B Learning Factory starts from their promise of a safe, nurturing, and educational environment for every child in their care.

Early learning, every day

A preschool program shaped around how young children actually learn: through play, stories, and hands-on activities that build early reading, counting, and social skills.

Full-day child care

Open Monday through Friday from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM, so working parents have dependable, all-day coverage they can plan their week around.

Room to grow

With capacity for 104 children, the center welcomes little ones from age 1 through age 5, from first steps all the way to kindergarten readiness.

Play is the work of childhood

The center's days are built on guided play and group activity. Children practice sharing, taking turns, and expressing themselves while they explore.

A nurturing classroom helps children feel secure enough to try new things, which is where real early learning begins.

Stories that build young minds

Reading time and storytelling give children new words, new ideas, and a love of books long before they can read on their own.

Small-group activities keep every child engaged and give caregivers time for the one-on-one attention little learners need.

Come see the classrooms for yourself

Call during center hours to ask about availability for your child's age group and to arrange a tour of T and B Learning Factory.

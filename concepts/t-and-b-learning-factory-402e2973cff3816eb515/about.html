About the Center

Where little learners get a strong start

T and B Learning Factory combines preschool learning with dependable full-day child care, so Biloxi families get one place that covers both.

A preschool and child care center

T and B Learning Factory serves children from age 1 through age 5, with a licensed capacity of 104. That range means toddlers and preschoolers each get age-appropriate care, learning, and play under one roof.

The center's stated mission is a safe, nurturing, and educational environment: three words that shape how each day is planned, from morning drop-off to afternoon pickup.

Built for working families

The center opens at 6:00 AM and closes at 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Parents get a full day of dependable care without rearranging their workday.

Consistent routines, familiar caregivers, and a calm daily rhythm help children settle in and feel at home away from home.

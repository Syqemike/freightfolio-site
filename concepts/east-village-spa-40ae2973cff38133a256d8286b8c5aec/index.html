About

East Village Spa is a day spa at 300 E Locust St Ste 313 in Des Moines, in the Historic East Village. In business since 2008 with a team of more than 20 licensed massage therapists, estheticians, and nail technicians, the spa focuses on therapeutic relationships with its guests and on healthy products and safe techniques.

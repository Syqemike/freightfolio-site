D'Andrews Bakery and Cafe is a modern bakery with Southern sensibility in downtown Nashville. It was a 2024 James Beard Award semifinalist for Outstanding Bakery. The bakery offers desserts, pastries, coffee and tea, artisanal sandwiches, salads, and soup, plus custom cakes for events.

Services in detail

Everything Steam It Up offers, in one place. Licensed and insured, free estimates, emergency services. Serving Metairie, LA and surrounding areas since 2005.

Carpet Cleaning

Deep steam cleaning for wall-to-wall carpet, in homes and in commercial spaces. Traffic lanes, spots, and ground-in soil get the full treatment.

  • Residential rooms, hallways, and stairs
  • Offices and commercial spaces
  • Free estimates before any work starts

Upholstery Cleaning

Sofas, loveseats, chairs, and other fabric furniture. Cleaning lifts out dust and body oils so cushions look and smell fresh again.

  • Sofas and sectionals
  • Chairs and ottomans
  • Residential and commercial furniture

Offsite Rug Cleaning

Area rugs get cleaned offsite, where they can be treated with the time and care delicate fibers need.

  • Area and accent rugs
  • Handled offsite for thorough cleaning

Tile and Grout

Tile surfaces and grout lines scrubbed clean of built-up grime that mopping leaves behind.

  • Kitchen and bathroom tile
  • Grout lines restored to a cleaner color

Vinyl and Wood Cleaning

Vinyl and wood flooring cleaned to bring back their finish without harsh treatment.

  • Vinyl flooring
  • Wood flooring

Water Restoration

Help after water damage to floors and fabrics. Because timing matters, emergency services are available. Call right away and describe what happened.

  • Water-damaged carpet and upholstery
  • Emergency service available

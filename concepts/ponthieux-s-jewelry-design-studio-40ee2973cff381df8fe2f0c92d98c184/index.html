About

Ponthieux's Jewelry Design Studio is a family-owned custom jewelry design studio in Greenville, South Carolina, open since 2001. The studio designs custom pieces in house, with wax-model previews before casting and an average of 6 to 8 weeks from design to finished piece. Repairs, appraisals, restorations, and watch adjustments are also part of the work.

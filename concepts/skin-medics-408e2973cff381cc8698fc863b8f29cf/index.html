About the clinic

Care from a physician who has done this work for more than two decades.

Dr. Wood has performed aesthetic procedures since 2002. He was trained by the pioneers of Botox for cosmetic use and is a member of the American Society for Laser Medicine and Surgery and the American Academy of Anti-Aging Medicine. The clinic offers injectables, laser treatments, body contouring, IV therapy, facials, chemical peels, and massage, with a treatment plan shaped around each client.

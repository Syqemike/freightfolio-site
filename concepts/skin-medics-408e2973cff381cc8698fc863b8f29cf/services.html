Treatments

Injectables, laser, body, and spa care, under one roof.

Treatment costs are set at consultation. Call or email to ask about a treatment, to ask about cost, or to book a free consultation. CareCredit financing is available for treatments over $1,000.

01

Injectables

Dr. Wood performs injectables at the clinic, including neuromodulators and fillers, and uses Botox for hyperhidrosis, TMJ, and migraines as well as for cosmetic use.

Botox, Xeomin, Jeuveau, Daxxify, Radiesse, Sculptra, Restylane, Juvéderm, Belotero, PRF EZ Gel, Kybella, and Skinvive.

02

Laser treatments

Laser care at the clinic covers hair removal, skin renewal, and targeted treatment for veins, acne, rosacea, wrinkles, and tattoos.

IPL Photofacial, laser hair removal, Clear + Brilliant, Laser Genesis, laser tattoo removal, laser vein treatment, laser skin resurfacing, laser wrinkle reduction, laser acne treatment, laser rosacea treatment, and laser skin tightening.

03

Skin analysis and skin treatments

The clinic starts with a complimentary VISIA skin analysis. Skin treatments include microneedling, radio frequency microneedling in a series of 4 to 6, thread lifting, and PRP and PRF for scar revision and hair restoration.

VISIA Skin Analysis, microneedling, radio frequency microneedling, thread lifting (PDO), PRP and PRF, and Plasma Pen Fibroblast.

04

Body contouring

CoolSculpting is FDA-approved and takes about 1 hour per applicator, with an average of about 20% fat reduction. The clinic also offers BTL Exilis and Bodysculpt, and a weight-loss program with Semaglutide and Tirzepatide.

CoolSculpting, BTL Exilis, Bodysculpt, and the weight-loss program.

05

IV therapy

IV therapy blends at the clinic are built around hydration, immunity, migraine relief, and recovery, with custom blends available. NAD, Glutathione, and B12 injections are also offered.

Hydration, Immune Booster, Migraine, Hangover, Beauty, NAD IV, and Custom IV, plus NAD, Glutathione, and B12 injections.

06

Facials and chemical peels

Facials range from the Signature Facial and HydraFacial MD to acne, teen, and back facials, plus a lunch-break touch-up facial. Chemical peels run from Enzyme and Mandelic Acid to the ZO 3-Step Peel and TCA.

Signature Facial, HydraFacial MD, Dermaplaning with Facial, Acne Facial, Teen Facial, Back Facial, and the lunch-break touch-up facial. Enzyme, Mandelic Acid, ZO Stimulator, Glycolic Acid, Salicylic Acid, Pyruvic Acid, Blue Peel Radiance, Modified Jessner, VI Peel, ZO 3-Step Peel, and TCA.

07

Massage and body treatments

Jenny Menesses is a licensed massage therapist and certified medical aesthetician with almost 20 years of experience. She offers deep-tissue massage and migraine treatment at the clinic.

Deep-tissue massage, migraine treatment, Salt and Sugar Glow, Mud Wrap, and airbrush tanning.

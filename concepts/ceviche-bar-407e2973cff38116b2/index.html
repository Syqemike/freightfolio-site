A Cuban kitchen and bar

Ceviche Bar is a Cuban kitchen and bar in the Historic East Village of Des Moines that brands itself Ceviche DSM. It is famous for authentic Cubanos and mojitos, and its menu includes ceviche, including salmon ceviche, the Cuban sandwich, seafood paella, ropa vieja, pork chops and chuletas, tostones, yuca, black bean soup, arroz y frijoles, Cuban flan, and chocolate espresso cake, plus mojitos and other cocktails. It offers patio seating, happy-hour specials, and reservations accepted per listings. No menu prices are published in research, so call or email with questions.

Infrared-heated flow-style yoga for all levels, in Ann Arbor since 2010.

Ita Yoga Studio in Ann Arbor offers infrared-heated flow-style yoga for all levels, in business since 2010 and led by owner Ita Reyes. Classes include Grounding Flow, Yoga Flow, Yin and Power Yoga, plus video on demand, private sessions, yoga for athletes, and teacher training. A single class is 30 dollars; five classes are 140 dollars, ten are 260 dollars, and monthly unlimited is 155 dollars.

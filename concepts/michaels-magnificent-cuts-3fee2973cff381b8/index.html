The Man Behind the Chair

★

Michael Mallory opened Michael's Magnificent Cuts in Memphis in 1997. Nearly three decades later, the shop still runs on the same promise: sit in the chair, and walk out feeling magnificent.

It is an old-school barber studio offering haircuts, hot lather shaves, and beard trims, plus hair design, hair coloring, and facials. Consultations are free, walk-ins are welcome, and appointments are available.

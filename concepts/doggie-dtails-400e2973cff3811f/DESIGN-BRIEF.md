# Design Brief: Doggie D'Tails (North Augusta, SC)

## Business read (verified facts only)
- Dog grooming, 452 W Martintown Rd, North Augusta, SC 29841, (803) 278-2100.
- 4.9 from 32 Google reviews. Hours NOT verifiable (directories list bogus 24/7): no hours section at all; "Call for current hours" in visit copy.
- Verified services: baths, nail trims, full grooming packages, ear cleaning, teeth brushing, premium/hypoallergenic shampoos, accessories and treats. No prices.
- No real website. Similar-name social pages are UNRELATED businesses: no social links included.
- No real business photos; all imagery is labeled stock photography.

## Design personality
Modern, minimal, confident. A clean product-sheet feel: hairline service menu, generous whitespace, nature-inspired color.

## Art direction
- Palette: warm off-white (#f4f1ea), pine green (#3e5641), sage (#a9b79a), coral accent (#e0886a). No gradients.
- Typography: Sora (modern geometric sans) for structure; Newsreader (literary serif, italic accents) for headlines.
- Hero: rounded photo frame with a bottom overlay bar pairing serif headline with booking panel.
- Section order: Hero / Services (hairline "menu" list) / Shop (pine band: accessories and treats) / Gallery / Rating proof band / Visit (no hours section).
- Texture: hairline rules, small uppercase labels, rounded cards. No illustrations, no SVG art.

## Differentiation
The only site with a hairline menu-style services list and the only one with a dedicated rating proof band. Pine/sage/coral palette is unique in the batch. The only site with no hours section at all (by requirement) and no social links (by requirement). Sora + Newsreader pairing appears nowhere else in the batch.

## Copy rules honored
No em/en dashes. No banned AI phrases. Rating shown exactly once (proof band: "4.9 from 32 Google reviews"). Services use the exact verified item list; accessories and treats get their own section, not a service. Hours are not shown; visit copy says only "Call for current hours". No social links (similar-name pages are unrelated). CTAs: "Call to book" to tel:+18032782100.

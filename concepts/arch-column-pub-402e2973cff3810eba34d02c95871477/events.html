THE WEEKLY LINEUP

Four nights a week, the same way every week. Karaoke midweek and on Saturdays, drag on Thursdays, and long Fridays. The bar is closed Sunday through Tuesday.

WED
5pm - 1am

KARAOKE NIGHT

Wednesday is the first karaoke night of the week. Sign up, pick your song, and take the mic in front of a room that wants you to nail it.

THU
5pm - 12am

DRAG SHOW NIGHT

Thursday belongs to the drag show. It is the room's regular weekly show night, hosted at the bar. Come ready for a crowd.

FRI
5pm - 1:30am

OPEN LATE

Friday keeps the doors open until 1:30 in the morning. Cheap ice-cold drinks, the patio in season, and no reason to hurry home.

SAT
6pm - 1:30am

KARAOKE NIGHT

Saturday is the second karaoke night, with doors at 6pm and the bar open until 1:30am.

GOOD TO KNOW

  • The patio is open spring through fall.
  • Outside food is welcome. There is no kitchen, so bring dinner from anywhere.
  • Planning a birthday or a group night out? Call the bar to work it out.

Arch & Column Pub

THE PUB ITSELF

A locally owned, LGBT-focused community pub on Business Loop 70 in Columbia, run by owner Pitt.

It is a dive bar in the best sense: unpretentious, welcoming, and built around the people who show up. Owner Pitt runs the room himself, and the bar serves as a gathering spot for Columbia's LGBTQ community and anyone looking for a friendly place to land.

The drinks are cheap and ice-cold. There is no kitchen, so outside food is welcome, bring takeout from anywhere and make a night of it. The outdoor patio is open spring through fall.

The week runs Wednesday through Saturday. Karaoke takes Wednesdays and Saturdays, and the weekly drag show takes Thursdays. Fridays run late. The bar is closed Sunday through Tuesday.

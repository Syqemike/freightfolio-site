The room

A DIVE BAR, ON PURPOSE

No pretense. Wood counters, a back bar stocked for whatever the night calls for, and a patio when the weather cooperates. Outside food is welcome, so bring dinner from anywhere and settle in.

Real photo: the back bar shelves at Arch & Column Pub.

Why people come back

BUILT AROUND THE COMMUNITY

Owner-operated by Pitt, the pub is a local gathering spot for Columbia's LGBTQ community and everyone who wants a friendly room to land in.

CHEAP, ICE-COLD DRINKS

The drinks are strong and cold, and they go easy on the wallet. That is the whole drinks philosophy.

KARAOKE TWICE A WEEK

Wednesday and Saturday nights are karaoke nights. Every voice is welcome, from regulars to first-timers.

DRAG ON THURSDAYS

Thursday night is the weekly drag show.

OUTSIDE FOOD WELCOME

There is no kitchen, so bring food in from wherever you like. The patio is open spring through fall.

Sessions

Six kinds of sessions, each in natural light.

Every session is photographed in natural light, in the studio or outdoors. Prices are listed with each session below.

Newborns

Newborn portraits in the studio or at home, with siblings and family portraits included.

How a session works

Booking a session

Sessions start with an inquiry to the studio. Addie replies within three business days. Booking is confirmed with a contract and a half-down retainer. Finished galleries arrive in two to six weeks, with no cap on the number of photos delivered. Print credit is $15 with a Mini session and $25 with a Full session.

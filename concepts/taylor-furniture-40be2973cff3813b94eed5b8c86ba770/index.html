About

Taylor Furniture is a furniture store at 11000 Highway 63 in Moss Point, Mississippi. The store carries bedroom, dining, living, and kids furniture, plus mattresses and bedding, from brands including Ashley, Vaughan Bassett, Liberty, Sealy, Simmons, Riverside, Flexsteel, Craftmaster, and Mayo. Delivery runs across the Mississippi Gulf Coast. The store's own site is a thin single page, so this concept shows what a fuller site could look like.

What the store carries

Bedroom furniture

Living room furniture

Sofas, chairs, and living room pieces.

Kids furniture

Furniture sized for kids rooms.

Mattresses and bedding

Mattresses from Sealy and Simmons, plus bedding.

Delivery across the Gulf Coast

Delivery covers Gautier, Pascagoula, Moss Point, Lucedale, Ocean Springs, Vancleave, D'Iberville, Biloxi, and Gulfport. Call (228) 475-6364 or email sales@taylorfurnitureonline.com to ask about a delivery.

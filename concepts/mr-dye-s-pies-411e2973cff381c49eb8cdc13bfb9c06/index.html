Founded by Johnathan Dye

Mr. Dye's Pies is a handcrafted pie shop inside 3rd Street Market Hall in downtown Milwaukee, founded by Johnathan Dye. The shop sells slices, halves, and whole pies, along with savory pasties, breads, Irish sea moss gel, and elderberry syrup. Hours are Sunday 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM, Monday through Thursday 11:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and Friday and Saturday 11:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Call (414) 759-4992.

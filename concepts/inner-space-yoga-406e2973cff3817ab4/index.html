A studio built around the heart

Inner Space Yoga is a heart-centered yoga, movement, and healing arts studio in Kansas City’s Union Hill. Its offerings include Morning Tonic, Candle Lit Evening Yoga, movement with meditation, and monthly New Moon Sound Baths. Donation-based drop-ins run three times weekly, and memberships are available per the listing summary. The own site is a short welcome blurb plus contact block with no class schedule, prices, or booking, so call with questions. This is the Kansas City studio, not the Inner Space listings in Knoxville, Santa Ana, or the Bronx.

About

Kazoola Eatery and Entertainment is a soul food bar-grill and live music venue in downtown Mobile, Alabama, at 558 Dauphin St. The menu covers crispy wings, burgers, crawfish boil, and chicken and waffles, with brunch. The venue has an outdoor courtyard and an upstairs cigar lounge.

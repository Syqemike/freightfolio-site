About

H2K Nail Spa is a family-owned nail spa in Metairie, Louisiana, at 801 Veterans Memorial Boulevard, Suite B. The spa's pedicures include Classic, Deluxe, Triple Relaxation, Cannabis CBD, Gentlemen Supreme, and Volcano Detox, plus gel manicures including Japanese gel, acrylic nails, dip powder, nail art, and waxing, and Princess services for kids. The salon emphasizes cleanliness, rigorous hygiene, well-trained technicians, and affordable pricing. Walk-ins are welcome. Hours are Monday to Friday 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM and Saturday 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Call (504) 304-5985 to book.

The Shop

Angelo's Touch is a family barbershop on Norwood Street, run by a father and son who have been cutting hair in Everett for years. It is a clean, comfortable shop with well-lit mirrors and easy chairs, the kind of place where regulars settle in and first-timers are treated the same.

The barbers handle classic cuts as well as modern styles and trends. Whether you need a simple trim or a bold new look, the focus stays on the details of the cut and on you walking out satisfied.

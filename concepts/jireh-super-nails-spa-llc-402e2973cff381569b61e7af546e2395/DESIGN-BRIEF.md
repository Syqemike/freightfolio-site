# Design Brief: Jireh Super Nails & Spa LLC (Dundalk, MD)

## Aesthetic
SPA ZEN. Serene, ritual-focused, unhurried. The page should feel like walking
into a quiet room: soft light, warm neutrals, generous breathing space. Nothing
shouts. Distinct from batch-28 siblings: not editorial-plum asymmetric
(Crystal Nails), not bright coastal (Posh), not forest-green classic (Polished).

## Typography
- Display: Marcellus (Roman inscriptional serif, calm and elegant), headings only.
- Body: Karla (friendly, legible humanist sans). Wide letterspaced kickers for
  quiet rhythm.

## Palette
- Sage #7a8b6f (primary, links, accents)
- Warm stone #efe9e1 (section backgrounds, cards)
- Deep clay #a0714f (sparingly: buttons hover, key accents)
- Soft off-white #faf8f3 (page background)
- Ink #3a352e (body text)
No gradients, no glowing blobs, no decorative icons. Solid calm color fields and
hairline dividers only.

## Imagery plan

photos, no social, GBP not found). All 7 images are licensed stock photography
from Pexels, and EVERY caption on the site carries a "Stock photo" label:
- hero.jpg: nail polish bottles with eucalyptus (Pexels 7615876) -> index hero
- soak.jpg: warm foot soak with bath salts (Pexels 19695948) -> services/ritual
- footbath.jpg: petal foot bath in warm clay tones (Pexels 15949785) -> gallery
- manicure.jpg: nail filing close-up (Pexels 16041439) -> services manicures
- pedicure.jpg: pedicure session (Pexels 34930123) -> services pedicures
- candles.jpg: tealight candles, towel, stones (Pexels 1926811) -> visit hero

## Layout
- Full-width hero: layered soft photo with a centered translucent stone type
  panel (soft scrim, not a gradient), business name prominent, sign-reading note.
- Ritual-style service sections: single column, generous whitespace, wide image,
  calm heading, short plain paragraph, hairline divider between.
- Gallery: horizontal scroll-snap calm strip with captions.
- Visit: spa-like calm page: address, exact hours, phone, directions link.

## Customer journey
Land on a calm hero -> see the name, what they do, hours at a glance -> drift
through Services (three simple rituals) -> Gallery (atmosphere) -> Visit
(address, hours, call to book). One goal: get the visitor to call
(410) 284-2772. No online booking exists, so there is no "Book Now" button.

## Content constraints (grounded in research only)
- Name shown as "Jireh Super Nails & Spa" with note: signage reads "Super Nails".
- Services: manicures, pedicures, spa treatments only. No prices invented.
- Hours exactly: Mon-Thu 10:00 AM-7:00 PM; Fri 9:00 AM-7:00 PM;
  Sat 9:00 AM-6:00 PM; Sunday closed.
- Rating shown ONCE (index): "4.5 from 68 Google reviews".
- "Design Preview" disclosure near the top of every page; noindex meta.
- Copy: plain sentences, no em dashes, no banned AI phrases.

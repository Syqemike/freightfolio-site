About

Success By Design Learning Center is a tutoring and learning center in Mandeville, Louisiana, at 723 North Causeway Boulevard. Founder Cherry Smith leads private education, tutoring, test prep, paper editing, academic counseling, life skills coaching, and curriculum development, with personalized lesson plans for each student. The center supports homeschool and alternative-education families and is a certified proctor for BYU, Bob Jones University, and ACT exams. Hours are Monday to Thursday, 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM, and Friday, 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM. Call (985) 373-6590 to ask about a consultation.
